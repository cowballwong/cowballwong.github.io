HK Swept Path 泊車軌跡

Drop in a car park plan, mark the entrance, the exit and the bays, and your browser drives a vehicle from the Transport Department's design manual (TPDM) into every bay. Each bay turns green, amber or red against our reading of APP-111's "one distinct manoeuvre" guideline, with the clash shown where a vehicle cannot get in. Export the swept paths as layered DXF, PNG or a one-page PDF. A sketch-stage check before the plan goes to your traffic consultant.

放入停車場平面圖，標示入口、出口及車位，瀏覽器便會駕駛運輸署《運輸策劃及設計手冊》所列車輛駛入每一個車位，按我們對屋宇署 APP-111「一次清晰的操控動作」指引的理解，將每個車位評為綠、黃或紅，並於駛不進的位置標示碰撞範圍。掃掠軌跡可匯出為分層 DXF、PNG 或單頁 PDF。供草圖階段自行檢查，再交交通顧問。

    Click two points on a known dimension (a grid line, a bay, the scale bar), then type the distance.在一段已知尺寸（軸線、車位或比例尺）上點兩下，再輸入距離。

    Walls and columns牆及柱

    Dark, thick strokes become obstacles; thin lines (text, dimensions, bay lines) are dropped, and so are small filled shapes with a pointed end, such as aisle direction arrows. Every shape ignored this way is ringed in orange: check each ring, because an irregular shape there may be a real column. Walls drawn only as an outline with hatching are not detected: trace them with Wall, or the check drives straight through them. Check the grey overlay before you run.深色粗線會成為障礙物；幼線（文字、尺寸、車位線）及帶尖端的細小實心圖形（例如車道方向箭咀）會被略去。每個被略去的圖形均以橙圈標示：請逐一核對，因圈內的不規則圖形可能是真實的柱。只以輪廓加剖面線繪畫的牆不會被偵測：請用「牆」描出，否則檢查會直接穿牆而過。執行前請先核對灰色障礙物圖層。

    Assumption: 300 mm buffer is common practice, not an HK rule. Beside the bay being checked (structure and parked vehicles within 0.8 m of its long sides) the vehicle needs only 100 mm, or no HKPSG 2.5 m bay would pass.假設：300 毫米緩衝為一般做法，並非香港法定要求。正在檢查的車位兩旁（長邊外 0.8 米內的結構及已泊車輛）只要求 100 毫米，否則所有 2.5 米闊的標準車位都不會合格。

    DXF: SAVEAS DWG in your CAD. Opens in AutoCAD, BricsCAD, ArchiCAD and Revit. Same units and origin as your DXF; image plans export in mm from the plan's top-left corner.DXF：可於你的 CAD 另存為 DWG；AutoCAD、BricsCAD、ArchiCAD 及 Revit 均可開啟。單位及原點與你的 DXF 相同；圖片圖則以毫米匯出，原點為圖則左上角。

    How it works運作方式
    1. Plan. A PDF (page 1), PNG, JPG or DXF. Nothing is uploaded.
    2. Scale. Click two points on a known dimension and type it in mm (DXF: from the drawing units). Dark, thick strokes become walls and columns; brush, erase or add walls and columns where the tool got it wrong. Small filled pointed shapes such as aisle arrows are ignored and ringed in orange: check each ring; walls drawn only as a hatched outline must be traced with the Wall tool.
    3. Mark. Drag the entrance and the exit as arrows in the direction of travel. Drag bay rows along the aisle edge; bays snap to the HKPSG size of the vehicle you pick.
    4. Check. The vehicle drives in from the entrance to every bay and back out to the exit, with every other bay taken by a parked vehicle of the same type. Green: in with one distinct manoeuvre (at most one change of gear, no reversal of the steering lock on the final move) and out again, with the full buffer (300 mm; 100 mm beside the bay itself). Amber: needs extra shunts, or only fits with the smaller buffer. Red: cannot get in or out; the clash is painted where the template manoeuvre hits.
    5. Export. DXF with layers for bays, wheel paths, body envelope, clearance envelope, clashes, vehicle outlines and traced obstacles; PNG; a one-page A3 PDF.
    1. 圖則。PDF（第一頁）、PNG、JPG 或 DXF。檔案不會上載。
    2. 比例。在已知尺寸上點兩下並輸入毫米數（DXF 按圖則單位）。深色粗線會成為牆及柱；如有錯漏，可用畫筆、擦膠，或加牆、加柱修正。車道箭咀等帶尖端的細小實心圖形會被略去並以橙圈標示，請逐一核對；只以剖面線輪廓繪畫的牆須用「牆」工具描出。
    3. 標示。以箭咀拖出入口及出口的行車方向。沿車道邊拖出一排車位；車位按所選車輛的《香港規劃標準與準則》尺寸排列。
    4. 檢查。車輛由入口駛入每個車位，再駛往出口，其餘車位均假設已泊同類車輛。綠：以一次清晰的操控動作駛入（最多轉波一次，最後一步不需反方向扭軚），並可駛出，保持全數緩衝（300 毫米；車位兩旁 100 毫米）。黃：需額外前後移動，或只能以較小緩衝通過。紅：駛不進或駛不出；以標準操控示範碰撞位置。
    5. 匯出。DXF 分層包括車位、車輪軌跡、車身掃掠範圍、緩衝範圍、碰撞、車身輪廓及障礙物；另有 PNG 及單頁 A3 PDF。

    Limits限制
    • Not a submission非入則工具A sketch-stage check, not a substitute for a swept path analysis by your traffic consultant, and not accepted by any authority as such.只供草圖階段自行檢查，不能取代交通顧問的掃掠軌跡分析，亦不獲任何部門接納為正式文件。
    • Vehicle model車輛模型Low-speed bicycle (Ackermann) model; the trailer follows a tractrix. Steering can only change gradually while moving (full lock to full lock over about 2 m for a car) and freely when stopped. No tyre slip, no speed effects.低速單車（Ackermann）模型；拖架按曳物線計算。行車時扭軚有速度限制（私家車由左盡至右盡約 2 米），停車時可自由扭軚。不計輪胎側滑及車速影響。
    • Assumptions假設Marked "assumption" in the vehicle data: track = width − 0.25 m; coach axle positions; whether the TPDM "turning diameter swept" column is kerb to kerb; the 300 mm buffer; the steering rate. All editable.車輛數據中標示「假設」的數值：輪距 = 車闊 − 0.25 米；旅遊巴車軸位置；運輸署「掃掠迴轉直徑」是否路壆至路壆；300 毫米緩衝；扭軚速度。全部可以修改。
    • Accuracy準確度Obstacles sit on a 100 mm grid, so clearances are good to about ±70 mm; image plans also depend on your two scale clicks. Plan view only: no ramps, gradients or headroom.障礙物以 100 毫米網格表示，緩衝距離誤差約 ±70 毫米；圖片圖則亦取決於你點選比例的準確度。只計平面：不計斜道、坡度及淨高。
    • Search搜尋Each bay gets a search limit, a fixed number of search steps, so the result is the same on any device. "No path found within the search limit" means the search stopped, not that no path exists; "Cannot get in" or "Cannot get out" means it ran out of places to try at the buffer and gear changes allowed. Amber or red can also mean a cleaner path exists that the search did not reach; those bays say so, and their grade can change when anything near the car park is edited, even outside its walls. On our 66-bay one-way loop test plan, 62 bays came out green and 4 amber, each amber marked as a search that stopped at its limit. The painted clash shows the standard manoeuvre. Measured on a recent laptop: sample A, 42 bays, about 26 s; a 66-bay one-way loop, about 110 s. Red-heavy plans, phones and Safari take longer; you can cancel at any time.每個車位均有搜尋上限（固定的搜尋步數），因此在任何裝置上結果相同。「在搜尋上限內找不到路徑」代表搜尋已停止，並非證明不可行；「駛不進」或「駛不出」代表在容許的緩衝及轉波次數下已無其他可能。黃色或紅色亦可能代表有更簡單的路線而搜尋未能找到，這類車位會註明，而其評級亦可能因停車場附近的任何修改而改變，即使修改位於牆外。在我們 66 個車位的單程環形車道測試圖中，62 個車位評為綠色、4 個為黃色，每個黃色車位均註明搜尋已達上限。紅色碰撞範圍以標準操控示範。於近年的手提電腦實測：示例 A（42 個車位）約 26 秒；66 個車位的單程環形車道約 110 秒。紅色車位多的圖則、手機及 Safari 需時較長；可隨時取消。
    • Drawing圖則Walls drawn only as an outline with hatching (typical blockwork) are not detected; left untraced, the vehicle drives through them and bays can show a false green. Small filled shapes with a pointed end (aisle arrows, arrowheads) are ignored; L-shaped, T-shaped and other irregular columns are kept. Every ignored shape is ringed in orange and must be checked: if one is a real column or wall stub (a wedge-shaped one, say), add it back with Column or Wall.只以輪廓加剖面線繪畫的牆（常見於磚牆）不會被偵測；若未描出，車輛會穿牆而過，車位可能被誤評為綠色。帶尖端的細小實心圖形（車道箭咀、箭頭）會被略去；L 形、T 形及其他不規則的柱會保留。每個被略去的圖形均以橙圈標示，必須逐一核對：如屬真實的柱或短牆（例如楔形），請用「柱」或「牆」加回。
    • Traffic direction行車方向One-way flow inside the car park is not modelled: only the entrance and exit carry a direction, so a path may drive against a one-way aisle.不計停車場內的單程行車方向：只有入口及出口設有方向，因此路線有可能逆向駛經單程車道。
