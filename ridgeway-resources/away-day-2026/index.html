1 Creation 創造

God saw all that he had made, and it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning — the sixth day. … By the seventh day God had finished the work he had been doing; so on the seventh day he rested from all his work. Then God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it he rested from all the work of creating that he had done. Genesis 1:31 – 2:3 · 創世記 1 章 31 節至 2 章 3 節

Sabbath as the climax of creation. Not because God was tired, but to set a pattern: completion, sufficiency, "very good".

安息是創造的頂點。並不是因為神疲累，而是祂以「歇工」為這個世界蓋上「甚好」的印章。安息本身就是創造的一部分。

2 Fall 墮落

Cursed is the ground because of you; through painful toil you will eat food from it all the days of your life. … By the sweat of your brow you will eat your food until you return to the ground. Genesis 3:17 – 19 · 創世記 3 章 17 至 19 節

Three downward steps after the Fall:

  • Restless wandering — Cain
  • Oppression & violence — Noah's flood era
  • Slavery of Babylon — Babel
"Come, let's make bricks and bake them thoroughly." … "Come, let us build ourselves a city, with a tower that reaches to the heavens, so that we may make a name for ourselves." Genesis 11:3 – 4 · 創世記 11 章 3 至 4 節

「磚」是與安息相反的記號。由巴別塔開始，「燒磚等於奴役」這個符號在聖經會反覆出現。

神給亞伯拉罕一塊安息之地的應許，但同時預告：應許實現之前必須先經歷奴役與救贖。

📖 Talk 1 · Rest in the Old Testament 第一講 · 舊約裏面的安息
THEMATIC OVERVIEW: REST
主題聖經導覽：安息
Introduction
引言
Rest is very topical at the moment. People are tired. We live in a 24/7 interconnected world. You can always log on and check your work emails. And lots of people work from home … the workplace has invaded the home, so work is always there. The cost-of-living crisis means people feel they need to work harder to earn enough or to secure a job. And there are so many other pressures in modern life, children and all their activities, social engagements, hobbies, staying up to date with what’s going on in the news and culture.
「安息」這個題目近來十分切身。人人都疲倦。我們生活在一個 24/7 高度連繫的世界，隨時可以登入查看公司電郵；很多人在家工作，工作場所入侵了家庭，工作好像永遠擺脫不了。生活成本上升，令人覺得要更努力工作才能掙得足夠收入或保住飯碗。再加上現代生活還有許多其他壓力——子女與其活動、社交應酬、興趣愛好、追上時事與文化潮流。
And the reality is that the church can add to that … there are always rotas to serve on, more things to do. Because we’re encouraged to serve, and to serve sacrificially, that can mean that serving at church starts to become a long list of more things to do.
而現實是，教會也可能加重這些重擔——總有事奉的編更，總有更多事情要做。因為我們被鼓勵要服事，並且要犧牲地服事，於是教會的服事，往往就變成了一張更長的「待辦清單」。
People are just tired, and so it’s important to rest.
人就是這樣疲倦，所以「休息」很重要。
Now that can sound very like the culture around us. Society is always telling us to look after ourselves, to set healthy boundaries, to eat healthy, to go to the gym, and to make sure we have enough me time… and there is, of course, some merit to that advice.
不過，這樣聽起來，很像我們身處的世俗文化所講。社會經常告訴我們要好好照顧自己、設定健康的界線、健康飲食、上健身房，還要確保有足夠的「自我時間」——這些建議，當然也有它一定的道理。
But in the Bible, Rest is more significant. It’s not just about physical rest. And it’s not primarily about ourselves. It’s spiritual, it’s about our relationship with God. God is the source of all good things, and so he is the source of true rest … which is not inactivity, but love, peace, mercy, harmony and a good relationship with God. It’s as we go to God as the source that we are fulfilled and find rest.
但聖經所講的「安息」，意義更深。它不單只是身體的休息，也不主要是關乎我們自己。它是屬靈的，是關乎我們與神的關係。神是一切美善的源頭，所以祂也是真正安息的源頭——這安息並不是甚麼也不做，而是愛、平安、憐憫、和諧、與神有美好的關係。當我們以神為源頭去尋求祂時，我們才能得到滿足與安息。
On the other hand, if we reject God. Then inevitably, we are drawn to other things. When people are seeking fulfilment in other things – comfort, security, pleasure, success – those things become idolatrous. And when people are consumed with desire for those things, they’ll do whatever it takes to get them … they can’t rest … they become slaves, driven by their desires. More than that, they become oppressive… when people are so determined to have things, that’s when we are likely to cut corners and trample on people to get their own way. There’s no rest for anyone; there’s violence and war.
反過來說，如果我們拒絕神，我們必然被其他事物吸引。當人在其他事物上尋求滿足——舒適、安全、享樂、成就——這些事物便成了偶像。當人被那些慾望牢牢抓住時，他們會不擇手段去得到——他們無法安息——他們成了奴僕，被自己的慾望驅使。不只如此，他們也會變得壓迫人——當人決心要得到一些東西時，便會走捷徑，踐踏別人去達到目的。沒有人能安息；只有暴力與爭戰。
So rest in the Bible is very important, not a side topic; it’s right at the heart of our relationship with God. If we can’t rest, it shows that we are spiritually sick.
所以聖經中的「安息」絕不是邊緣題目，而是我們與神關係的核心。如果我們無法安息，正正顯出我們屬靈上有病。
It’s a very important topic, but it can be a bit confusing. In the Old Testament, God’s people were told to keep the Sabbath, one day in seven, but in the New Testament, that doesn’t seem to be a major theme, and Christians today have different approaches. Some keep one day in seven special; they won’t shop on Sunday. Others are very relaxed. What’s the right answer, what should we do … what does rest look like today?
這個題目很重要，但也容易令人混亂。在舊約，神的子民被吩咐要守安息日——七日中的一日；但在新約，這個主題似乎不再那麼突出，今日的基督徒對這個題目也有不同的看法。有些人特別將七日中的一日分別出來——星期日不購物；有些人則很寬鬆。哪個答案才正確？我們今日應該怎樣行？「安息」在今日應該是甚麼樣子？
Well, to answer that question, we are going to do a bible overview … actually, a thematic bible overview of rest. You see, whenever we have a question, we should go to the Bible to find the answer, but there are different ways to do so.
要回答這個問題，今日我們會做一個聖經導覽——更準確地說，是一個關於「安息」的主題式聖經導覽。當我們有問題的時候，固然應該翻開聖經找答案，但「找答案」可以有幾種不同的方法。
One way is to find a few passages that deal with that topic quite thoroughly, and do a deep dive into those passages to answer the question.
第一種，是找幾段比較深入處理某個題目的經文，然後深入研讀，從中得出答案。
Another approach is systematic theology. That is to survey the key passages and then to lay out the findings logically, perhaps starting with a definition, what is rest … it’s the sort of approach you’ll find in theological textbooks.
另一種，是「系統神學」——就是把所有相關的關鍵經文掃過一次，再把結論按邏輯整理出來，可能由「甚麼是安息」這個定義開始——你在神學教科書裏面所見的，多數就是這種方法。
But what we are doing today is biblical theology. That’s just a fancy way of saying, tell me the story. That’s when you are thinking about a particular topic, start at the beginning of the Bible, and trace the storyline all the way to the end. You can do for the Bible story as a whole, that’s a bible overview, and you can do it for a theme, that’s a thematic bible overview. You can do it for any topic, and the advantage is that it lets us see what the Bible is saying without imposing our own framework.
但今日我們做的，叫做「聖經神學」。其實只是一個比較學術的講法，意思就是「講故事畀我聽」。當你在思考一個主題時，由聖經第一頁開始，將整條故事線一直追蹤到最後一頁。可以針對整本聖經的故事——這就是「聖經導覽」；也可以針對某一個主題——這就是「主題式聖經導覽」。任何題目都可以這樣做。它的好處，是讓我們純粹聆聽聖經自己在說甚麼，而不會強加自己的框架。
Now, when you take this approach, it’s common to divide the Bible story into different sections. Different people divide the story slightly differently, but most have seven, eight or nine different sections. I’ve gone with eight, five in the Old Testament: 1) Creation, 2) Fall, 3) Promise, 3) Exodus, 4) Kingdom, 5) Exile, and three in the New Testament: 6) Christ, 7) Church, 8) New Creation. This is quite a common way to do things, and at each stage, we’ll see what we learn about rest.
當你採用這種方法時，通常會將聖經故事分成不同階段。每個人的分法略有不同，但大多數人會分為七、八，或九個階段。我選了八個——舊約五個：第一、創造；第二、墮落；第三、應許；第四、出埃及；第五、王國；第六、被擄。新約三個：第七、基督；第八、教會；第九、新創造。這是頗常見的分法。在每個階段，我們都會看一看聖經教導我們關於「安息」的甚麼。
Now that’s what we are doing. If you have never done this kind of thing, I hope you’ll see that this is a great way to answer any big question – start at the beginning and trace it through.
這就是我們今日要做的事。如果你從未試過這種讀經方式，希望你能看見：要回答任何重大問題，這都是一個很好的方法——由起頭開始，順著故事線一直追下去。
And it’s very good for thinking about this topic of rest, because it brings out some helpful details we might not have realised. Now, if you have flicked through the handout, you might have noticed that I’ve put quite a lot of details in there. Apologies if this seems overwhelming … we won’t get bogged down in the details, the goal is for us to pick up the overall story, but it’s easier for us to do that if we have the key verses to hand.
而對於「安息」這個題目，這方法尤其有用，因為它會帶出一些我們未必留意過的細節。如果你翻過手上的講義，可能已經發覺我放了不少資料在裏面。如果看起來有點吃力，請見諒——我們不會迷失在細節裡，目標是抓住整個大故事；只是有了關鍵經文在手，會比較容易做到。
So without any further ado … let’s get started.
那麼，閒話休提，我們開始。
1. CREATION
一、創造
The story of rest starts with creation. Right at the beginning, God worked, and God rested. Look with me at Genesis 1;31:
關於「安息」的故事，由創造開始。最起初，神工作，然後神安息。請與我一同看創世記 1 章 31 節：
31 God saw all that he had made, and it was very good. And there was evening, and there was morning—the sixth day. 2 Thus the heavens and the earth were completed in all their vast array. 2 By the seventh day God had finished the work he had been doing; so on the seventh day he rested from all his work. 3 Then God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it he rested from all the work of creating that he had done.
「神看着一切所造的都甚好，有晚上、有早晨，是第六日。天地萬物都造齊了。到第七日，神造物的工已經完畢，就在第七日歇了祂一切的工，安息了。神賜福給第七日，定為聖日，因為在這日，神歇了祂一切創造的工，就安息了。」
We see here that God worked. Work is not a bad thing; it is a good thing. God is a God who invents and creates things. He’s made us in his image, and so we work too. Work was originally designed to be good. But after God had worked, he rested. Now can’t be because God is tired. After all, God is an eternal God; he doesn’t have a body that can get tired in the first place. So what does it mean for God to rest?
我們在這裏看到——神工作。工作不是壞事，工作本身是好的。神是一位發明、創造的神。祂按祂的形象造了我們，所以我們也工作。工作起初被設計為美好的事。但神工作之後，祂安息了。這並不是因為神疲倦——畢竟祂是永恆的神，祂沒有會疲倦的身體。那麼神安息又是甚麼意思呢？
Well, rest is about the relationship. God stops working to enjoy what he has made. We see that in chapter three, where we are told that God used to come to walk in the Garden of Eden with Adam and Eve. The rest is relationship rest. This, you might say, is the goal, or the purpose of creation. The purpose of creation wasn’t for God to just relentlessly keep doing and doing and doing … but to be, to enjoy what he has made.
「安息」是關乎關係的。神停下祂的工，去享受祂所造的一切。我們在第三章看到，神曾與亞當夏娃在伊甸園中同行。這個安息，是「關係的安息」。你可以說，這正是創造的目標、創造的目的——並不是要神不停地做、做、做，而是要去「存在」、去享受祂所造的一切。
So too our purpose in life is not to do things, but to be, most of all to be in the relationship with god who made us. That’s how God made the world, but sadly, it didn’t stay like this for very long. Before long, there was the fall … Adam and Eve turned from god and went their own way.
同樣地，我們人生的目的，不單是去做事，而是去「存在」——最重要的，是與創造我們的那位神同在。這是神對世界的設計。但很可惜，事情並沒有維持很久。不久之後，便發生了「墮落」——亞當夏娃從神面前轉身，走自己的路。
FALL
二、墮落
Look with me at verse seventeen: 17 To Adam he said, “Because you listened to your wife and ate fruit from the tree about which I commanded you, ‘You must not eat from it,’
請看第十七節：「神又對亞當說：『你既聽從妻子的話，吃了我所吩咐你不可吃的那樹上的果子，地必為你的緣故受咒詛；你必終身勞苦，才能從地裏得吃的。地必給你長出荊棘和蒺藜來，你也要吃田間的菜蔬。你必汗流滿面才得糊口，直到你歸回了土，因為你是從土而出的。你本是塵土，仍要歸於塵土。』」
“Cursed is the ground because of you;
    through painful toil you will eat food from it
    all the days of your life.
18 It will produce thorns and thistles for you,
    and you will eat the plants of the field.
19 By the sweat of your brow you will eat your food
until you return to the ground,
    since from it you were taken;
for dust you are and to dust you will return
突然之間，一切都改變了。人類沒有與神保持愛的關係，反倒背叛祂，選擇了魔鬼一邊，與神為敵。伊甸園的和諧破滅，他們開始彼此責備、彼此爭吵。
Suddenly, everything changed. Instead of being in a loving relationship with God, humanity rebelled against him, and sided with the devil, and became his enemies. The harmony of the Garden of Eden was broken, and they started blaming and arguing with each other.
神必須懲罰他們——他們犯了叛國罪，要受叛國之罰，就是死刑——他們被定了死罪。但因為他們拒絕了神的安息，所以他們也被定了痛苦勞碌之罰。他們既然不肯接受伊甸園裏和諧的安息，便被趕出伊甸園。在園外，生活與工作會變得艱難，安息也大大減少。
God had to punish them … which was treason; they had to face the punishment for treason, which is the death penalty; they were condemned to die. But because they had rejected God’s rest, they are also condemned to painful work. They wouldn’t accept harmony's rest in the Garden of Eden, so they were cast out of Eden, where life and work would be difficult, and where there wouldn’t be much rest.
所以重要的是要明白：我們所感受到的疲倦，部分是來自神——神將人類趕離祂的同在、趕出伊甸園，所以人生永遠不會完全輕鬆。即使你樣樣做得正確，這個世界仍然會有疲憊，因為我們在伊甸園以外，活在咒詛之下。但聖經告訴我們，神這樣做有美善的用意——就是要讓我們醒覺過來——以痛楚的方式提醒我們：罪是不對的。
So it’s important to realise that part of the tiredness we have comes from God … God has sent humanity out of his presence, out of Eden, so life is never going to be completely easy. Even if do everything right, there will still be exhaustion in this world, because we are out of Eden and under a curse. And the Bible shows us that God did this with good intentions, to bring us to our senses … a painful reminder that sin is wrong.
這聽起來可能還不太糟，但如果你讀過創世記頭幾章，你就知道事情很快變得更壞。爭吵變成謀殺。該隱殺了亞伯，被定為「在地上流離飄盪」。但比這更壞的還在後頭——暴力與拜偶像不斷蔓延，到了第六章，神便宣告：「人終日所思想的盡都是惡。」因為世界變得如此壓迫與不公義，神便降下洪水，重新洗白一切，並且救了挪亞。但即使如此，惡仍繼續敗壞世界。
Now that might not sound so bad, but if you have read the opening chapters of Genesis, you know that things quickly escalate from there. Arguments turn to murder. Cain kills his brother Abel and is condemned to be a restless wanderer on the earth. But things get even worse than that … violence and idolatry continue and continue, until in chapter five we are told in chapter six that every inclination was evil all the time. Because the world was so oppressive and unjust, God sent the flood to wipe the slate clean … and he rescued Noah … but even then … evil continued to ruin the world.
到第十一章，是巴別塔的故事——「巴別」其實就是「巴比倫」的另一個名字。他們的目標，是為自己「傳揚名聲」：
In chapter eleven, we have the story of the Tower of Babel, which is just another name for Babylon. Their goal is to build a name for themselves …
「他們彼此商量說：『來吧，我們要做磚，把磚燒透了。』他們就拿磚當石頭，又拿石漆當灰泥。他們說：『來吧，我們要建造一座城和一座塔，塔頂通天，為要傳揚我們的名，免得我們分散在全地上。』」
They said to each other, “Come, let’s make bricks and bake them thoroughly.” They used brick instead of stone, and tar for mortar. 4 Then they said, “Come, let us build ourselves a city, with a tower that reaches to the heavens, so that we may make a name for ourselves; otherwise we will be scattered over the face of the whole earth.”
很明顯，這個工程不只是宏大，更是拜偶像。他們大量造磚，要建第一座摩天大樓——巴比倫塔。這聽起來，跟今日的人毫無分別——人因為野心要建最高的樓、做最大的公司、賺最多的錢。當人類被這種野心驅動時，工作必然變成奴役，社會必然變得壓迫。在聖經接下來的章節裏，「巴比倫」就成了這種壓迫制度的象徵。
It’s clear that this project is not just ambitious, but idolatrous. They are making massive amounts of bricks to make the first skyscraper, the Tower of Babylon… sounds just like people today, who are driven by their ambition to build the tallest tower, or the biggest company, or earn the most money. When humanity is driven by ambition like this … inevitably work becomes slavery, and society becomes oppressive, and that is what Babylon becomes symbolic of in the rest of the bible. Now, as the whole world becomes engrossed in this race to build cities … God calls one man to leave a city.
然而，當整個世界都瘋狂地追求建造城市時，神呼召一個人——離開那座城。
PROMISE
三、應許
He calls Abraham … look with me at chapter 12:1-2: The Lord had said to Abram, “Go from your country, your people and your father’s household to the land I will show you. 2 “I will make you into a great nation and I will bless you; I will make your name great, and you will be a blessing.
祂呼召亞伯拉罕。請看 12 章 1 至 2 節：「耶和華對亞伯蘭說：『你要離開本地、本族、父家，往我所要指示你的地去。我必叫你成為大國，我必賜福給你，叫你的名為大；你也要叫別人得福。』」
God promised to make Abraham into a great nation, to give him a land and to bless him. So Abraham left Ur, a big city, to go to a far-off land that was unimpressive … but a land that would be a place of rest. Now it doesn’t explicitly say rest in these verses; it says blessing, but that’s part of blessing, rest in God. And in other parts of the Bible, as we’ll see, we are told that this land is meant to be a land of rest.
神應許要使亞伯拉罕成為大國，賜他一塊地，並賜福給他。於是亞伯拉罕離開了大城市吾珥，前往一塊看似毫不起眼、卻將會成為「安息之地」的遙遠之地。這幾節經文沒有用「安息」這個字，只用「賜福」；但「安息」其實是「賜福」的一部分——在神裏面的安息。聖經其他地方亦會提及，這塊地確實被命定為「安息之地」。
And so Abraham believes God and trusts him to leave the big city of Ur and go to the promised land, where God says he will guide him. Now God takes him to the promised land … and he has a son called Jacob, and Jacob has a son called Joseph … and Joseph, as you know, was the son with the fancy coat, who was sold to Egypt.
於是亞伯拉罕信神、信靠祂，離開了大城吾珥，前往應許之地，由神親自帶領。神帶他到應許之地，他生了一個兒子叫雅各，雅各又生了約瑟——約瑟，就是那個有彩衣、被賣到埃及的兒子。
Now, all this time, God was working his perfect plan to use Joseph to rescue his people from a terrible famine that was coming, so he had deliberately sent Joseph there in advance. And at the same time, God knew that eventually, his people would be enslaved in Egypt. That wasn’t a mistake, that wasn’t a failure; it was God deliberately at work.
在那段日子裏，神一直在執行祂完美的計劃——要藉約瑟在饑荒臨到之前先一步預備，拯救祂的子民。同時，神也清楚知道，祂的子民最終會在埃及作奴隸。那不是失誤，也不是失敗，那是神蓄意安排的工。
Then the Lord said to him, “Know for certain that for four hundred years your descendants will be strangers in a country not their own and that they will be enslaved and mistreated there.”
「耶和華對亞伯蘭說：『你要的確知道，你的後裔必寄居別人的地，又服事那地的人；那地的人要苦待他們四百年。』」
And so they went to Egypt … God knew everything that would happen. And in Egypt, they did indeed become slaves, but this was all part of God’s plan to rescue his people.
於是他們去了埃及——神早已知道一切將要發生的事。在埃及，他們果然成了奴隸。但這一切，都是神拯救祂子民計劃的一部分。
4 EXODUS
四、出埃及
This is like what we had already seen at Babel, Babylon, slavery, and making bricks for building projects:
這就重複了我們在巴別、巴比倫所看見的圖畫——奴役、做磚、建造工程：
They made their lives bitter with harsh labour in brick and mortar and with all kinds of work in the fields; in all their harsh labour, the Egyptians worked them ruthlessly.
「埃及人嚴嚴的使以色列人作工，使他們因作苦工覺得命苦；無論是和泥、是作磚、是作田間各樣的工，在一切的工上都嚴嚴的待他們。」
There they were ruthlessly worked by the Egyptians, in bondage to them. Egypt was like another kind of Babylon. But God by his great hand rescued them through Moses … he sent the ten plagues, he destroyed Pharaoh's army in the Red Sea, and he brought them to Mount Sinai.
埃及人狠狠地驅使他們，使他們成為奴僕。埃及就像另一個巴比倫。但神以祂大能的手藉摩西拯救他們——降下十災，在紅海毀滅法老的軍隊，將他們帶到西奈山。
Now at Mount Sinai, God made a covenant with them. Almost like a wedding promise. He promised to look after and care for them. And the people promised to live God’s way.
在西奈山，神與他們立約——幾乎像一個婚約一樣。神應許要看顧他們、眷愛他們；百姓也應許要按神的方式生活。
This wasn’t living God’s way to earn their way into heaven. Sometimes people have the mistaken idea that in the Old Testament, they did the right thing to get into heaven; that’s not true. They were rescued from their old way of living to live for God.
這不是靠遵守律法去掙取進入天國的門票。有時人有一個錯誤的想法，以為舊約的人是靠做對事情來換取得救——這是不真實的。他們其實是被神拯救出來，脫離舊有的生活方式，從此為神而活。
It’s the same today; we can’t earn our way into heaven. No, we’ve been rescued, and once we're rescued, we are required to follow God’s way.
今日也是一樣——我們不能靠自己賺取進入天國的門票。不能。我們是被拯救的，而被拯救之後，我們要按神的方式生活。
Now, in the Old Testament… they didn’t have the Holy Spirit… instead, they had God’s law. And God’s law required that they should rest.
在舊約時代，他們沒有聖靈，只有神的律法。而神的律法要求他們要安息。
This was the fourth of the commandments:
第四條誡命這樣說：
8 “Remember the Sabbath day by keeping it holy. 9 Six days you shall labour and do all your work, 10 but the seventh day is a sabbath to the Lord your God … For in six days the Lord made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, but he rested on the seventh day.
「當記念安息日，守為聖日。六日要勞碌作你一切的工，但第七日是向耶和華你神當守的安息日……因為六日之內，耶和華造天、地、海，和其中的萬物，第七日便安息。」
Creation rest: Do you see the rationale… God is one who rests, and you are now his people; you shouldn’t be like the nations around you; you should rest like God. This is a creation pattern.
「創造的安息」——你看見當中的根據了嗎？神是一位安息的神，而你現在是祂的子民，你不要像你周圍的列邦——你應該像神那樣安息。這是一個「創造的模式」。
So this creation pattern is one of the reasons. The other reason is redemption rest; we see this in Deuteronomy, where the Ten Commandments are restated, but a different reason is given for the Sabbath …
這是其中一個原因。另一個原因是「救贖的安息」。我們在申命記裏看到這一點——十誡在那裏被重新述說，但卻給安息日提出了不同的理由：
Remember that you were slaves in Egypt and that the Lord your God brought you out of there with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. Therefore the Lord your God has commanded you to observe the Sabbath day.
「你也要記念你在埃及地作過奴僕；耶和華你神用大能的手和伸出來的膀臂將你從那裏領出來。因此，耶和華你的神吩咐你守安息日。」
Redemption rest: this is redemption risk … they were slaves in Egypt, but now God had rescued them, so they were not to live like slaves any more. They had been redeemed.
「救贖的安息」——這是救贖的記念。他們從前在埃及作奴隸，現在被神拯救出來——所以他們不要再像奴隸般生活。他們已經被贖回了。
So the Sabbath is tied to two big reasons: God rested, and it’s a creation pattern. And God saved you, you are free, so don’t act as slaves.
所以安息日承擔著兩個重大原因：神安息了——這是創造的模式；神救了你——你是自由人，不要再像奴隸般生活。
It was in the Ten Commandments, so they had to do it, but actually, because this idea of rest for God’s people was SO vital, there was quite a lot of else that Moses taught the people about rest …
這是十誡的一部分，所以他們必須遵守。但其實，因為「神子民的安息」這個觀念是如此重要，摩西還教導百姓許多其他關於安息的事。
Such as sabbath rest for the land: “For six years you are to sow your fields and harvest the crops, 11 but during the seventh year let the land lie unplowed and unused. Then the poor among your people may get food from it, and the wild animals may eat what is left. Do the same with your vineyard and your olive grove.
譬如「地土的安息」：「六年你要耕種田地，收藏土產，只是第七年要叫地歇息，不耕不種，使你民中的窮人有吃的；他們所剩下的，野獸可以吃。你的葡萄園和橄欖園，也要照樣辦理。」
This is good farm practice; if you plant the same crop in the same field year after year, it leeches out the nutrients. And so every seven years, there was meant to be a sabbath rest for the land. Because God’s people were not to always be chasing getting more, they were to practice restraint and even give the land a chance to rest.
這也是優良的耕作原則——年復年在同一塊田裏種同一種作物，土地會枯竭。所以每七年，田地要有一個安息。神的子民不應該不停追求更多——他們要操練節制，甚至讓地土也能歇息。
And because this was so important, Moses described it as a sign:
而因為這樣重要，摩西將安息日描述為一個「記號」：
15 For six days work is to be done, but the seventh day is a day of sabbath rest, holy to the Lord. Whoever does any work on the Sabbath day is to be put to death. 16 The Israelites are to observe the Sabbath, celebrating it for the generations to come as a lasting covenant. 17 It will be a sign between me and the Israelites forever, for in six days the Lord made the heavens and the earth, and on the seventh day he rested and was refreshed.’”
「六日要做工，但第七日是安息聖日，是向耶和華守為聖的；凡在安息日做工的，必要把他治死。故此，以色列人要世世代代守安息日為永遠的約。這是我和以色列人永遠的證據；因為六日之內耶和華造天地，第七日便安歇舒暢。」
Sabbath was so important that breaking the sabbath carried a death penalty. Now that might seem rather extreme … why would working on the Sabbath be such a big deal? The reason is that it was a sign of the covenant. God had rescued them from slavery and brought them to the promised land. To work all the time would be to return to slavery, and to return to the rebellion of Eden.
安息日是如此重要，以致違反安息日要被治死。可能你會覺得這太過嚴苛——為何在安息日做工，就是這麼嚴重的事？因為這是「立約的記號」。神將他們從奴役中救出來，帶他們進入應許之地。如果他們不停工作，就等於回去做奴隸，等於重蹈伊甸園的悖逆。
So Sabbath was meant to be taken seriously by every individual, but it also had a communal aspect. Because every Sabbath, they would rest, enjoy time together, and spend time with God together. And you see that in the other celebrations that were part of the Jewish calendar.
所以安息日不只是個人要認真守，它也有「群體的層面」——每個安息日，他們會一同休息、一同享受時光、一同與神親近。我們也能在猶太曆其他節期看到這一點：
4 “‘These are the Lord’s appointed festivals … The Lord’s Passover … For seven days present a food offering to the Lord. And on the seventh day hold a sacred assembly and do no regular work.”
「耶和華的節期，你們要宣告為聖會的，乃是我的節期……七月初一日，你們要守為聖安息日，要吹角作紀念，當有聖會。」
Now this passage mentions Passover, but the rest of the chapter mentions all the other festivals (& Firstfruits), Pentecost, Tabernacles, Trumpets, and Day of Atonement. Now we don’t have time to go into all of these … but these were all holidays where no work was allowed, so they were special sabbaths. And some of them were extended holidays. Three times a year, at Passover, Pentecost, and Tabernacles, God’s people were meant to travel to Jerusalem to have a week-long Sabbath. Each of these was after a different harvest, so people had just been working hard, and it was time to rest, feast, and worship. So there was this community aspect to sabbath rest. And there was also a justice aspect to rest. True sabbath rest meant peace and care for one another.
這段提到逾越節，但本章還提到許多其他節期——初熟節、五旬節、住棚節、吹角節、贖罪日。我們今日無法逐一講解，但所有這些都是不可作工的假期，所以全部都是「特別的安息日」。其中有些更是延長假期——一年三次，在逾越節、五旬節、住棚節，神的子民要上耶路撒冷度過一個一星期長的安息。每一次都是在某個收成之後——他們剛剛努力工作了一段日子，是時候休息、宴樂、敬拜。所以安息日是有「群體層面」的；它也有「公義層面」——真正的安息日意味著彼此和睦、彼此眷顧。
“‘Count off seven sabbath years—seven times seven years—so that the seven sabbath years amount to a period of forty-nine years. 9 Then have the trumpet sounded everywhere on the tenth day of the seventh month; on the Day of Atonement sound the trumpet throughout your land. 10 Consecrate the fiftieth year and proclaim liberty throughout the land to all its inhabitants. It shall be a jubilee for you; each of you is to return to your family property and to your own clan.
「你要計算七個安息年，就是七七年；這便為你成了七個安息年，共是四十九年。當年七月初十日，你要大發角聲；這日就是贖罪日，要在遍地發出角聲。第五十年，你們要當作聖年，在遍地給一切的居民宣告自由。這年必為你們的禧年，各人要歸自己的產業，各歸本家。」
So every seventh year was meant to be a rest year for the land, but after seven times seven years, that’s forty-nine, the fiftieth year was a jubilee year. So all of the land sold on long-term lease went back to the original owners. And all of the people who had put themselves under contract to work for someone else were released. This happened on the day of atonement, the holiest day of the calendar. Now, this system was important because it prevented people from buying up all the land and creating a system with some mega-wealthy people and some destitute people. After fifty years, all the land reverted to its original powers.
每七年是地土的安息年；而七乘七等於四十九，第五十年就是「禧年」。所有以長期租約賣出的田地，都歸還給原本的擁有者；所有將自己賣身為僕的人，都得釋放。這發生在贖罪日——一年中最神聖的一日。這個制度非常重要，因為它防止有人壟斷所有土地，造成極富者與赤貧者並存的社會。每五十年，所有土地就會回到原來的擁有者手中。
So hopefully when we put all those things together showed that after the people of israel came out of Egypt, moses laid the blueprint for what their new society should be like … and sabbath was a really key part of that … not just resting one day in seven, but rest for the land, a sign of God’s relationship with his people, a celebratory aspect to it, and a social justice aspect to it.
希望將這些放在一起後，我們看見：以色列人離開埃及之後，摩西為他們新的社會立下了藍圖——而「安息」是其中極關鍵的一塊：不只是七日中休一日，也包括地土的安息、神與祂子民立約的記號、慶典的層面、社會公義的層面。
And it comes back to this idea, that if you turn from God, you end up chasing idols. And if you chase idols, you are driven by your personal desires … that means you can’t rest, and you use and abuse the resources, like the land, and you use and abuse the people around you, they are all expendable. But when you come to God as you should, and rest in him, then you can live in harmony and celebrate life together.
這又回到我們之前的觀念——如果你轉離神，你必然追逐偶像；如果你追逐偶像，你便被自己的慾望驅使——這意味著你無法安息，你會利用、濫用資源（譬如地土），也會利用、濫用身邊的人——他們對你而言都是可丟棄的。但當你正確地來到神面前，在祂裏面安息時，你便能夠彼此和睦地生活，並一同慶祝生命。
That was the blueprint for Israelite society, but sadly, it never worked out. The first generation who had seen the miracles in Egypt rebelled against God and turned from him. Writing years later, David wrote of them in Psalm 95, a very famous psalm.
這便是以色列社會的藍圖。但很可惜，從來沒有真正實現。第一代見過埃及神蹟的人，背叛神、轉離祂。多年之後，大衛在詩篇 95 篇——一首非常著名的詩篇——這樣描寫他們：
10 For forty years I was angry with that generation; I said, ‘They are a people whose hearts go astray, and they have not known my ways.’ 11 So I declared on oath in my anger, ‘They shall never enter my rest.’
「四十年之久，我厭煩那世代，說：『這是心裏迷糊的百姓，竟不曉得我的作為。』所以我在怒中起誓，說：『他們斷不可進入我的安息！』」
They were meant to enter the promised land, which was symbolic of rest … but they never did, because they rejected God and turned from him. And from there, things continued to go badly.
他們本應進入應許之地——那地就是「安息」的象徵——但他們沒有進入，因為他們拒絕神、轉離祂。從那時起，事情繼續往不好的方向走。
5 kINGDOM
五、王國
Now, they did make it into the promised land under Joshua, and eventually, they became a kingdom, the kingdom of Israel. But there was never real rest. At best, there was partial rest. Even Joshua, one of the greatest leaders, wasn’t able to give them rest; that’s why Hebrews says:
雖然他們最終在約書亞帶領下進入應許之地，並且建立了「以色列王國」，但他們從來沒有得到真正的安息。最多只是部分的安息。即使是約書亞，這位偉大的領袖，也未能給他們安息。所以希伯來書這樣說：
For if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken later about another day.
「若是約書亞已叫他們享了安息，後來神就不再提別的日子了。」
The point is that Joshua was a great military leader who brought them into the land and gave them some rest from their enemies, but of course, it wasn’t complete rest, because the prophets still spoke of rest to come.
意思是：約書亞是一位偉大的軍事領袖，將他們帶入應許之地，使他們從仇敵手中得到一些安息。但這當然不是完全的安息——因為先知後來仍然在預言「將要來的安息」。
Similarly, King David was a great military leader, and gave them some partial rest in the land from enemies:
同樣，大衛王也是一位偉大的軍事領袖，使百姓在應許之地得到從仇敵而來的部分安息：
7 After [King David] was settled in his palace and the Lord had given him rest from all his enemies around him, 2 he said to Nathan the prophet, “Here I am, living in a house of cedar, while the ark of God remains in a tent.”
「王住在自己宮中，耶和華使他安靜，不被四圍的仇敵擾亂。王對先知拿單說：『看哪，我住在香柏木的宮中，神的約櫃反在幔子裏。』」
David defeated enemies, established the kingdom, built up Jerusalem and wanted to build a temple … but it was just a partial time of peace and rest.
大衛擊敗仇敵、堅立王國、建立耶路撒冷，並渴望建造聖殿——但這也只是「部分的太平與安息」。
And it didn’t last long. His son Solomon, who started well as the wisest man on earth, made many mistakes. We tend to talk about the hundreds of wives he had that led his heart astray. But the thing that split the kingdom was his building projects.
而且為時不久。他的兒子所羅門起初是地上最有智慧的人，但犯了許多錯誤。我們經常講他那幾百個妻子如何引誘他的心離棄神。但真正令國家分裂的，其實是他的「建造工程」。
15 Here is the account of the forced labour King Solomon conscripted to build the Lord’s temple, his own palace, the terraces, the wall of Jerusalem, and Hazor, Megiddo and Gezer … Lower Beth Horon, 18 Baalath, and Tadmor in the desert … as well as all his store cities and the towns for his chariots and for his horses…
「所羅門王挑取服苦的人，是為建造耶和華的殿、自己的宮、米羅、耶路撒冷的城牆、夏瑣、米吉多、基色……下伯和崙、巴拉、和國中曠野裏的達莫，又建造他一切的積貨城、車城、馬兵的城……」
He was such a fanatical builder that he ended up oppressing his own people. So after he died, the people came to his son, cap in hand, desperate for the load to be lightened, saying:
他是個極狂熱的建造者，最終竟壓迫自己的子民。所以他死後，百姓來到他兒子面前，懇求他減輕重擔，說：
Your father put a heavy yoke on us, but now lighten the harsh labour and the heavy yoke he put on us, and we will serve you
「你父親使我們負重軛、作苦工，現在求你使我們作的苦工、負的重軛輕鬆些，我們就事奉你。」
But Rehoboam, instead of answering the people wisely, and saying, " Yes, I won’t continue to enslave and oppress you like my father did, instead of saying that, he answered harshly, saying, " My father scourged you with whips, I’ll scourge you with scorpions. A man in desperate need of a PR department. And so the kingdom split over the issue of the oppression of the people.
但羅波安沒有以智慧回應——沒有說「是的，我不會像我父親那樣繼續奴役、壓迫你們」——他卻嚴厲地回答：「我父親用鞭子責打你們，我要用蠍子鞭責打你們。」這個人實在很需要一個公關部門。於是國家就因「壓迫百姓」這件事分裂了。
And of course, this pattern continued. The prophets promised that God would bring judgment if it continued.
當然，這個模式繼續發展下去。先知警告：如果繼續這樣，神必降下審判。
13 “Woe to him who builds his palace by unrighteousness, his upper rooms by injustice, making his own people work for nothing, not paying them for their labour
「那行不義蓋房，行不公造樓，白白使用人的手工不給工價的，有禍了。」
17 But your eyes and your heart are set only on dishonest gain, on shedding innocent blood and on oppression and extortion.
「你的眼和你的心，專顧貪婪，流無辜人的血，行欺壓和強暴。」
It's this failure to be a place of justice that is one of the big accusations of the prophets. But it’s all linked together. If you reject God and his way … you inevitably turn to idols to get the things you want. And because you are driven by desires, you can’t keep the Sabbath, because you are a slave to your desires, and because you are a slave to your desires, you resort to oppression and injustice to get what you want.
「不能成為公義之地」——這是先知最大的指控之一。但所有事情其實是連在一起的：如果你拒絕神和祂的方式，必然會轉去拜偶像來得到你想要的東西；既然被慾望驅使，你就無法守安息日，因為你已經是慾望的奴隸；既然是慾望的奴隸，你就會用壓迫與不公來達到目的。
But keeping the Sabbath was a way of saying, I’m stopping to spend time with God and his people, because I’m not enslaved to my desires. I can stop.
但守安息日就等於在說：「我停下來，與神和祂的子民同在，因為我並非慾望的奴隸。我可以停下來。」
And interestingly, when the people are sent into exile, this is given as the reason. Remember that when the covenant was formed at Sinai, Moses had said that the people would face discipline if they failed to live God’s way. And the final punishment was exile.
有趣的是——當百姓被擄之時，原因正是這個。記住：在西奈山立約時，摩西說過，如果他們不按神的方式生活，將會被管教。最終的懲罰，就是被擄。
6 EXILE
六、被擄
All of this had been foretold by Moses in Leviticus and Deuteronomy. But listen to the reason given.
這一切都早已被摩西在利未記和申命記預言。請聽當中所給的理由：
27 If in spite of this you still do not listen to me … 33 I will scatter you among the nations … 34 Then the land will enjoy its sabbath years … 35 All the time that it lies desolate, the land will have the rest it did not have during the sabbaths you lived in it.
「你們若仍不聽從我……我要把你們散在列邦中……地荒涼的時候，就要享受眾安息。地荒涼的時候，就要守息，因為你們住在其上的時候不曾使地享受所當守的安息。」
Do you see the reason given here? You have not kept the Sabbath, so you are being kicked out of the land, and the land will then have a chance to rest. Or listen to the explanation in 2 Chronicles:
你看到這裏給出的理由嗎？「你沒有守安息日，所以被趕出這塊地，地土便有機會安息。」歷代誌下也作出同樣的解釋：
20 He carried into exile to Babylon the remnant, who escaped from the sword, and they became servants to him and his successors until the kingdom of Persia came to power. 21 The land enjoyed its sabbath rests; all the time of its desolation it rested, until the seventy years were completed in fulfillment of the word of the Lord spoken by Jeremiah.
「凡脫離刀劍的，迦勒底王都擄到巴比倫去，作他和他子孫的僕婢，直到波斯國興起來。這就應驗了耶和華藉耶利米口所說的話：地享受安息；因為地土荒涼便守安息，直滿了七十年。」
Do you see how the punishment fits the crime here? They refused to rest, so they were sent off to Babylon, to be slaves there. If they wouldn’t rest with God, they would be slaves in Babylon. And then, again, the land can have the rest it was denied to the people.
你看到這個「罪有應得」的對應嗎？他們拒絕安息，所以被送去巴比倫做奴隸。如果他們不肯與神同享安息，他們就要在巴比倫為奴。然後地土，反過來能享受百姓所剝奪的安息。
So Sabbath is a big part of the reason for this … because Sabbath is a sign of the covenant, and because Sabbath is all about resting in God and having a loving relationship with him.
所以「安息日」是這次被擄的重大原因之一——因為安息日是立約的記號，而安息日的核心，就是在神裏面歇息、與祂保持愛的關係。
Now, when they were in exile in Babylon, the prophet Daniel received a number of visions and revelations, and one of these is in Daniel nine. And you may remember the context. He was praying about the restoration of Israel because Jeremiah had prophesied that it would last only seventy years, a Sabbath-type number. Seventy years for the land to have its sabbath. So he’s praying about this, and specifically confessing their sins … asking for forgiveness … and then he gets a message from an angel, which says:
被擄到巴比倫期間，先知但以理領受了多次異象與啟示。其中一次記在但以理書第九章。你也許還記得當時的背景：他正在禱告祈求以色列復興——因為耶利米預言被擄只會持續七十年——一個「安息式的數字」——七十年讓地土享受安息。所以他在禱告，認他們的罪，求神赦免——然後他從天使那裏領受一個信息：
“Seventy ‘sevens’ are decreed for your people and your holy city to finish transgression, to put an end to sin, to atone for wickedness, to bring in everlasting righteousness, to seal up vision and prophecy and to anoint the Most Holy Place
「為你本國之民和你聖城，已經定了七十個七。要止住罪過、除淨罪惡、贖盡罪孽，引進永義，封住異象和預言，並膏至聖者。」（但以理書 9 章 24 節）
Daniel 9:24
這又是一道數學題——但這次不是禧年的「七個七」，而是「七十個七」——四百九十年——大約五百年。將要來的，是一個「超級禧年」、一個「超級超級的安息日」。禧年是債務取消之時；而這個超級禧年，將是「罪債」取消之時——這當然是一個關於主耶穌基督的預言。但以理在主耶穌降臨之前約五百年領受這異象——關乎一個將要來、所有罪債都會取消、人的罪過與失敗都要被赦免的時候。
Now this is another sum … but instead of seven sevens like the jubilee, it is seventy sevens, so four hundred and ninety years … that is about five hundred years. There will be a super-jubilee, a super-duper-sabbath. Now the jubilee was a time for debts to be cancelled, and this super-jubilee, or super-duper-sabbath, would be a time for their sin to be cancelled, and of course, this is a prophecy of the Lord Jesus Christ. Now, Daniel received this vision about 500 years before Jesus came. And it’s about a time when all their debts will be cancelled, and there will be forgiveness for their sins and failures.
這個異象很重要——它解釋了一件事：百姓並不夠資格從巴比倫歸回，但何以仍能歸回？正是因為將來那「超級禧年」、那「超級超級的安息日」必要實現。
That vision is important, as it explains how it’s possible for them to come back from Babylon, when they are not good enough, it’s because of the super-jubilee, the super-duper-sabbath, which is coming in the future.
雖然這個未來的安息已被應許，但舊約其餘的部分卻多多少少有點反高潮——他們確實逃出了巴比倫，正如耶利米所預言的：
But although this sabbath is promised in the future, the rest of the Old Testament is somewhat anticlimactic… they do escape Babylon. Just as Jeremiah had prophesied:
「巴比倫傾倒了！傾倒了！她一切雕刻的神像都打碎於地。」
‘Babylon has fallen, has fallen! All the images of its gods lie shattered on the ground!’”
許多人歸回以色列，重建聖殿與聖城。歸回之後，先知不再經常指控他們拜偶像——似乎略有改變——但卻很反高潮。例如尼希米記的結尾：
And a large number return to Israel, and they rebuild the temple and the city. And after returning to the land, the prophets do not accuse them of idolatry so often; it seems that there has been a bit of a change, but it’s very anticlimactic. Take for example, the end of Nehemiah
「我就斥責猶大的貴冑說：『你們怎麼行這惡事，犯了安息日呢？從前你們列祖豈不是這樣行，以致我們神使一切災禍臨到我們和這城嗎？現在你們還犯安息日，使忿怒越發臨到以色列！』……有一兩次，賣各樣貨物的人在耶路撒冷城外住宿。我就警戒他們說：『你們為何在城外住宿呢？若再這樣，我必下手拿辦你們。』從此以後，他們在安息日不再來了。我吩咐利未人潔淨自己，來守城門，使安息日為聖。」
… I rebuked the nobles of Judah and said to them, “What is this wicked thing you are doing—desecrating the Sabbath day? 18 Didn’t your ancestors do the same things, so that our God brought all this calamity on us and on this city? Now you are stirring up more wrath against Israel by desecrating the Sabbath.”… Once or twice the merchants and sellers of all kinds of goods spent the night outside Jerusalem. 21 But I warned them and said, “Why do you spend the night by the wall? If you do this again, I will arrest you.” From that time on they no longer came on the Sabbath. 22 Then I commanded the Levites to purify themselves and go and guard the gates in order to keep the Sabbath day holy …
這段記載有幾方面其實都不對勁。首先，百姓顯然仍然在違犯安息日——他們的心並未跟隨神。他們可能不再像從前那樣拜偶像，但仍然不順服。其次，尼希米的反應變得相當猛烈，幾乎到了律法主義的地步。而被擄歸回之後，這似乎成了以色列的一個趨勢——一種猛烈的律法主義——而這當然就是後來我們在法利賽人身上看見的。
This is just wrong in a number of ways … clearly the people are still breaking the Sabbath, their hearts are not following god. They might not be worshipping the idols to the same extent, but they are still disobedient. But then Nehemiah becomes quite fierce about it, almost to the point of legalism. And this seems to be the growing trend in Israel after the exile, a kind of fierce legalism, which, of course, is what we see the Pharisees doing.
結語
Conclusion
我們講到這裏。內容不少，你或者未必記得所有細節，但希望大圖畫是清楚的——「安息日」由神的安息開始，並非因為祂需要休息，而是因為「安息」是創造與工作的高峰。工作之後，便享受關係。所以一切都是關乎在神裏面的和諧、平安、賜福。
Wow, we’ve reached the end … we’ve covered a lot, and you may not remember all the details, but hopefully the big picture is clear … that Sabbath started with god’s rest, not because he needed to rest, but because rest is the climax of creation and work. After you work, you enjoy relationships. And so, it’s all about harmony, peace and blessing in God.
但我們看到，人類背叛神，因此失去了伊甸園裏祂的安息。當我們轉離神時，我們便拜偶像，被慾望奴役，無法安息，並且壓迫他人。
But we saw that humanity rebelled against God, and so they lost his Sabbath rest in Eden, and instead … when we turn from God, we commit idolatry, we are enslaved to our desires, and we can’t rest, and we oppress others.
對以色列百姓而言，因為他們沒有聖靈，這些原則便以幾條簡單的規則記下來給他們遵守——休一日；讓別人也休息；讓地土每七年休息一次；每五十年取消債務；定期以筵席慶祝神的恩慈——一切都是美好的。
For the people of Israel, because they didn’t have the holy spirit, they had these principles put down in some simple rules for them to follow – take a day off, let other people rest, let the land rest every seven years, cancel debts every fifty years, and celebrate God’s goodness in regular parties … it was all meant to be good …
但神的子民不肯遵守，一次又一次地轉離。最終被送去巴比倫作奴隸——這就是「拒絕安息」的懲罰：神將我們交給我們的奴役。
But God’s people didn’t want to keep it; they turned away again and again, and eventually were sent to be slaves in Babylon; that’s the punishment for refusing to rest. God gives us over to our slavery.
但在巴比倫，他們領受了一個「超級超級安息日」、「超級禧年」的應許——這就是我們下一講要繼續看的。讓我們一同禱告。
But in Babylon, they received this promise of a super-duper-sabbath, the super-jubilee … and that’s what we will find out about in the next talk. Let’s pray.

Because there is a range of views about what should be done, and how we should approach the topic of rest … what does it look like to rest, and should we still celebrate Sabbath, or keep Sunday special? How do we go away and put this all into practice?
這就是我們在聖經導覽中所看到的。經過聖經的整體掃過之後，我們現在需要花一點時間消化所看見的，反省當中的意義，並思考如何實際應用。因為對於這個題目，基督徒之間有不同的看法：應該怎樣行？我們如何看待安息日？「安息」實際上是甚麼樣子？我們仍要慶祝安息日，或將星期日分別出來嗎？我們如何將這一切實踐出來？
Well, we are going to have three sections to this talk: points of view on rest, principles of rest, and practicalities of rest. So first of all:
這一講會分三部分：第一，對「安息」的不同觀點；第二，「安息」的原則；第三，「安息」的實踐。
Points of view about rest
對「安息」的不同觀點
It is important to acknowledge that Bible-believing Christians have different views on the Sabbath; we saw that in Romans. Now that doesn’t mean we have to accept all as equally valid, no, Paul tells us to do what is right in our own mind, that means we are to think about it … and try to find the right answer, but to do so respectfully.
首先要承認：相信聖經的基督徒，對「安息日」這件事有不同看法——我們在羅馬書已經看到。但這並不代表我們要將所有看法都當成同樣有道理。不——保羅告訴我們要「在自己心裏意見堅定」，意思是我們要思考，努力找出正確的答案，但同時要彼此尊重。
Now, when dealing with this topic, it comes back to a bigger question: how should we read and apply the Old Testament law? And there are a range of views on this; there is a spectrum.
當處理這個題目時，我們要回到一個更大的問題：我們應該如何讀並應用舊約律法？這個問題也有不同看法，而且是一個光譜。
On one hand, some people stress continuity. The Bible gives us God’s big plan to save, and all of his promises, Old Testament and New, are all focused on Jesus; it’s one big plan. And the law, yes, some things are fulfilled in Jesus, like the sacrificial system and the civil laws. But the moral law of the Old Testament, especially the Ten Commandments, still applies; we are still legally under the Ten Commandments, and we must still obey the moral part of the Old Testament law.
光譜的一端強調「延續性」（continuity）。這派認為：聖經給我們的是神拯救人類的整個大計劃，無論是舊約抑或新約所有應許，全部都聚焦於耶穌——是同一個大計劃。律法當中，有一部分在耶穌裏成就了，譬如獻祭制度和民事法。但舊約的「道德律」，特別是十誡，仍然有效——我們今日仍在十誡之下，仍然要遵守舊約律法當中的道德部分。
The other approach is discontinuity. That says yes, the promises are all about the Holy Spirit, but there are two different operating systems. In the Old Testament, you have the laws pointing forward to Jesus, and in the New Testament, the church has the Holy Spirit guiding them. And there isn’t anywhere in the New Testament that divides the law into sacrificial, civil and moral law, and there isn’t anywhere that says you are still under the moral law. No, it says you are free from the law; you don’t have to keep it. It still has good principles, but you are not legally under the law.
另一端強調「不延續性」（discontinuity）。這派認為：對，所有應許都是關乎聖靈的——但舊約和新約是兩套不同的「運作系統」。在舊約，律法指向耶穌；在新約，教會由聖靈帶領。新約並沒有將律法分為「獻祭」、「民事」、「道德」三類，也沒有任何地方說我們仍然受「道德律」約束。相反，新約說我們從律法中得了自由——你不需要去守律法。律法仍有美好的原則，但你不再法律上受律法約束。
One approach stresses continuity, and the other discontinuity when it comes to law.
一端強調延續，另一端強調不延續。
And within that spectrum, there will be a range of views. I’ve put fruit down here to give us a sense of it.
而在這個光譜之內，又有不同的觀點。我在這裏列舉四種，讓大家有個印象：
Seventh-day Adventists – first, the seven-day Adventists would say, "Well, the Bible tells us we must rest on the seventh day, which is Saturday." They say, well, it’s in Genesis two, God rested. And it’s in the Ten Commandments, which is God’s moral law. Both of those things still apply, and so we are legally required … that is required by God’s law to keep the Sabbath on the Saturday.
第一，第七日安息日會（Seventh-day Adventists）——他們會說：「聖經告訴我們必須在第七日安息」——即星期六。他們的理據是：創世記第二章告訴我們，神在第七日安息了；十誡——也即神的道德律——也吩咐這事；這兩樣到今日仍然有效。所以我們是受神的律法所約束，必須在星期六守安息日。
Christian Sabbath – another view of the Christian Sabbath. It operates on similar logic, but on Sunday. Those who go for this view say, well, God rested on the seventh day, and we are told to follow this in the Ten Commandments, and the Ten Commandments still apply. We are legally required to keep God’s ten commandments, and the sabbath is one of those. But when the early church met, they changed the sabbath from Saturday to Sunday. So, we don’t keep Saturday's Sabbath; we keep the Lord’s Day, which is a Christian Sabbath.
第二，「基督教安息日」觀（Christian Sabbath）——這個觀點的邏輯類似，但守的是星期日。他們說：神在第七日安息了，十誡叫我們跟隨這事，十誡到今日仍然有效，我們是受十誡所約束，安息日是其中一條。但當早期教會聚會時，把安息日由星期六改成星期日。所以我們不守星期六的安息日，我們守「主日」——一個「基督教式的安息日」。
So those views are both continuity views, saying we need to keep the sabbath because it’s in the Ten Commandments. The other two are discontinuity views, saying we are not required to keep them, because the law is fulfilled in Jesus.
頭兩個觀點都是「延續性」觀——主張我們要守安息日，因為它在十誡裏面。後兩個觀點是「不延續性」觀——主張我們不必須守安息日，因為律法已在耶穌裏成就。
Fulfilment in Christ – the fourth view is a more extreme version of this… it says, the law is all fulfilled in Christ, so there are no special days. And yes, it makes sense for us to all come to church on the same day, but there is really nothing further than that. You can do what you want, because true rest in Jesus.
第三，「創造模式」觀（Creation Pattern）——這派說：「我們不在律法之下，亦不被十誡法律性地約束，因為耶穌就是律法的成就。」所以沒有甚麼「特別的日子」。但似乎創造仍有一個「七日休一日」的模式——雖然我們不再像舊約那樣「違反就要被治死」，但這仍是智慧而美好的事——我們都需要安息。「安息」是創造的模式。
Creation Pattern – the third view is similar, but less extreme. It says, "We are not under the law; we are not legally bound to the ten commandments, because Jesus is the fulfilment of the law …" So yes, there are no special days. But there does seem to be a pattern of creation: resting one day in seven. And although we are not legally required to keep it under pain of death, as in the Old Testament, this is a wise and good thing to do; we all need rest. Rest is a creation pattern.
第四，「在基督裏的成就」觀（Fulfilment in Christ）——這是「不延續性」的一個更極端版本。它說：律法都已在基督裏成就，所以沒有任何「特別的日子」。當然，大家在同一日返教會仍然合理——但僅此而已。你想點都得，因為真正的安息已經在耶穌裏。
These second two views, three and four, are approaches to the law based on discontinuity. You have continuity and discontinuity. And it’s very important that we respect each other, even if we don’t agree with other people, in the way Romans 14 tells us to do.
第三和第四這兩個觀點都是建基於「不延續性」。所以你看：延續性—對—不延續性。我們一定要彼此尊重，即使不同意對方的看法，也要按羅馬書 14 章所教導的那樣去做。
Now my own approach is point three. For me … it just doesn’t make sense to divide the Old Testament law into sacrificial, civil and moral, and then say we are still under the moral law. I can’t see that anywhere; it seems to me that we are freed from the law and under a new system through the power of the Holy Spirit. It is all fulfilled in Christ. But I wouldn’t go so far to say Christians can do whatever they like. To me, it’s a question of wisdom: it seems wise to acknowledge that there is a basic pattern to the way the world works, and we need to recognise that and get some rest. And that brings us to our second point:
我自己的立場是接近第三點。對我而言⋯⋯把舊約律法分成「獻祭、民事、道德」三類，再說我們仍然在「道德律」之下，這個分法似乎沒有甚麼根據；我看不到聖經這樣分。在我看來，我們是已經從律法中得了釋放，並透過聖靈的能力進入了一個新的系統。一切都已在基督裏成就。但我也不會去到極端，說基督徒可以為所欲為。對我而言，這是個「智慧」的問題——承認這個世界有一個基本運作模式是合理的，我們需要認識並按那模式得安息。
Principles for rest
這就帶我們到第二部分：
Physical Rest
「安息」的原則
We have to admit that we need physical rest. If we just say that rest is fulfilled in Jesus … so it’s about coming to him for rest, it seems to miss this obvious point. We all need physical rest. Even Jesus got tired and rested. Think of the story of the calming of the story, Jesus was asleep after a long day's work, getting some rest. So we need rest, and it makes sense for us to have a plan or a pattern to get that rest, so that we rest before we reach the point of exhaustion and keel over.
第一，身體的安息
Redemption Rest
我們必須承認自己需要身體的休息。如果我們只說「安息已在耶穌裏成就，所以單去到祂那裏就可以了」，似乎錯失了這個顯然的事實——我們所有人都需要身體休息。連耶穌也會疲倦、會休息。想想耶穌在風暴中睡覺的故事——祂工作了一整日後睡覺，得到一點休息。所以我們需要休息，並且需要有計劃和模式去得到那休息——免得我們撐到精疲力盡才倒下來。
Secondly, we need to remember that rest is not just physical rest; it’s redemption rest. That’s the reason given in Deuteronomy five. You are rescued. You were slaves in Egypt, but now you are free. We need to remember that. We were slaves of sin, but now we are free. Free from the penalty of sin, we are forgiven and also set free from sin in our lives. Sin creates slavery and oppression. So we need to draw boundaries and not get sucked into that. We hear a voice in our head saying, " There is always more work to do … but we need to say, I’m set free from that rat-race. I’m not going to be sucked into the endless pursuit of selfish ambition and evil desires. Now, sometimes it can be hard to say no when our boss tells us directly to do a piece of work; we might be able to do it. But other times we need to exercise discretion. It might mean our careers don’t advance as quickly, but life isn’t all about career and promotions.
第二，救贖的安息
Relationship Rest
其次，我們要記得：安息不單是身體的休息，也是救贖的安息——這正是申命記第五章所給的理由。「你被救出來了。你曾經是埃及的奴隸，但現在你自由了。」我們要記住這事。我們曾是罪的奴隸，現在自由了——從罪的刑罰中得自由，被赦免，並且被釋放，不再被罪轄制。罪帶來奴役和壓迫。所以我們要設立界線，不要被吸入那個漩渦中。我們腦中總有一把聲音說：「永遠都做不完⋯⋯」但我們要說：「我已經從那個鼠兒競賽中得了釋放。我不會再被無止境的自私野心和罪惡慾望吞沒。」有時候，當老闆直接吩咐你做某件工作，你或者真的能做。但有時候，我們需要運用判斷力——可能升職加薪會慢一點，但人生並不是只關於事業和升職。
Then there’s relationship rest. True rest is found in Jesus, and so really resting means spending time with him. We should be seeking to do that every day … opening the Bible to hear his words, praying to him in response. We should all be doing that every day. But rest is also about enjoying time with others. Adam and Eve enjoyed time with God and each other in the Garden of Eden. So it makes sense for us to take time off together. If you have your day off one day, I have it another day, and they have it another day … well, we can’t spend time together enjoying relationships with one another or with God. To me it seems to make sense to synchronise our time resting from work, to be with each other, to enjoy relationships.
第三，關係的安息
Celebration Rest
接著，是關係的安息。真正的安息在耶穌裏，所以真正的安息就是與祂同在。我們應該天天這樣做——打開聖經聽祂的話、向祂禱告作回應。我們每一個人都應該日日如此。但安息也包括與其他人共度時光。亞當夏娃在伊甸園裏，與神和彼此享受時光。所以我們一同休息也是合理的。如果你某一日休息，我另一日休息，他又另一日休息——那麼我們就無法一起享受彼此的關係，亦難以一同親近神。對我來說，把休息的時間同步化是有道理的——讓我們可以彼此相伴，享受關係。
That brings us on to celebration rest. Rest is not just about sitting back, sipping a cup of tea and keeping your breathing steady and your pulse low. It’s meant to be a party; it’s meant to be something wonderful. We see that in the Old Testament, the festivals involved a week-long party, celebrating together after working hard in the harvest. And we see that in the New Creation; heaven is described as a wonderful wedding banquet with aged wine. That kind of rest isn’t boring at all, it’s wonderful. So, church on Sunday is meant to have a celebratory aspect, celebrating and praising God for how wonderful he is and enjoying time together. Of course, arranging a celebration takes some work, and it takes some commitment for us to all turn up, but that’s important. Rest is not meant to be boring; it’s meant to be wonderful.
第四，慶祝的安息
Now we might think, well, this all sounds like a bit much, I don’t have time to rest in this way … but remember that …
這就帶我們到「慶祝的安息」。安息不是只坐下來、慢慢呷一啖茶、保持呼吸均勻、心跳緩和。它是一場筵席——是奇妙的事。我們在舊約看到，那些節期是長達一週的慶祝——在收成後辛勤勞動之後，一同慶祝。新創造也是如此——天國被描述為一個盛大的婚宴，有陳年美酒。這種安息一點也不沉悶，反而非常美妙。所以星期日返教會應有「慶祝」的層面——一同慶祝，敬拜神是何等奇妙，享受彼此的時光。當然，籌備一場慶祝需要付出工夫，需要大家委身一同出席——但這很重要。安息不應該沉悶——應該是奇妙的。
God’s Punishment for failing to rest is SLAVERY
這時你或許會想：「咦，這聽起來太多了，我哪有時間這樣安息？」但請記住⋯⋯
… that’s what happened to his people; they wouldn’t rest, so he punished them with slavery in Babylon. Now God doesn’t punish us like that in the New Testament, but he will hand us over to the consequences of our sins. So if you won’t rest, you will be a slave. But he’s saved us from that; we don’t need to revert to slavery. Ok, finally, so practical suggestions, so we have some ideas of what to actually do:
「拒絕安息」的刑罰是「奴役」
Practical suggestions for rest
⋯⋯這就是發生在神子民身上的事。他們不肯安息，所以神用「巴比倫的奴役」懲罰他們。新約時代神並非這樣懲罰我們，但祂會將我們交給罪的後果。所以如果你不肯安息，你便要做奴隸。但祂已經將我們從那狀態中拯救出來——我們不需要再回到奴役。
Have a day off each week (energy in & energy out)
最後，幾個實際的建議：
Sleep- we need to acknowledge that we need physical rest. For one, we need sleep. I have one of those tracker watches that tracks my sleep and tells me how I’m doing … and if I go to bed too late, my sleep isn’t very good, and my watch tells me. God has created us to need sleep. So, you need to find out how much sleep you need and get enough sleep.
「安息」的實踐建議
We also need to take days off to rest. To me, it’s clear in the Bible that God has created a pattern of seven, and following that pattern is the best way for the world to work. We take for granted in the West that we have a weekly pattern, and that it’s a seven-day cycle … but it comes from the Bible. The Roman Constantine adopted it in the fourth century, and since then, all of the West has followed it. People have tried to get rid of it. After the French Revolution, the French tried to introduce a ten-day week, a decimal week, but it didn’t work. In the 1920s, the Soviets tried to introduce a five-day workweek with factories running continuously and different days off. That didn’t work. There seems to be something about the seven days. God made it that way, and we should respect it. so can I encourage you to try and make sure that you are getting a day off each week. And I think, personally, having some clear boundaries helps us to do that … things you don’t do on your day off, otherwise, work just creeps in. And if we are married, we should have a conversation with our spouse and family about this and agree on those boundaries together.
第一，每星期有一日休息（能量進來和能量出去）
Now, what should we do on our day off? What counts as work? I think a helpful way to think about this is energy in, and energy out. Resting doesn’t mean doing nothing; it’s what reenergises us. Some people are reenergised by going for a long walk, or even a run. Some people like seeing friends. We should do things that put energy in, not take energy out. Now we mustn’t take that to extremes, some people have a day off and shouldn't run a half-marathon, and seeing five different people … even if those individual things energise you, it means you aren’t getting enough rest … we have to be sensible.
睡眠——我們要承認自己需要身體的休息。其中一樣，我們需要睡眠。我有一隻能追蹤睡眠的手錶——如果我太晚睡，我的睡眠質素就會差，手錶會告訴我。神創造我們需要睡眠。所以你要找出自己需要多少睡眠，並確保得到足夠睡眠。
Take Holidays – second take holidays.
我們也需要休息日。對我而言，聖經很清楚地表明：神創造了一個「七日的模式」——按這模式生活，是世界運轉最好的方法。在西方我們把每星期七日這個模式視為理所當然——但這其實是來自聖經。羅馬皇帝君士坦丁在第四世紀採納了這個模式，自此整個西方都跟隨。也有人試過取消它——法國大革命之後，法國人試過一星期十日的「十進位星期」，行不通。1920 年代，蘇聯試過五日工作週，工廠不停運轉，員工錯開休息——也行不通。這個「七日」似乎有它的道理。神這樣造的，我們應該尊重。所以我鼓勵大家：嘗試確保每星期有一日休息。我個人覺得，設下清晰的界線會幫助我們——譬如「休息日不做哪幾樣」——否則工作就會慢慢蠶食。如果我們已婚，我們應該與配偶和家人就此商量好，一同達成共識。
To me, it seems very Christian to take holidays. That’s what the believers did in the Old Testament. There were many set holidays besides the Sabbath, including the festivals, which lasted a week. So after the harvest, you’ve been working hard. You take a break, you get off the farm, and go to Jerusalem, and have a party, and worship the lord. It’s a holiday. So why not take a holiday? That principle seems to be in the Bible. It doesn’t need to be expensive; it doesn’t need to be two weeks in a five-star hotel in Hawaii. It might just be a few nights in a holiday cottage somewhere as a change of scene. I think going on very expensive holidays, can become quite worldly – that’s what the world around us wants to do, they think, I’ve only got a few years before die, so I want to travel the globe and enjoy luxuries … we want to avid that bucket-list-mentality I think, but there’s nothing wrong with holidays per se. and why not include a Christian dimension. That’s what they did in ancient Israel: they went to worship God at the temple. So could we do that? Perhaps try reading a Christian book, or, even better, a book with the people you are going on holiday with, and discuss it. Or if you are not into reading books, listen to some sermons and discuss them on holiday, it doesn’t need to take up all the time, but it brings that Christian aspect to the time away.
那麼，休息日該做甚麼？甚麼算是「工作」？我覺得有一個有用的方法去想：「能量進來，能量出去」。安息並非甚麼也不做——而是讓我們重新得力的事。有人因長途散步——甚至跑步——而恢復精力；有人喜歡見朋友。我們應做能讓「能量進來」的事，而非耗能量的事。但也不要太極端——有人休息日要去跑半馬，又見五個不同的朋友——即使每樣個別來說都讓你充電，整體加起來就等於沒得到足夠休息。要懂分寸。
Go to Church ( … and so try to keep Sundays special?)
第二，放假
Maybe this is obvious … but going to church is a key part of rest. This is part of the reason for synchronising our rest. We all rest together, so we can go to church together, worship God together, and enjoy relationships with each other and with God. Sometimes people seem to see church as optional. I go to church, so long as I don’t have other plans. If I have other plans, then I do those. And of course, sometimes there might be things we can’t avoid, but we should plan to be in church every week. We shouldn’t make plans that clash with church; it is a key part of our rest with God and his people every week.
對我而言，放假是非常合乎聖經教導的事。這就是舊約信徒所做的事。除了安息日之外，他們有許多既定假期——包括那些長達一週的節期。所以當收成後，你已經辛勤工作了一段日子，你便放下農場的事，上耶路撒冷，舉行筵席，敬拜耶和華——這是「放假」。所以為何不放假呢？這個原則是聖經裏面有的。它不需要昂貴——不需要兩星期五星級夏威夷酒店；可能只是幾個晚上在某處渡假小屋換一下環境而已。我覺得花費極大的奢華假期會變得相當世俗——這是身邊的世俗文化想做的事，他們覺得「我一生短短幾十年，所以我要環遊地球、享受奢華⋯⋯」我們要避免那種「人生 must-do 清單」的心態。但放假本身並沒有錯。為何不加入一點屬靈元素呢？這正是古以色列人所做的事——他們放假時前往聖殿敬拜神。我們可以這樣嗎？或者試試讀一本基督教書，更好的是，與一同放假的人讀同一本書，互相討論。如果你不喜歡讀書，可以聽道，再一同討論。這不需要佔據所有時間，但能為這段假期帶來屬靈的層面。
And because this is so important, I think I would also argue for keeping Sunday special, for spending time with God’s people. I don’t mean that we are legally required by the Ten Commandments to do this … I just mean that it seems a sensible decision to make time for God’s people. Sometimes you see people who come to church every Sunday, without fail. The service ends, and they have left within a couple of minutes. They are not taking the time to connect with God’s people. And I think that misses the point of sabbath rest, connecting with the people of God. If you have taken time to come to church, to spend time with God’s people because you believe that is good and precious … then why rush off right away? Why not instead follow through on the decision … and take time to go for lunch, have coffee, or go for a walk, or have them back to your home to spend time together. There is something to be said for making Sunday a special time with God’s people, and not rushing off the moment the service finishes. And finally …
第三，返教會（並嘗試將星期日分別出來）
Rest with Jesus
可能這很顯然⋯⋯但返教會是「安息」的關鍵一環。這也是同步休息日的其中一個原因。我們一同休息，所以我們可以一同返教會，一同敬拜神，享受彼此的關係，同時親近神。有時候，有人把返教會視為「可有可無」——「如果我有其他活動，我就不去」。當然，有時候真的有事情無法避免，但我們的計劃應該是「每星期返教會」。我們不應計劃會與教會時間衝突的事——因為這是我們每星期與神同在、與神的子民同在的關鍵時間。
True rest is found in the Lord Jesus Christ. We need to go to him. Jesus said … “Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.” We need to remember that all our strategies for resting, self-care, boundaries … those will not work. The only one who can truly satisfy the restlessness of our hearts is the Lord Jesus Christ. If we are gripped by anxiety and worry, if we are struggling with sin and doubt, if we feel overcome with suffering and burdens …. Weighed down in this world, we need to go to Jesus. He is the only one who can satisfy and save. So we need to be taking our troubles to him, each and every day, resting in him, because he says: “Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.”
而正因為這事這樣重要，我也想為「將星期日分別出來」進言——花時間與神的子民同在。我不是說我們是因為十誡而被法律性地規定要這樣做⋯⋯我只是說，為神的子民騰出時間是合理而智慧的決定。有時你會看到一些人，每個星期日都準時返教會——但聚會一完，幾分鐘內就走了。他們沒有花時間與神的子民連繫。我認為這錯失了「安息日」的精髓——與神的子民連繫。如果你已經花時間返教會，已經願意與神的子民共度時光，因為你相信這事美好寶貴⋯⋯為甚麼要立刻趕走呢？為何不貫徹這個決定，多留一點時間——一齊吃午飯、飲咖啡、散散步，或請他們到家裏共度時光。為「星期日為神的子民留出特別時間」進言是有道理的——不要聚會一完就趕走。
最後⋯⋯
第四，在耶穌裏安息
真正的安息在主耶穌基督裏。我們要到祂那裏。耶穌說：「凡勞苦擔重擔的人，可以到我這裏來，我就使你們得安息。」我們要記住：一切休息策略——自我照顧、設界線——終究都不能解決根本問題。唯一真正能滿足我們心中那永不安息的，就是主耶穌基督。如果我們被焦慮愁煩所抓住，如果我們在罪和疑惑中掙扎，如果我們覺得被痛苦和重擔壓得喘不過氣——在這個世界中被壓得透不過氣的時候——我們需要去耶穌那裏。祂是唯一能滿足、能拯救我們的那一位。所以我們需要日復日將自己的憂愁帶到祂面前，在祂裏面安息——因為祂說：「凡勞苦擔重擔的人，可以到我這裏來，我就使你們得安息。」
