Good Friday Sermon 受難日講道
The Value of the Cross (Matt 26–27) 十字架的價值（馬太福音 26–27章）
………………………………
Introduction 引言
This is a picture of Mount Vernon in the US. To many Americans it's a revered place. It's seen as having amazing worth and value. To the outside it's quite a modest 18th Century building; the architecture is ok. It's quite simple. 這是美國維農山莊的照片。對許多美國人而言，這是一處令人景仰的地方，被視為具有極大的價值。從外觀看，它只是一座相當樸素的十八世紀建築；建築風格尚可，十分簡樸。
Why the reverence? Why the value? Because it was where George Washington lived, the Founding Father of the US, much loved by many. 為何如此崇敬？為何如此珍貴？因為那是喬治·華盛頓的故居——這位深受愛戴的美國國父曾在此居住。
From the outside it's an ordinary house. But one has to go deeper to see its true significance. 從外表看，它只是一座平凡的房子。但若要看見其真正的意義，就必須深入了解。
The cross of Christ: what happened that first Good Friday… to some it's just a tragic death. It's an example of human injustice. And there have been many more like it. 基督的十字架：那第一個受難日所發生的事……對某些人來說，那不過是一場悲劇性的死亡，是人間不公義的一個例子。類似的事件歷史上比比皆是。
Why for Christians is the cross of Christ so valuable? Why is his death and the object on which he died: our most treasured symbol? 為什麼對基督徒而言，基督的十字架如此寶貴？為什麼祂的死，以及祂受死所在的刑具，成為我們最珍視的象徵？
One has to go deeper. 我們必須深入探究。
We'll do that by looking at Jesus' agony in the garden of Gethsemane: there's a huge clue as to why his cross is so monumental. 我們將透過察看耶穌在客西馬尼園中的痛苦掙扎來深入了解：那裏有一個重要的線索，揭示祂的十字架為何如此意義重大。

Part 1: The Cup of Wrath Jesus Drank 第一部分：耶穌所喝的忿怒之杯
What makes for a good death? 怎樣才算是好的死亡？
Some say it's the way you face death. To go down with dignity; to go down still holding to your principles. 有人說，關鍵在於你如何面對死亡。帶著尊嚴離去；至死仍堅守自己的原則。
Key example would be Socrates. 最典型的例子就是蘇格拉底。
Socrates was a Greek philosopher. Lived in Athens 2,500 years ago. When he was 70 he was put on trial for essentially disrespecting the gods and being a pain in the backside… He was sentenced to death by poisoning. But when his sentence was handed down to him, Socrates famously remained very calm. He continued to stand up for his own views; continued to denounce the leaders. Right up to his death he was stoic, fearless even. 蘇格拉底是一位希臘哲學家，約兩千五百年前住在雅典。他七十歲時因被控不敬神明及擾亂社會而受審……最終被判以毒藥處死。然而，當判決宣佈時，蘇格拉底著名地保持了極度的冷靜。他繼續堅持自己的觀點，繼續斥責當權者。直到死前，他都是堅忍不拔、甚至無所畏懼的。
We see much of that with Jesus. Jesus famously predicts his death before it happens. He does nothing to run from it. He is silent before his accusers. He prays for people even as he dies. 在耶穌身上我們也看到許多類似之處。耶穌在受死之前預言了自己的死亡。祂絲毫沒有逃避。祂在控告者面前保持沉默。甚至在死亡之際仍為眾人禱告。
But in contrast to Socrates, Jesus agonises about his death. We see that in the Garden of Gethsemane. And that points us to what's really happening at the cross. 然而，與蘇格拉底形成對比的是，耶穌為祂的死而極度痛苦掙扎。我們在客西馬尼園中看見了這一幕。而這正指向十字架上真正發生的事。
Jesus is overwhelmed with sorrow. He's dreading what's to come. We know from Luke's account that Jesus sweats drops of blood — so intense is his wrestling with what's about to happen… 耶穌憂傷到了極處。祂對即將臨到的事充滿恐懼。 路加的記載告訴我們，耶穌汗如血滴——祂內心的掙扎何等劇烈……
Now is Jesus just overwhelmed by the pain that's coming? The sense of injustice? No… That's not what moves Jesus to overwhelming sorrow… 耶穌只是因為即將來臨的痛苦而崩潰嗎？是因為不公義的感覺嗎？不是……那並非讓耶穌極度憂傷的原因……
When Jesus faces that cross he's facing something far more scary and significant. 當耶穌面對十字架時，祂所面對的是遠比肉體之苦更可怕、更重大的事。
Clear in the mention of a cup: v.39: "My Father, if it is possible, may this cup be taken from me" + v.42 again for a second time he mentions it. 這在「杯」的提及中清楚可見。第39節：「我父啊，倘若可行，求你叫這杯離開我。」第42節，祂再次提到。
I don't know what you hate to drink: I'm A Celebrity: vomit fruit! Now that's just gross… 我不知道你最討厭喝什麼： 《我是名人》節目中的嘔吐果！那真的很噁心……
This cup makes Jesus sweat blood. It fills him with dread. Why? 這杯讓耶穌汗如血滴，讓祂充滿恐懼。為什麼？
About what this cup represents. For that we need help from the OT. 關鍵在於這杯所代表的含義。為此，我們需要從舊約中尋求幫助。
Ps. 11:5–6 (ESV): The Lord tests the righteous, but his soul hates the wicked and the one who loves violence. Let him rain coals on the wicked; fire and sulfur and a scorching wind shall be the portion of their cup. 詩篇 11:5–6（和合本）：耶和華試驗義人，惟有惡人和喜愛強暴的人，他心裏恨惡。他要向惡人密布網羅，有烈火、硫磺、熱風，作他們杯中的分。
Isa. 51:17: Awake, awake! Rise up, Jerusalem, you who have drunk from the hand of the Lord the cup of his wrath, you who have drained to its dregs the goblet that makes people stagger. 以賽亞書 51:17（和合本）：耶路撒冷啊，興起！興起！站起來！你從耶和華手中喝了他忿怒之杯，喝了那使人東倒西歪的爵，以至喝盡。
This cup is a metaphor/image describing God's anger at and punishment of human sin/human wickedness/human evil. 這杯是一個隱喻/意象，描述上帝對人類罪惡、人類邪惡的憤怒和懲罰。
To drink the cup is to face in full the wrath of God. Why it's described as sulfur, fire, makes you stagger and fall… 喝這杯就是完全承受上帝的忿怒。所以經文用硫磺、烈火來形容，讓人東倒西歪……
BTW — God's anger, God's punishment not arbitrary. It's not because God has a short temper. It's not because he can't get his way. 順帶一提——上帝的憤怒、上帝的懲罰並非任意妄為。不是因為上帝脾氣暴躁，也不是因為祂不能如願。
God's anger and punishment is a good thing. It's like a judge punishing the perpetrator of a terrible crime. That sentence given is not a bad thing. It's the right response. 上帝的憤怒和懲罰是好的。就如同法官懲處嚴重罪行的罪犯。判決本身並非壞事，而是正確的回應。
Who's facing this cup of God's anger? The bible is clear: everyone. Why? What's our crime? We all rebel from God: our default setting is to have us rule our lives not our Creator; no wonder that leads usually to us wanting to do what's wrong, to celebrate others' misfortune, to keep things for ourselves rather than giving… so many ways that rebellion leads to problems… 誰要面對上帝忿怒的杯？聖經清楚地說：每一個人。為什麼？我們的罪行是什麼？我們都悖逆上帝：我們的本性就是要自己掌管人生，而非讓造物主作主；難怪這往往導致我們想做錯事、幸災樂禍、只顧自己而不願付出……悖逆以如此多的方式帶來種種問題……
We all deserve to drink from that cup. 我們每一個人都當承受那杯。
But here we see Jesus drinking from that cup instead. That's what his death is. He's drinking down, to the very dregs, the right and fair punishment that sin deserves. 但在這裏，我們看見耶穌代替我們喝了那杯。祂的死正是如此。祂將罪所應得的公正懲罰，一滴不剩地喝盡。
Why is Jesus in agony and dread? Not only must he experience physical death, but he also must taste eternal death — he must experience what it's like to be forsaken by God. 為什麼耶穌如此痛苦恐懼？ 祂不僅要經歷肉體的死亡，更要嚐到永遠的死——祂必須體驗被上帝離棄是何等滋味。
No wonder Jesus asks in that brief moment whether there's another way… 難怪耶穌在那短暫的時刻詢問是否還有別的路……
This is a Jesus who for all eternity has known and enjoyed the Father's perfect love of him. Has basked in that perfect love. And who has loved his Father. 這位耶穌從亙古到永遠都認識並享受著天父對祂完美的愛。祂一直沐浴在那完美的愛中，也一直愛著祂的父。
What could be more dread-inducing than to know he's about to experience NOT that love but God's punishment. No wonder on the cross, as we read in 27:46 Jesus cries out "My God, my God why have you forsaken me?" 還有什麼比知道自己即將經歷的不是那份愛，而是上帝的懲罰更令人恐懼的呢？難怪在十字架上，正如我們在27:46所讀到的，耶穌大聲呼喊：「我的神！我的神！為什麼離棄我？」
And all he hears from his end is silence. A dreadful silence. God is silent. 而祂所聽到的只有沉默。一片可怕的沉默。上帝沉默了。
That is what's happening as we see Jesus hanging there. The physical agony yes but also the spiritual agony. 這就是當我們看見耶穌懸掛在那裏時正在發生的事。肉體上的痛苦固然存在，但更有靈裏的極度痛苦。
But we also see a Jesus who shows total, unwavering obedience. It is a fearful time. But also a time for him to have complete trust in his Father. For his will to be done. His Father's will is also Jesus' will. This is their planned mission. It needs to be done. And Jesus will do it. 但我們同時也看到一位展現完全、毫不動搖之順服的耶穌。那是充滿恐懼的時刻，但也是祂完全信靠天父的時刻。讓父的旨意成就。父的旨意也是耶穌的旨意。這是他們計劃好的使命。必須完成。耶穌會去完成。
This scene in the garden is the opposite of that first garden in the Bible. There Adam hid from God. Now Jesus comes and, in the face of his most terrifying ordeal, he says, "here I am!" For the sake of the world that I love I will face this. 園中的這一幕與聖經中第一個園子的場景截然相反。在那裏，亞當躲避上帝。如今耶穌來到，面對祂最恐怖的試煉，說：「我在這裏！」為了我所愛的世界，我願面對這一切。
The cross is terrifying. But it's also the most loving thing that's ever been done. Because Jesus does this for you and for me… 十字架是可怕的。但它也是有史以來最充滿愛的作為。因為耶穌為你、為我承受了這一切……

Part 2: The Cup of Salvation Jesus Offers 第二部分：耶穌所賜的救恩之杯
Again, what makes for a good death? We look up to a death for the sake of a good cause; for the sake of others. 再問一次，怎樣才算是好的死亡？ 我們敬佩那些為了美好的理由、為了他人而犧牲的死亡。
Every November we remember those service men and women who gave their lives during the War for the sake of protecting freedoms and lives back home. 每年十一月，我們紀念那些在戰爭中獻出生命的軍人，他們為了保護國人的自由與生命而犧牲。
Don't want to undermine that sacrifice in any way. Those deaths are rightly remembered. 我無意以任何方式貶低那些犧牲。那些死亡理當被紀念。
Why do Christians value the cross so much? 為什麼基督徒如此珍視十字架？
It is the ultimate death for the sake of others. 因為那是為他人而死的終極犧牲。
We've seen already: there's a reality that's far more significant than just the issues of the world down here. That bigger reality is about how we relate to our Creator. We don't just need a sacrifice that preserves freedom; fights evil… We need a sacrifice that deals with the bigger issue of God's anger at sin, his punishment of sin, the death we all face because of sin. 我們已經看到：有一個遠比這世上的問題更重大的現實。那更大的現實，關乎我們與造物主的關係。 我們需要的不僅是維護自由、對抗邪惡的犧牲…… 我們需要的是一個能解決更根本問題的犧牲——上帝對罪的忿怒、對罪的懲罰、以及我們眾人因罪而面對的死亡。
And Jesus' death is that sacrifice. His death works. We know that from Matt 27. What happens immediately after Jesus breathes his last? 而耶穌的死正是那個犧牲。祂的死是有效的。我們從馬太福音27章知道這一點。耶穌斷氣之後，立刻發生了什麼事？
Matt. 27:51: "At that moment the curtain of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom." 馬太福音 27:51（和合本）：「忽然，殿裏的幔子從上到下裂為兩半。」
Curtain being ripped — big deal? The one place in Israel where God was seen to be present — the temple — also had that huge thick curtain blocking people from coming too close. That curtain was the symbol of our human predicament. Our sin stops us getting close. 幔子被撕裂——有什麼大不了？在以色列，上帝被視為臨在的那唯一之處——聖殿——有一道巨大厚重的幔子，阻擋人們靠近。那幔子正是人類困境的象徵。我們的罪使我們無法親近上帝。
Jesus dies: the curtain is torn. 耶穌死了：幔子裂開了。
That imagery of Jesus drinking down the cup of God's anger leads to a cup of salvation being poured out for us. 耶穌喝盡上帝忿怒之杯的畫面，引出了為我們傾倒的救恩之杯。
Ps. 116:13: "I will lift up the cup of salvation and call on the name of the Lord." 詩篇 116:13（和合本）：「我要舉起救恩的杯，稱揚耶和華的名。」
Jesus takes the cup of God's anger so we can drink from the cup of salvation. The way back to God is open again. 耶穌承受了上帝忿怒的杯，使我們可以喝救恩的杯。通往上帝的路重新敞開了。
At the cross, "Jesus endured our hell so that we might be free to enter his heaven." 在十字架上，「耶穌承受了我們的地獄，使我們得以自由進入祂的天堂。」
The cross changes everything. V.52: Details of people coming back to life. 十字架改變了一切。第52節：記載了人從死裏復活的事蹟。
Jesus' agony meant death itself has been forever changed. It will never be the same again. 耶穌的痛苦意味著死亡本身已永遠被改變。死亡再也不一樣了。
With Jesus' death comes a way back to God. No more punishment. No more death. We can call on our God, know our God and have life with our God. 因著耶穌的死，通往上帝的路開通了。不再有懲罰。不再有死亡。我們可以呼求我們的上帝，認識我們的上帝，與我們的上帝同享生命。

Application 應用
That cup of wrath is drunk by Jesus and we are given the cup of salvation: how do we receive that cup? By holding out our hands as if we would a normal cup! We come to Jesus and say "please take my sin — please may I have your salvation" — and that's it. Death is no longer death for you either. Life with your Creator is yours. That's all. 忿怒之杯由耶穌喝了，救恩之杯賜給了我們：我們如何領受這杯？就像接過一個普通的杯子一樣伸出雙手！我們來到耶穌面前說：「求你除去我的罪——求你賜我你的救恩」——就是這樣。死亡對你而言也不再是死亡。與造物主同在的生命是屬你的。就這麼簡單。
What might be stopping you this morning? 今天早上，有什麼攔阻你呢？
If you're not a believer: Don't be on good terms with yourself, without Jesus. This talk of sin and God's anger can sound off-putting. Especially when we're taught so much that deep down we're all fine. In the gospel, the greatest barrier to coming to Jesus is not immorality, but morality. Those who knew their own sinfulness flocked to Jesus to be with him. Those who thought they were better than they were questioned, doubted and stayed away from Jesus. Please don't make the same mistake. Realising you're not who even you want to be is fairly easy to see. But that's just what we need to see to come to Jesus and receive the cup of salvation. 如果你還不是信徒： 不要在沒有耶穌的情況下自我感覺良好。這些關於罪和上帝忿怒的話聽起來可能令人不快，尤其是當我們被教導認為每個人本質上都沒問題的時候。在福音中，阻擋人來到耶穌面前的最大障礙不是不道德，而是自以為道德。那些認識到自己罪性的人蜂擁到耶穌面前與祂同在。那些自認為比實際更好的人則質疑、懷疑並遠離耶穌。請不要犯同樣的錯誤。意識到自己連自己期望的樣子都達不到，其實並不難。而那正是我們來到耶穌面前、領受救恩之杯所需要看見的。
Do you see how weighed down Jesus was by the prospect of the wrath of God at sin? Of a separation from his Father's goodness and love? How much agony he was in at the thought? That was Jesus! What about for those who face that judgment for themselves when God comes to finally judge his world? Can you imagine what it feels to head away from God at the end: away from his goodness and into eternal separation from him? Jesus thought that a terrible prospect. Do we see it like that? 你看到耶穌面對上帝對罪的忿怒時何等沉重嗎？面對與天父的良善和慈愛分離時何等沉重？祂為此何等痛苦？那可是耶穌啊！那麼，當上帝最終審判世界時，那些要親自面對審判的人又會如何？你能想像在終末之時離開上帝是什麼感覺嗎——離開祂的良善，進入與祂永遠的隔絕？耶穌認為那是一個可怕的前景。我們是否也這樣看呢？
Brothers and sisters who do follow Jesus: value the cross. It is our everything! 跟隨耶穌的弟兄姊妹們：要珍視十字架。那是我們的一切！
