Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
Rest is Coming 安息將至
(Hebrews 希伯來書 4:1-13)

Intro 引言 引言
There's a lot of unrest in the world. I came across this map this week. All the areas with colour are places where there is currently conflict: ranging from big scale wars to minor skirmishes. The world at large is not very restful. 今天的世界充滿了不安。這個禮拜我看見一張地圖，圖上所有著色的地方，都是現正發生衝突的地區，由大規模的戰爭到小型的衝突都有。整個世界都極不安寧。
Unrest within society? More divisive politics? Culture wars? Protests? 在社會層面也不安寧——政治日趨分裂、文化戰爭、抗議示威，不斷出現。
Maybe we feel unrest on the inside. 或許我們內心也感到不安。
Meaning crisis. In the West as the sea of Christianity recedes ever more, people see what's left behind — and it's hard to find meaning now. That's why many in Gen Z are returning to traditional forms of religion in big numbers. 這是「意義的危機」。在西方，基督教的潮水節節退去，人們看見潮水退後所剩下的，便發現很難再找到生命的意義。這正是為何 Z 世代之中，有大批人重新回到傳統的宗教形式。
I spent quite a few years of my life incredibly restless. And it's not a nice feeling. The grass was always greener on the other side; nothing gave me what I wanted. I couldn't be content. I couldn't sit still. 我自己有好多年的時間，內心極度不安寧。那種感覺一點也不好受。總覺得別處的草地比較青綠；沒有一樣東西能滿足我的渴求。我無法知足，也無法靜下來。
What about you? When your head hits the pillow at night, are you able to rest — or do you start thinking and worrying? 你又如何呢？晚上頭一沾枕頭，你能夠安然休息嗎？還是腦海裡開始浮現各樣的思慮和憂愁？
Today's passage points us to something good: REST. 今天的經文要把我們的目光指向一件美好的事——安息。
A rest that can be ours if — as has been our big theme of the past few weeks — if we keep going as Christian believers. 這份安息可以屬於我們，但有一個條件——正如過去幾個禮拜我們所強調的主題——只要我們作為基督徒一直堅持下去。
Chapters 1-2 of Hebrews were about how Jesus is all we need him to be and does all we need him to do to secure our salvation. To bring us back to God. And with him, that is where we are heading. But we're not there yet. 希伯來書第一至二章，告訴我們耶穌就是我們所需要的一切，祂也成就了我們得救所需的一切，把我們帶回到神面前。在祂裡面，我們正朝著這個目的地前進。但——我們還未到達。
In chapters 3-4: more about our response. 到了第三、四章，作者更多地談到我們應有的回應。
Similar in our passage today. We must keep going — but there's a focus on the incentive: look at what lies ahead! 今天的經文也是一樣。我們必須繼續向前——但作者更強調我們前進的動力：看看前面所等待我們的是甚麼！

A warning from past failure (vv.1-2) 從過去的失敗看見的警告（1-2 節） 從過去的失敗發出的警告（1-2 節）
Read Hebrews 4:1-2. 請讀希伯來書 4:1-2。
"Therefore, since the promise of entering his rest still stands, let us be careful that none of you be found to have fallen short of it. For we also have had the good news proclaimed to us, just as they did; but the message they heard was of no value to them, because they did not share the faith of those who obeyed." 「我們既蒙留下，有進入他安息的應許，就當畏懼，免得我們中間或有人似乎是趕不上了。因為有福音傳給我們，像傳給他們一樣；只是所聽見的道與他們無益，因為他們沒有信心與所聽見的道調和。」
Remember, in the background to what the writer is saying is this journey that the Israelites made out of Egypt, through the wilderness, towards the promised land of Canaan. It is used as an image for what the Christian life is like. 請記得，作者所說一切的背景，是以色列人從埃及出來、經過曠野、走向應許之地迦南的那段旅程。這段旅程被用來描繪基督徒的生命。
There's a T-shirt slogan: "It's about the journey, not the destination." 有一句很流行的 T 恤標語說：「重點在旅程，不在目的地。」
According to Hebrews, that is totally the wrong way round. What we learn as we look back at our spiritual ancestors is that the destination is the key thing. As we heard last week, it doesn't matter how well you set out — and those guys started well — it's about whether you go the distance and cross the line. 但按希伯來書來看，這句話完全顛倒了。我們從屬靈的列祖身上所學到的，正是：目的地才是關鍵。正如上禮拜所聽到的——你起步多好並不重要（那一代人起步是不錯的）——重要的是你能否走完全程、衝過終點線。
That wilderness generation had ahead of them the Promised Land: the land of Canaan — which was for them meant to be a place of rest. 那曠野的一代，前面有應許之地等著他們——迦南地——對他們而言，那是預備為他們的安息之地。
Exodus 33:14 — The Lord replied, "My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest." 出埃及記 33:14——耶和華說：「我必親自和你同去，使你得安息。」
A land overflowing with milk and honey; peace; shade; comfort. Their oasis at the end of their desert journey. 那是流奶與蜜之地，有平安、有蔭涼、有舒適——是他們走完曠野旅程後的綠洲。
But they never arrive. They get right up to the edge — they can see in — but they don't enter. They stop listening and decide going elsewhere is preferable (even talking about going back to Egypt!). 但他們始終沒有踏進去。他們已經走到邊界——可以望進去——卻沒有進入。他們不再聽從，反而決定別的方向更好（甚至想要回到埃及！）。
What's the warning? 這當中的警告是甚麼？
They started well but didn't go the distance. 他們起步不錯，卻沒有跑完全程。
Now again, we need to be careful with warnings such as this. 面對這樣的警告，我們需要小心處理。
Part of the pastor's job is to comfort the disturbed and disturb the comfortable. Hebrews certainly does both! 牧者的工作之一，是安慰那些不安的人，攪動那些過於安逸的人。希伯來書這兩樣都做得徹底！
Anxious about your salvation? Do I really believe? Am I really going to make it? 你會否為自己的救恩感到焦慮？「我是否真的相信？我是否真的能堅持到最後？」
If that's you this morning, then the writer here would remind you of Hebrews 1. Jesus has sat down. He's done everything to win your salvation and get you home, and it's complete. It's in the bag. So these warnings are not there to undermine your confidence but to help keep you on track and keep you going! 若你今早正是這樣，作者要提醒你回到希伯來書第一章。耶穌已經坐下了。祂為贏得你的救恩、把你帶回天家所要做的一切，已經完成了；事就這樣成了。所以這些警告不是要動搖你的把握，而是要幫助你不偏離正道、繼續向前！
Over-confident? What I mean by that: we think we're going to make it even though, at the moment — to use Hebrews' language — we're drifting or sliding in the Christian life. Jesus is a supporting character now, not the main one. God's word (as we'll come back to later) is less important to us, or we don't listen to it much anymore. 或者你過於自信？我所指的是：我們以為自己一定能到達終點，但其實此刻——用希伯來書的說法——我們在基督徒生命裡正在「隨流漂去」、正在向下滑落。耶穌已經淪為配角，不再是主角了。神的話語（待會會再談到）對我們已經不再重要，或我們已經很少再聽。
And here's the real danger: maybe we say to ourselves, well back in whatever year it was, I made a commitment. I prayed a prayer. I got baptised. So I'm okay, right? 真正的危險就在這裡：我們也許會跟自己說：「在好多年前的某一年，我作了決志。我作過那篇禱告。我也受了浸。那我應該沒問題了吧？」
If that's you, then Hebrews tells us: No. 若你這樣想，希伯來書要對我們說：不是的。
There's a word that keeps repeating throughout this whole section. The word "Today". The most important question we must ask is NOT did I make a commitment to Jesus back then — the question is am I listening to, following behind and believing in Jesus TODAY. 在整段經文裡，有一個字不斷出現——「今日」。我們要問自己最重要的問題，並不是「我從前是否曾經向耶穌作過委身」，而是「我今日是否正在聽從祂、跟隨祂、相信祂」。
Today — is your faith in Jesus as alive as it was back then? 今日——你對耶穌的信心，是否仍像當初那樣鮮活？
If not, then — and we need to hear this — we are in danger of missing out. Just as they did back then. 若不是的話，我們就要聽清楚——我們有可能會錯失這一切。正如當年那一代人一樣。
It's about the destination. We mustn't be those who fall short. 重點在目的地。我們絕不可成為「趕不上」的那一群人。
For some of us this morning, that warning will help us keep going. But it's not just about warnings. It's not just about the stick. There's the carrot too… 對我們當中一些人來說，這個警告會成為一股推動我們繼續向前的力量。但作者所講的不只是警告，不只是責備的「棍子」——還有吸引我們的「胡蘿蔔」……

A promise of future rest (vv.3-10) 將來安息的應許（3-10 節） 將來安息的應許（3-10 節）
Ahead of us is something glorious. 在我們前面的，是極其榮美的事。
It's quite nice talking about rest when it's exam season. Some of you are going through exams at the moment or about to. Some of us look back to those days with great relief that we don't need to do exams anymore. Not to rub it in... 在考試季節談論安息，特別合時。你們當中有些人正在考試，或快要考試；我們有些人則慶幸自己不再需要應考——當然，這不是要刺激你。
But there is something wonderful about exam season. And it's that hope that one day soon they'll end and there will be that morning when you wake up and you don't need to think about your revision schedule, get out all your textbooks; you won't have to worry about the next exam on the horizon — one morning soon, that will all be gone. 但考試季節本身有一件美好的事，就是那份盼望——盼望不久之後考試就會結束。將會有那麼一個早上，你一覺醒來，不再需要想溫習進度、不再需要把所有課本搬出來、不再需要擔心下一場考試——某個快要來的早上，這一切都會過去。
And when that morning finally comes — man, does it feel good. 而當那一個早晨終於來到——那種感覺，真的很美。
What keeps us going in the Christian life is that picture of upcoming REST. 在基督徒的生命裡，使我們堅持下去的，正是那幅將要來臨的安息圖畫。
Now when the writer talks about this rest, it can be a bit confusing. But the logic is fairly simple. 當作者談到這個「安息」時，可能會令人有點困惑。但其實邏輯相當簡單。
His main burden is to show us that rest still lies ahead of us and is open for us, just as it was back then for the original wilderness generation. 他主要的負擔，是要讓我們看見：這份安息今日仍擺在我們前面、仍向我們敞開，正如當年向曠野那一代人敞開一樣。
After all, someone might say, hang on — people did enter Canaan under Joshua in the end. Yes, says the writer, but the rest of Canaan was not the ultimate rest that God had promised. 也許有人會問：「等一下，後來不是有人在約書亞帶領下進了迦南嗎？」作者回答：「是的，但迦南的安息，並不是神所應許那終極的安息。」
Read verses 7-8. 請讀第 7-8 節。
"Therefore God again set a certain day, calling it ‘Today.' This he did when a long time later he spoke through David, as in the passage already quoted: ‘Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts.' For if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken later about another day." 「所以過了多年，就在大衛的書上，又限定一日，如以上所引的說：『你們今日若聽他的話，就不可硬著心。』若是約書亞已叫他們享了安息，後來神就不再提別的日子了。」
His point is this: after they had entered the land — much later in fact — King David in Psalm 95 could still call people in his day to keep going, to strive "Today" to enter that rest to come. Which means that entering Canaan had not completely fulfilled that promise of rest. There was a greater rest to come. 作者的論點是這樣：以色列人進入迦南之後——其實已經過了很久——大衛王在詩篇 95 篇裡仍然呼籲他那個世代的人要繼續努力，要「今日」就竭力進入那將要來的安息。這就表明：進入迦南並未完全應驗那安息的應許。仍有一個更大的安息將要來臨。
What is that greater rest? It's actually joining in with God's own rest. A rest that God has enjoyed from the beginning. 那個更大的安息是甚麼？其實，就是與神一同進入祂自己的安息——一個神從起初就享受著的安息。
Hebrews 4:3-4 takes us back to Genesis 2. 希伯來書 4:3-4 把我們帶回到創世記第二章。
Genesis 2:1-3 — "Thus the heavens and the earth were completed in all their vast array. By the seventh day God had finished the work he had been doing; so on the seventh day he rested from all his work. Then God blessed the seventh day and made it holy, because on it he rested from all the work of creating that he had done." 創世記 2:1-3——「天地萬物都造齊了。到第七日，神造物的工已經完畢，就在第七日歇了他一切的工，安息了。神賜福給第七日，定為聖日，因為在這日，神歇了他一切創造的工，就安息了。」
This rest is not the rest of someone who is exhausted. God can't get tired. God has just made his creation (Genesis 1) — and it's very, very good. This is the rest of God enjoying, delighting in and being completely satisfied with his creation. 這個安息，並不是因為疲倦才需要的休息。神不會疲乏。神剛剛完成了祂的創造（創世記第一章）——一切都甚好。這份安息，是神在祂的創造中享受、喜悅、完全滿足的安息。
There is no morning or evening with the seventh day, as there is with the previous six. Why? Because it is ongoing! God has been enjoying this rest from that day to this. And that is the rest he calls us to join him in. 在創世記裡，前六日都記載「有晚上、有早晨」，唯獨第七日沒有。為甚麼？因為這日是持續不斷的！神由那日直到今日，仍然享受著這份安息。而這正是神呼召我們進入、與祂同享的安息。
But our ultimate hope is the hope already described in 3:1 as our "heavenly calling" and as the "hope in which we glory" (3:6) — that is where our journey ends. 但我們最終的盼望，正是 3:1 所說的「同蒙天召」，亦即 3:6 所說的「可誇的盼望」——這就是我們旅程的終點。
Rest with God in complete enjoyment, delight and satisfaction. 與神同在的安息，是完全的享受、完全的喜樂、完全的滿足。
That's where Jesus takes us too. In fact, that's where Jesus is now — beckoning us onwards. 這也是耶穌要帶我們去的地方。事實上，耶穌此刻已經在那裡——招手呼喚我們前行。
And we need this rest because ever since Genesis 3 — when Adam and Eve disobeyed and turned away from God; when sin entered our world — we live in a world of deep unrest. 我們需要這份安息，因為自從創世記第三章——亞當夏娃違背神、轉身離開神、罪進入世界以來——我們所活的世界，是一個深陷不安的世界。
And even as we follow Jesus back there, we're still now in the desert. In the wilderness. We have to strive, to make every effort to keep going. But verse 10 — when we finally arrive, we'll be at rest. All that striving will end. 即使我們已經跟隨耶穌走在歸家的路上，我們此刻仍身處曠野——身處荒野。我們必須竭力，必須盡一切努力繼續前進。然而第 10 節說——當我們終於抵達時，就要進入安息，一切的勞苦掙扎都會結束。
It's important to say that when we come to Jesus, we do experience something of rest now. 我們要清楚說明：當我們來到耶穌面前，我們現在已經能嚐到一些安息的滋味。
Hinted at in verse 3? 第 3 節是否已經暗示了這一點？
When we come to Jesus in this life, we do find rest for our souls. 當我們在今生來到耶穌面前，我們的心靈確實得享安息。
We all have a God-shaped hole in our hearts. Because we're all away from home and cut off from our Maker. And that makes us restless — but coming to Jesus now does fill that hole. 我們心裡都有一個「神形狀」的空洞。因為我們都遠離了家鄉、與創造我們的主隔絕。這令我們無法安寧——但當我們來到耶穌面前，這個空洞就被填滿。
Can I just say on a personal note: that is true. 讓我以個人的見證說一句：這是真的。
Rest in Jesus means rest and relief from so many things: it means rest from being worried about what people think of me all the time. What people think of me is not a concern, because God knows the most about me and still loves me. That gives me rest. I'm free to enjoy social interaction without all that worry. 在耶穌裡的安息，意味著從許多事情中得到舒緩：例如，從「時刻擔心別人怎樣看我」這件事得釋放。別人怎樣看我，已經不再是壓力，因為最了解我的神，仍然愛我。這就讓我得安息，能夠無憂無慮地享受與人相處。
It's rest from needing everything I do to be perfect, or else feel like a failure. Being desperate to achieve and the stress of not achieving actually makes it hard to do anything well. I can enjoy what I do and not make it into something that defines me or gives me value. My value lies in what Jesus has done for me, not what I do for him. That is very restful. It also makes things more enjoyable. 也是從「凡事必須做到完美，否則就覺得自己是失敗者」這種捆綁中得釋放。那種拼命追求成就、又害怕失敗的壓力，反而令我們甚麼也做不好。我可以享受手上的工作，而不必把它當作定義自己、賦予自己價值的標籤。我的價值，在於耶穌為我所做的，而不是我為祂做了甚麼。這就帶來真正的安息，也讓所做的事更有滋味。
Maybe you're here and you have FOMO all the time; or there's something in life you keep doing but it never satisfies you enough so you keep doing it… Jesus gives rest from those too. 你可能此刻坐在這裡，常常有「錯失恐懼症」（FOMO）；或者有某件事你不斷地做，卻怎樣也得不到滿足，於是只能繼續做下去……耶穌也能讓你從這些當中得安息。
We all have a God-shaped hole. When we come to Jesus, he fills it and gives us rest. 我們都有那個「神形狀」的空洞。當我們來到耶穌面前，祂就把它填滿，賜我們安息。
Rest now. But the emphasis is on future rest to come. When everything around us too will be at complete rest. No more conflict. No more culture wars. No more meaning crisis. Everything will be at peace and at rest. 現在已經有安息。但重點仍然在於那將來的安息——將來不只我們得享安息，連我們周圍的一切也會完全進入安息。再沒有衝突，再沒有文化戰爭，再沒有意義危機。萬物都將處於平安與安息之中。
Keep going, because that is coming! 繼續向前走吧——因為那一日必定來臨！
If you want to hear more about rest and what this practically looks like and means — come to our Away Day! That is all on the subject of rest. Questions about the Sabbath today, etc. 如果你想更深入了解安息——它在實際生活中是怎樣的、有甚麼意義——歡迎參加我們的 Away Day！整個內容都圍繞「安息」這個主題，包括今日基督徒怎樣看待安息日等問題。

An appeal to act in the present (vv.11-13) 在當下作出回應的呼籲（11-13 節） 在今日作出回應的呼籲（11-13 節）
Look back for a warning from the past. 向後看——從過去得到警戒。
Look forward to that amazing promise of rest that still lies ahead and open. 向前看——那擺在我們前面、仍然敞開的奇妙安息應許。
And so, make every effort to make sure you don't miss out yourself. 因此，要竭盡全力，確保自己不致錯過。
Today — how are you going? 今日——你的光景如何？
Read Hebrews 4:11-13. 請讀希伯來書 4:11-13。
"Let us, therefore, make every effort to enter that rest, so that no one will perish by following their example of disobedience. For the word of God is alive and active. Sharper than any double-edged sword, it penetrates even to dividing soul and spirit, joints and marrow; it judges the thoughts and attitudes of the heart. Nothing in all creation is hidden from God's sight. Everything is uncovered and laid bare before the eyes of him to whom we must give account." 「所以，我們務必竭力進入那安息，免得有人學那不信從的樣子跌倒了。神的道是活潑的，是有功效的，比一切兩刃的劍更快，甚至魂與靈，骨節與骨髓，都能刺入、剖開，連心中的思念和主意都能辨明。並且被造的沒有一樣在他面前不顯然的；原來萬物在那與我們有關係的主眼前，都是赤露敞開的。」
How do we know how we're doing spiritually? Well, how are we responding to God's word? 我們怎樣知道自己屬靈光景如何？要看我們對神話語的回應。
No surprise the writer takes us there. God's word is a major theme in Hebrews. 難怪作者把我們的目光帶到這個方向——神的話語，正是希伯來書一大主題。
Chapter 1: God has spoken — how? Through the prophets (OT); supremely in his Son (NT). 第一章：神已經說話了——怎樣說？藉著眾先知（舊約），更尊貴的是藉著祂的兒子（新約）。
2:1 — we are to pay attention to what we have heard. 2:1——我們應當鄭重所聽見的道。
4:2 — we are those who have had the good news proclaimed to us. 4:2——我們也是有福音傳給我們的一群。
In chapters 3-4 we get this refrain three times: "Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts." 在第三、四章裡，這句話重複出現了三次：「你們今日若聽他的話，就不可硬著心。」
How we respond to the word of God is how we respond to God himself. 我們怎樣回應神的話語，就是怎樣回應神自己。
The word of God we hear is an awesome power. And it comes with God's own authority. 我們所聽見的神的話，是極大的能力，並帶著神自己的權柄。
We often feel like we're the ones who get to judge the word. The Bible seems so out-dated, we might say; the culture says this so God's word needs to change, others say; maybe we come to it, read it and dismiss it. Forget about it. 我們常常以為自己是審判神話語的人。我們可能說：「聖經太過時了」；又有人說：「文化已經這樣，神的話應該與時並進」；又或者我們讀過之後，把它打發掉、置諸腦後。
What's actually happening when we come to God's word is that we are the ones being scrutinized, judged, opened up. The Bible exposes us. It exposes whether we have hearts that are hard or hearts that are willing to listen and respond. 其實，當我們來到神的話語面前，被仔細察驗、被審判、被剖開的，是我們自己。聖經把我們暴露出來。它顯明我們的心究竟是剛硬的，還是願意聽從、願意回應的。
Friends, if you hear God's word — don't harden your heart. 弟兄姊妹，若你今日聽見神的話——求你不要硬著心。
Maybe that's a call for you to respond for the first time, in putting your trust in the one who is at the centre of that word: the Lord Jesus. The one who leads us to and gives us rest. 對你來說，這也許是頭一次的呼召——把信心放在這話語的中心，就是主耶穌——那位帶領我們進入安息、賜我們安息的主。
In February 2010 I sat in an old church building in Durham. I was there because, yeah, I was at a point in life when I was open to hearing what it said. That night I heard it preached from the Good Samaritan that I was lost — that I needed God — I needed his forgiveness for the way I'd lived — and that forgiveness came freely in Jesus. The word was exposing my need for Jesus and the emptiness of life without him. It broke down the barriers I'd always put up to taking that step. Not long later, I acted upon that and gave my life to him. 2010 年 2 月，我坐在杜倫一座古老的教堂裡。那時我正處於人生一個願意敞開心去聆聽聖經的時刻。那一晚，我聽到一篇從「好撒瑪利亞人」而來的講道——讓我看見自己迷失了——我需要神——我需要祂赦免我從前的生活——而這份赦免是在耶穌裡白白賜下的。神的話顯明我對耶穌的需要，也顯明沒有耶穌的人生何等空虛。那話語拆毀了我一直築起、攔阻自己跨出那一步的牆。不久之後，我就回應那話語，把生命交託給祂。
Some of us need to do that for the first time. 我們當中有些人，今日就是要頭一次作出這樣的回應。
Some of us need to hear the word this morning and to hear the warning to not drift and to come back. Maybe the word is revealing that we are not actually going the right way. 我們有些人則需要今早聽見這話、聽見警告——不要隨流漂去，要回轉過來。神的話也許正在顯明：我們其實已經偏離了正確的方向。
How we respond today affects that Last Day (v.13) when we stand before God and give an account. 今日我們怎樣回應，會影響那將來的最後審判之日（第 13 節）——那日我們要站在神面前，向祂交賬。
How we respond today affects whether at that Last Day we'll hear the words "Welcome into my rest" or whether we hear the devastating words "You shall not enter." 今日我們怎樣回應，將決定那一日我們是聽見「歡迎你進入我的安息」，還是聽見那叫人心碎的話「你不得進入」。
Don't put off responding. You don't know whether you'll have another chance. 不要拖延作出回應。你不知道自己是否還有下一次機會。
To respond — to come to Jesus; to keep going with him — means rest is coming. 去回應——來到耶穌面前；繼續與祂同行——就是說：安息將至。
And what a rest it will be. 那將會是何等美好的安息。

Rest is Coming

安息將至

Hebrews 希伯來書 4:1-13 · Bible Study Guide 聖經學習指南
"There remains, then, a Sabbath-rest for the people of God." · 「必另有一安息日的安息為神的子民存留。」(Hebrews 希伯來書 4:9)
📖 Summary 摘要
English

The world is restless — politics, culture, and the depths of our own hearts. Hebrews 4 lifts our eyes to a better promise: a rest that still lies ahead and is still open for us. We are pilgrims, like the wilderness generation, journeying towards God's rest, and the writer urges us to make sure we don't fall short like they did.


This rest is nothing less than God's own rest from Genesis 2 — a rest of complete enjoyment, delight and satisfaction in all he has made. Joshua's Canaan was only a foretaste; David in Psalm 95 still calls "Today" to enter it. The ultimate Sabbath rest is yet to come, when we finally arrive home with God.


So today, the question is: are you still listening? God's word is alive and active, exposing the hardness or willingness of our hearts. Don't drift; don't put off responding. Come to Jesus, keep going with him, and the rest he secured will be yours. Rest is coming — and what a rest it will be.

繁體中文

這個世界並不安寧——政治、文化、我們內心深處，都充滿不安。希伯來書第四章把我們的目光抬高，看見一份更美的應許：一份仍擺在前面、仍向我們敞開的安息。我們是寄居的旅客，正如曠野那一代人，正走向神的安息。作者懇切呼籲我們：千萬不要像他們一樣趕不上。


這份安息，正是創世記第二章中神自己的安息——一份在祂所造萬物中享受、喜悅、完全滿足的安息。約書亞所帶領進入的迦南，只是初嚐的滋味；大衛在詩篇 95 篇仍呼喚「今日」要進入這安息。那終極的安息日仍然在前面等候——直到我們最終回到神的家。


所以今日的問題是：你仍在聆聽嗎？神的話是活潑的、有功效的，能顯明我們的心是剛硬還是願意。不要隨流漂去，不要拖延回應。來到耶穌面前，與祂繼續同行，祂為你成就的安息必歸你所有。安息將至——那將是何等美好的安息。

💡 Key Themes 主要主題
1. The promise still stands — but so does the warning 應許仍然有效，警告也仍然有效
English
The wilderness generation heard the same good news we hear, but it did not benefit them — they did not "share the faith of those who obeyed." Their failure is held up as a warning that we, too, can fall short. The same gospel that saves can be heard fruitlessly when it isn't mixed with faith.
繁體中文
曠野那一代人聽過與我們今天一樣的福音，卻沒有得著益處——因為他們「沒有信心與所聽見的道調和」。他們的失敗成為警鐘：我們也可能「趕不上」。同一個福音可以拯救人，也可以被人聽過卻無動於衷——若沒有信心去領受。
2. The destination, not just the journey 目的地比旅程更關鍵
English
The world says "it's about the journey, not the destination." Hebrews flips this completely. What matters is whether we cross the line, not how brightly we started. The wilderness generation started well; they didn't go the distance. Perseverance to the destination is what proves the journey was real.
繁體中文
世界常說：「重點在旅程，不在目的地。」希伯來書卻把這句話完全顛倒。重要的是我們有沒有跑到終點，而不是起步多燦爛。曠野一代起步不錯，卻沒有走完全程。能堅持到目的地，才是這段旅程是否真實的明證。
3. The rest we long for is God's own rest 我們所渴慕的，是神自己的安息
English
The rest of Hebrews 4 isn't merely the end of effort — it is sharing in God's own seventh-day rest from Genesis 2. Not the rest of exhaustion, but the rest of complete delight, enjoyment and satisfaction in all he has made. To enter is to step into the joy that has been God's from the beginning.
繁體中文

4. Rest now, fuller rest to come 現在已嚐安息，將來更全然安息
English
Coming to Jesus already fills the God-shaped hole in our hearts: rest from approval-anxiety, performance pressure, FOMO, restless chasing. English
The key word in Hebrews 3-4 is "Today." Faith is not about the prayer I prayed or the baptism I had years ago — it is about whether I am listening to, following and trusting Jesus today. Past commitment without present trust is exactly the wilderness pattern Hebrews warns against.

天父，祢是那位在第七日安息的神——不是因為祢疲倦，而是因為祢所造的一切都甚好。謝謝祢為我們這群罪人、寄居的旅客打開門，讓我們得以與祢一同享受祢自己的安息。

主耶穌，謝謝祢是我們偉大的大祭司，已經先行進去、坐下，並招手呼喚我們回家。求祢赦免我們——赦免我們在許多地方隨流漂去、心硬不聽、輕看祢的話、又在那些根本不能賜安息的地方四處追尋安息。赦免我們把「今日」推遲為「將來」，又把「將來」拖延為「永不」。

求祢藉聖靈，使我們在祢活潑、有功效的話語下變得柔軟；願這話語穿透我們的偽裝，把我們的心赤露敞開在祢面前。求祢使我們的聽見與信心調和。當路途漫長，求祢穩固我們的腳步，使我們的眼目定睛在目的地——那為神的子民存留的安息。

今日，若我們聽見祢的話，求我們不要硬著心。求祢帶領我們——並我們的教會家——一直走回家，進入那將要來的安息。奉耶穌的名祈求，阿們。
