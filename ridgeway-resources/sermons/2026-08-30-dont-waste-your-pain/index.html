Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
Don't waste your pain 不要浪費你的痛苦
(Lamentations 耶利米哀歌 5:1-22)

Don't waste your pain — Lamentations 5:1-22 Big idea: the remnant of survivors is still waiting anxiously for an end to their pain, but they are making spiritual progress, because they are becoming more God-centred in their focus and in their desires. 不要浪費你的痛苦——耶利米哀歌 5:1-22 中心信息：倖存的餘民仍然焦急地等候痛苦的終結，然而他們在屬靈上正在進步，因為他們的專注與渴望，正越來越以神為中心。
………………………………
Introduction: the future is made today Illustration. On the wall of the gym at Hertfordshire Sports Village there is a line that stops you in your tracks: "The future is created by what you do today, not tomorrow." 引言：將來是今天造成的 比喻。在赫特福德郡運動村（Hertfordshire Sports Village）的健身室牆上，有一句話叫人停下來細想： 「將來是由你今天所做的事造成的，不是明天。」
………………………………
When we are in pain, or in the middle of a struggle, it is very tempting to write the whole season off as "wasted". That is: life goes on hold until this passes. That is a mistake! The potential for progress in a season like this is huge. So don't waste your pain! 當我們身處痛苦之中，或正在掙扎的時候，我們很容易把整段日子一筆勾銷，當作是「白白浪費」的。 就是說：人生要暫停，等這段日子過去再算。 這是一個錯誤！ 在這樣的日子裡，成長的空間其實極大。 所以，不要浪費你的痛苦！
………………………………
Where we are: the pain is not over We come to the end of the story of Lamentations — and the pain is not over. We get the sense that some time has passed, but the remnant of survivors is really struggling. If anything, they are even more vocal about their situation than they were before. 我們身處何處：痛苦仍未過去 我們來到耶利米哀歌故事的結尾——而痛苦仍未過去。 我們可以感覺到，已經過了一段日子，但倖存的餘民依然十分艱難。 甚至可以說，他們比從前更加大聲地訴說自己的處境。
But notice what chapter 5 actually is. It is one long, sustained, communal prayer of lament — for the first time in the whole book! That in itself is progress. Illustration. Eugene Peterson wrote (Five Smooth Stones for Pastoral Work, p.132): "Prayer is suffering's best result." 但請留意第五章究竟是甚麼。它是一篇長篇、持續、全會眾一同獻上的哀歌禱告——在全卷書中還是第一次！ 單是這一點，已經是進步。 比喻。畢德生（Eugene Peterson）在《Five Smooth Stones for Pastoral Work》第 132 頁寫道： 「禱告是苦難所結出最好的果子。」
Up until now their praying has been arrow prayers — sporadic cries and desperate questions. Now it has become a sustained petition to the Lord, laying the social and economic devastation out before him. They put it all on the table (v.1-18). Note this, however: however much time has passed, the pain has not. The questions are still there. 直到如今，他們的禱告都只是箭一般的短禱——零星的呼喊、絕望的質問。 現在卻成了向耶和華持續不斷的懇求，把社會與經濟的荒涼盡都陳明在祂面前。 他們把一切都攤在桌面上（1-18 節）。 然而要留意：無論過了多少時間，痛苦並沒有過去。 那些問題仍然在那裡。
………………………………
The clock is ticking — but where is the answer? You might have missed it, but the end of poem 4 was good news. Read Lamentations 4:22 (v.22). "Your punishment will end, Daughter Zion; he will not prolong your exile." The clock is ticking — in a good way. But where is it? The wait is agony. 時間正在倒數——但答案在哪裡？ 你可能沒有留意，第四首哀歌的結尾其實是好消息。 請讀耶利米哀歌 4:22（22 節）。 「錫安的民哪，你罪孽的刑罰受足了，耶和華必不使你再被擄去。」 時鐘正在走——而且是往好的方向走。 但那應許究竟在哪裡呢？等待實在煎熬。
Question. Are you in a similar place? In which case, verse 20 is probably you (v.20). "Why do you always forget us? Why do you forsake us so long?" "How long, O Lord?" How much more? 問題：你是否也在類似的處境中？ 若是這樣，第 20 節所寫的，很可能就是你（20 節）。 「你為何永遠忘記我們？為何許久離棄我們？」 「耶和華啊，要到幾時呢？」還要多久？
And the book ends without an answer to that question! No Hollywood ending. Not every loose end is tied up. (Is life not often like that?) But there is progress of a sort — progress in them. Spiritual progress. So things ARE moving forward! 而全卷書就這樣結束，沒有回答這個問題！ 沒有荷里活式的大團圓結局，也沒有把所有線索都收好。 （人生不也常常是這樣嗎？） 但仍然有一種進步——是他們身上的進步，屬靈的進步。 所以，事情確實在向前走！
………………………………
Going through a tough time? Whatever the reason — whether it is God's discipline or simply the brokenness of this world — don't waste your pain! Of course we want the pain to end. But while we are waiting, let your pain do two things. 你正在經歷艱難的日子嗎？ 無論原因是甚麼——是神的管教，還是這個世界本身的破碎——都不要浪費你的痛苦！ 我們當然希望痛苦快些結束。但在等候的日子裡，讓你的痛苦成就兩件事。
………………………………
1. Centre your faith Let your faith take centre stage — not your circumstances. It is beginning to happen here. Read verse 19 (v.19). "You, Lord, reign forever; your throne endures from generation to generation." 一、讓信心歸回中心 讓你的信心站在舞台中央——而不是你的環境。 在這裡，這事已經開始發生。請讀第 19 節（19 節）。 「耶和華啊，你存到永遠；你的寶座存到萬代。」
Right at the end of the book, this is the clearest expression of faith we have heard from this community so far. And note: it IS faith. Because the circumstances cut the other way! The temple is gone. The house of God is in ruins. So it looks as if it is all over. No! Unlike some pagan gods, the Lord is not confined like that. The real sanctuary is in heaven — and no one can touch him there. Nothing has changed. 在全卷書的結尾，這是這群人到目前為止最清晰的一次信心宣告。 請留意：這確實是信心。因為環境所指向的，正好相反！ 聖殿沒有了，神的殿成了廢墟。 看來一切都完了。 不！耶和華與某些外邦的神明不同，祂並不被這樣的地方所限制。 真正的聖所在天上——在那裡，沒有人能觸動祂。 甚麼也沒有改變。
Perhaps they are recovering a conviction they had lost; perhaps it is simply moving more to the fore. Either way, faith is beginning to move to the centre. But this is not just the affirming of a bare fact. It is seasoned with hope. Note the words "from generation to generation". This is not the bold, confident hope of chapter 3. It is not a victorious, "bring it on" faith. But it is moving in the right direction. Because there can still be a happy ending here! 也許他們正在尋回一份曾經失落的信念；也許只是把它重新擺到前面來。 無論如何，信心正開始移到中心的位置。 但這不只是乾巴巴地肯定一個事實，其中還帶著盼望的味道。 請留意「存到萬代」這幾個字。 這並不是第三章那種大膽、篤定的盼望，也不是那種得勝的、「放馬過來」的信心。 但方向是對的。因為這裡仍然可以有一個美好的結局！
………………………………
Filter your experience through your faith We need to filter our experiences through our faith — not the other way around. Illustration. The "Copernican Revolution" has become a catchphrase for a complete change in thinking. It has its origins in the work of Nicolaus Copernicus, the 16th-century mathematician, astronomer and churchman. Up until that point it was generally believed that the earth was the centre of the solar system, and that all the other bodies — including the sun — orbited around it. As a result of his work it was discovered that the sun is the centre, and the earth orbits it. The sun is in charge; the sun is the reference point. Not the earth. 用信心過濾你的經歷 我們需要用信心去過濾我們的經歷——而不是倒過來。 比喻。「哥白尼革命」已經成為「思想徹底改變」的代名詞。 它源於哥白尼（Nicolaus Copernicus）的研究；他是十六世紀的數學家、天文學家，也是教會中人。 在那以前，人普遍相信地球是太陽系的中心，其他星體——包括太陽——都圍繞地球運行。 他的研究卻顯明：太陽才是中心，地球是圍繞太陽運行的。 太陽才是主導、才是參照點，不是地球。
Question. Which is the reference point for you? Which one is in charge? Is your faith in charge, or is your life in charge? Our circumstances need to orbit our faith — not the other way around. Question. As you go on, is that becoming more and more the case? 問題：對你來說，哪一樣才是參照點？哪一樣在主導？ 是你的信心在主導，還是你的生活在主導？ 應該是我們的環境圍繞我們的信心運行——而不是倒過來。 問題：日子一天一天過去，這情況在你身上是否越來越真實？
This is never easy, because the stuff of life is right in our faces. We cannot simply soar above it — any more than Jesus could, when he was overwhelmed with grief in the garden (Matthew 26:38). "My soul is overwhelmed with sorrow to the point of death. Stay here and keep watch with me." But we can practise the discipline of centring our faith. 這從來都不容易，因為生活裡的一切就擺在我們眼前。 我們無法單憑一躍就飛越過去——正如耶穌在客西馬尼園中憂傷至極時，也不能這樣做（馬太福音 26:38）。 「我心裡甚是憂傷，幾乎要死；你們在這裡等候，和我一同警醒。」 但我們可以操練「讓信心歸回中心」這門功課。
And note: it should be a little easier for us. Because we have already seen the happy ending. In that sense the waiting is over! Easter Saturday has gone. Christ is risen! THE new day has dawned! 而且要留意：對我們來說，應該容易一點，因為我們已經看見了那美好的結局。 在這個意義上，等待已經結束了！復活節的星期六已經過去。 基督已經復活了！那真正的新日子已經破曉！
Tomorrow is a new day. Who knows what deliverance the Lord might bring you? No situation is ever utterly hopeless. And even if the wait goes on — if you are a child of God by faith, your time will come. "Weeping may tarry for the night, but joy comes with the morning." (Psalm 30:5) 明天是新的一天。誰知道主會為你帶來怎樣的拯救呢？ 沒有一個處境是完全絕望的。 即使等待仍要繼續——只要你藉著信成為神的兒女，你的時候必會來到。 「一宿雖然有哭泣，早晨便必歡呼。」（詩篇 30:5）
………………………………
2. Renew your heart They are definitely on the move spiritually! There is nothing necessarily unspiritual about asking difficult questions. However, up to now there has been very little real self-examination. They have never taken a good hard look at themselves. That is beginning to change too! Not just faith — repentance! 二、更新你的心 他們在屬靈上確實正在前進！ 提出艱難的問題，本身不一定是不屬靈的。 然而直到如今，他們甚少真正省察自己，從來沒有好好地正視自己。 如今這一點也開始改變了！不單是信心，更是悔改！
(a) The first clear confession of sin Read verse 16 (v.16). "The crown has fallen from our head. Woe to us, for we have sinned!" This is not merely "we know we are not perfect". That is not a profound admission — everybody says that. That is not repentance. For the first time, this is: all of this is because of us, and we have only ourselves to blame. （一）第一次清楚地認罪 請讀第 16 節（16 節）。 「冠冕從我們的頭上落下；我們犯罪了，我們有禍了！」 這不只是說「我們知道自己並不完美」。 那不是甚麼深刻的承認——人人都會這樣說，那不是悔改。 但這一次是了：這一切都是因為我們，我們只能怪自己。
Back in chapter 3 the prophet had urged them to do exactly this (3:40-42). "Let us examine our ways and test them, and let us return to the Lord. Let us lift up our hearts and our hands to God in heaven, and say: 'We have sinned and rebelled and you have not forgiven.'" It needs to happen! There can be no restoration without it. And that is still true today. 早在第三章，先知已經這樣勸勉他們（3:40-42）。 「我們當深深考察自己的行為，再歸向耶和華。我們當誠心向天上的神舉手禱告。我們犯罪背逆，你並不赦免。」 這是必須發生的事！沒有這一步，就沒有復興。 今天仍然如此。
Question. Do you realise that? Peter said the same thing to the crowds in the same city, 600 years later: "Repent, then, and turn to God, so that your sins may be wiped out, that times of refreshing may come from the Lord." (Acts 3:19) Perhaps you have never before been honest with God — or with yourself — about your need. Question. Why not change that today? 問題：你是否明白這一點？ 六百年之後，彼得在同一座城，向群眾說了同樣的話： 「所以，你們當悔改歸正，使你們的罪得以塗抹，這樣，那安舒的日子就必從主面前來到。」（使徒行傳 3:19） 或許你從來未曾向神、也未曾向自己誠實面對自己的需要。 問題：何不就在今天改變這一點？
………………………………
(b) They now want more than an end to their suffering It was so desperately needed — and only now is it beginning to happen. Read verse 21 (v.21). "Restore us to yourself, Lord, that we may return; renew our days as of old." Yes, they want the pain to end. But now there is more: they want to be restored to the Lord. They want him — again! Not just his mercy and his forgiveness. （二）他們如今所渴望的，不只是苦難的終結 這是他們極其需要的一步——卻到現在才開始發生。 請讀第 21 節（21 節）。 「耶和華啊，求你使我們向你回轉，我們便得回轉。求你復新我們的日子，像古時一樣。」 不錯，他們仍然希望痛苦結束。但如今還有更深的渴望：他們要被領回到耶和華面前。 他們要的是祂自己——再一次！不單是祂的憐憫和赦免。
Illustration. What is the difference between a diamond and a lump of coal? Loads! One is very rare and expensive; the other is abundant and cheap. They have very different uses: fine jewellery and heavy-duty cutting tools on the one hand; fossil fuel to be burned on the other. They could not be more different! And yet they are made from the same basic element — carbon! So what accounts for the difference? Heat and pressure! Diamonds are formed deep inside the earth's mantle, under extreme heat and pressure, over a very long period of time. And the result is something very beautiful and precious. 比喻。鑽石和一塊煤炭有甚麼分別？ 分別可大了！一樣稀有昂貴，一樣普遍廉價。 用途也截然不同：一樣用來製作名貴首飾和重型切割工具，一樣是拿來燃燒的化石燃料。 兩者可謂天差地遠！ 然而，它們卻是由同一種基本元素構成的——碳！ 那麼，是甚麼造成這個分別？是高溫與壓力！ 鑽石是在地幔深處，在極高的溫度與壓力之下，經過極其漫長的時間形成的。 結果就是這樣美麗而寶貴。
Question. Can we let the heat and the pressure of our pain do that in us? Will something precious and beautiful emerge? Question. Could you come to know him in a deeper way, and enjoy sweeter fellowship with him? Pain can do that! 問題：我們可否讓痛苦的高溫與壓力，在我們身上成就這件事？ 會不會有一些寶貴而美麗的東西，因此而生出來？ 問題：你會不會因此更深地認識祂，享受與祂更甘甜的相交？ 痛苦可以做到這件事！
………………………………
If that is to happen, it will need more than just pain. Note the order in verse 21 — it starts with God (v.21). "Restore us to yourself, Lord, that we may return." The word translated "return" is literally "be restored" — it is the same word. We will need the Spirit of God. David, suffering the fallout of what happened with Bathsheba, prayed: "Create in me a pure heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me." (Psalm 51:10) 若要這事發生，單有痛苦是不夠的。 請留意第 21 節的次序——是從神開始的（21 節）。 「耶和華啊，求你使我們向你回轉，我們便得回轉。」 譯作「回轉」的那個字，直譯是「被領回、被復興」——原文是同一個字。 我們需要神的靈。 大衛在拔示巴事件的餘波中受苦時，這樣禱告： 「神啊，求你為我造清潔的心，使我裡面重新有正直的靈。」（詩篇 51:10）
And Paul could say: "For to me to live is Christ." (Philippians 1:21) Can you say that yet? That is where I want to get to when I grow up! Question. What about you? And the good news is that this is also where he wants to get you to. Because that way everybody wins. So don't waste your pain. Let him use it! 保羅也能夠這樣說： 「因我活著就是基督。」（腓立比書 1:21） 你可以這樣說了嗎？這是我長大之後想要到達的地方！ 問題：你呢？ 好消息是：這也正是祂要帶你到達的地方。因為這樣一來，人人都得益。 所以，不要浪費你的痛苦。讓祂使用它！

Don't waste your pain

不要浪費你的痛苦

Lamentations 耶利米哀歌 5:1-22 · Bible Study Guide 聖經學習指南
"Restore us to yourself, Lord, that we may return; renew our days as of old." (Lamentations 5:21)
「耶和華啊，求你使我們向你回轉，我們便得回轉。求你復新我們的日子，像古時一樣。」（耶利米哀歌 5:21）
📖 Summary 摘要
English

Lamentations ends where much of life is actually lived — in the middle. The exiles' pain is not over. Time has passed, but the ruins remain, the hunger remains, and the questions remain. The book closes without a tidy answer: "Why do you always forget us?" is never directly answered. There is no Hollywood ending.


And yet something has changed — in them. Chapter 5 is, for the first time, one long sustained communal prayer. Their arrow prayers have become a considered petition laid before the Lord. Eugene Peterson's line captures it: "Prayer is suffering's best result." Two marks of real spiritual progress appear at the very end of the book.


First, faith moves to the centre (v.19). With the temple in ruins it looks as though everything is over — but the Lord is not confined to a building. His throne endures from generation to generation. They are learning to filter their experience through their faith rather than their faith through their experience — a Copernican revolution of the heart, where circumstances orbit faith and not the reverse.


Second, the heart is renewed (v.16, v.21). For the first time there is a clear confession of sin — not the vague "nobody's perfect" that costs nothing, but "we have sinned". And their desire has grown: they no longer want only an end to the suffering, they want the Lord himself. Like carbon under heat and pressure, something precious can be formed. But note the order in verse 21 — it starts with God: "Restore us to yourself, Lord, that we may return." So don't waste your pain. Let him use it.

繁體中文

耶利米哀歌結束的地方，正是我們大部分人生真正身處的地方——中途。被擄者的痛苦仍未過去。日子過了一段，但廢墟仍在、飢餓仍在、疑問仍在。全書結束時並沒有給出一個整齊的答案：「你為何永遠忘記我們？」始終沒有得到直接的回覆。這裡沒有荷里活式的結局。


然而，有些事情確實改變了——是在他們身上。第五章是全卷書中第一次出現的、長篇而持續的群體禱告。從前箭一般的短禱，如今成了深思熟慮、陳明在耶和華面前的懇求。畢德生一句話說得貼切：「禱告是苦難所結出最好的果子。」在全書的結尾，出現了兩個真實屬靈進步的記號。


第一，信心移到中心（19 節）。聖殿成了廢墟，看來一切都完了——但耶和華並不被一座建築物所限制，祂的寶座存到萬代。他們正在學習用信心去過濾自己的經歷，而不是用經歷去過濾自己的信心——這是內心的一場「哥白尼革命」：環境圍繞信心運行，而不是倒過來。


第二，心得著更新（16 節、21 節）。他們第一次清楚認罪——不是那種毫無代價、含糊其辭的「人誰無過」，而是「我們犯罪了」。他們的渴望也長大了：如今所求的不再只是苦難的終結，而是耶和華祂自己。正如碳在高溫與壓力之下，可以形成極其寶貴的東西。但請留意第 21 節的次序——是從神開始的：「耶和華啊，求你使我們向你回轉，我們便得回轉。」所以，不要浪費你的痛苦，讓祂使用它。

✝️ Key Scriptures 重要經文

💡 Key Themes 主要主題
1. Pain is not wasted time 痛苦不是白白浪費的時間
English
The great temptation in a season of suffering is to put life on hold until it passes — to treat the whole stretch as dead ground. The sermon says that is a mistake: the potential for progress is huge precisely here. "The future is created by what you do today, not tomorrow."
繁體中文
在受苦的日子裡，最大的試探就是把人生按下暫停鍵，等痛苦過去再說——把整段日子當作荒地。講道指出這是錯誤的：成長的空間恰恰就在這裡。「將來是由你今天所做的事造成的，不是明天。」
2. Sustained prayer is itself progress 持續的禱告本身就是進步
English
Chapter 5 is the book's first sustained communal lament. Up to now there have only been arrow prayers — sporadic cries and desperate questions. Now the devastation is laid out in an ordered petition before the Lord. "Prayer is suffering's best result" (Eugene Peterson).
繁體中文
第五章是全卷書第一次出現持續的群體哀歌。直到如今，他們只有箭一般的短禱——零星的呼喊、絕望的質問。現在他們把荒涼的光景有條有理地陳明在耶和華面前。「禱告是苦難所結出最好的果子」（畢德生）。
3. Faith at the centre, not circumstances 信心居中，不是環境居中
English
Verse 19 is faith precisely because the circumstances cut the other way: the temple is rubble, yet "your throne endures from generation to generation." The Lord is not a local deity confined to a building; the real sanctuary is in heaven, where no one can touch him. Nothing has changed.
繁體中文
第 19 節之所以是信心，正因為環境所指向的完全相反：聖殿已成瓦礫，他們卻說「你的寶座存到萬代」。耶和華不是被一座建築物所限制的地方神明；真正的聖所在天上，那裡沒有人能觸動祂。甚麼也沒有改變。
4. A Copernican revolution of the heart 內心的哥白尼革命
English
Copernicus showed that the sun, not the earth, is the reference point. The question for us is the same: which is in charge — faith or life? We are to filter our experiences through our faith, letting our circumstances orbit our faith rather than the reverse. This is never easy; even Jesus was overwhelmed with grief in the garden. But it can be practised as a discipline.
繁體中文
哥白尼證明了太陽而非地球才是參照點。同樣的問題也擺在我們面前：究竟是信心在主導，還是生活在主導？我們應當用信心過濾自己的經歷，讓環境圍繞信心運行，而不是倒過來。這從來不容易；連耶穌在客西馬尼園中也曾憂傷至極。但這是可以操練的功課。
5. Real repentance, not vague admission 真實的悔改，不是含糊的承認
English
"We know we are not perfect" costs nothing and everybody says it. Verse 16 is different: "Woe to us, for we have sinned!" This is the first clear confession in the book — what chapter 3 had urged and what restoration cannot happen without. Peter preached the same thing in the same city six centuries later.
繁體中文
「我們知道自己並不完美」這句話毫無代價，人人都會說。第 16 節卻不同：「我們犯罪了，我們有禍了！」這是全卷書第一次清楚的認罪——正是第三章所勸勉的，也是復興不可缺少的一步。六百年後，彼得在同一座城傳講同樣的信息。
6. Wanting God himself, not just relief 渴望神自己，而不只是解脫
English
Verse 21 asks for more than an end to suffering: "Restore us to yourself." They want him again — not merely his mercy and forgiveness. Diamonds and coal are both carbon; heat and pressure over long time make the difference. Pain can produce something precious: a deeper knowledge of God and sweeter fellowship with him.
繁體中文
第 21 節所求的，超過苦難的終結：「求你使我們向你回轉。」他們要的是祂自己——不單是祂的憐憫和赦免。鑽石與煤炭同樣是碳；分別在於長時間的高溫與壓力。痛苦可以生出寶貴的東西：更深地認識神，與祂有更甘甜的相交。
7. It starts with God 一切從神開始
English
Note the order in verse 21: "Restore us to yourself, Lord, that we may return." We do not turn ourselves around by willpower; we need the Spirit of God, as David knew when he prayed for a pure heart and a steadfast spirit. And the goal — "for to me to live is Christ" — is exactly where God wants to bring us. Everybody wins.
繁體中文
請留意第 21 節的次序：「耶和華啊，求你使我們向你回轉，我們便得回轉。」我們無法靠意志力叫自己回轉；我們需要神的靈，正如大衛求神為他造清潔的心、賜他正直的靈。而那個終點——「因我活著就是基督」——正是神要帶我們到達的地方。人人都得益。

🎯 Application 生活應用
English
Stop putting life on hold. Name the thing you have been postponing "until this season passes" — and take one small step in it this week. The future is created by what you do today.
Turn arrow prayers into sustained prayer. Set aside fifteen unhurried minutes to lay the whole situation before God, out loud or in writing, the way chapter 5 does. Do not tidy it up first.
Say one true thing about God daily. Even while nothing has changed outwardly, say verse 19 back to him: "You, Lord, reign forever." Let the fact take the centre before the feelings follow.
Make one specific confession. Move from "I'm not perfect" to naming it before God — and, if it involves someone else, to them. Confession is where restoration starts.
Ask for more than relief. Change what you are praying for: not just "end this", but "restore me to yourself". Ask him to make the pressure produce something precious.
繁體中文
不要再把人生按下暫停鍵。說出那件你一直推說「等這段日子過去再算」的事——本週就在其中踏出小小的一步。將來是由你今天所做的事造成的。

每天說出一句關於神的真話。即使外在環境毫無改變，也把第 19 節說回給祂聽：「耶和華啊，你存到永遠。」讓事實先站在中心，感覺隨後才跟上。
作一次具體的認罪。從「我不完美」進到在神面前指名說出來——若牽涉別人，也向那人說出來。復興是從認罪開始的。

🙏 Prayer 禱告
English
Lord, you reign forever; your throne endures from generation to generation. Nothing that has fallen around us has touched you, and nothing that we are waiting for is beyond you.

We bring you the parts of our lives that are still in ruins — the pain that has gone on longer than we expected, the questions that have not been answered, the mornings we get up and nothing has changed. We do not pretend that it is easy, and we do not pretend that we understand.

Forgive us for the times we have looked at our circumstances and let them define you, instead of looking at you and letting you define our circumstances. Forgive us, too, for the vague confessions that cost us nothing. We have sinned — and we say it plainly today.

By your Spirit, do more in us than simply ending the pain. Restore us to yourself, that we may return. Renew our days. Let the heat and the pressure form something precious: a deeper knowledge of you, a sweeter fellowship with you, a heart that can honestly say, "for to me to live is Christ".

And thank you that we wait as people who have already seen the ending — Easter Saturday has gone, Christ is risen, and joy comes with the morning. Do not let us waste this pain; use it, we pray. In Jesus' name, Amen.
繁體中文
主啊，祢存到永遠；祢的寶座存到萬代。在我們四周倒塌的一切，絲毫沒有動搖祢；我們所等候的一切，也沒有一樣超過祢的能力。

我們把生命中仍是廢墟的部分帶到祢面前——那比我們預期更漫長的痛苦、那些尚未得到答案的疑問、那些早晨起來卻甚麼也沒有改變的日子。我們不假裝這是容易的，也不假裝自己已經明白。

求祢赦免我們：赦免我們常常望著環境，讓環境去定義祢，而不是望著祢，讓祢去定義我們的環境。也求祢赦免我們那些毫無代價、含糊其辭的認罪。我們犯罪了——今天我們坦白地承認。

求祢藉著聖靈，在我們身上作的，不只是叫痛苦停止。求祢使我們向祢回轉，我們便得回轉；求祢復新我們的日子。求祢讓這高溫與壓力，塑造出寶貴的東西：更深地認識祢、與祢有更甘甜的相交、有一顆能夠誠實地說「因我活著就是基督」的心。

也謝謝祢，讓我們等候的時候，已經看見了結局——復活節的星期六已經過去，基督已經復活，早晨便必歡呼。求祢不容我們浪費這份痛苦，求祢使用它。奉耶穌的名祈求，阿們。
