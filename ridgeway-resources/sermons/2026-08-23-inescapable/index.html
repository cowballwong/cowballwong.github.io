Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
Inescapable 無處可逃
(Lamentations 耶利米哀歌 4:1-22)

Inescapable — Lamentations 4:1-22 Big idea: when God comes in judgement, all must suffer the consequences — especially wicked spiritual leaders — and no one and nothing can provide security, except Jesus Christ. 無處可逃——耶利米哀歌 4:1-22 中心信息：當神施行審判的時候，所有人都必須承受後果——尤其是那些邪惡的屬靈領袖；除了耶穌基督以外，沒有任何人、任何事物能夠給我們保障。
………………………………
Introduction: still in the ruins Illustration. A gloriously hot, dry summer. We have been struggling to cope, especially at night. And yet I did not want to wish it away — because I love summer, and I knew it would soon be gone. 引言：仍然身處廢墟之中 比喻。今年的夏天酷熱乾燥，陽光燦爛。我們都難以招架，尤其是晚上。 然而我卻不願意把它趕走——因為我喜愛夏天，而且我知道它很快就會過去。
Last week we stood at the high point of the book — chapter 3. Full of hope! Because although there was ruin all around, there was very good reason to say: this situation will not last for ever. However, there remains the "now" to deal with. And the "now" could hardly be worse. 上週我們站在全卷書的高峰——第三章。 滿有盼望！ 因為儘管四周盡是廢墟，我們仍有充分的理由說：這光景不會永遠持續下去。 然而，我們仍然要面對「現在」。 而這個「現在」，實在壞得不能再壞。
………………………………
As we make our way back to what Robin Ham calls the "wild edges", we are still in shock. True, the invaders have now left. But the silence that remains is broken up by… Starving people scratching the ground, searching for food. Mothers quietly sobbing as they cradle dying children. 當我們再次回到羅賓・咸（Robin Ham）所說的「荒野邊緣」時，我們仍然驚魂未定。 不錯，侵略者如今已經離開。但留下來的死寂，卻不斷被這些聲音打破—— 飢餓的人在地上翻掘，四處尋找食物。 母親抱著垂死的孩子，低聲飲泣。
A measure of how bad it is… Read verse 6 (v.6): "The punishment of my people is greater than that of Sodom, which was overthrown in a moment without a hand turned to help her." 究竟有多慘烈？ 請讀第 6 節（6 節）： 「都因我眾民的罪孽比所多瑪的罪還大；所多瑪雖然無人加手於她，還是轉眼之間被傾覆。」
Doubly shocking… 1. Judah has become Sodom 2.0! Exhibit A of wickedness in the Old Testament is now them. 這裡有雙重的震撼—— 一、猶大已經變成「所多瑪 2.0」！ 舊約中邪惡的頭號證據，如今竟然就是他們自己。
2. It was so bad that it was better to be Sodom!? Given that Sodom was destroyed when fire rained down from heaven, that is quite a claim. But at least that was quick. This is like slow torture — dehumanisation and degradation. 二、情況壞到一個地步，竟然說做所多瑪人還好一些！？ 要知道所多瑪是在天降烈火之下被毀滅的，這樣的說法實在驚人。 但至少那是瞬間的事。 眼前這一切卻像是慢性的酷刑——把人的尊嚴一點一點地剝去。
Note: this was once the glorious city of Zion, Jerusalem. The jewel of the whole earth. Once so full of life and colour. Now a scorched and blackened shell. Read verse 11 (v.11): "The LORD has given full vent to his wrath; he has poured out his fierce anger. He kindled a fire in Zion that consumed her foundations." 請留意：這裡曾經是榮美的錫安城、耶路撒冷。 全地的明珠。 曾經如此充滿生命與色彩。 如今卻只剩下一個被烈火燒焦、漆黑的空殼。 請讀第 11 節（11 節）： 「耶和華發怒成就他所定的，倒出他的烈怒；在錫安使火著起，燒毀錫安的根基。」
One final time we are invited to survey the scene. And to draw three main lessons. We need to — because God is still just, and still must judge sin in all its forms. 我們最後一次被邀請，去細察這幅景象。 並且從中領受三個主要的功課。 我們必須這樣做——因為神仍然是公義的，祂仍然必須審判一切形式的罪。
………………………………
1. The judgement is felt by all The big focus here: every kind of person is sucked into the vortex of destruction. None is immune from the catastrophe. From the highest to the lowest, and all in between. 一、審判臨到所有人 這裡的焦點十分清楚：各式各樣的人，都被捲入這場毀滅的漩渦之中。 沒有一個人能夠倖免於這場災難。 由最高位的，到最卑微的，以及當中所有的人。
Illustration. We know what that feels like — from the Covid pandemic. When a mystery virus emerged in early 2020, there was a degree of panic. Because there was no natural immunity, and no vaccine available — not even to buy on the black market. Even the late Queen was forced to broadcast her message from lockdown. Then, a few months later, she had to sit alone at her husband's funeral. 比喻。我們對這種感受並不陌生——新冠疫情的日子。 2020 年初，一種神秘病毒出現，社會上瀰漫著某種程度的恐慌。 因為人沒有天然的免疫力，也沒有疫苗——就算在黑市也買不到。 連已故的英女皇，也要在封城的處境中發表講話。 幾個月之後，她更要獨自一人，坐在丈夫的喪禮上。
Here is a democracy of degradation. From the rich and powerful, through ordinary men and women, down to the most vulnerable… Read verse 4 (v.4): "Because of thirst the infant's tongue sticks to the roof of its mouth; the children beg for bread, but no one gives it to them." 這裡是一種「人人平等」的墮落與屈辱。 由富有有權勢的人，到平凡的男女，直到最軟弱無助的一群—— 請讀第 4 節（4 節）： 「吃奶孩子的舌頭因乾渴貼住上膛；孩童求餅，無人擘給他們。」
Terrible — but a reminder: when a society, a people, a nation is bent on evil and the Lord "gives them over", it is always the children who suffer most. That should wake us up to how comfortable we have become with sin. Serious sin is never "victimless". 何等可怕——但這也提醒我們：當一個社會、一群人、一個國家一意孤行地行惡，而主「任憑他們」的時候，受害最深的往往是孩子。 這應當叫我們警醒，看見自己對罪已經何等習以為常。 嚴重的罪，從來都不是「沒有受害者」的。
Think again of family breakdown. Who suffers most when a marriage breaks down through cheating, or abuse, or a selfish drive for a fresh start? The children. 再想一想家庭破碎的問題。 當一段婚姻因著不忠、虐待，或是自私地追求「重新開始」而破裂時，誰受的傷害最深？ 是孩子。
………………………………
So there are innocent victims — but no guilty party escapes either. That is true for all within Judah. But — and here is the good news — it is also true of the invaders and persecutors! Back in chapter 2 we asked, "What about them?" Read verses 21-22 (v.21-22): "Rejoice and be glad, Daughter Edom, you who live in the land of Uz. But to you also the cup will be passed; you will be drunk and stripped naked… But he will punish your sin, Daughter Edom, and expose your wickedness." 所以，這裡有無辜的受害者——但有罪的一方，也沒有一個能夠逃脫。 這對猶大境內所有人都是真的。 但——這也是好消息——這對那些侵略者和逼迫者同樣是真的！ 早在第二章我們就曾問過：「那他們呢？」 請讀第 21 至 22 節（21-22 節）： 「住烏斯地的以東民哪，只管歡喜快樂；苦杯也必傳到你那裏；你必喝醉，以致露體。……以東的民哪，他必追討你的罪孽，顯露你的罪惡。」
The Edomites were the offspring of Esau, the brother of Jacob. Near neighbours — but anything but friends. When the Babylonians approached the borders of Israel, the Edomites cheered them on! Popcorn out, and sat back to enjoy the show. But they had better enjoy it while it lasts — because it is soon coming to them. Their days are numbered. Thirty years later, the Babylonians would come for them. 以東人是以掃的後裔，而以掃是雅各的兄弟。 他們是近鄰——卻絕對不是朋友。 當巴比倫大軍逼近以色列邊境的時候，以東人在旁邊拍掌叫好！ 拿著爆谷，坐下來看好戲。 但他們最好趁著還有機會盡情享受——因為同樣的事很快就會臨到他們身上。 他們的日子屈指可數。 三十年之後，巴比倫人就來對付他們了。
………………………………
Illustration. The Christian charity Open Doors estimates that over 388 million Christians suffer persecution and discrimination worldwide. From Sudan to Somalia. From India to Iran. Let us be praying. But let us also remember: justice and judgement are coming. The Lord is watching, and the Lord will not forget (Revelation 6:10-11). 比喻。基督教機構「敞開的門」（Open Doors）估計，全球有超過三億八千八百萬名基督徒正在遭受逼迫和歧視。 由蘇丹到索馬里。 由印度到伊朗。 讓我們為他們禱告。 但也要記得：公義和審判必然來到。 主正在觀看，主必不忘記（啟示錄 6:10-11）。
………………………………
But this is not about "other people". It is about everyone! Jesus is the "judge of the living and the dead". That means me — and you. 但這並不是「別人的事」。 這是關乎每一個人的事！ 耶穌是「審判活人死人的主」。 那就是說：包括我，也包括你。
………………………………
2. The elites are leading the way In chapter 2 we saw that the prophets were not doing their job in the years before the fall. Especially: they were not calling out sin — except Jeremiah. Here it goes up a level. They are leading the charge into worldliness and wickedness. 二、精英階層帶頭犯罪 在第二章我們看見，在城破之前的那些年間，先知們並沒有盡上他們的職分。 尤其是：他們沒有指出人的罪——除了耶利米以外。 到了這裡，情況更進一步。 他們竟然帶頭衝進世俗和罪惡之中。
Read verses 13-16 (v.13-16): "But it happened because of the sins of her prophets and the iniquities of her priests, who shed within her the blood of the righteous… 'Go away! You are unclean!' people cry to them. 'Away! Away! Don't touch us!'… The LORD himself has scattered them; he no longer watches over them. The priests are shown no honour, the elders no favour." 請讀第 13 至 16 節（13-16 節）： 「這都因她先知的罪惡和祭司的罪孽；他們在城中流了義人的血。……人向他們喊著說：不潔淨的，躲開，躲開！不要挨近我！……耶和華發怒，將他們分散，不再眷顧他們；人不重看祭司，也不厚待長老。」
A desperate situation and a disaster for the nation. Priests, prophets, elders — the religious leaders. Rather than setting a godly example and bringing the people back to God, they were some of the worst offenders. Even murder! Such a descent into wickedness — the slaying of the innocent. Note the phrase "the blood of the righteous" (Matthew 23:35). 這是絕望的處境，也是整個國家的災難。 祭司、先知、長老——都是宗教領袖。 他們本應作敬虔的榜樣，把百姓帶回神那裡；結果卻成為罪行最深重的一群。 甚至到了殺人流血的地步！ 如此墮入邪惡之中——竟然殺害無辜的人。 請留意「義人的血」這句話（馬太福音 23:35）。
A shocking example — Jeremiah 26:20-23. On the king's orders they hunted down the prophet Uriah, who had been warning the people and was on the run. He was cut down with the sword. But the Lord sees, and the Lord knows. 一個令人震驚的例子——耶利米書 26:20-23。 他們奉王的命令，追捕先知烏利亞；他曾警戒百姓，後來只能逃亡。 最終他被刀劍殺害。 但主看見，主也知道。
………………………………
The ordinary people were not innocent. The nation as a whole was up to its neck in idolatry, corruption, injustice and immorality. But the leaders were leading — so theirs is the heavier weight of guilt. Those who teach will be judged more severely (James 3:1) — and their judgement is terrible indeed. 一般百姓並不是無辜的。 整個國家都深陷在拜偶像、貪腐、不義和淫亂之中，直到頸項。 但既然是領袖在帶頭，他們就要承擔更重的罪責。 作教師的要受更重的判斷（雅各書 3:1）——他們所受的審判，實在可怕。
Look now… The prophets and seers cannot see (v.14). The priests are unclean (v.15). A terrible and humiliating end. God cannot be mocked. These men of privilege and position deserve everything they get. 現在看看他們的下場—— 先知和先見都看不見（14 節）。 祭司都成了不潔淨的（15 節）。 何等可怕、何等羞辱的結局。 神是輕慢不得的。 這些享有特權和地位的人，他們所受的一切都是罪有應得。
………………………………
There have been many murderous clerics through history. Illustration. The Rwandan genocide of 1994. In just over 100 days, up to one million people died — mostly from the minority Tutsi community. Even more shocking: Rwanda was one of the most Christianised nations in Africa. Many church pastors were involved in trying to protect the victims — but many also took part in the killing. The Lord sees, and the Lord knows. 歷史上，有不少手上沾滿血腥的神職人員。 比喻。1994 年的盧旺達種族滅絕。 在短短一百天之內，多達一百萬人死亡——大部分是少數族群圖西族的人。 更令人震驚的是：盧旺達當時是非洲基督教化程度最高的國家之一。 許多教會的牧者曾努力保護受害者——但也有許多人參與了屠殺。 主看見，主也知道。
Illustration. In the religious upheavals of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, many were killed on both sides. Illustration. More recently, consider the scale of sexual abuse among clergy and church leaders. It may not be literal "blood" — but lives have still been destroyed. 比喻。在十六、十七世紀的宗教動盪之中，雙方都有許多人被殺害。 比喻。再看近代，神職人員和教會領袖當中性侵犯事件的規模。 那或許不是字面上的「流血」——但同樣是毀掉了人的一生。
Jesus said: "It would be better for them to have a large millstone hung around their neck and to be drowned in the depths of the sea." (Matthew 18:6) 耶穌說： 「凡使這信我的一個小子跌倒的，倒不如把大磨石拴在這人的頸項上，沉在深海裏。」（馬太福音 18:6）
How reassuring, then, that we have a prophet, a priest and a king who is… Always wonderfully good. Never takes advantage, never cruel, never corrupt. He leads us nowhere except into the green pastures of divine blessing and fullness. 因此，我們有這樣一位先知、祭司和君王，是何等令人安心—— 祂永遠美善無瑕。 祂從不佔人便宜，從不殘忍，從不腐敗。 祂所帶領我們去的，只有那滿有神恩福與豐盛的青草地。
………………………………
3. The security that never was This is not just physical devastation, or even human degradation. It is also a massive psychological blow. When the heavy hand of divine judgement landed on them, everything they had relied on for security crumbled around them. Naked and defenceless. 三、從來不存在的保障 這不單是物質上的毀壞，甚至不單是人性尊嚴的墮落。 這也是一次巨大的心理打擊。 當神審判的重手臨到他們身上，他們一切所倚靠、所視為保障的，都在四周崩塌下來。 赤身露體，毫無防衛。
Illustration. "There is no danger that Titanic will sink. The boat is unsinkable and nothing but inconvenience will be suffered by the passengers." Philip Franklin, Vice-President of the White Star Line — the company that owned and operated her — in 1912. Not long afterwards, her broken hull lay at the bottom of the icy waters of the North Atlantic. And she had taken 1,500 passengers with her. They had it covered. It would be fine! No! 比喻。「鐵達尼號絕無沉沒的危險。這艘船是不沉之船，乘客最多只會感到一點不便而已。」 這是 1912 年白星航運公司（擁有並營運該船）副總裁富蘭克林（Philip Franklin）所說的話。 不久之後，這艘船斷裂的船身，就躺在北大西洋冰冷的海底。 並且帶走了一千五百名乘客的性命。 他們以為一切都在掌握之中。以為一定沒問題！ 結果並非如此！
………………………………
There were lots of reasons why these people thought they would be all right. A sad delusion. A false complacency. We have already seen them… The temple would insulate them. It is the house of the Lord! Then the city walls! Well built. Both now lie in ruins. Psychologically devastating. 這些人有許多理由，以為自己一定不會有事。 那是可悲的幻覺。 是虛假的安逸。 我們已經看過了—— 聖殿會保護他們。那可是耶和華的殿！ 還有城牆！建造得堅固無比。 如今兩者都成了廢墟。 在心理上，這是徹底的摧毀。
Others still today… a. Wealth and privilege (v.5, 7) As anywhere, some can buy their way out of trouble. Or rely on their connections. Not this time. Read verses 5 and 7 (v.5, 7): "Those who once ate delicacies are destitute in the streets. Those brought up in royal purple now lie on ash heaps… Their princes were brighter than snow and whiter than milk." 今天我們也有其他同類的倚靠—— 甲、財富與地位（5、7 節） 正如任何地方一樣，有些人可以用錢買通、脫身。 或是靠關係擺平。 但這一次不能。 請讀第 5 和第 7 節（5、7 節）： 「素來吃美好食物的，現今在街上變為孤寒；素來臥朱紅褥子的，現今躺臥糞堆。……錫安的貴冑素來比雪純淨，比奶更白。」
b. The king — to look after them To secure the borders, to protect and provide. Note "the LORD's anointed" (v.20). But not this king. Read verse 20 (v.20): "The LORD's anointed, our very life breath, was caught in their traps. We thought that under his shadow we would live among the nations." 乙、君王——以為他會照顧他們 以為他能守衛邊境、保護供養百姓。 請留意「耶和華的受膏者」（20 節）。 但這一位王卻不能。 請讀第 20 節（20 節）： 「耶和華的受膏者好比我們鼻中的氣，在他們的坑中被捉住；我們曾論到他說：我們必在他蔭下，在列國中存活。」
Zedekiah was a wicked man (2 Kings 24:19). Without the Lord on his side, he could do nothing to prevent the collapse of the city. Note: he could not even save himself. He fled to the hills when the walls were breached. But he was hunted down. His eyes were cut out, and he was dragged off into exile. 西底家是個邪惡的人（列王紀下 24:19）。 沒有主與他同在，他根本無力阻止這城的傾覆。 請留意：他連自己也救不了。 城牆被攻破的時候，他逃往山區。 但他還是被追捕到。 他的雙眼被剜出，然後被擄去異地。
c. The insurance of another nation Probably Egypt — coming to their aid. But that too came to nothing. Read verse 17 (v.17): "Moreover, our eyes failed, looking in vain for help; from our towers we watched for a nation that could not save us." Another security that never was! 丙、另一個國家作保險 很可能是埃及——以為他們會來相助。 但這同樣落了空。 請讀第 17 節（17 節）： 「我們仰望人來幫助，以致眼目失明，還是枉然；我們所盼望的，竟盼望一個不能救人的國！」 又一個從來不存在的保障！
………………………………
We have our own list of things we think will insulate us against bad things. Wealth and privilege. Education, a good CV. Platinum health insurance. And so on. None of them can! 我們自己也有一份清單，以為這些東西可以保護我們免受災禍。 財富與地位。 學歷、漂亮的履歷。 最高級的醫療保險。 如此類推。 但沒有一樣做得到！
More foolishly still, we have a list of things we think will protect us when we stand before the throne of God. Regular church-going. Moving in the right circles. An impressive moral CV. Do not rely on anything other than the finished work of Jesus. The only refuge from the wrath to come is the one he has provided for us. If you have not yet come to him in repentance and faith… Today! 更愚昧的是，我們還有另一份清單，以為這些可以在神的寶座前保護我們。 恆常返教會。 在合適的圈子中來往。 一份亮麗的道德履歷。 不要倚靠任何東西，只要倚靠耶穌已經完成的救贖。 唯一能躲避那將來忿怒的避難所，就是祂親自為我們預備的那一位。 如果你還未曾以悔改和信心來到祂面前—— 就在今天！

Inescapable

無處可逃

Lamentations 耶利米哀歌 4:1-22 · Bible Study Guide 聖經學習指南
"Your punishment will end, Daughter Zion; he will not prolong your exile." (Lamentations 4:22)
「錫安的民哪，你罪孽的刑罰受足了，耶和華必不使你再被擄去。」（耶利米哀歌 4:22）
📖 Summary 摘要
English

Lamentations 3 was the high point of the book — hope in the middle of the ruins. Chapter 4 brings us back to the "now". The invaders have gone, but Jerusalem is a scorched shell: people scratch the ground for food, mothers cradle dying children, and the poet says the punishment is worse than Sodom's, because Sodom's end at least came quickly (v.6).


Standing over the ruins one last time, the sermon draws three lessons. First, the judgement is felt by all — from the wealthy to the most vulnerable, nobody is immune, and it is always the children who suffer most when a nation is given over to evil. Nor do the guilty escape: Edom cheered the Babylonians on, and thirty years later the same cup was passed to them (v.21-22).


Second, the elites led the way. Prophets, priests and elders did not merely fail to call out sin; they led the charge into wickedness, even shedding the blood of the righteous (v.13). Those who teach are judged more severely, and their end is humiliating: seers who cannot see, priests declared unclean.


Third, the securities they trusted never existed. Temple, city walls, wealth, the king, and a promised ally all crumbled together. Our own list is no stronger — savings, status, insurance, a respectable church record. The only refuge from the wrath to come is the one God himself has provided: the finished work of Jesus Christ, our good prophet, priest and king.
