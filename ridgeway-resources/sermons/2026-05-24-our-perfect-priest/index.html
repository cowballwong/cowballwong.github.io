Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
Our Perfect Priest 我們完美的大祭司
(Hebrews 5:1-10 希伯來書 5 章 1 至 10 節)

Intro 引言：Our Perfect Priest — Hebrews 5:1-10引言：我們完美的大祭司——希伯來書 5 章 1 至 10 節
………………………………………………
One of the greatest challenges for the people of God is to hold the right mental picture of our God. That is the very reason we have the Bible.神的子民其中一個最大的挑戰，就是對我們的神，能否持守一個正確的形像。這正是神賜下聖經給我們的原因。
Left to ourselves, we can only come up with a God who looks like us, or like the people we know. And because we — and the world around us — are messed up in all kinds of ways, the picture we paint of God is bound to be wrong. It is distorted.若任由我們自己去想像，我們所能描繪的神，不過是像我們自己，或像我們身邊的人。而我們和我們所處的世界，本身就在各樣的方面已經破碎，所以我們對神的描繪，必然失準。那是扭曲了的神。
When life is bumbling along, we can get away with a distorted picture. But when we feel the heat — the weight of sin, the pressure of suffering — then the ground beneath our feet begins to give way.當人生風平浪靜的時候，扭曲的神觀，似乎並不構成甚麼問題。可是，當我們感受到罪與苦難的壓迫時，腳下的地基就會開始崩塌。
Worse still — God himself starts to become part of the problem, rather than the solution.更糟的是——「神自己」竟然成為我們問題的一部分，而不再是答案。
………………………………………………
As we keep going, a right view of God — and especially the heart of God — is the key to everything.我們繼續向前走，對神有正確的認識，尤其是認識「神的心」，是一切的關鍵。
Peace. Joy. Perseverance. Strength. Obedience. Even sanity itself. All of them depend on it.平安、喜樂、忍耐、力量、順服，甚至連心智的清明，全都建基於此。
So the question is this: how does our God feel about us — in our sin and in our suffering?於是問題就是：「我們的神對我們有怎樣的感受？尤其當我們陷在罪中、處在苦難中時？」
………………………………………………
Hebrews is full of warnings — warnings to keep us on the path of repentance and faith, warnings about the consequences of drifting away.希伯來書全書充滿了警告——警告我們要走在悔改與信心的道路上，警告我們若偏離正道會招致甚麼後果。
But we will only see those warnings as being for our good — rather than as something to worry us — if we know his heart.可是，唯有我們真正認識祂的心，我們才會看見這些警告其實是「為我們的好處」而設，而不是叫我們惶惶不安的話。
Hebrews 5:1-10 is a vital piece of this puzzle.希伯來書 5 章 1 至 10 節，正是這幅圖畫中的一塊關鍵拼圖。
………………………………………………
This passage is a continuation of 4:14-16 — last week's text. The writer is still painting the picture — showing us what it means for Jesus to be our great High Priest.這段經文是上週「4 章 14 至 16 節的延續」。作者繼續描繪——讓我們看見耶穌作我們「大祭司」是甚麼意思。
And why he is exactly the kind of priest that sinners and sufferers need.並且為甚麼祂正是罪人與受苦的人所需要的「那一種大祭司」。
………………………………………………
At first glance, it is hard for us to get excited or engaged about priests and high priests — because that is not the world we inhabit.乍看之下，我們很難對「祭司」和「大祭司」這些話題感到興奮或投入，因為那並非我們今日所處的世界。
But it certainly was the world of the original audience.可是，這對最初的讀者來說，卻是切身的事。
They were believers in Jesus who came from a Jewish religious background. And they were beginning to long for the old days and the old ways — because they were forgetting that Jesus is better.他們是從猶太宗教背景歸信耶穌的信徒。他們開始懷念昔日的舊俗、舊路——因為他們漸漸忘記了，耶穌乃是「更美」的那一位。
Jesus wasn't rubbishing the old priestly system. He was fulfilling and completing it. He is like those old priests — mediators who stood in the gap — but he is better.耶穌並非否定昔日的祭司制度，祂是「成全和完成」這個制度的那一位。祂與舊約的祭司有相似之處——同樣是站在罪人與神之間的中保——但祂是「更美」的。
………………………………………………
Two things are true of every high priest — including Jesus — in 5:1-10.希伯來書 5 章 1 至 10 節指出，「兩件事」是每一位大祭司——包括耶穌——所共有的。
1. Appointment by God (v4-6).一、「由神所立」（4 至 6 節）。
2. Had to understand the people (v1-3, v7-10). That is — they had to have solidarity with the people in their humanness. They had to be one of us.二、「必須體諒百姓」（1 至 3 節、7 至 10 節）。意思是說，他們必須與百姓在「同為人」這件事上，有真切的「認同」。他們必須是我們當中的一份子。
That was true of the priests of old. But it is even more so for the ultimate, flawless High Priest.舊約的祭司是這樣，那位至終、毫無瑕疵的大祭司，更「是這樣」。
………………………………………………
We won't say more on appointment today. The rest of our time will focus on the second — solidarity. Jesus with us and for us — in our weakness.今日我們不再多談「由神所立」這一點。其餘時間，我們會集中在第二點——「認同」。耶穌「與我們同行、為我們而立」——在我們的軟弱中。
In the struggle with sin. In the struggle with suffering. He doesn't just know what we need. He is what we need.在我們與罪掙扎、與苦難搏鬥的時候。祂不僅「知道」我們需要甚麼。祂自己「就是」我們所需要的。
As we journey from here to heaven, often on our hands and knees — that matters more than anything.我們從今日到天家的這段路上，常常要四肢着地、艱難前行——這位大祭司，是我們最大的倚靠。
………………………………………………
Our text today is v2 first, and v8 second. And we'll start by remembering one verse from last week: Hebrews 4:15.今日我們先看第 2 節，再看第 8 節。我們先記住上週的一節經文——「希伯來書 4 章 15 節」。
1. Though not a sinner, he deals gently (v2)一、雖然祂從未犯罪，祂仍能體諒我們（第 2 節）
………………………………………………
Read v1-2: "Every high priest is selected from among the people and is appointed to represent the people in matters related to God… He is able to deal gently with those who are ignorant and are going astray, since he himself is subject to weakness."請讀第 1 至 2 節：「凡從人間挑選的大祭司，是奉派替人辦理屬神的事……他能體諒那愚蒙的和失迷的人，因為他自己也是被軟弱所困。」
………………………………………………
Question. How do you imagine Jesus — when you mess up? When you can't shake off the sin?問你一個問題：當你跌倒、犯罪、無法擺脫罪的纏累時，你「想像」中的耶穌，是怎樣的耶穌？
Do you imagine him exasperated with you in your sin, your failure, your shame? At best, rolling his eyes in resigned frustration. At worst, giving you one last chance.你會否想像祂對你的罪、失敗、羞恥感到惱怒？最好的情況下，是無奈地翻白眼；最差的情況下，是冷冷地給你「最後一次機會」？
Saying to himself: "After all I've done for them…!?"心裡對自己說：「我為他們付出了那麼多，他們竟然還這樣！？」
If that's how you picture him, you couldn't be more wrong.若那是你心中對耶穌的圖畫，那「與真實的耶穌相去甚遠」。
………………………………………………
Let me illustrate. The American pastor and theologian Dane Ortlund has written a book called Gentle and Lowly. It is about the heart of Jesus for sinners and sufferers.讓我引一段話。美國牧師兼神學家「Dane Ortlund」寫過一本書，書名是「溫柔謙卑」。整本書的內容，就是論耶穌對罪人和受苦者的心。
On Hebrews 5:2 he writes: "Looking inside ourselves, we can anticipate only harshness from heaven. Looking out to Christ, we can anticipate only gentleness." (p.57)他論到希伯來書 5 章 2 節時寫道：「我們望向自己的內裡，所能預期的只有天上嚴厲的對待；我們望向基督，所能預期的卻只有溫柔。」（第 57 頁）
………………………………………………
In the first place, the verse is about Old Testament priests. They themselves were sinners. They had to offer sacrifices for their own sins (v3).首先，這節經文原本是論到舊約的祭司。他們自己也是罪人，必須先為自己的罪獻祭（第 3 節）。
Jesus is not that — see 4:15. He never sinned. But gentleness — that belongs to Jesus too!耶穌「並非如此」——請看 4 章 15 節。祂從未犯過罪。然而，「體諒、溫柔」——也屬於耶穌！
How do we know? Because of the phrase "subject to weakness" in v2. It was already used for Jesus in 4:15.我們從哪裡知道？因為第 2 節用了「被軟弱所困」這個詞。同一個詞，4 章 15 節已經用在耶穌身上。
So this is true of him too — him now — him forever.所以這也描繪「耶穌」——「如今的祂」——「永遠的祂」。
………………………………………………
"Deal gently" comes precisely because he is full of weakness.「體諒」這份能力，正是因為祂自己「被軟弱所充滿」。
Now — Jesus is the divine, eternal Son (v5). In his divine nature, he is not weak. But in his human nature, he is weak — just like us!當然——耶穌是「永恆的神的兒子」（第 5 節）。在祂神的本性裡，祂並不軟弱。但在祂的「人性」裡，祂是軟弱的——「與我們一樣」！
………………………………………………
Of course Jesus never crossed the line into sin. If he had, he would be no better than the other priests. But — in the words of the Puritan Goodwin — he still knew the oppression of it.當然，耶穌從未跨過罪的界線。若祂曾犯過罪，祂就「不會勝過」其他所有的祭司。但是——用清教徒 Goodwin 的話來說——「祂依然親身嘗過罪的壓迫」。
Because he knew the same kind of weaknesses that make it harder to resist sin. Like what?因為祂親身嘗過令人難以抗拒罪的那些軟弱。哪些軟弱呢？
HALT. Hungry. Angry. Lonely. Tired.「HALT」——飢餓（Hungry）、忿怒（Angry）、孤單（Lonely）、疲倦（Tired）。
At our weakest — in the face of sin — Jesus knew all of that. "Subject to weakness." So he gets what that is like. That is why his heart goes out to us — and why he is gentle with us.在我們最軟弱、最被罪試探的時刻——耶穌全都嘗過。「被軟弱所困」。所以祂「親身明白」那是甚麼滋味。這正是祂的心向我們發動的原因——「這正是祂對我們溫柔的原因」。
………………………………………………
Question — what kind of moral failure, in his people, brings out this gentleness of Jesus?問你一個問題：祂的子民有「哪一種」道德上的失敗，會引出耶穌的溫柔？
Every kind. "Ignorant". "Going astray". Probably accidental, and probably deliberate. Makes no difference.「每一種」。「愚蒙的」、「失迷的」。無論是無心之過，還是故意而為。「毫無分別」。
Dane Ortlund again: "What elicits tenderness from Jesus is not the severity of the sin but whether the sinner comes to him!" (p.54)Ortlund 再次寫道：「能引出耶穌溫柔的，不是罪的嚴重程度，而是這個罪人會否來到祂面前。」（第 54 頁）
If a person refuses to come to Jesus — refuses to come for atonement — that is different. The wrath of God remains on him (John 3:36). For God hates sin, and all the ruin it causes.若有人拒絕來到耶穌面前——拒絕來尋求赦罪——「那就是另外一回事」。神的忿怒仍歸在他身上（約翰福音 3 章 36 節）。因為神恨惡罪，恨惡罪所帶來的一切毀壞。
But for those who do come — the tender heart of the Saviour is always waiting for us.可是，對於「真的來到祂面前的人」——救主溫柔的心，「永遠在那裡等候我們」。
No matter what. Jesus deals gently — and only gently — every single time we come to him. No matter how often.「無論你做過甚麼」。「我們每一次來到耶穌面前，祂的對待，只有體諒，只有溫柔」。無論你回來了多少次。
………………………………………………
Question — do you see this?再問一句：你「看得見」這個圖畫嗎？
Not irritated. Not annoyed. Not fed up. No scowl. No scold. No lashing out.「不是」惱怒。「不是」厭煩。「不是」受夠了。沒有怒目相對。沒有責罵。沒有發作。
Just glad. Every time. It is the only way he knows how to be.只有「歡喜」。「每一次」。這是祂唯一知道的待人之道。
Dane Ortlund's book updates an old Puritan work by Thomas Goodwin — "The Heart of Christ towards Sinners on earth" (1651). These are among my favourite sentences ever written outside the Bible:Ortlund 這本書，其實是改寫自清教徒 Thomas Goodwin 的舊作——「基督對地上罪人的心」（1651 年成書）。其中有幾句話，是我在聖經以外最喜愛的句子之一：
"Your very sins move him more to pity than to anger."「你的罪，叫祂憐憫多於發怒。」
That is true. So don't stay away — draw near! (4:16)那是「真實的」。所以「不要遠離祂」——「坦然來到祂面前」（4 章 16 節）！
………………………………………………
2. Though not a sinner, he learned to obey (v8)二、雖然祂從未犯罪，祂仍學了順從（第 8 節）
………………………………………………
Read v8-9: "Although he was a son, he learned obedience from what he suffered, and, once made perfect, he became the source of eternal salvation for all who obey him."請讀第 8 至 9 節：「他雖然為兒子，還是因所受的苦難學了順從。他既得以完全，就為凡順從他的人成了永遠得救的根源。」
………………………………………………
If we were surprised that Jesus deals gently, we might be even more surprised that Jesus had to learn to obey.若「耶穌會體諒我們」已經令我們驚訝，那麼「耶穌需要學習順從」這件事，可能更令我們驚奇。
How can that be — given what 4:15 says about him?既然 4 章 15 節說祂從未犯罪，這怎麼可能呢？
………………………………………………
There are different kinds of learning obedience.「學習順從」其實可以有不同的意思。
One kind — going from behaving wrongly to behaving rightly. Needing correction. That is how kids learn. That is not what is happening here.其中一種——是由原本錯誤的行為，轉變為正確的行為。「需要被矯正」。這是小孩子學習的方式。「這不是這節經文所講的順從」。
But there's another kind — learning as you go, as circumstances change, as you encounter new situations.另一種——是「一邊走、一邊學」，隨著環境改變、隨著遇上新處境，慢慢地學。
Some things can only be learned from experience. Illustration — learning to be a plumber, or a doctor. You can't just read books. You have to do the practice, the training, the learning on the job.有些事，只能從親身的經歷中才學得到。舉個例子：學做水喉匠，或學做醫生。你不能只靠讀書。你必須「親身去做、去受訓、邊做邊學」。
Same goes for discipleship — learning to trust and obey God. You can't do it from a textbook. You learn it on the job!「作主門徒」也是同樣的道理——學習信靠神、學習順服神。你不能單靠讀書去學。你必須「在路上一邊走，一邊學」！
Same with Jesus. He learned to obey in every situation — as each new situation arose. He could not learn to obey in situations he had never been in! Any more than we can.耶穌也是這樣。祂「在每一個處境裡」學習順從——隨著每一個新處境的出現而學習。祂不能在祂從未經歷過的處境中，就先學會順從！正如我們也不能。
………………………………………………
And — again, just like us — Jesus learned most in times of trial and struggle. Note the phrase: "from what he suffered."並且——「同樣與我們一樣」——耶穌學得最多的時候，是在「試煉與掙扎」之中。請留意這句：「因所受的苦難」。
That is always the way. You can't learn patience until your patience is tested. You can't learn perseverance until things get hard. You can't learn contentment until you are deprived of something.事情向來如此。除非你的忍耐被試驗，否則你學不到忍耐。除非處境變得艱難，否則你學不到堅忍。除非你被剝奪了某些東西，否則你學不到知足。
You see? It's true of us — but it's also true of Jesus. And he knows it isn't easy.你看見嗎？這對我們是真的——「對耶穌也是真的」。而祂也親身明白，這條路並不容易。
………………………………………………
Read v7: "In the days of his flesh, Jesus offered up prayers and supplications, with loud cries and tears, to him who was able to save him from death."請讀第 7 節：「基督在肉體的時候，既大聲哀哭、流淚禱告、懇求那能救他免死的主。」
Question. Have you ever cried out in desperation — at something pressing in on you, killing you, (v7)? Do you know what it's like to be at the end of your rope?問你一個問題：你曾否「在絕望中呼喊」——面對那些壓迫你、令你幾乎承受不住的事「（第 7 節）」？你可知道「到了山窮水盡」是甚麼滋味？
Our Saviour knows.我們的救主，「祂知道」。
………………………………………………
Jesus has been to some of the deepest and darkest places. None more so than Gethsemane — and what followed at Calvary.耶穌曾踏足過最深最暗的所在。「最深的，莫過於客西馬尼園」——以及隨之而來在各各他所發生的一切。
In agony, sweating blood, crying out. Tears of anguish. More desperate than he had ever been.祂在劇痛中，汗如血珠滴下，放聲呼喊。流著痛苦的眼淚。「比祂一生中任何時刻都更絕望」。
And he prayed what?祂禱告了「甚麼」？
It must primarily have been — "save me from death." The Gospels tell us: "If there is some other way…"祂禱告的核心，必然是——「救我脫離這死」。福音書告訴我們，祂也禱告：「倘若可行，求你叫這杯離開我」。
What was about to happen was so terrible — we will never fully understand. The Lord was about to lay on him the iniquity of us all. Imagine the weight of all that sin and shame!即將發生的事，是極其可怕的——我們永遠不能完全明白。「耶和華使我們眾人的罪孽都歸在他身上」。想像那一切罪、那一切羞恥，重重壓在祂身上的份量！
And his prayer was answered. The cross had to happen — but the resurrection was close behind. He did not stay dead.祂的禱告蒙了應允。「十字架是必須發生的」——但「復活」緊隨其後。祂並沒有留在死裡。
………………………………………………
But the main point isn't what Jesus prayed for, or what the answer looked like. The main point is v8 — he was a fellow sufferer, and it was at those times that he was learning.可是，重點並不在於祂禱告甚麼、答案如何。「重點在第 8 節」——祂是一位「與我們同受苦的同伴」，並且正是在那些苦難中，祂正在「學習」。
Learning what? Learning what faithfulness looked like — what it meant to trust and obey when the pressure was on. That means two things.學習甚麼？學習「忠心的樣式」——當壓力臨到時，信靠與順服是甚麼意思。這帶出兩個結論。
………………………………………………
1. He is able to earn salvation for us (v9a)（一）祂能為我們成就救恩（第 9 節上）
Because we so often don't trust and obey.因為我們「實在太多時候做不到」信靠與順服。
Read v9: "And, once made perfect, he became the source of eternal salvation for all who obey him."請讀第 9 節：「他既得以完全，就為凡順從他的人成了永遠得救的根源。」
"Made perfect" here is not a moral thing. It is a vocation thing. It means perfectly qualified through his faithfulness to the end, no matter what.這裡的「得以完全」「不是指道德上的完全」。乃是指「使命上的完全」。意思是說，祂藉著「無論如何也忠心到底」的順從，「達致完全合資格」的地步。
We need a Great High Priest who was weak — but not a High Priest who failed.我們需要一位「曾經軟弱」的大祭司——但絕「不是一位失敗的大祭司」。
We need more than sympathy. We need rescue.我們需要的，不只是同情。我們需要的是「救拔」。
………………………………………………
2. He can help us live that life too (v9b)（二）祂也能幫助我們活出順服的生命（第 9 節下）
Not only did he live this faithfulness himself — he can help us to live it too.祂不只自己活出了這份忠心——「祂也能幫助我們去活出這份忠心」。
If we are those who obey him (v9) — then in our weakness, we are going to need him.若我們是那些「順從祂」的人（第 9 節）——那麼在我們的軟弱中，我們必然「需要祂」。
And the good news is — we can have him. He has made himself available. He knows what it takes.好消息是——「我們可以擁有祂」。祂已經自己預備好。祂知道走這條路需要甚麼。
In your sin and in your suffering — don't stay away. Draw near. And let him help you.在你的罪與你的苦難中——「不要遠離祂」。「坦然來到祂面前」。然後讓祂「親自幫助你」。
That is his heart.「這就是祂的心」。

Our Perfect Priest

我們完美的大祭司

Hebrews 5:1-10 希伯來書 5 章 1 至 10 節 · Bible Study Guide 聖經學習指南
"Although he was a son, he learned obedience through what he suffered. And being made perfect, he became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey him." · 「他雖然為兒子，還是因所受的苦難學了順從。他既得以完全，就為凡順從他的人成了永遠得救的根源。」(Hebrews 希伯來書 5 章 8 至 9 節)
📖 Summary 摘要
English
Hebrews 5:1-10 is the second half of a single sermon-image: Jesus as our Great High Priest. It picks up where 4:14-16 left off, and now the writer slows down to show us what kind of priest Jesus is. Two things mark every priest — appointment by God, and solidarity with the people. The writer focuses on the second. Jesus, the eternal Son, took on a human nature subject to weakness. Because he has been hungry, angry, lonely, tired — and pressed by suffering to the very limit (Gethsemane) — he deals gently with us when we mess up, and he learned obedience in such a way that he can both earn salvation for us and help us walk the path of obedience ourselves. The pastoral application is direct: do not stay away from a Saviour like this — draw near.
繁體中文
希伯來書 5 章 1 至 10 節，是同一幅圖畫的下半部——「耶穌作我們的大祭司」。 經文延續 4 章 14 至 16 節，作者放慢腳步，讓我們看清楚耶穌是「哪一種大祭司」。 每一位大祭司都有兩個記號——由神所立、並與百姓認同。作者集中講第二點。 耶穌是永恆的神的兒子，卻取了「會被軟弱所困的人性」。因為祂親身經歷過飢餓、忿怒、孤單、疲倦， 也曾被苦難逼迫到極限（客西馬尼園的禱告），所以當我們跌倒時，祂能「體諒我們」；祂所「學習的順從」， 使祂既能「為我們成就救恩」，也能「幫助我們行在順服的道路上」。 這段經文的牧者勸勉非常直接：不要遠離這位救主——「坦然來到祂面前」。

💡 Key Themes 主要主題
1. The picture we hold of God shapes everything 我們對神的圖畫，左右一切
English
Left to ourselves, we paint a distorted God who looks too much like the people who have failed us. That distortion holds up in calm weather but collapses under the weight of sin and suffering. The right picture of God's heart is the key to peace, perseverance, and even sanity.
繁體中文
若任由我們自己去想像，我們所描繪的神，往往太像那些曾傷害我們的人。平日無風無浪時，這幅扭曲的圖畫尚可勉強應付；但在罪與苦難的重壓之下，它便會崩塌。對神「心」的正確認識，是平安、忍耐、甚至心智清明的關鍵。
2. Solidarity is the priest's calling 與人認同——這是大祭司的呼召
English
Every high priest had to be one of the people he represented. He could not stand in the gap unless he had walked in their shoes. Jesus, the eternal Son, took on a weak human nature precisely so he could be a priest who truly understands us — not from above, but from the inside.
繁體中文
每一位大祭司，都必須是他所代表的百姓當中的一份子。若沒有走過他們的路，他就無法替他們站在神與人之間。耶穌——永恆的神的兒子——正是為了能成為真正體諒我們的祭司，才取了會被軟弱所困的人性。祂的明白，不是俯視式的，而是「從裡面」的明白。
3. Gentleness — every time, no matter what 體諒——每一次，無論如何
English
Jesus' tenderness is not triggered by the smallness of your sin — it is triggered by your willingness to come. Ortlund: "Your very sins move him more to pity than to anger." The only condition for finding gentleness is to draw near.
繁體中文
引發耶穌溫柔的，不是你的罪有多微小，乃是你是否願意來到祂面前。Ortlund 寫道：「你的罪，叫祂憐憫多於發怒。」唯一找到祂溫柔的條件，就是「肯來」。
4. Learning obedience — vocation, not morality 學習順從——是使命，不是道德
English
Jesus' "learning" was not a child learning right from wrong. It was a faithful Son learning, step by step, what obedience meant in each new situation — most fully in Gethsemane. He is "made perfect" in the sense of fully qualified, by faithfulness to the very end.
繁體中文
耶穌的「學習」，不是小孩子分辨對錯的學習。乃是忠心的兒子，按著每一個新處境，逐步學習順從是甚麼意思——最深的一刻，便是客西馬尼園。祂之所以「得以完全」，意思是因著祂忠心到底的順從，達致完全合資格的地步。
5. He doesn't only save — he also helps us obey 祂不僅拯救——祂也幫助我們順從
English
Verse 9 has two halves. Eternal salvation is for all who obey him — and he is also the source of that obedience in us. Because he has been there, he knows what we need. The path is the same: draw near and let him help.
繁體中文
第 9 節有兩個層面。一方面，永遠的救恩是為「凡順從他的人」預備的；另一方面，這份順從的源頭，也是祂自己。因為祂走過這條路，所以祂知道我們需要甚麼。道路只有一條：坦然來到祂面前，讓祂幫助你。

🙏 Prayer 禱告
English

Lord Jesus, our Great High Priest —

You know what it is to be hungry, angry, lonely and tired. You know what it is to cry out, with loud tears, at the end of your rope. And from that very place you deal gently with us — not annoyed, not exasperated, only glad that we came.

Forgive us for the small picture we so often hold of your face. Forgive us for staying away when shame told us to. We come back today. Help us not to dress up the cries we bring you. Teach us to trust and obey, the way you did — one situation at a time. And as you have become the source of eternal salvation to all who obey you, be the source of obedience in us this week.

In your name we pray, Amen.

繁體中文

主耶穌，我們的大祭司——

祢知道飢餓、忿怒、孤單、疲倦是甚麼滋味。祢知道走到山窮水盡、大聲呼喊、痛哭流淚是甚麼滋味。然而正是從這份親身的明白裡，祢溫柔地待我們——不是惱怒、不是厭煩，只是因我們肯回來而歡喜。

求祢赦免我們常常把祢的面容描繪得太小、太冷。求祢赦免我們因羞愧而不敢親近祢的時候。今日我們再次回到祢面前。求祢幫助我們，不要修飾我們所帶到祢面前的呼喊。求祢教我們學習信靠與順服，正如祢曾經學習——一個處境，一個處境地學。並且求祢——那位「為凡順從祢的人成了永遠得救的根源」的主——在我們裡面，也成為這一週順服的源頭。

奉祢的名祈求，阿們。
