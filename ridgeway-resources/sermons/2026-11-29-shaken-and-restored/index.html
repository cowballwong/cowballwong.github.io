Shaken and Restored 震動與復興
(Amos 9:5-15 阿摩司書 9:5-15)

Shaken and Restored (Amos 9:5-15)震動與復興（阿摩司書 9:5-15）
Intro引言
Welcome to Advent.歡迎大家進入將臨期。
Church has traditionally called these next 4 Sundays Advent because it’s leading up to the big event of Christmas: the arrival of Jesus.教會傳統上把接下來的四個主日稱為「將臨期」，因為這段日子引領我們迎向聖誕節這件大事：耶穌的降臨。
Claim is not just that a special baby is born at Christmas. The claim is that this baby realises a hope that many have been waiting for.聖誕節所宣告的，不單是一個特別的嬰孩誕生了；而是這個嬰孩實現了許多人一直等候的盼望。
Jesus’ coming is going to fix many things. He’s the answer to many questions and problems.耶穌的降臨要修補許多事情。祂是許多疑問與難題的答案。
So a helpful way to look at what Christmas means is to see what the people were waiting for. Only then can we truly get what Christmas is about.所以，要明白聖誕節的意義，一個有幫助的方法，就是看看當時的人在等候甚麼。惟有這樣，我們才能真正明白聖誕節的意義。
Never want to start a movie half-way through. Finding Nemo. All these fish going on a journey…but if you know the beginning you know it’s a rescue mission. To find a fish separated from his father.我們從不想從一齣電影的中段開始看。就像《海底奇兵》：一群魚踏上旅程……但如果你看過開頭，就知道這是一次拯救行動——要找回一條與父親失散的小魚。
So we’re going to use the Minor Prophets over the next month or so to see what people were waiting for and hoping for ahead of Christmas.因此，在接下來一個月左右，我們會藉着小先知書，看看在聖誕節之前，人們在等候甚麼、盼望甚麼。
The Minor Prophets are the group of 12 books from Hosea to Malachi. The word Minor is nothing to do with their content. Minor is about their length. They are much shorter than prophecies like Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel. So much shorter actually that traditionally all 12 books could fit on one ancient scroll. And so they were often grouped together.小先知書是指由何西阿書至瑪拉基書這十二卷書。「小」字與內容無關，而是指篇幅。它們比以賽亞書、耶利米書、以西結書等先知書短得多；事實上短到傳統上這十二卷書可以寫在同一卷古代書卷上，所以常常被歸在一起。
Not only for that reason.但原因不止於此。
They share many of the same themes. What is God up to in his world? How do the nations of the world fit into what God is doing? What does it mean to live well for God? Many reflect on the truth that God is a gracious God. That judgment is not usually the last word.它們有許多共同的主題：神在祂的世界裏正在做甚麼？世上的列國在神的作為中處於甚麼位置？為神好好活着是甚麼意思？許多篇章都反映一個真理：神是滿有恩典的神，審判通常不是最後的定論。
2 things for us.這對我們有兩點意義。
The Minor Prophets, and the OT at large, looks forward to the coming of Jesus. We live obviously on the other side of Jesus coming. But we’re also still waiting.小先知書，以至整本舊約，都期待耶穌的來臨。我們當然活在耶穌來臨之後；但我們同時仍在等候。
Advent isn’t just about Jesus’ first coming; it also points to his second. We wait with the church around the world for Jesus to return. And so much is still left to realise. Our hopes have not all yet been fulfilled. Far from it. As we’ll see.將臨期不單關乎耶穌的第一次來臨，也指向祂的第二次來臨。我們與世界各地的教會一同等候耶穌再來。還有許多事情尚待實現；我們的盼望並未全然應驗——遠遠未到。我們稍後會看到這一點。
Advent reminds us today that we wait with hope.將臨期今天提醒我們：我們是懷着盼望等候。

1. A diagnosis (9:5-10)一、診斷（9:5-10）
To wait for a better future means the present has a problem.等候更美好的將來，意味着現在出了問題。
Amos itself shows that very clearly.阿摩司書本身就非常清楚地顯明這一點。
Amos is a prophet to the Northern Kingdom of Israel in the middle of the 8th Century BC. The kingdom had split after Solomon in two: Israel in North; Judah in the South.阿摩司是主前八世紀中葉向北國以色列說話的先知。所羅門之後，王國分裂為二：北邊是以色列，南邊是猶大。
Amos comes to a people who think all is well. They’re God’s chosen people after all. They know about this Day of the Lord that’s spoken of in the future: a day when God wraps up human history and will judge all the wicked and bring those who love him to himself. And Israel thinks they’re going to get a hero’s welcome from God. But Amos comes to burst that bubble.阿摩司來到一群自以為一切安好的人中間。畢竟他們是神的選民。他們知道將來要來的「耶和華的日子」：那日神要為人類歷史作結，審判一切惡人，並把愛祂的人接到自己那裏。以色列以為自己會得到神英雄式的歡迎。但阿摩司來，就是要戳破這個幻想。
5:18: you think that will be a great day for you. It’s going to be a disaster. God will judge yes – and you’re included.五章 18 節：你們以為那日對你們是大好日子，其實那將是一場災難。神的確要審判——而你們也在其中。
“Woe to you who long for the day of the LORD! Why do you long for the day of the LORD? That day will be darkness, not light.” (Amos 5:18)「想望耶和華日子來到的有禍了！你們為何想望耶和華的日子呢？那日黑暗沒有光明。」（阿摩司書 5:18）
Biggest shock comes near the beginning of the book: Amos lists the nations around them God is going to bring judgment on because of their evil – and at that end of that list is included – shock horror – Israel herself!最大的震撼出現在書卷的開頭：阿摩司列出周圍因作惡而要受神審判的列國——而名單的最後，竟然——令人震驚——包括了以色列自己！
Why? It’s hard to know where to start: it’s not that they’re not religious. Religious centres always packed with people. But it’s empty worship. Day to day the strong trample over the weak; it’s a do what feels right culture – no regard for God. They’re not even worshiping the right god in many places! They’re rotten from top to bottom.為甚麼？真不知從何說起。並不是他們不虔誠——宗教場所總是擠滿了人；但那是空洞的敬拜。日常生活中，強者踐踏弱者；那是一種「自己覺得對就去做」的文化，全不把神放在眼裏。在許多地方，他們甚至沒有敬拜那位真神！他們從上到下都腐敗了。
In fact Amos labels them in our passage “a sinful kingdom” (v.8). Ouch.事實上，阿摩司在今天的經文中稱他們為「有罪的國」（8 節）。何等刺耳！
“Surely the eyes of the Sovereign LORD are on the sinful kingdom. I will destroy it from the face of the earth.” (v.8a)「主耶和華的眼目察看這有罪的國，必將這國從地上滅絕。」（8 節上）
Remember these are God’s chosen people.要記得，這些人是神的選民。
Christmas wouldn’t be necessary if everything down here were great. Even God’s chosen people are rotten and what does that say for the world at large?如果世上一切都美好，聖誕節就不必要了。連神的選民都腐敗了，那麼整個世界又如何呢？
There is a term in biology for an animal whose behaviour reveals whether its wider ecosystem is good or bad. It’s called an indicator species.生物學上有一個名詞，指某種動物的狀況能反映牠所處的整個生態系統是好是壞，稱為「指標物種」。
Salmon in rivers. Salmon need cold, clean and connected rivers to breed well. If the salmon population starts struggling and dying out, that indicates a wider problem in their ecosystem: maybe the river has a blockage somewhere: the water has become too warm or too polluted.例如河裏的三文魚。三文魚需要寒冷、潔淨而暢通的河流才能好好繁殖。如果三文魚的數目開始減少、甚至絕跡，就表示牠們的生態系統出現了更大的問題：也許河道某處被堵塞了，或者水溫太高，或者污染太嚴重。
Israel in Amos are an indicator species. This darkness they are in is indicative of the darkness the world is in. If even God’s people, those who go way back with God, are rife with evil and injustice and immorality then what does that say about the rest of the world?阿摩司書中的以色列就是一個指標物種。他們所處的黑暗，反映出整個世界所處的黑暗。如果連神的子民——那些與神有悠久關係的人——都充滿邪惡、不公與不道德，那麼世界其餘的地方又如何呢？
Felt it yourself? That’s all of us. We’re all like Louis XVI’s son. Louis XVI was the King of France who was executed in the French Revolution. His son was the rightful ruler of France but he lived a life in “filth and rags”. He no longer looked anything like a king. That’s true for all of us says the Bible. Made in God’s image to rule his world but now we just live mired in sin.你自己也感受到嗎？我們每一個人都是如此。我們都像路易十六的兒子。路易十六是在法國大革命中被處決的法國國王。他的兒子本是法國合法的君主，卻活在「污穢與襤褸」之中，再沒有半點君王的樣子。聖經說，我們每一個人都是這樣：按着神的形像被造，本要治理祂的世界，如今卻深陷在罪中。
A rebellion against God takes place in each of our hearts. And is seen worldwide.背叛神的事在我們每個人的心裏發生，也在全世界顯明出來。
Amos reminds us (v.5): this is God’s world and he holds absolute power and he’s totally against evil and wickedness. The Bible says one day he will do away with all evil and wickedness. We know that because in history he has acted and judged. The people Amos has come to will be taken away into exile by their neighbour Assyria. God is behind that. He’s saying I can’t tolerate evil and wickedness.阿摩司提醒我們（5 節）：這是神的世界，祂掌握絕對的權能，並且完全與邪惡和罪惡為敵。聖經說，總有一天祂要除去一切邪惡和罪惡。我們知道這一點，因為祂在歷史中曾經出手施行審判。阿摩司所面對的這群人，將被他們的鄰國亞述擄去。這背後是神的作為。祂是在說：我不能容忍邪惡與罪惡。
“The Lord, the LORD Almighty — he touches the earth and it melts, and all who live in it mourn… the LORD is his name.” (vv.5-6)「主─萬軍之耶和華摸地，地就消化，凡住在地上的都必悲哀……耶和華是他的名。」（5-6 節）
But if that were the complete end then the story would just be a tragedy.但如果這就是全部的結局，這故事就只是一場悲劇。
But…that’s not the end. Not even for Israel. Exile is not the end.然而……這並不是結局。即使對以色列也不是。被擄並不是終點。
“Yet I will not totally destroy the descendants of Jacob,” declares the LORD. (v.8b)「卻不將雅各家滅絕淨盡。這是耶和華說的。」（8 節下）
Christmas is a hope given for those in darkness. For those cut off from God. Though we walk in darkness, there is light. There is hope.聖誕節是賜給在黑暗中之人的盼望，賜給與神隔絕的人。雖然我們行在黑暗中，卻有光，有盼望。
We all need Christmas. Israel needed it. We need it. If you need convincing of that this morning – look at the news. Ask yourself the question, “have you only ever done what is good and upright and just?” Have you worshipped your Maker and given him thanks for your life?我們都需要聖誕節。以色列需要，我們也需要。如果今早你還需要被說服，就看看新聞吧。問問自己：「我是否一直只行良善、正直、公義的事？」你有否敬拜造你的主，並為你的生命向祂獻上感謝？
Amos brings a diagnosis. But then, as we’ve hinted at, gives them something worth waiting for…阿摩司帶來了診斷。但正如我們已經暗示的，他接着給了他們一件值得等候的事……

2. A restoration (9:11-12)二、復興（9:11-12）
There always comes a point when we ask for help from outside. I can’t find where I’m going – I’ll ask for directions. I can’t get the answer – I’ll ask the teacher for help. My life is out of my control – I’m going to seek counselling.我們總有需要向外求助的時候。找不到路，就問路；想不出答案，就請老師幫忙；生活失控了，就去尋求輔導。
Christmas is a hope that comes not from us but from outside us.聖誕節的盼望不是出於我們自己，而是從我們以外而來。
Christmas is about God coming down to sort out our mess.聖誕節就是神親自降臨，收拾我們的爛攤子。
God speaks here about what he will personally do…神在這裏說到祂要親自做的事……
Which is why we need to pay particular attention to who’s doing the action of vv.11-12.所以我們要特別留意，11 至 12 節中行事的是誰。
“In that day I will restore David’s fallen shelter — I will repair its broken walls and restore its ruins — and will rebuild it as it used to be, so that they may possess the remnant of Edom and all the nations that bear my name,” declares the LORD, who will do these things. (vv.11-12)「到那日，我必建立大衛倒塌的帳幕，堵住其中的破口，把那破壞的建立起來，重新修造，像古時一樣，使以色列人得以東所餘剩的和所有稱為我名下的國。此乃行這事的耶和華說的。」（11-12 節）
Christmas is God providing hope where we ourselves could do nothing.聖誕節是神在我們自己無能為力之處，賜下盼望。
And that hope is all about a person coming. Christmas celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ.而這盼望完全在乎一位要來的人物。聖誕節所慶祝的，是耶穌基督的降生。
God’s promise of hope comes via a person.神所應許的盼望，是藉着一個人而來的。
Amos 9 tells us why.阿摩司書第九章告訴我們原因。
Restoration’s first step comes when God restores David’s fallen shelter (v.11).復興的第一步，是神重建大衛倒塌的帳幕（11 節）。
Amos is referring to the dynasty of David: Israel’s most famous King. Most celebrated and beloved king. A King who brought rest to the land and for the people.阿摩司所指的是大衛王朝。大衛是以色列最著名、最受頌揚、最受愛戴的君王，是一位為這片土地和百姓帶來安息的君王。
Who rules and leads you matters. I think we get that today – who the leaders of a country are: who’s making policy, who’s modelling how to live; who’s got power and can draw the country into wars – that makes a huge difference for the people living in that country.由誰來統治和帶領你，是至關重要的。我想今天我們都明白這一點——一個國家的領袖是誰：誰在制定政策、誰在示範怎樣生活、誰手握大權、能把國家拖入戰爭——這對住在那國家的人民影響極大。
The Kings in Israel during Amos’ time are truly terrible. Leading people astray. Leading them to worship what is not God. Leading them into disaster.阿摩司時代以色列的君王真是糟透了：他們使百姓走迷，帶領他們敬拜那不是神的偶像，把他們領進災禍之中。
The language here of fallen shelter, broken walls: Amos is saying this line of kings have fallen so below par that it’s hard to even call them a house.這裏所用「倒塌的帳幕」、「破口」等字眼，是阿摩司在說：這一脈君王已經敗落到一個地步，連稱之為一個「家」都難。
What is needed is a King who will lead his people back into right worship, right living, into a glorious new kingdom.所需要的，是一位君王，能帶領祂的子民回到正確的敬拜、正直的生活，進入一個榮耀的新國度。
That is why the Christmas hope is centred on a person.這就是為甚麼聖誕節的盼望以一個人為中心。
Jesus! What is said about him later in the NT:就是耶穌！新約後來這樣論到祂：
“You will conceive and give birth to a son, and you are to call him Jesus. He will be great and will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his father David, and he will reign over Jacob’s descendants forever; his kingdom will never end.” (Luke 1:31-33)「你要懷孕生子，可以給他起名叫耶穌。他要為大，稱為至高者的兒子；主神要把他祖大衛的位給他。他要作雅各家的王，直到永遠；他的國也沒有窮盡。」（路加福音 1:31-33）
Here’s Amos: we need someone in David’s line. That descendant is coming at Christmas. And what’s the impact?阿摩司說：我們需要一位大衛的後裔。這位後裔就在聖誕節來臨。祂帶來甚麼影響呢？
He won’t lead people astray: he will deal with the problem we talked about earlier.祂不會使人走迷；祂要處理我們剛才談到的那個問題。
“For my eyes have seen your salvation, which you have prepared in the sight of all nations: a light for revelation to the Gentiles, and the glory of your people Israel.” (Luke 2:30-32)「因為我的眼睛已經看見你的救恩， 就是你在萬民面前所預備的： 是照亮外邦人的光， 又是你民以色列的榮耀。」（路加福音 2:30-32）
The one born at Christmas brings salvation. He brings light into darkness. He saves people from judgment. He restores them to God.在聖誕節降生的那一位帶來救恩。祂把光帶進黑暗；祂拯救人脫離審判；祂使人與神復和。
Who’s the them? Not just Israel – but Gentiles too – non-Jews: Chinese; Australians; Inuits. Israel is an indicator species: what’s true of them is true of the whole world. God needs to restore his fallen world.「人」是指誰？不單是以色列，也包括外邦人——非猶太人：中國人、澳洲人、因紐特人。以色列是一個指標物種：在他們身上是真實的，在全世界也是真實的。神要復興祂墮落的世界。
Exactly what we see in Amos.這正是我們在阿摩司書中所看見的。
What happens when this new king comes – his kingdom expands beyond Israel.當這位新君王來臨時，會發生甚麼事？祂的國度擴展到以色列以外。
Edom (v.12) were a people group descended from Esau. And they were often a huge thorn in Israel’s side. They stand for God’s enemies.以東（12 節）是以掃的後裔，常常是以色列的眼中釘、肉中刺。他們代表神的仇敵。
Also, Edom in the Hebrew sounds very much like Adam – representing humanity at large. This King brings back into the fold a lost world who previously hated God.此外，「以東」在希伯來文中的發音與「亞當」非常相近——代表整個人類。這位君王把一個原本恨惡神、失喪的世界帶回羊圈之中。
“Possess” is not language of military conquest unlike some other religions. After all look at what happens through this possessing: the nations are given citizenship. They don’t bear the mark of their conquerors but God himself. Jesus himself said his kingdom is not of this world. If it were then his servants would fight. There’s no fighting. As many see it this refers to the mission of the church.「得」這個字，不像某些其他宗教那樣是軍事征服的語言。畢竟，看看這「得」帶來甚麼結果：列國得着了公民的身分。他們所帶的不是征服者的記號，而是神自己的名。耶穌親自說過，祂的國不屬這世界；若屬這世界，祂的臣僕必要爭戰。但這裏沒有爭戰。許多人認為，這是指教會的使命。
This possessing happens as the name of King Jesus is declared to the world as their only hope for salvation.這「得」的過程，就是君王耶穌的名向世界宣揚，作為人得救唯一的盼望。
No accident we see Amos 9 repeated in Acts Ch.15: a book about the expansion of Jesus’ kingdom throughout all the world.我們在使徒行傳第十五章再次看見阿摩司書第九章的話，這絕非偶然——使徒行傳正是一卷記述耶穌的國度擴展到全世界的書。
“After this I will return and rebuild David’s fallen tent. Its ruins I will rebuild, and I will restore it, that the rest of mankind may seek the Lord, even all the Gentiles who bear my name.” (Acts 15:16-17)「此後，我要回來，重新修造大衛倒塌的帳幕，把那破壞的重新修造建立起來，叫餘剩的人，就是凡稱為我名下的外邦人，都尋求主。」（使徒行傳 15:16-17）
Christmas is the beginning of the world at large going from enemies of God; from being a “sinful world” to now bearing God’s name. And it all starts with this King Jesus being born.聖誕節是整個世界從神的仇敵、從「有罪的世界」，轉變為歸在神名下的開始。而這一切，都從君王耶穌的降生開始。
Hostility is ended because everyone who hears the gospel of King Jesus, no matter what tribe they’re from or tongue they speak, are themselves given peace with God.敵意得以終止，因為每一個聽見君王耶穌福音的人，無論來自哪一個部族、說哪一種語言，都得以與神和好。
Have you yourself heard and responded to King Jesus?你自己有沒有聽見君王耶穌，並且回應祂？

3. A replenishment (9:13-15)三、豐盛（9:13-15）
Go Fund Me campaign. Money raised after a video emerged online of a bus monitor in US being bullied. Money was raised to pay for her to be able to go on holiday. Boy did she get more than that.曾經有一個 GoFundMe 籌款活動。網上流傳一段影片，一位美國校巴看管員被欺凌，於是有人發起籌款，讓她可以去度假。結果她所得的遠遠不止於此。
In the end the campaign raised more than half a million US dollars. Paid for a holiday and the money went to a number of charities that help those facing bullying.最終，這次籌款籌得超過五十萬美元。除了支付她的假期，餘款還捐給多間幫助受欺凌者的慈善機構。
Amos ends not just with restoration. But replenishment. Fullness that seems to go way beyond what might have been hoped for originally.阿摩司書的結尾不單是復興，更是豐盛——那份豐滿似乎遠遠超過人原本所能盼望的。
“The days are coming,” declares the LORD, “when the reaper will be overtaken by the plowman and the planter by the one treading grapes. New wine will drip from the mountains and flow from all the hills, and I will bring my people Israel back from exile… I will plant Israel in their own land, never again to be uprooted from the land I have given them,” says the LORD your God. (vv.13-15)「耶和華說：日子將到，耕種的必接續收割的；踹葡萄的必接續撒種的；大山要滴下甜酒；小山都必流奶。我必使我民以色列被擄的歸回……我要將他們栽於本地，他們不再從我所賜給他們的地上拔出來。這是耶和華─你的神說的。」（13-15 節）
God doesn’t just promise a fresh start. That would still be enormously gracious. But Amos 9 shows a promise that goes way beyond: it’s a return to paradise. Peace, bounty, fullness = Eden 2.0. A land as overflowing with goodness as God himself.神所應許的不單是一個新的開始——單是這樣已是莫大的恩典。但阿摩司書第九章所展示的應許遠超於此：那是重返樂園。平安、豐收、滿足，就是一個新的伊甸園；一片如神自己一樣滿溢良善的土地。
This land is not one place in the world. It is the world. A world restored.這片土地不是世上的某一處地方，而是整個世界——一個得着復興的世界。
Now you might think hang on – Jesus came 2,000 years ago. His message and the church he built has existed for 2,000 years. But this year alone, 2026, has seen wars. Millions upon millions in poverty. Christians are still ill, struggling. We don’t have overflowing wine.你或許會想：且慢——耶穌在二千年前已經來了，祂的信息和祂所建立的教會已存在了二千年。但單是今年，2026 年，世上就有戰爭；千千萬萬的人活在貧窮之中；基督徒仍然患病、仍然掙扎。我們並沒有滿溢的新酒。
Where’s this replenishment?這豐盛在哪裏呢？
It goes back to what I said at the start about Advent. Advent is about someone “coming”. And the Bible says these promises we see in Amos will happen but not all at the same time.這要回到我在開始時所說關於將臨期的話。將臨期是關乎某一位的「來臨」。聖經說，我們在阿摩司書所看見的這些應許必定會實現，但不是同時全部實現。
This promise of replenishment for the world at large will happen when Jesus comes…again. And we still wait for that. With solid hope.這個使全世界得着豐盛的應許，要在耶穌……再來的時候實現。我們仍在等候那日——懷着穩固的盼望。
Why solid hope?為甚麼說是穩固的盼望？
A solid hope because it’s based on the promises of God himself. We can’t fix our future. Elon Musk can’t. Silicon Valley can’t. Only God gives a solid hope of better days.說它穩固，因為它建基於神自己的應許。我們無法修補自己的將來；馬斯克不能，矽谷也不能。惟有神能賜下對美好日子的穩固盼望。
It’s also solid for another reason. The first two of Amos’ promises have happened, and are happening!它穩固還有另一個原因：阿摩司的頭兩個應許已經實現，而且正在實現！
God himself has come in Jesus. He has begun and continues to build his church around the world.神自己已在耶穌裏降臨；祂已開始、並且繼續在世界各地建立祂的教會。
And so we can be absolutely confident the next stage will come. It’s in the diary. We don’t know when. But we can wait with hope.所以，我們可以完全確信下一個階段必定來臨。這已寫在日程上；我們不知道是何時，但我們可以懷着盼望等候。
One of the reasons I left Facebook many years ago was because after a while the only photos people would post would be photos of them having an absolute blast on holiday while I was stuck somewhere writing exams.我多年前離開 Facebook，其中一個原因是：過了一段時間，大家貼出來的就只有他們度假玩得不亦樂乎的照片，而我卻困在某處應付考試。
Friends, don’t though make that same mistake with this. If you’re not a Christian believer, these promises can be yours too. This good news is not just for others. You don’t just have to see them from an outsider’s point of view. It’s for you too. Amos reminds us that Christmas calls for us to see our true condition before God; but to see King Jesus sent so we could be restored back to God and one day be with him in paradise.朋友們，不要在這件事上犯同樣的錯誤。如果你還不是基督徒，這些應許也可以屬於你。這好消息不單是為別人預備的；你不必只以局外人的角度觀看，它也是為你預備的。阿摩司提醒我們，聖誕節呼召我們看清自己在神面前的真實光景；但也看見君王耶穌被差來，使我們得以與神復和，有一天與祂同在樂園裏。
A word about hope in suffering…關於苦難中的盼望……
The story of 2 men captured and imprisoned in a dungeon. Before each is put in jail, one discovers his wife and child are dead; the other discovers his wife and child are alive and waiting for him. In the first couple of years the first man wasted away and gave up. But the second man endured, stayed strong and walked out a free man in the end. Both men are in the same scenario but they respond differently based on their different futures. The future determined how they handled the present.有一個故事，講述兩個人被捕並囚禁在地牢裏。入獄之前，其中一人得知妻兒已經死去；另一人得知妻兒仍然活着，正等候他回去。最初一兩年，第一個人日漸消瘦，終於放棄了；但第二個人堅忍到底，保持堅強，最後以自由之身走出牢獄。兩人處境相同，卻因為各自的將來不同而有不同的反應。將來決定了他們如何面對現在。
Our future, says Amos, almost unbelievably is a restored and replenished world. The life we always wanted but have never had. All thanks to King Jesus. God says to us – have hope while you wait.阿摩司說，我們的將來——幾乎令人難以置信——是一個得着復興、充滿豐盛的世界；是我們一直渴望卻從未擁有過的生命。這一切都因着君王耶穌。神對我們說：在等候的時候，要存着盼望。

Shaken and Restored

震動與復興

Amos 9:5-15 阿摩司書 9:5-15 · Bible Study Guide 聖經學習指南
“In that day I will restore David’s fallen shelter — I will repair its broken walls and restore its ruins — and will rebuild it as it used to be.” (Amos 9:11)
「到那日，我必建立大衛倒塌的帳幕，堵住其中的破口，把那破壞的建立起來，重新修造，像古時一樣。」（阿摩司書 9:11）

📖Summary 摘要

English
Advent: waiting with hope. Christmas is not only the birth of a special baby; it is the fulfilment of a long-awaited hope. To understand it, we look at what God’s people were waiting for — and we too still wait, for Jesus’ second coming.
A diagnosis (vv.5-10). Israel assumed the Day of the Lord would be good news for them, but Amos calls them “a sinful kingdom.” Like an indicator species, their rottenness reveals the darkness of the whole world — and of every human heart. God is sovereign and will judge evil, yet he will “not totally destroy the descendants of Jacob.”
A restoration (vv.11-12). Hope comes from outside us: God himself will rebuild “David’s fallen shelter.” That promise is fulfilled in Jesus, the Son of David, whose kingdom reaches beyond Israel to Edom and all nations — not by military conquest but by the gospel (Acts 15).
A replenishment (vv.13-15). God promises more than a fresh start: a return to paradise, a whole world overflowing with goodness. This final stage awaits Jesus’ return, but it is a solid hope, because the first promises have already come true. Our future shapes how we live in the present.
中文
將臨期：懷着盼望等候。聖誕節不單是一個特別嬰孩的誕生，更是人們久候之盼望的實現。要明白它，就要看看神的子民在等候甚麼——而我們今天也仍在等候耶穌第二次來臨。
診斷（5-10 節）。以色列以為耶和華的日子對他們是好消息，阿摩司卻稱他們為「有罪的國」。他們好像一個指標物種，他們的腐敗顯明了整個世界——以及每個人內心——的黑暗。神掌權，必審判罪惡；然而祂「卻不將雅各家滅絕淨盡」。
復興（11-12 節）。盼望是從我們以外而來的：神要親自建立「大衛倒塌的帳幕」。這應許在大衛的子孫耶穌身上應驗；祂的國度越過以色列，伸展到以東和萬國——不是藉軍事征服，而是藉福音（使徒行傳十五章）。
豐盛（13-15 節）。神所應許的不止是一個新開始，而是重返樂園，一個滿溢良善的世界。這最後階段要等到耶穌再來才實現，但這是穩固的盼望，因為先前的應許已經應驗。我們的將來，塑造我們今天怎樣生活。

💡Key Themes 主要主題

English
1. The honest diagnosis. Religious activity does not equal right standing with God. If even God’s chosen people were “a sinful kingdom,” the whole world — and each of us — needs rescue. Christmas would be unnecessary if all were well.
2. Judgment is real, but not the last word. The sovereign Lord who “touches the earth and it melts” will not tolerate evil, yet he promises not to destroy Jacob completely. His grace outlasts his judgment.
3. Hope comes through a King. God personally restores David’s fallen house by sending Jesus, the Son of David, whose reign never ends. Who leads us matters — and he leads us back to true worship and right living.
4. A kingdom for all nations. Edom — Israel’s enemy and, by sound, a picture of “Adam” — is brought in. The kingdom grows not by the sword but by the gospel, as former enemies receive God’s name and peace with him.
5. Already and not yet. Restoration has begun in Jesus’ first coming and the growth of the church; full replenishment — Eden restored — awaits his return. The promises fulfilled so far make our hope solid.
中文
一、誠實的診斷。宗教活動不等於在神面前站立得穩。如果連神的選民都是「有罪的國」，整個世界——以及我們每一個人——都需要拯救。若一切安好，聖誕節就不必要了。
二、審判是真實的，卻不是最後的定論。那位「摸地，地就消化」的掌權者不能容忍罪惡，卻應許不將雅各家滅絕淨盡。祂的恩典比祂的審判更長久。
三、盼望藉一位君王而來。神差遣大衛的子孫耶穌，親自重建大衛倒塌的家；祂的國沒有窮盡。誰帶領我們至關重要——祂帶領我們回到真正的敬拜和正直的生活。
四、屬於萬國的國度。以東——以色列的仇敵，其發音又令人聯想到「亞當」——被納入神的國。這國度不是靠刀劍擴展，而是靠福音；昔日的仇敵得以歸在神的名下，與神和好。
五、已然與未然。復興已在耶穌第一次降臨和教會的增長中開始；完全的豐盛——伊甸的重現——則要等祂再來。已經應驗的應許，使我們的盼望穩固。

❓Thinking Questions 思考問題

Read Amos 5:18 and 9:7-10. Why did Israel expect the Day of the Lord to be good news for them, and what does Amos say instead? (Observation)
細讀阿摩司書 5:18 和 9:7-10。為甚麼以色列以為耶和華的日子對他們是好消息？阿摩司卻怎樣說？（觀察）
Israel was full of religious activity yet called “a sinful kingdom” (v.8). What made their worship empty? Where might the same pattern show up in church life today? (Interpretation)
以色列充滿宗教活動，卻被稱為「有罪的國」（8 節）。是甚麼使他們的敬拜變得空洞？今天的教會生活中，可能在哪些地方出現同樣的模式？（解釋）
The sermon called Israel an “indicator species.” What does their condition reveal about the world — and about your own heart? Look at the question asked in the sermon: have you only ever done what is good, upright and just? (Reflection)
講道把以色列比作「指標物種」。他們的光景揭示了這世界——以及你自己的內心——怎樣的狀況？試回應講道中的問題：你是否一直只行良善、正直、公義的事？（反思）
In vv.11-12, notice how often God says “I will.” Why does it matter that restoration comes from outside us? How do Luke 1:31-33 and Luke 2:30-32 show Jesus fulfilling this promise? (Interpretation)
留意 11-12 節中神多少次說「我必」。復興是從我們以外而來，這為何重要？路加福音 1:31-33 和 2:30-32 怎樣顯示耶穌應驗了這個應許？（解釋）
Edom, Israel’s enemy, is brought into God’s kingdom — not by conquest but by the gospel (Acts 15:16-17). Who might you consider “outside” or even hostile to God? How does this passage change the way you see them? (Reflection)
以色列的仇敵以東被帶進神的國——不是靠征服，而是靠福音（使徒行傳 15:16-17）。在你眼中，誰是神國度以外、甚至敵對神的人？這段經文怎樣改變你對他們的看法？（反思）
With wars, poverty and illness still around us, where is the “replenishment” of vv.13-15? How does knowing that the first promises have already come true make our hope “solid”? (Interpretation and reflection)
世上仍有戰爭、貧窮和疾病，13-15 節所說的「豐盛」在哪裏？知道頭兩個應許已經應驗，怎樣使我們的盼望變得「穩固」？（解釋與反思）
Think of the two prisoners: their future shaped how they endured the present. What difficulty are you facing now, and how would living in the light of a restored world change the way you face it this Advent? (Application)
想想那兩個囚犯：他們的將來決定了他們怎樣忍受現在。你現在正面對甚麼困難？在這個將臨期，若你活在「世界必得復興」的光中，你面對困難的方式會有甚麼改變？（應用）

🌱Application 生活應用

Take an honest look. This week, set aside time to examine your heart before God. Name specific ways you have gone your own way, and bring them to him in confession rather than assuming all is well.
誠實地省察。這星期撥出時間在神面前省察自己的內心。具體說出自己在哪些事上偏行己路，向神認罪，而不是假定一切安好。
Bow to the true King. Who or what is really leading your life — career, opinion, comfort, technology? Deliberately hand that leadership back to King Jesus, the Son of David.
降服於真正的君王。到底是誰或甚麼在真正帶領你的生命——事業、輿論、安逸、科技？刻意把這帶領權交還給大衛的子孫——君王耶穌。
Share the good news this Advent. Christmas is God’s invitation to the nations. Pray for one friend who does not yet know Jesus, and invite them to a carol service or a conversation about what Christmas really means.
在將臨期分享好消息。聖誕節是神向萬國發出的邀請。為一位還未認識耶穌的朋友禱告，並邀請他參加報佳音崇拜，或與他談談聖誕節的真正意義。
Wait with hope in suffering. If you are in a hard season, write down one promise from Amos 9:13-15 or Revelation 21 and keep it where you will see it daily. Let your future shape your present.
在苦難中懷着盼望等候。如果你正經歷艱難的日子，抄下阿摩司書 9:13-15 或啟示錄二十一章的一個應許，放在每天都看得見的地方。讓你的將來塑造你的現在。
