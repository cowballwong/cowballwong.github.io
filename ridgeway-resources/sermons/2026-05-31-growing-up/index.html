Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
Growing Up 成長
(Hebrews 5:11-6:3 希伯來書 5 章 11 節至 6 章 3 節)

Intro 引言：Growing Up — Hebrews 5:11-6:3引言：成長——希伯來書 5 章 11 節至 6 章 3 節
………………………………………………
When I was younger — and I feel ready now to admit this to the world — I ate baby porridge every breakfast. Really simple: I added hot water to it, and I thought it was really tasty. I was probably still eating it into my early teenage years.我年少時——今日我終於願意向全世界承認——每天早餐都吃嬰兒米糊。做法非常簡單：加點熱水沖開，我就覺得很好吃了。這個習慣大概一直保持到我十多歲的少年期。
Of course, my family gave me grief. And if any of my friends were staying over, I would eat something else. A young teenager eating baby food?!當然，家人為此沒少取笑我。若有朋友來家裏過夜，我就會改吃別的。一個少年人竟然吃嬰兒食物？！
There was another issue too: as your body grows and develops it needs more nutritious food. Staying on baby food will not give your body what it really needs.另外還有一個問題：身體在成長發育時，需要更有營養的食物。一直停留在嬰兒食物上，根本無法滿足身體真正的需要。
………………………………………………
In our passage this morning the writer to the Hebrews presses pause. This whole section up to Chapter 10 is about Jesus as our High Priest. The writer has been on that theme since the end of Chapter 4, and he wants to go deeper and plumb the depths even more so we can see more of Jesus in ever greater ways. But he pauses. Why?今早這段經文中，希伯來書的作者按下了暫停鍵。由這裏直到第十章，整段都在講「耶穌——我們的大祭司」這個主題。作者自第四章末便開始論述這個題目，他本想繼續深入，把這個真理挖得更深，好讓我們從更宏大的角度認識耶穌。但他停下來了。為甚麼？
He wants to give them deep, rich, nutritious food. But their diet is still basically just milk. He wants to feed them spiritual wagyu beef… but he is not convinced they are ready for it.他想餵他們吃豐富有營養的食物，但他們的飲食仍然只是「奶」。他想餵他們屬靈的「和牛」上等牛排……卻擔心他們未到能消化的階段。
They need to mature.他們需要長大成熟。
And that theme of maturity and growing up is the big idea of this passage.「成熟」與「成長」這個主題，正是這段經文的「中心思想」。
………………………………………………
Up to now the Christian life has been pictured as a journey (Chapters 3-4) — the blueprint has been that journey the wilderness generation made from Egypt to the promised land. And the point of this picture is to say: keep going!在此之前，基督徒人生的圖畫是一段「旅程」（第三、四章）——藍本就是出埃及那一代從埃及到應許之地的曠野旅程。這幅圖畫的重點是說：「不要停下，繼續向前走！」
Now the image changes. The Christian life is like growing up. It is like maturing from spiritual babies to spiritual adults.現在圖畫變了。基督徒人生像是「成長」——是由屬靈的嬰孩，漸漸長成屬靈的成年人。
And to do that we need a nutritious diet that keeps us developing in healthy ways…要做到這一點，我們需要有營養的飲食，使我們健康地不斷成長……
That diet comes first and foremost through what we hear and understand about our great and gracious God. We mature the more we know God through the words of God.這份飲食「首要而最重要」的，就是透過我們對這位偉大而滿有恩典的神所聆聽和明白的真理。我們透過神的話更深認識神，就會更加成熟。
………………………………………………
Are we maturing and growing up? Are we going deeper with our God? Do we know him more now compared to last week? Last month? Last year?我們有否在成長？有否與神更深相交？比起上週、上個月、上一年，我們對祂的認識有否加深？
Good questions for us to ask.這些問題值得我們捫心自問。
The writer in our passage will uncover their immaturity — but he will not give up on them. He will urge them onwards. It is a rebuke meant to spur them on to greater maturity.這段經文中，作者揭示他們的不成熟——但他並沒有放棄他們。相反，他催促他們繼續前行。這是一個「為要激勵他們走向更成熟」的責備。
………………………………………………
The diagnosis: spiritual immaturity (5:11-14)診斷：屬靈的不成熟（5 章 11 至 14 節）
………………………………………………
Important: If we think the good news of Jesus is just about receiving a ticket to heaven and that is it, then what Hebrews is saying here will not really make sense to us. If we are just waiting for heaven, why bother developing and maturing?一個重要的前提：若我們以為耶穌的福音只不過是領取一張上天堂的入場券，便了事，那麼希伯來書這裏所說的，對我們而言便沒有甚麼意義。若我們只是在等候上天堂，何必去發展和成熟？
But here is the thing: Jesus did not die and rise just to save us. He also wants to transform us. He wants us to do — and enables us to do — what we were originally designed to do as human beings, and which we are unable to do before we received his life-transforming grace.但事實是：耶穌的死與復活，不只是要拯救我們。祂還要「改變我們」。祂要我們去做——而且使我們有能力去做——我們原本作為人受造時所被設計去做的事；那是我們未領受祂改變生命的恩典之前，根本做不到的。
That is: to know God more and live more for him.那就是：「更多認識神」、並且「更多為祂而活」。
………………………………………………
In the words of maybe the most famous Q&A from the most famous catechism:正如歷史上最著名的要理問答中，最有名的一條問答所言：
"What is our chief end? To glorify God and enjoy him forever."「人的首要目的是甚麼？人首要的目的是榮耀神，並永遠以祂為樂。」
And that starts from day one when Jesus saves us. That is why he saved us. And that is what our life is about.這從耶穌拯救我們的「第一天」便開始。這就是祂拯救我們的原因。這也就是我們人生的意義所在。
And so that is why, for the writer to the Hebrews, the expectation is for us to mature and develop and grow. To go deeper with God.因此，在希伯來書作者眼中，他對我們的期望是要成熟、要發展、要成長，要與神更深相交。
And that explains what he is doing in his letter. He is showing them that in every way Jesus is better. Who he is, his authority, his coming as both God and man, his high priestly work — Jesus is better… and the deeper we explore that, the more we mature.這也解釋了他寫這封書信的用心。他向他們顯明，無論從哪一個角度看，耶穌都是「更美」的。祂的身份、祂的權柄、祂作為神同時也是人的降臨、祂大祭司的職事——耶穌都是更美的……我們愈深入探討這一切，就愈加成熟。
And he wants to keep going, but he sees a problem. There is a lack of maturity. How so?他本想繼續講下去，但他看到一個問題：「缺乏成熟」。怎麼看出來？
………………………………………………
Struggling to listen and struggling to live…聆聽上的困難，與生活上的困難……
………………………………………………
Struggling to listen聆聽上的困難
………………………………………………
"We have much more to say about this, but it is hard to make it clear to you, because you no longer try to understand." Literally: Sluggish in hearing (v.11).「論到麥基洗德，我們有好些話，並且難以解明，因為你們聽不進去。」原文直譯：「聽覺遲鈍」（11 節）。
How does that show itself? They will not go beyond the basics. And so when the writer wants to go deeper, they cannot handle it.這怎樣表現出來？他們不肯越過最基本的真理。所以當作者想深入講解時，他們承受不來。
They are stuck, as it were, on the ABCs of the Christian life. We will come back to them later.他們彷彿停留在基督徒人生的「ABC（基礎入門）」階段。稍後我們會再回來談這些「ABC」。
It is like a maths teacher wanting to teach his high school students about algebra and how useful it can be, but the students are still stuck on basic addition and subtraction.這就好像一位數學老師想教中學生代數，並向他們解釋代數有多實用，但學生卻仍然停留在基本的加減運算。
The mature Christian is the one who listens well. As we read and hear the Bible, things do not just go in one ear and out the other. There is not that feeling that by Monday morning everything from Sunday is forgotten. The mature Christian knows the ABCs, but does not just stay there — they press on. They can never exhaust knowing God in ever deeper ways.成熟的基督徒是「善於聆聽」的人。當我們讀經和聽道時，內容不只是一耳進、一耳出。不會星期一早上一覺醒來，星期日所聽到的便已忘得一乾二淨。成熟的基督徒當然知道那些「ABC」，但不會只停留在那裏——他們會「竭力向前」。在更深認識神這條路上，他們永遠探索不完。
………………………………………………
Struggling to live生活上的困難
………………………………………………
Verses 13-14.13 至 14 節。
Not listening leads to not knowing the teaching about righteousness (v.13). What does that mean? It is explained in v.14: distinguishing good and evil.聽不進去，會導致「不熟練仁義的道理」（13 節）。這是甚麼意思？14 節作出了解釋：「能分辨好歹」。
Listening and going deeper into wonderful truths about God leads to right living.聆聽神奇妙的真理、並深入其中，會引導我們進入「正確的生活」。
It helps us know more and more what is pleasing to God and good for us spiritually. It means we avoid more and more what is unpleasing to God, and what harms us spiritually.這幫助我們愈來愈認識「甚麼是神所喜悅的」、甚麼是對我們屬靈生命有益的事。也意味著我們愈來愈遠離那些神不喜悅、會傷害我們屬靈生命的事。
In our lives: do we struggle to know whether the actions we do week by week, the things we spend time on and give attention to — can we gauge whether things help us spiritually or harm us spiritually? Are we aware of what in life brings us closer to our Father or what can make him feel more distant? Do we just blindly do what others around us do who are not Christians? Are we easily confused or troubled by what non-Christians say about God? The latest argument against God? Does that often lead to a crisis of confidence in Jesus and the Bible?回到我們的生活：我們每週所做的事、所花時間和關注的事——我們能否判斷其中哪些「對我們屬靈有益」、哪些「對我們屬靈有害」？我們是否清楚，生活中哪些事拉近我們與天父的距離、哪些事讓我們覺得祂變得遙遠？我們會否盲目地跟著身邊那些不認識基督的人去做？我們會否輕易被不信主的人對神的言論所困擾？對抗神最新的論調？這些論調會否常常令我們對耶穌和聖經失去信心？
Friends, all of us can struggle with this. But it can also be a sign of immaturity.弟兄姊妹，我們每一個人都可能在這些事上掙扎。但這也可能是「不成熟的徵兆」。
And that is not what God wants for us.而這「並非神對我們的心意」。
God wants us to move forward with confidence. To live a life where we increasingly bring him glory and enjoy him. Where we are not just tossed to and fro between doubt and uncertainty, and not just doing what harms us.神希望我們充滿信心地向前邁進；過一種愈來愈榮耀祂、以祂為樂的人生；不再在懷疑與不確定之間飄來搖去，也不再只是不停做那些傷害自己的事。
Maturity in listening leads to maturity in living.在聆聽上的成熟，會引致在生活上的成熟。
………………………………………………
Exactly what Paul said:正如保羅所講：
Romans 12:2: "Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind. Then you will be able to test and approve what God's will is — his good, pleasing and perfect will."羅馬書 12 章 2 節：「不要效法這個世界，只要心意更新而變化，叫你們察驗何為神的善良、純全、可喜悅的旨意。」
The more we know God, the more we know how to live well.我們愈認識神，就愈懂得「怎樣過好的人生」。
It is hard to live well for a God we do not know well.若我們對神認識不深，便很難為祂活出美好的人生。
"The heart cannot love what the mind does not know."「心靈無法愛它頭腦所不認識的對象。」
We get that in other areas of life: how can you please your spouse more and more if you do not continue to know them in a deeper way? True also of friendships.我們在人生其他層面也明白這個道理：若你不繼續更深認識配偶，又怎能愈來愈體貼他/她的心？友誼也是一樣。
If we are here this morning and we want to live more for our Father; if we want to know what pleases him, how we can bring him glory, how we can resist what is wrong — then the more we listen and go deeper in our understanding of God, the more we know how to live life well and bring him glory.若我們今早坐在這裏，渴望更多為天父而活；若我們想知道甚麼是祂所喜悅的，怎樣使祂得榮耀，怎樣抵擋一切錯謬——那麼，我們愈是聆聽、愈是深入認識神，就愈會懂得怎樣過美好的人生、並使祂得著榮耀。
………………………………………………
Couple of issues 兩個值得留意的問題兩個值得留意的問題
………………………………………………
First, not going deeper with God and knowing him better means we are simply more likely to be led astray. Especially when, as with the original audience, there is great pressure to go astray from outside — pressure to give up on Jesus and seek another path.「第一」，「不去更深認識神」意味著我們更容易「被引入歧途」。尤其是當外在環境有強大的壓力——正如原讀者所面對的——要逼我們離開耶穌、另尋他路時。
Think of those nature documentaries where a lion or cheetah hunts a herd of animals. Often they target the young and the new-borns — why? Unstable on their feet. Unsure of where to run. Easily separated from their mother. And they are soon cornered.想想自然紀錄片裏，獅子或獵豹獵殺羊群的場面。牠們往往專挑小羊或剛出生的——為甚麼？因為小羊腳步不穩、不知該往哪裏跑、容易與母親走散，很快便被逼到絕境。
Not maturing in our Christian faith means we are much more vulnerable to being led astray and deceived.若我們在信仰上不成熟，便會「極容易」被誘惑、被欺騙。
Things are much more enticing when we neglect thinking on and savouring the far better things found in our far-better Lord and Saviour.當我們「忽略了去思想和細嚐」我們那位更美的主、救主裏面所擁有的、更美的事物時，外面那些事物便會顯得格外吸引。
………………………………………………
Second issue is this — and this is quite striking:「第二」個問題如下——這一點頗為震撼：
Verse 12: though by this time you should be teachers, you need someone to teach you the elementary truths of God's word all over again.12 節：「看你們學習的工夫，本該作師傅，誰知還得有人將神聖言小學的開端另教導你們，並且成了那必須吃奶、不能吃乾糧的人。」
Immaturity means selfishness.不成熟，意味著自我中心。
Toddlers cannot cook, cannot do the laundry, cannot cut the grass, cannot clean. We have tried! Not much giving; mostly about taking. Now that is fine — because they are toddlers. But we would agree: if adults act like that — that is selfish. It is the same with believers. Immature believers, instead of being able to help, encourage and teach fellow believers, are only able to keep taking because of their immaturity. Our growth in maturity helps others around us.幼兒不會煮飯、不會洗衣、不會剪草、不會打掃。我們都試過了！他們很少付出，大多時候是在接受。這沒有問題——因為他們還是幼兒。但若「成年人是這樣」，我們都會說這是自私。信徒也是一樣。不成熟的信徒，因著自身的不成熟，無法去幫助、鼓勵、教導其他信徒，只能不斷地接受。我們在成熟上的成長，「會幫助到我們身邊的人」。
………………………………………………
Friends, are we mature or immature in our faith? Do we know our God better and deeper than before? And is that issuing out in living well, and making good decisions that bring our heavenly Father glory?弟兄姊妹，我們在信仰上是成熟還是不成熟？比起以前，我們對神的認識是否更深、更廣？這份認識，又是否流露在美好的生活、和那些榮耀天父的決定上？
"One of the great tragedies in discipleship is being an infant when you should be an adult, a student when you should be a teacher. Not that being an infant or a student or drinking milk is a bad thing — but staying there is."「在門徒訓練裏，其中一個最大的悲劇，就是當你本該是成年人時，你卻仍是嬰孩；當你本該是老師時，你卻仍是學生。作嬰孩、作學生、喝奶，本身並非壞事——但停留在那裏，卻是真正的悲劇。」
………………………………………………
The Call: it is not too late to grow! 呼召：要成長，現在還不太遲！呼召：要成長，現在還不太遲！
………………………………………………
Read 6:1-3.請讀 6 章 1 至 3 節。
"Therefore let us move beyond the elementary teachings about Christ and be taken forward to maturity, not laying again the foundation of repentance from acts that lead to death, and of faith in God, instruction about cleansing rites, the laying on of hands, the resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment. And God permitting, we will do so."「所以，我們應當離開基督道理的開端，竭力進到完全的地步，不必再立根基，就如那懊悔死行、信靠神、各樣洗禮、按手之禮、死人復活，以及永遠審判各等教訓。神若許我們，我們必如此行。」
………………………………………………
I do not know if you knew this: there was meant to be a rival to the Eiffel Tower, right here in London — we are talking over 100 years ago. The picture behind me shows what was planned. It was going to be called Watkin's Tower (named after the man funding the project), and was going to be built in Wembley Park. It was going to be bigger than the Eiffel Tower. Back then, of course, the Eiffel Tower was the tallest building in the world. They built the foundations… and then it stopped. The money ran out. And it was shelved. The area is now covered by Wembley Stadium.不知道你是否聽過這件事：原來百多年前，倫敦這裏曾計劃建一座「與艾菲爾鐵塔抗衡的高塔」。我身後的圖片就是當年的設計圖。它的名字叫「沃特金鐵塔」（以出資興建的人命名），選址在「溫布利公園」。原本要比艾菲爾鐵塔還要高。那時候艾菲爾鐵塔正是全世界最高的建築物。他們把地基都建好了……然後便停工了。資金用盡。整個項目被擱置。今天那個位置，就是溫布利球場所在地。
All would agree — not a success! They had built the most important part: the foundations. But to spend all that money just on the foundations is not the point!誰都會同意——這「不算是成功的項目」！他們確實把最重要的部分——地基——建好了。但花了那麼多錢只建地基，「根本不是目的」！
We want to build on them.我們是要「在地基之上繼續建造」。
Friends, if we are not maturing in our understanding beyond the ABCs, then we are like a building that is only the foundations.弟兄姊妹，若我們的理解只停留在「ABC」階段，沒有繼續成熟，那麼我們就像一座建築物，「只有地基」而已。
………………………………………………
Now these foundations may well be vital…當然，這些地基本身可能極其重要……
The writer lists these foundations. Three areas:作者列出了這些地基，可分為「三方面」：
1. "Repentance from acts that lead to death, and of faith in God" — this is about our initial response to God, how we are saved. The gospel message and our call to respond.一、「懊悔死行、信靠神」——這指的是我們對神的「起初回應」，亦即我們如何得救。福音的信息，以及我們被呼召作出的回應。
2. "Instruction about cleansing rites, the laying on of hands" — this is Jewish language (remember the audience!) to do with entry into the church — certainly baptism.二、「各樣洗禮、按手之禮」——這是猶太人的用語（記住——他們本是猶太背景的信徒！），講的是「加入教會」這件事——當然包括洗禮。
3. "The resurrection of the dead, and eternal judgment" — this is about what happens at the end times when Jesus returns. What is our hope?三、「死人復活，以及永遠審判」——這是論到末後的事，即耶穌再來時將要發生甚麼。我們的盼望是甚麼？
So they know the basics: how one becomes a Christian; how one joins a fellowship; what is our hope concerning the future.所以這些信徒是知道基本的：一個人怎樣成為基督徒、怎樣加入信徒的群體、對將來的盼望是甚麼。
These need to be understood…這些當然都需要明白……
But as vital as these things are, these are the lower limit of what we are called to know. We should not be content with that being all.但即使這些事再重要，它們也只是我們所被呼召去認識之事的「最低限度」。我們「不應滿足於」只認識這些。
………………………………………………

English
Hebrews 5:11-6:3 is a deliberate pause in the writer's long sermon on Jesus as our great High Priest. He longs to take his readers deeper, but he sees a problem: they are still on spiritual milk when they should have moved on to solid food. The diagnosis comes in two parts: they are sluggish in hearing (still stuck on the ABCs of the faith) and sluggish in living (unable to distinguish good from evil). Immaturity in listening always shows up as immaturity in living — and immaturity also means selfishness, an inability to nourish others. But the writer does not give up on them. He calls them — and us — to build on the foundations rather than re-laying them. God's word, theology, and doctrine are not for intellectuals; they are the means by which our joy, love and obedience deepen. The picture: a building that is just foundations is no building at all.
