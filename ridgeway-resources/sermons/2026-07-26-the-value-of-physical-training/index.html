Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
The Value of Physical Training 操練身體的價值
(1 Timothy 提摩太前書 4:1-8)

Introduction引言
As a society we are more fitness conscious than ever. Just think of Park Run — the charity that organises a weekly 5km run in parks, including one at Verulamium.我們這個社會對健康體能的關注,前所未有。想想 Park Run 就知道了 —— 這個慈善機構每星期在公園舉辦五公里跑,連 Verulamium 也有一場。
It started in 2004 with just 13 participants. Last month they organised their one millionth event. There are now almost 3,000 Park Runs every week across more than 23 countries — from 13 people to hundreds of thousands.它 2004 年起步時,只有十三個人參加。上個月,他們辦了第一百萬場活動。如今每星期在超過二十三個國家舉行接近三千場 Park Run —— 由十三人變成數以十萬計的人。
How many runners did you see on the way to church? A record number of gyms have opened in the past year. How many people do you know who track their steps on their watch? We love exercise…你今早來教會的路上,見到多少個跑步的人?過去一年開設的健身室數目創了新高。你認識多少人用手錶記錄自己走了多少步?我們熱愛運動……
And at the same time — exercise is badly needed. We are the most sedentary people who have ever lived. Usually because of work: more and more of it is done at a desk in front of a screen. And when we are not working, we are often still plugged into something.但與此同時 —— 我們實在非常需要運動。我們是人類歷史上最久坐不動的一代。多數是因為工作:愈來愈多工作是坐在書桌前對著螢幕完成的。而不工作的時候,我們往往仍然黏著某個螢幕。
Many feel guilty about a lack of exercise. The number one new year’s resolution each year is to do more exercise.很多人為自己缺乏運動而內疚。每年最多人立下的新年願望,就是要多做運動。
So what does the Bible say about exercise and fitness? 1 Timothy 4 gives us an important principle to navigate this issue. As often happens, the Bible travels a path between two extremes: ignoring exercise, and idolising exercise.那麼,聖經對運動與健康體能有甚麼話說?提摩太前書第四章給了我們一個重要原則來面對這個課題。正如聖經常有的做法,它走的是兩個極端之間的一條路:既不忽視運動,也不把運動當作偶像。
………………………………
We must not ignore exercise我們不可忽視運動
God does value exercise. He is a good God who gives us good things to enjoy, and they should not be laughed at — exercise and fitness included.神確實看重運動。他是良善的神,把美好的事物賜給我們享用,這些都不該被輕看 —— 運動與體能也在其中。
God gives us a body to live in. We are embodied beings. We are physical matter, and we know from Genesis that this is all good.神賜給我們身體居住其中。我們是有身體的受造者。我們是有形質的,而從創世記我們知道,這一切都是好的。
“When I run I feel God’s pleasure,” said Eric Liddell, the famous Olympian and later missionary to China.著名奧運選手、後來成為中國宣教士的李愛銳(Eric Liddell)說過:「當我奔跑的時候,我感受到神的喜悅。」
We are not just brains or souls. Our bodies matter. Culture today is confused on this — not surprisingly. The more time we spend online, disembodied, the more confused we become. No: we live the Christian life with our bodies as well as our minds. We are to honour God with our bodies, and to care for them.我們不僅是頭腦或靈魂。我們的身體是要緊的。今日的文化在這一點上相當混亂 —— 這並不奇怪。我們愈是活在網絡裡、脫離身體,就愈是混亂。不是這樣的:我們是用身體、也用心思去過基督徒的生活。我們要用身體榮耀神,並且好好照顧它。
………………………………
But we must not idolise it either但我們也不可把它當作偶像
At the same time, we must not idolise exercise and fitness. Verse 8 says it is only of some value. Compared with godliness, it is of only minor importance.但與此同時,我們也不可把運動與體能當作偶像。第 8 節說,操練身體「益處還少」。與敬虔相比,它只是次要的。
Many exercise in order to give their life meaning and purpose, and it can become something that dominates. Thirty hours a week in the gym — it can become escapism, or a way of avoiding other people.許多人以運動來賦予生命意義和目標,結果它可以變成一件支配一切的事。一星期在健身室三十小時 —— 它可以成為逃避現實的出口,或是逃避與人相處的方法。
We must get our priorities right. The big question is: how are we living for Jesus more and more? How are we doing at putting sin to death and putting on righteousness?我們必須把優先次序擺正。真正要問的是:我們如何愈來愈為耶穌而活?我們在治死罪、穿上公義這件事上,做得怎樣?
But there is a connection between physical training and spiritual matters, as we shall see. We will look at some benefits of exercise for us as Christians, a couple of key spiritual truths that exercising can teach us, and then finish with the key question of motivation.不過,操練身體與屬靈的事之間,確實有一種關聯,我們稍後會看到。我們會看看運動對基督徒的一些益處、運動能教導我們的幾個重要屬靈真理,最後以動機這個關鍵問題作結。
………………………………
An important caveat一個重要的說明
Some of us cannot exercise in the ways we would like, because of illness or age. Some feel the pain of no longer being able to do what we once did. That is not something to feel guilty over. God knows our life situations. He knows when exercise is too much and too demanding.我們當中有些人因著疾病或年紀,無法按自己想要的方式運動。有些人為著再不能做從前做到的事而感到難過。這不是需要內疚的事。神知道我們各人的處境,他知道甚麼時候運動對我們是太多、太吃力。
Some of us are waiting for, and looking forward to, a spectacular bodily upgrade. Our bodies feel very lowly — one day they will be made glorious.我們當中有些人正在等候、並且盼望一次何等榮耀的身體更新。我們的身體如今看來卑賤 —— 但有一天,它必要變成榮耀的。
“who, by the power that enables him to bring everything under his control, will transform our lowly bodies so that they will be like his glorious body.” (Philippians 3:21)「他要按著那能叫萬有歸服自己的大能,將我們這卑賤的身體改變形狀,和他自己榮耀的身體相似。」(腓立比書 3 章 21 節)
We may also be in a season of life — with young children — which makes time much harder to find. If you are in these situations, there are still many ways to glorify the Lord, and ways to feel his pleasure in what you do.我們也可能正處於某個人生階段 —— 例如家裡有小孩 —— 令時間變得很難擠出來。若你正身處這些景況,仍然有許多方法可以榮耀主,也仍然可以在所做的事上感受到他的喜悅。
And we are not talking here about hitting the gym four days a week or running marathons. For some of us this could simply mean going for a walk more often than we do at the moment.還有,我們這裡談的並不是一星期上四天健身室,或者去跑馬拉松。對我們某些人來說,這可能只是比現在多一點出去散步而已。
Five brief points…以下五個簡短的重點……
………………………………
1. Exercise helps our minds一、運動幫助我們的思想
Why is that important? We know the benefits for mental health, and that is good. Perhaps your anxiety could do with a walk each day.為甚麼這很重要?我們都知道運動對精神健康有益處,這是好的。也許你的焦慮,正需要每天出去走一走。
It is also good for our spiritual lives. Jesus calls all of us who follow him his disciples. “Disciple” means “learner”. Jesus is our teacher; we learn from him. That requires the use of our minds.它對我們的屬靈生命同樣有益。耶穌稱一切跟從他的人為門徒。「門徒」的意思就是「學習的人」。耶穌是我們的夫子,我們向他學習。這需要用上我們的心思。
“Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.” (Matthew 22:37)「你要盡心、盡性、盡意愛主你的神。」(馬太福音 22 章 37 節)
How does this connect with exercise? Exercise helps brain function: movement activates the brain, helps grow new brain cells, and strengthens the cells that help us log new information. It improves our ability to focus and to retain what we learn.這與運動有甚麼關係?運動有助腦部功能:活動身體會啟動大腦,幫助新的腦細胞生長,也強化那些幫助我們記錄新資訊的細胞。它改善我們專注和記住所學的能力。
Can you engage well with God’s word? Can you sit down for a period and study the Bible? Can you listen well to sermons? Can you read a good book about God? These are important questions in an age of distraction and noise.你能好好投入神的話語嗎?你能坐下來一段時間查考聖經嗎?你能專心聽講道嗎?你能讀一本關於神的好書嗎?在這個充滿分心與嘈雜的世代,這些都是重要的問題。
Using our minds is a key way to love God well and to be a disciple. Exercising helps us do that.善用我們的心思,是好好愛神、作門徒的一個重要途徑。而運動幫助我們做到這一點。
………………………………
2. Exercise helps us persevere in godliness二、運動幫助我們在敬虔上堅忍
It is interesting that Paul connects physical training, to some degree, with godliness — spiritual training. And there is a connection.值得留意的是,保羅在某程度上把操練身體與敬虔 —— 即屬靈的操練 —— 連在一起。而兩者之間確實有關聯。
Think of that moment you have probably experienced at some point: you are running but you want to give up. The lactic acid is pumping round your body. You cannot take another step, cannot run another lap, swim another length, lift another weight — you have hit the wall.想想你大概曾經經歷過的那一刻:你在跑步,但很想放棄。乳酸在全身湧動。你再跨不出一步、跑不完多一個圈、游不了多一趟、舉不起多一下 —— 你撞牆了。
A Christian writer describes having that moment at the gym: he could not lift the weights any more. He was about to give up — when along came his trainer. “Yes you can,” said the trainer. And it turned out he could. What he thought was the wall turned out not to be the wall.有一位基督徒作者描述自己在健身室的這一刻:他再也舉不動那些重量了。他正要放棄 —— 這時教練走了過來,說:「你做得到。」結果他真的做得到。他以為那是極限,原來並不是。
He then describes the next morning in the kitchen with his young son, who was in the high chair drinking milk. As often happens, the bottle went crashing to the floor and milk went everywhere. He was sore from the gym — his legs were killing him, and it ached to bend down and mop it up. But he did it.接著他描述第二天早上,他和年幼的兒子在廚房裡,兒子坐在高椅上喝奶。像常常發生的那樣,奶瓶「啪」一聲掉在地上,奶灑得滿地都是。他因為前一天的操練而全身痠痛 —— 雙腿疼得要命,彎下腰去抹地更是難受。但他還是做了。
Three seconds later, the bottle was thrown down again. Milk everywhere. He was ready to snap and to shout. He knew he should not respond like that — not how Jesus would want him to respond — but he felt powerless.三秒之後,奶瓶又被扔到地上。滿地都是奶。他快要發作、快要吼出來。他知道自己不該這樣反應 —— 這不是耶穌所要的反應 —— 但他覺得自己無能為力。
But then he remembered what had happened at the gym. What he thought was the wall, was not. He could keep going.然後,他想起健身室裡發生的事。他以為的極限,原來不是極限。他還可以撐下去。
It applied here too. He might feel he was running out of patience, but he was not. Why not? Because he has God’s Spirit in him. He has an unending reservoir of grace to help him. So he picked up the cup again and smiled at his young son.這裡也一樣。他也許覺得自己的忍耐快要用完,其實並沒有。為甚麼?因為神的靈住在他裡面。他有無盡的恩典泉源可以支取。於是他再次把杯子拾起來,向年幼的兒子微笑。
What happened? His physical training was, to some degree, actually training his soul. We should not overplay this. But it did make him realise that he could react in a godly way to a hard situation — that he could be patient rather than angry.發生了甚麼事?他操練身體,在某程度上其實也在操練他的靈魂。我們不該把這一點誇大。但這確實讓他意識到,他可以用敬虔的方式回應艱難的處境 —— 他可以選擇忍耐,而不是發怒。
We may have moments when we say: I cannot forgive that person again; I cannot hold back when that person really annoys me; I cannot be patient with this new problem. But actually, yes we can. The Holy Spirit is there to make change possible. God pours out enough grace to make us able. Part of it is believing that we can — and exercise trains us to know that we can do things that are hard.我們也會有這樣的時刻,對自己說:我不能再一次原諒那個人;那個人這麼惹我生氣,我不可能忍住不發作;面對這個新難題,我沒辦法有耐性。但其實,我們可以。聖靈就在那裡,使改變成為可能。神澆灌下足夠的恩典,叫我們能夠做到。其中一部分,是相信自己做得到 —— 而運動正好操練我們去明白:困難的事,我們是做得到的。
It is no coincidence that Paul often describes the Christian life with athletic metaphors.保羅時常用競賽的比喻來描述基督徒的生活,這絕非偶然。
………………………………
3. Exercise teaches us the Jesus-pattern三、運動教導我們耶穌的模式
Here is an important point: our bodies can teach our souls things. Of course they can — God made us body and soul.這裡有一個重要的觀點:我們的身體可以教導我們的靈魂。當然可以 —— 神造我們,是有身體也有靈魂的。
Think of the importance of sleep. Making our bodies rest as much as they should teaches us something.想想睡眠的重要。讓身體得到它該有的休息,本身就在教導我們一些事。
“In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, LORD, make me dwell in safety.” (Psalm 4:8)「我必安然躺下睡覺,因為獨有你耶和華使我安然居住。」(詩篇 4 篇 8 節)
Sleep reminds us every day that we are creatures. We are not God. We do not sustain the world. Sleep reminds us that our God, who never sleeps, is in charge — not me.睡眠每天提醒我們:我們是受造之物。我們不是神。維持這個世界的不是我們。睡眠提醒我們,那位永不打盹的神在掌權 —— 不是我。
Exercise can also teach our souls things. Exercise reminds us that the path to glory comes through suffering. Suffering can indeed strengthen our faith.運動同樣可以教導我們的靈魂。運動提醒我們:通往榮耀的路,是經過苦難的。苦難確實可以堅固我們的信心。
We understand this in other areas of life. How do we give our children strong immune systems? Exposure to illness. The more they get sick, the more their immune systems are strengthened. To prevent excessive allergies, they need exposure to allergens.在生活其他方面,我們是明白這個道理的。怎樣讓孩子有強壯的免疫系統?就是讓他們接觸病菌。他們生病愈多,免疫系統就愈強壯。要防止過敏太嚴重,反而需要讓他們接觸致敏原。
The same is true in exercise. Our bodies actually need pain, stress and suffering in order to stay healthy. Bones and muscles need to be strained in order to remain strong. And again — this does not only apply to weight training. One of the keys to longevity in physical health is simply going for regular walks.運動也是一樣。我們的身體其實需要疼痛、壓力和辛苦,才能保持健康。骨骼和肌肉需要被施加壓力,才會強健。而且再說一次 —— 這不是只有舉重才算。身體健康長壽的其中一個關鍵,不過是有規律地散步而已。
It is no coincidence that in God’s world, the way our bodies stay fit communicates a great gospel truth: suffering is not an impediment to the good life — rather, it is the path to it. That was Jesus’ path.在神的世界裡,我們身體保持強健的方式,竟然傳遞出一個重大的福音真理,這絕非偶然:苦難不是美好生命的阻礙 —— 它正是通往美好生命的道路。這也是耶穌所走的路。
“he humbled himself by becoming obedient to death — even death on a cross! Therefore God exalted him to the highest place and gave him the name that is above every name.” (Philippians 2:8-9)「既有人的樣子,就自己卑微,存心順服,以至於死,且死在十字架上。所以神將他升為至高,又賜給他那超乎萬名之上的名。」(腓立比書 2 章 8 至 9 節)
That is the Jesus pattern. The pattern of the cross. Suffering now, glory later.這就是耶穌的模式,十字架的模式:先受苦,後得榮耀。
Now, Jesus has to be our Saviour before he is our model. His death for our sins is not something we can imitate — we cannot suffer for others in that sense. But his pattern is ours. The gospel is not just a message; it is a template for our lives. We find life by losing it. We are called to imitate his example of costly love.不過,耶穌必須先是我們的救主,然後才是我們的榜樣。他為我們的罪而死,這是我們無法效法的 —— 我們不能在那個意義上為別人受苦。但他的模式,就是我們的模式。福音不只是一個信息,更是我們生命的樣式。我們藉著失去生命而得著生命。我們蒙召去效法他那捨己的愛。
“But if you suffer for doing good and you endure it, this is commendable before God. To this you were called, because Christ suffered for you, leaving you an example, that you should follow in his steps.” (1 Peter 2:20-21)「你們若因行善受苦,能忍耐,這在神看是可喜愛的。你們蒙召原是為此,因基督也為你們受過苦,給你們留下榜樣,叫你們跟隨他的腳蹤行。」(彼得前書 2 章 20 至 21 節)
We have the same expectation: suffering now, glory later. Why? Our imitation of Jesus points others to the one who alone can save. And our suffering itself reassures us that glory is coming.我們有著同樣的盼望:先受苦,後得榮耀。為甚麼?因為我們效法耶穌,會把別人指向那位唯一能拯救的主。而我們所受的苦,本身也讓我們確信:榮耀必要來到。
As one writer puts it: exercise is one of the things in the world particularly well suited to teach us the cruciform shape of Christ’s life, because it asks us to embody it.正如一位作者所說:運動是世上特別適合教導我們認識基督生命那「十架形狀」的事物之一,因為它要求我們用身體把它活出來。
Culture today wants things to be as easy as possible. Life should be completely smooth; any bumps are bad. Exercise points us to the opposite spiritual truth: glory comes through sacrifice.今日的文化總希望事情愈容易愈好。人生應該一帆風順,任何顛簸都是壞事。運動卻把我們指向相反的屬靈真理:榮耀是經過捨己而來的。
………………………………
4. Exercise teaches us that God brings about change slowly四、運動教導我們:神使人改變是慢慢來的
Every few years I start playing football again regularly. But after a long period out, it takes time to get fit. The first time I play in a while, my legs cramp after about an hour. And even when I train, I do not see results straight away. I still cramp up. I still feel exhausted. But over time, slowly, things improve.每隔幾年,我就會重新開始定期踢足球。但停了很久之後,要重新練回體能是需要時間的。久違地上場那一次,大約一小時之後雙腿就抽筋。就算開始操練,也不會馬上見到成果 —— 我還是會抽筋,還是會累得不行。但隨著時間過去,慢慢地,情況就有改善。
With exercise and fitness, change often happens slowly. This is important to realise. When we expect too much to change all at once, or too quickly, we get dispirited and often give up.在運動和體能這件事上,改變往往是慢慢發生的。認清這一點很重要。當我們期望一下子改變太多、或者改變得太快,我們就會灰心,並且常常放棄。
That is the body once again teaching us a vital spiritual lesson. Sometimes when we become Christians we see some immediate change — that was true for me. But much change happens slowly. Inch by inch.這又是身體在教導我們一個極重要的屬靈功課。有時候我們信主之後,確實立刻看見一些改變 —— 我自己就是這樣。但更多的改變是慢慢發生的,一寸一寸地。
Many things happen slowly on God’s timing. The kingdom of God starts from a mustard seed and grows slowly. Our spiritual lives grow slowly. Do not expect everything all at once.許多事情是按神的時間、慢慢成就的。神的國從一粒芥菜種開始,慢慢長大。我們的屬靈生命,也是慢慢長成的。不要期望一切都一次過完成。
Want to pray more? Set your alarm for 5am to pray for two hours? You will soon give up. What about praying two or three minutes a day? What about saying the Lord’s Prayer once a day, then moving to five minutes a day, and so on? Over time, that turns you into a person of prayer.想多禱告嗎?把鬧鐘調到清晨五時,然後禱告兩小時?你很快就會放棄。不如每天禱告兩三分鐘?不如每天念一次主禱文,然後慢慢增加到每天五分鐘,如此類推?日子有功,這會把你塑造成一個禱告的人。
And exercise teaches us another important thing here. The strange thing about exercise is that as we break our muscles down, it is not my conscious effort that makes them stronger again. It simply happens — I train, but then something else makes them grow.運動在這裡還教導我們另一件重要的事。運動有一個奇妙之處:當我們把肌肉「拆散」之後,使它們重新變強的,並不是我有意識的努力。它就是這樣發生了 —— 我操練,然後有另一種力量叫它們生長。
Living the Christian life requires effort. But it is not we who supply the growth. We can commit to doing more, make a Scripture reading plan, commit to praying more — and that is all well and good. But it is only through the indwelling of the Holy Spirit that we change. God brings the growth. He supplies the power.過基督徒的生活是需要努力的。但供應生長的,並不是我們自己。我們可以立志多做一點、訂立讀經計劃、決心多禱告 —— 這些都是好的。但唯有藉著聖靈住在我們裡面,我們才會改變。神叫人生長,他供應能力。

5. Exercising to love God and others五、為愛神愛人而運動
Exercise goes back thousands of years. The ancient Greeks loved exercise — but why? Often it was so they could train up strong men to fight in wars that were always just around the corner.運動的歷史可以追溯到幾千年前。古希臘人熱愛運動 —— 但為甚麼?往往是為了訓練出強壯的男丁,好去打那些隨時會爆發的仗。
Why do many exercise today? Many, if they are honest, do it because they want to look good, and only when they look good can they feel good about themselves. It is about body image. Others do it because that is what gives them meaning.那麼今日許多人為甚麼運動?坦白說,許多人是因為想外表好看,而且唯有外表好看,他們才對自己感覺良好。這是關乎身體形象。也有些人運動,是因為那是他們生命意義的來源。
What is the difference between a non-Christian exercising and a Christian exercising? Well — what is the goal? What do we want our bodies to be made fit for? Instagram? Or something else?那麼,非基督徒運動與基督徒運動,分別在哪裡?問題是 —— 目標是甚麼?我們想要自己的身體為著甚麼而強健?為了 Instagram?還是為著別的?
1 Timothy 4:1-5 reminds us that God is the crucial factor. Are we doing what we do out of conscious gratitude to God, with an awareness of our desire to honour him through it? Verse 5: what we do is made holy by the word of God and prayer.提摩太前書 4 章 1 至 5 節提醒我們,神才是關鍵所在。我們所做的,是否出於對神自覺的感恩,並且清楚意識到自己渴望藉此榮耀他?第 5 節說,我們所做的,是「藉著神的道和人的祈求成為聖潔了」。
We get this with meals. As Chesterton put it: “You say grace before meals. All right. But I say grace before the play and the opera, and grace before I open a book, and grace before sketching, painting, swimming, fencing, boxing, walking, playing, dancing; and grace before I dip the pen in the ink.”我們在吃飯這件事上明白這個道理。正如切斯特頓(G. K. Chesterton)所說:「你在飯前謝恩,很好。但我在看戲和聽歌劇之前謝恩,在翻開一本書之前謝恩,在素描、繪畫、游泳、擊劍、拳擊、散步、玩耍、跳舞之前謝恩;也在我把筆蘸進墨水之前謝恩。」
In other words, Christians should want to exercise in order to glorify God. We understand from his word that our bodies are important, and a means to honour him. We want it to be a grateful response to him.換句話說,基督徒運動,應該是為了榮耀神。我們從他的話語明白,我們的身體是要緊的,也是榮耀他的途徑。我們願意這是對他的感恩回應。
Consciously pray something like this: “Father, make this run holy to you this morning.” Or: “Give me energy, help me endure, keep me from injury, and use this exercise to boost my brain, my joy and my body. And help me love others well today. May you be glorified in my body.”可以有意識地這樣禱告:「父啊,求你使我今早這一趟跑步,在你面前成為聖潔。」或者:「求你賜我力氣,幫助我堅持,保守我不受傷,並使用這次運動來強健我的頭腦、我的喜樂和我的身體。也求你幫助我今天好好去愛身邊的人。願你在我身上得著榮耀。」
Any other motivation? Love others. How might staying fit and healthy help us love others?還有別的動機嗎?愛人。保持強健和健康,怎樣能幫助我們去愛人呢?
Being able to get down on the floor — and back up again easily — to wrestle and play with your children. Staying fit enough not to throw out your back during normal household tasks. Being strong enough to support an elderly parent on a walk. Being healthy enough to pick up your grandchildren. Staying healthy enough to walk with your spouse, or to push them in a wheelchair when they no longer can.能夠蹲到地上 —— 而且輕易站得起來 —— 跟孩子扭打嬉戲。體能足夠好,做家務時不會扭傷腰背。有足夠力氣扶著年邁的父母散步。夠健康,可以抱起孫兒。夠健康,可以陪配偶散步,或在他們再走不動的時候,推著輪椅陪他們。
Who can we love by staying healthy?我們保持健康,是為了可以去愛誰呢?

The Value of Physical Training

操練身體的價值

1 Timothy 提摩太前書 4:1-8 · Bible Study Guide 聖經學習指南
“For physical training is of some value, but godliness has value for all things, holding promise for both the present life and the life to come.” (1 Timothy 4:8)
「操練身體,益處還少;惟獨敬虔,凡事都有益處,因有今生和來生的應許。」(提摩太前書 4 章 8 節)
📖 Summary 摘要
English
We have never been more fitness-conscious — Park Run went from 13 runners in 2004 to its millionth event last month — and never more sedentary. So what does the Bible say? 1 Timothy 4:8 walks a path between two ditches. We must not ignore exercise: God made us embodied creatures, called that good, and Eric Liddell was right that we can feel his pleasure in running. But we must not idolise it either — Paul says it is of only “some value”, minor next to godliness, and thirty hours a week in the gym can quietly become escapism. Between those ditches sit five gifts. Exercise helps our minds, and a disciple is a learner who must love God with the mind (Matthew 22:37). It helps us persevere in godliness: what we thought was the wall usually is not — and the patience we could not find for the second spilled bottle of milk is there after all, because the Spirit supplies an unending reservoir of grace. It teaches us the Jesus-pattern: bones and muscles only stay strong by being strained, which is the gospel written into our bodies — suffering now, glory later (Philippians 2:8-9; 1 Peter 2:20-21). It teaches us that God grows us slowly: not a 5am two-hour prayer marathon, but two minutes a day that over years makes a person of prayer — and note that it is not our conscious effort that rebuilds the muscle, just as it is the indwelling Spirit, not our effort, that supplies spiritual growth. Finally, motivation: not Instagram, but gratitude — “Father, make this run holy to you” — and love, so that we can still get down on the floor with our children, support an elderly parent, and walk beside a spouse who no longer can. Who can we love by staying healthy?

🎯 Key Themes 主要主題
English

1. Between two ditches — neither ignore nor idolise. Scripture rarely lets us take the easy extreme. Our bodies are good and God-given, so contempt for them is not holiness; but exercise is of “some value” only, so building a life on it is idolatry with a fitness tracker.

2. We are embodied, not merely minds. The more time we spend online and disembodied, the more confused our culture becomes about this. We live the Christian life with our bodies as well as our minds, and we honour God with them.

3. The body teaches the soul. Sleep teaches us we are creatures and not God. Strain teaches us that suffering is the road to glory, not a detour around it. God has written lessons into how we are made.

4. Suffering now, glory later — the cruciform shape. Jesus must be Saviour before he is model; we cannot imitate his atoning death. But his pattern is ours: we find life by losing it.

5. Slow growth, supplied power. Change comes inch by inch, on God's timing — and just as the muscle rebuilds without our conscious effort, it is the indwelling Spirit, not our willpower, who supplies spiritual growth.

6. Motivation decides everything. The same run can be vanity or worship. Gratitude to God and love of neighbour are what separate a Christian's exercise from Instagram's.

💭 Thinking Questions 思考問題
English

1. Paul says physical training is “of some value”. Which ditch do you personally lean towards — ignoring your body, or idolising its condition? What is the evidence in your week?

2. The sermon says a disciple is a “learner”. Honestly, how is your capacity to focus — to sit with the Bible, to listen to a sermon, to read a good book? What is shaping that capacity, for better or worse?

6. Whose growth is it? Where are you relying on willpower where you should be relying on the Spirit — and how would the difference show?

8. For those who cannot exercise as they would like — through illness, age or season of life — what does it mean for you to honour God with the body you have today, without guilt?

🌱 Application 生活應用
English

Start absurdly small. Not the 5am two-hour prayer marathon and not the four-day gym plan. A daily walk. Two minutes of prayer. The Lord's Prayer once a day. Small enough that you will still be doing it in six months — that is the whole point.

Pray before you move. Try it this week, literally: “Father, make this walk holy to you. Give me energy, help me endure, keep me from injury, and help me love others well today.” The same activity, a different world.

Name the wall. Pick the one relationship where your patience keeps running out. Next time it happens, remember: the Spirit's reservoir has not run dry. Respond once in a way you did not think you had in you.

Audit the motive, not just the habit. Ask why you exercise — or why you avoid it. Body image? Meaning? Escape from people? Bring the honest answer to God rather than tidying it up first.

Guard the mind. If you cannot sit with Scripture for fifteen minutes, treat that as a real problem worth solving — and note that a daily walk is part of the solution, not a distraction from it.

Exercise for someone. Choose the person: a child to get down on the floor with, a parent to walk beside, a spouse to still be strong for. Let love, not vanity, set the goal.

父啊,感謝你造我們有身體、也有靈魂,並且稱之為好。感謝你,我們的身體不是要逃離的難堪之物,而是要好好管理的恩賜,並且有一天要在榮耀中復活。求你赦免我們輕看你所賜身體的地方,也赦免我們把它當作偶像、要它承載唯有你才能承擔的重量。求你藉著身體教導我們:我們是受造之物,你才是神;通往榮耀的路要經過苦難,正如你兒子所走的路;成長是緩慢的,而供應成長的是你的靈,不是我們的意志力。求你使我們每天微小的忠心,在你面前成為聖潔。在我們軟弱、患病、年老、或不過是疲累不堪的時候,求你提醒我們:你知道我們的本體,你對我們的喜悅不在乎我們的力氣,而我們這卑賤的身體,有一天必和基督榮耀的身體相似。求你幫助我們為著愛而強壯 —— 好抱得起孩子、扶得穩父母、陪得住那走不動的人。願你在我們的身上得著榮耀。奉耶穌的名求,阿們。
