Ridgeway Church — Bilingual 中英雙語

Hebrews 2:5–9 — One of Us (I) 29th March 2026 希伯來書2章5至9節 — 我們當中的一位（上） 2026年3月29日
………………………………
G. K. Chesterton — the great turn-of-the-century poet, philosopher, art critic, dramatist and Christian apologist — once wrote this: "Whatever else is or is not true, this one thing is certain — man is not what he was meant to be." 切斯特頓（G. K. Chesterton）——這位十九世紀末、二十世紀初的偉大詩人、哲學家、藝術評論家、劇作家及基督教護教者——曾經寫道：「無論其他事情是否屬實，有一件事是肯定的——人並非他原本應有的樣子。」
And that, in a nutshell, is why we need Jesus! 而這一句話，正正道出了我們為何需要耶穌！
………………………………
Here's a question for you: have you ever thought of "salvation" as becoming more human? That might sound strange. We often think of salvation as becoming something less than human — as though our future destiny is to be eternally disembodied spirits floating around somewhere. And if that's the case, it means denying something of who and what we actually are. 讓我問你一個問題：你有沒有想過，「救恩」其實是讓我們變得更加像一個「人」？這聽起來或許有點奇怪。我們常常以為救恩是讓我們變得不那麼像「人」——好像我們的終極命運就是成為永遠脫離肉體的靈魂，在某個地方飄浮。如果真是這樣的話，那就等於否定了我們本質的一部分。
But salvation is not like that at all! Salvation is God's great restoration project! And it begins with Jesus. Our restoration comes in and through him — because he is both our representative and our champion. 但救恩根本不是那樣的！救恩是上帝偉大的修復計劃！而這個計劃從耶穌開始。我們的修復是在他裏面、藉着他而成就的——因為他既是我們的代表，也是我們的冠軍。
………………………………
Last time we looked at how chapter 1 established the Son's superiority over the angels. Now, in verses 5 to 9, the comparison with angels continues — but this time, surprisingly, it involves us! And we come out on top. Why? Because Jesus is one of us, and he came out on top! That's what the whole of chapter 2 is about — and it's how we know that the best is yet to come. 上一次我們看了第一章如何確立聖子超越天使的地位。現在到了第五至九節，與天使的比較仍在繼續——但這一次，出人意料地，牽涉到的是我們！而且我們竟然居於上風。為甚麼？因為耶穌是我們當中的一位，而他已經得勝了！這就是整個第二章的主題——也正是我們如何知道最好的還在後頭。
………………………………
Now remember, this letter was written to a church that was having second thoughts about Jesus. And what we're going to see in chapter 2 is this: if you lose him, you lose yourself. 請記住，這封書信是寫給一間對耶穌產生動搖的教會。而我們將會在第二章中看到的是：如果你失去了他，你就失去了自己。
………………………………
We're going to look at chapter 2 in two parts. Today we're just covering verses 5 to 9, and there are two main sections. 我們將分兩部分來看第二章。今天我們只看第五至九節，當中有兩個主要段落。
………………………………
1. Humanity Frustrated We were made for more than this. 一、人類的挫敗 我們受造，是為了更大的事。
………………………………
So here's a question: what makes us "human"? After all, we're made from the same stuff — the same chemicals — as everything else in the world. Rocks, rabbits, radishes — we share the same basic building blocks. So what makes us unique? 那麼，問題來了：究竟是甚麼使我們成為「人」？畢竟，我們與世上其他一切事物都是由同樣的物質、同樣的化學元素所構成的。石頭、兔子、蘿蔔——我們共享着同樣的基本組成部分。那麼，是甚麼使我們獨特呢？
I came across an article online recently that listed what makes humans unique, and three things stood out to me: consciousness, empathy, and the pursuit of meaning and purpose. In other words — being aware of ourselves, feeling for others, and needing a reason for living. That makes a lot of sense! But why are those things there in the first place? The Bible's answer is this: we are made in the "image of God" (Genesis 1). 我最近在網上讀到一篇文章，列出了人類的獨特之處，其中有三項特別引起我的注意：意識、同理心，以及對意義和目的的追求。換句話說——對自我的覺察、對他人的感受，以及對生存理由的渴求。這很有道理！但為甚麼這些特質會存在呢？聖經的答案是：我們是按照「上帝的形象」被造的（創世記第一章）。
There's actually a great irony in atheism. In trying to make us "gods" — by removing the Creator — it has actually reduced us to nothing more than chemicals! Thankfully, that's not the truth. We are made like God. 其實無神論有一個極大的諷刺。它試圖把我們變成「神」——藉着否定造物主——結果卻把我們貶低為僅僅是一堆化學物質！感恩的是，事實並非如此。我們是按照上帝的形象被造的。
………………………………
This gives us tremendous value — and it's why the idea of "human rights" actually makes sense! But the reason the "image of God" is there isn't just about our value. It's about a role — a job. Notice that the context of Genesis 1 is functional. Being made in the "image of God" means we are fit for a task. 這賦予我們極大的價值——這也是「人權」這個概念之所以合理的原因！但「上帝的形象」之所以存在，不僅僅是關乎我們的價值，更是關乎一個角色——一項任務。請注意，創世記第一章的上下文是功能性的。按照「上帝的形象」被造，意味着我們是為了一項使命而裝備的。
"Then God said, 'Let us make mankind in our image, in our likeness, so that they may rule over the fish in the sea and the birds in the sky, over the livestock and all the wild animals, and over all the creatures that move along the ground.' So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them." (Genesis 1:26–27) 「神說：『我們要照着我們的形像，按着我們的樣式造人，使他們管理海裏的魚、空中的鳥、地上的牲畜和全地，並地上所爬的一切昆蟲。』神就照着自己的形像造人，乃是照着他的形像造男造女。」（創世記1:26-27）
What does that mean? It means we were made to be in charge of it. It's a master-and-servant arrangement — but under God, and for good! 這意味着甚麼？意味着我們受造是要管理這個世界。這是一種主人與僕人的安排——但是在上帝之下，並且是為了美善的目的！
………………………………
Psalm 8 is a poem — a song — celebrating exactly that. And that's why it's quoted here in verses 6 to 8. It takes the form of a question and answer. 詩篇第八篇正是一首慶賀這件事的詩歌。這就是為甚麼它在第六至八節被引用。它採用了一問一答的形式。
First, the question: "What is mankind that you are mindful of them, a son of man that you care for him?" (Hebrews 2:6) The question is asked because of the sheer vastness and glory of the universe. You look up at the spiral galaxies and the exploding supernovae and you think — what is man? Surely we're nothing! 首先是問題： 「人算甚麼，你竟顧念他？世人算甚麼，你竟眷顧他？」（希伯來書2:6） 提出這個問題，是因為宇宙的浩瀚與壯麗。當你仰望那些旋渦星系和爆炸的超新星時，你會想——人算甚麼？我們肯定微不足道吧！
No chance! Here's the answer: "You made them a little lower than the angels; you crowned them with glory and honour and put everything under their feet." (Hebrews 2:7–8a) Nothing? Hardly! We are only "a little lower" than the heavenly beings — the angels! In terms of our position, we have more in common with them than with the rest of the living world! 絕對不是！這是答案： 「你叫他比天使微小一點，賜他榮耀尊貴為冠冕，並將你手所造的都派他管理，叫萬物都服在他的腳下。」（希伯來書2:7-8上） 微不足道？完全不是！我們只是比天使「微小一點」而已！就地位而言，我們與天使的共通點，遠多於與其餘被造之物的共通點！
C. S. Lewis, in what is perhaps his most famous essay, "The Weight of Glory", wrote: "There are no ordinary people. You have never talked to a mere mortal." And that must be true — if this is who we are! 路易斯（C. S. Lewis）在他最著名的文章《榮耀的重量》（"The Weight of Glory"）中寫道：「世上沒有普通人。你從未與一個『僅僅是凡人』的人交談過。」如果這就是我們的身分，那這句話一定是真的！
………………………………
If you are a human being — this is who you are! You've got a part to play — maybe a tiny part — in this great project of God's. As each day you serve, and build, and care, and pray — this is who and what God has made you to be! 如果你是一個人——這就是你的身分！你在上帝這偉大的計劃中有一個角色要扮演——也許只是一個微小的角色。每一天你去服事、去建設、去關懷、去禱告——這就是上帝所造的你，這就是上帝為你設定的使命！
………………………………
But here's the problem. It's not quite working out, is it? "In putting everything under them, God left nothing that is not subject to them. Yet at present we do not see everything subject to them." (Hebrews 2:8b) Although we were meant to have mastery over our environment, the results have been… mixed. Sure, we can farm the land, build rockets, harness renewable energies, build AI platforms, and work together politically for a better world. But frustration is always in the mix. In reality, we are often at the mercy of forces working against us. We only have limited control. We were made for more than this! 但問題來了。事情並不如預期般順利，對嗎？ 「既叫萬物都服他，就沒有剩下一樣不服他的。只是如今我們還不見萬物都服他。」（希伯來書2:8下） 雖然我們本應掌管環境，但結果卻……好壞參半。不錯，我們可以耕種土地、建造火箭、利用可再生能源、開發人工智能平台，以及在政治上合作共建更美好的世界。但挫折總是如影隨形。現實中，我們往往受制於各種與我們作對的力量。我們只擁有有限的掌控力。我們受造，是為了更大的事！
………………………………
And here's the real kicker — it's our fault! The Bible explains that it all began with the "fall" in Genesis 3. That was the first time humankind chose to defy the Creator. And it's been the natural human way ever since. 而更殘酷的現實是——這是我們自己的過錯！聖經解釋，這一切始於創世記第三章的「墮落」。那是人類第一次選擇違抗造物主。自那以後，這就成了人類的天性。
What's the result? A broken world that fights back! Weeds take over our gardens. Health is taken from us. Machinery breaks down. Money loses its value. Accidents happen. 結果是甚麼？一個破碎的世界在反噬我們！雜草佔據了我們的花園。健康被奪去。機器故障。金錢貶值。意外接踵而來。
………………………………
And the most graphic, most painful, most ugly expression of that loss of control is death. It is the "last enemy" — the one that stalks every one of us. 而這種失控最直接、最痛苦、最醜陋的表現就是死亡。死亡是那個「最後的仇敵」——一直如影隨形地跟隨着我們每一個人。
The Welsh poet Dylan Thomas — a brilliant and gifted man from the early twentieth century — wrote one of the most famous poems in the English language: "Do Not Go Gentle into That Good Night." It's about resisting death with defiance rather than just accepting it. "Rage, rage against the dying of the light." We can "rage" all we like — but it will happen. Thomas himself died at the age of just 39. 威爾斯詩人狄蘭·湯瑪斯（Dylan Thomas）——一位二十世紀初才華橫溢的人——寫下了英語文學中最著名的詩篇之一：《不要溫順地步入那良夜》。這首詩講的是以抗爭之心面對死亡，而非逆來順受。「怒斥，怒斥那光芒的消逝。」我們可以盡情「怒斥」——但死亡終將來臨。湯瑪斯本人年僅三十九歲便離世了。
………………………………
It wasn't meant to be like this! We were made for more. So what's the answer? Can things ever be different? Yes! The tide has already started to turn! 本來不應該是這樣的！我們受造是為了更大的事。那麼答案是甚麼？事情有可能改變嗎？可以的！潮流已經開始逆轉了！
………………………………
2. Humanity Restored Jesus the man is already back where we belong! 二、人類的修復 耶穌——這位「人」——已經回到我們本應所在的位置！
………………………………
"Yet at present we do not see everything subject to them. But we do see Jesus, who was made lower than the angels for a little while, now crowned with glory and honour because he suffered death, so that by the grace of God he might taste death for everyone." (Hebrews 2:8b–9) 「只是如今我們還不見萬物都服他。惟獨見那成為比天使小一點的耶穌，因為受死的苦，就得了尊貴榮耀為冠冕，叫他因着神的恩，為人人嘗了死味。」（希伯來書2:8下-9）
We don't yet see everything back where it belongs. But we do already see Jesus where he belongs. And he is one of us! 我們尚未看見萬物回歸本位。但我們已經看見耶穌在他應有的位置上。而他，是我們當中的一位！
………………………………
There is a sense in which Psalm 8 is about us. But there is another sense in which it is all about Jesus! Because he is the best of us — perfectly qualified to represent us. And that means he can make it happen for us. He is the champion we need! 詩篇第八篇在某種意義上是關於我們的。但在另一種意義上，它完全是關於耶穌的！因為他是我們當中最好的——完全有資格代表我們。這意味着他能為我們成就這一切。他就是我們所需要的冠軍！
………………………………
There are lots of reasons why it's natural to read Psalm 8 as being about Jesus as well as about us. For one thing, it literally uses "him" not "them." And "Son of Man" became Jesus' favourite title for himself. The author of Hebrews was probably picking up on that double meaning! 有許多理由使我們可以很自然地把詩篇第八篇理解為既關乎我們，也關乎耶穌。首先，原文用的是「他」而非「他們」。而且「人子」後來成為耶穌最喜愛用來稱呼自己的名號。希伯來書的作者很可能正是在利用這個雙重含義！
………………………………
But there are two more direct ways verse 9 applies this psalm to Jesus. First: "made lower than the angels." For us, that phrase is a compliment — it means we're only a little below the angels! But for Jesus, it speaks of the extent of how far down he came. The eternal Son of God became a man. 但第九節還有兩個更直接的方式將這篇詩篇應用在耶穌身上。第一：「比天使微小一點」。對我們而言，這句話是一種讚美——意味着我們只比天使低一點點！但對耶穌而言，它述說的是他降卑的程度何等深遠。永恆的聖子成為了一個人。
Notice it says "for a little while." This doesn't mean he was only human for a little while — he is human forever! Rather, it was for a little while that he took on our fallen flesh. He became "one of us" — as we are now. Like us in every way, yet without sin. 請留意經文說「暫時」。這不是說他只做了短暫的人——他永遠都是人！而是說，他暫時取了我們墮落的肉身。他成為「我們當中的一位」——就像我們現在的樣子。在凡事上與我們一樣，只是沒有犯罪。
………………………………
Second: "now crowned with glory and honour." We already heard about this back in chapter 1, verses 3 and 13. Our crown has been tarnished by sin and brokenness. But not his — not now! He is in the place of highest honour, above everything else, with all things "subject" to him. He is the "image of God" perfectly fulfilled. 第二：「得了尊貴榮耀為冠冕」。我們在第一章第三節和第十三節已經聽過這一點。我們的冠冕已被罪惡和破碎所玷污。但他的卻沒有——現在沒有！他在至高的尊榮之位，超越一切，萬物都「服在」他之下。他完美地實現了「上帝的形象」。
………………………………
But the point is this: he is not there instead of us. He is there for us! On our behalf, as our representative. Through him, our true identity is restored. "In him," the image of God is fully renewed! Through him, we get our full God-given glory back. 但重點是：他在那裏，不是代替我們，而是為了我們！他以我們的代表身分在那裏。藉着他，我們真正的身分得以恢復。「在他裏面」，上帝的形象得以完全更新！藉着他，我們重新獲得上帝所賜予的全部榮耀。
As Paul writes in Ephesians: "And God raised us up with Christ and seated us with him in the heavenly realms in Christ Jesus." (Ephesians 2:6) 正如保羅在以弗所書中所寫： 「他又叫我們與基督耶穌一同復活，一同坐在天上。」（以弗所書2:6）
Our glory and honour, tarnished by sin, have been won back by our Saviour — for all those who are in Christ. 我們因罪而失色的榮耀與尊貴，已被我們的救主贏回——為所有在基督裏的人。
………………………………
And that means one day there will be a "world to come" (verse 5) that works properly for us! We were made for more than this — and that "more" is on its way! 這意味着將來有一天，會有一個「將來的世界」（第五節）為我們正常運作！我們受造是為了更大的事——而那「更大的事」正在來臨的路上！
So let's fight frustration with faith. And whatever you do — don't give up on Jesus! 所以，讓我們以信心對抗挫折。無論如何——不要放棄耶穌！
