Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
Marks of maturity 成熟的標記
(1 Corinthians 4:1-21 哥林多前書 4:1-21)

Marks of maturity 1 Corinthians 4:1-21 · 25th Oct 2026成熟的標記 哥林多前書 4:1-21 · 2026 年 10 月 25 日
Big idea Spiritually mature believers aim to please the Lord, reject worldly boasting and imitate the right people.中心思想 屬靈成熟的信徒，以討主喜悅為目標，拒絕屬世的誇口，並且效法對的人。
………………………………
What would you like to do or be when you grow up? Boys and girls! But plenty in Corinth needed to hear the same question!你長大以後想做甚麼？想成為怎樣的人？ 各位男孩女孩！ 其實，哥林多教會裏有不少人也需要聽聽同一個問題！
READ 3:1-2. “Brothers and sisters, I could not address you as people who live by the Spirit but as people who are still worldly—mere infants in Christ. I gave you milk, not solid food, for you were not yet ready for it. Indeed, you are still not ready.” (3:1-2)請讀三章 1-2 節。 「弟兄們，我從前對你們說話，不能把你們當作屬靈的，只得把你們當作屬肉體，在基督裏為嬰孩的。我是用奶餵你們，沒有用飯餵你們。那時你們不能吃，就是如今還是不能。」（三章 1-2 節）
Ironic – they thought they were the gold standard when it came to being “spiritual”. Great pride in their knowledge, giftedness and standing in the community! Turbo-charged believers. Setting the pace for everyone else! But Paul knows better.諷刺的是，他們自以為在「屬靈」這件事上是黃金標準。 他們為自己的知識、恩賜和在社會上的地位而大大自豪！ 彷彿是「渦輪增壓」的信徒， 為其他所有人定下步伐！ 但保羅看得更清楚。
With a mixture of astonishment, frustration, disappointment, grief and love, he writes to them: Get a grip!他懷着驚訝、無奈、失望、憂傷和愛交織的心情寫信給他們： 醒醒吧！
Today is the end of the first big section. Everything so far has been about… The cross Christian ministry Church life今天的經文是這卷書第一大段的結尾。 到目前為止，一切都圍繞着： 十字架 基督徒的事奉 教會生活
One big aim… To move the Corinthians to repent of the worldliness that is driving the divisions. Worldliness is especially seen in an obsession with power, influence, achievement and status.只有一個大目標： 就是推動哥林多人悔改，離棄那導致分裂的屬世心態。 屬世心態特別表現在對權力、影響力、成就和地位的迷戀上。
Paul has a heavy heart, because they are so out of step with the gospel message. At its very centre is Christ crucified. With passion, energy and love, he wants to move them (and us!) on to greater spiritual maturity.保羅心情沉重， 因為他們與福音信息如此格格不入。 福音的核心，就是釘十字架的基督。 他滿懷熱忱、幹勁和愛，要推動他們（還有我們！）邁向更成熟的屬靈生命。
Three marks. Spiritually mature believers…三個標記。 屬靈成熟的信徒……
………………………………
1. Aim at pleasing the Lord (v.1-5)一、以討主喜悅為目標（1-5 節）
For Paul, all that matters is being faithful to his God-given calling. The only opinion he cares about is God’s.對保羅來說，唯一重要的，是忠於神所賜的呼召。 他唯一在乎的，是神的看法。
READ v.1-4. “This, then, is how you ought to regard us: as servants of Christ and as those entrusted with the mysteries God has revealed. Now it is required that those who have been given a trust must prove faithful. I care very little if I am judged by you or by any human court; indeed, I do not even judge myself. My conscience is clear, but that does not make me innocent. It is the Lord who judges me.” (v.1-4)請讀 1-4 節。 「人應當以我們為基督的執事，為神奧秘事的管家。所求於管家的，是要他有忠心。我被你們論斷，或被別人論斷，我都以為極小的事；連我自己也不論斷自己。我雖不覺得自己有錯，卻也不能因此得以稱義；但判斷我的乃是主。」（1-4 節）
Q. Who does a pastor work for?! (Normally) an employee of the church (it pays the wages!) But he doesn’t really work for the church. He works for God! That shift in thinking makes a world of difference.問：牧師到底是為誰工作？！ （一般來說）牧師是教會的僱員（由教會支薪！） 但他其實不是為教會工作， 而是為神工作！ 思想上的這個轉變，帶來天壤之別。
NB Paul is not a local church pastor, but he is a gospel worker. NB a servant and steward of Christ and his gospel. A calling he must be faithful to (v.2) – no matter what.留意：保羅不是地方教會的牧師， 但他是福音工人， 是基督和祂福音的執事與管家。 這是他必須忠心持守的呼召（2 節）——無論如何。
The priority must always be to preach, teach and proclaim ‘the faith’. Regardless! Even if no-one is impressed. No recognition. And results are modest or delayed.首要的事永遠必須是傳講、教導、宣揚「真道」。 無論如何！ 即使沒有人欣賞， 沒有人認同， 成果微小或遲遲未見。
It is hard for preachers, gospel workers and church leaders to stay on that path, because we want something to show for it. Bums on seats, people saved! It won’t always be there!對講道者、福音工人和教會領袖來說，要堅守這條路並不容易， 因為我們總想有些成績可以拿出來： 座無虛席、有人得救！ 但這些不一定常常出現！
It’s not that he doesn’t want to see results! He does, and he did! But that is not everything. He won’t cut corners to get there.這不是說保羅不想看見成果！ 他想，而且他也確實看見了！ 但成果不是一切。 他不會為了達到目標而走捷徑。
At the end of the day – whose judgement, assessment or opinion is worth anything? READ v.3-4. Whether applause or criticism (he got both), he won’t be swayed or distracted. His one aim is to please the Lord!說到底，誰的判斷、評估或意見才真正有分量？請讀 3-4 節。 無論是掌聲還是批評（他兩樣都受過），他都不會因此動搖或分心。 他唯一的目標，就是討主的喜悅！
That doesn’t mean he is not open to correction. Church leaders must remain humble and teachable.這並不表示他不接受糾正。 教會領袖必須常存謙卑、受教的心。
NB Apollos was a gospel worker in Corinth after Paul. Clearly gifted, impressive, learned. YET – in an incident in Acts 18: ‘explained to him the way of God more clearly’ (Acts 18:26) Not ‘six foot above contradiction’.留意：亞波羅是繼保羅之後在哥林多的福音工人。 他顯然有恩賜、出眾、有學問。 然而，在使徒行傳十八章的一件事上： 「將神的道給他講解更加詳細」（使徒行傳十八章 26 節） 他並非「高高在上、不容反駁」。
It means he is not a people-pleaser. Not doing it to gain applause or to protect himself from criticism. Nor does he need that. Laser-focused on Christ crucified as the only way of salvation. He only really cares about the Lord and the job he is doing!意思是：他不是討人喜歡的人。 他做這些不是為了贏得掌聲，也不是為了保護自己免受批評， 他也不需要這些。 他全神貫注於釘十字架的基督——唯一的救恩之路。 他真正在乎的，只有主和主交給他的工作！
What is he working for, or towards? Sweet words… ‘Well done, good and faithful servant.’ (Matthew 25:21)他在為甚麼而努力？朝向甚麼目標？ 那句甜美的話…… 「好，你這又良善又忠心的僕人。」（馬太福音二十五章 21 節）
READ v.5. “Therefore judge nothing before the appointed time; wait until the Lord comes. He will bring to light what is hidden in darkness and will expose the motives of the heart. At that time each will receive their praise from God.” (v.5) Those words are sweeter and more precious than any number of awards, mentions or platforms.請讀 5 節。 「所以，時候未到，甚麼都不要論斷，只等主來，他要照出暗中的隱情，顯明人心的意念。那時，各人要從神那裏得着稱讚。」（5 節） 這句話比任何獎項、表揚或舞台都更甜美、更寶貴。
A lesson for pastors, gospel workers and church leaders: Stick to believing and proclaiming the gospel and Bible truth, even if… Even if the world is laughing or outraged. Even if the congregation is disappointed or unhappy.給牧師、福音工人和教會領袖的功課： 要堅持相信並宣講福音和聖經真理，即使…… 即使世界在嘲笑或憤怒， 即使會眾失望或不滿。
It is not easy to stay on that path, because we are human. Pray! And remember – our new pastor is working for God! Encourage him in it.要堅守這條路並不容易， 因為我們都是人。 要禱告！ 也要記得：我們的新牧師是為神工作的！ 要在這事上鼓勵他。
For all believers… In your life and situation, inside and outside church – are you living and serving for his praise? Or for that of others? Which matters more?對所有信徒而言…… 在你的生活和處境中，無論在教會內外，你是為了主的稱讚而生活、事奉嗎？ 還是為了別人的稱讚？ 哪一個對你更重要？
The applause of the world – including those who don’t know you or care about you! Or The “well done” of the Father – who loves you.世界的掌聲——包括那些根本不認識你、也不在乎你的人！ 還是 天父的那句「好」——祂是愛你的。
Be liberated from people-pleasing. Aim at pleasing the Lord. Know that he is proud of you!要從討好人的捆綁中得釋放。 要以討主喜悅為目標。 要知道，祂以你為榮！
………………………………
2. Reject worldly boasting (v.6-13)二、拒絕屬世的誇口（6-13 節）
It is natural for humans to take pride in achievement. Ill. A pastor friend… The only people who ask what class of degree you got are those who got firsts!人天生就會以成就為傲。 例子：一位牧師朋友說…… 唯一會問你拿了甚麼等級學位的人，就是那些拿了一級榮譽的人！
We don’t tend to look up to or envy those who are left behind or at the bottom of the pile. (More likely we are glad we’ve done better!) Those we look up to and envy… Great success, won awards, big bank accounts, millions of followers. [The reason why so many Gen Z want to become influencers] That’s the way the world works!我們不太會仰慕或羨慕那些落後的、排在最底層的人。 （反而更可能慶幸自己做得比他們好！） 我們仰慕和羨慕的是…… 大有成就的、得過獎的、銀行存款豐厚的、擁有數以百萬計追隨者的人。 〔這正是為何那麼多 Z 世代都想成為網紅〕 世界就是這樣運作的！
But when we bring all of that into church, it is a denial of who we are and what we are about. Why? Two reasons…但當我們把這一切帶進教會，就是否定了我們的身份，也否定了我們存在的意義。 為甚麼？ 有兩個原因……
(1) Whatever we are or have is a gift (v.6-7)（一）我們的所是所有，都是恩賜（6-7 節）
READ v.6-7. “Now, brothers and sisters, I have applied these things to myself and Apollos for your benefit, so that you may learn from us the meaning of the saying, ‘Do not go beyond what is written.’ Then you will not be puffed up in being a follower of one of us over against the other. For who makes you different from anyone else? What do you have that you did not receive? And if you did receive it, why do you boast as though you did not?” (v.6-7)請讀 6-7 節。 「弟兄們，我為你們的緣故，拿這些事轉比自己和亞波羅，叫你們效法我們不可過於聖經所記，免得你們自高自大，貴重這個，輕看那個。使你與人不同的是誰呢？你有甚麼不是領受的呢？若是領受的，為何自誇，彷彿不是領受的呢？」（6-7 節）
It is not clear what ‘Do not go beyond what is written’ means. But the main concern IS clear… Worldly arrogance (v.7). They think what they are and have gives them some grounds for boasting! No!「不可過於聖經所記」是甚麼意思，並不清楚。 但保羅主要關注的卻是清楚的： 就是屬世的驕傲（7 節）。 他們以為自己的所是所有，給了他們誇口的理由！ 不！
Anything you have or are is a gift from God. That goes for everyone! (Even those who are not Christians!) e.g. Generally – sport, music, leadership, languages, maths, etc. Spiritually! The Corinthians were boasting about certain gifts! E.g. tongues, prophecy. But gifts are gifts!!!!你所擁有的、你所是的，全都是神的恩賜。 人人都是如此！ （連不是基督徒的人也一樣！） 例如： 一般方面——體育、音樂、領導、語言、數學等等。 屬靈方面也是！ 哥林多人在誇耀某些恩賜！ 例如方言、先知講道。 但恩賜始終是恩賜！！！！
But worldly boasting is not just wrong because everything is a gift. Also…屬世的誇口之所以錯，不單因為一切都是恩賜， 還因為……
(2) Using the wrong measures (v.8-13)（二）用錯了量度的標準（8-13 節）
(Remember) their dividing up over different leaders was because they wanted to line up behind the most “impressive”. The ones who looked and sounded the part. Proud to introduce their friends to them. Might get some reflected glory from them! All that is just worldly immaturity.（記得）他們因不同的領袖而分黨，是因為想站在最「出眾」的人背後。 那些看起來、聽起來都很像樣的人。 他們以把朋友介紹給這些人為榮， 或許還可以沾一點光！ 這一切，不過是屬世的幼稚。
v.8-13 is heavy sarcasm, to embarrass them into changing course. “Faithful” sometimes looks like losing!8-13 節充滿強烈的諷刺， 要叫他們感到羞愧而改變方向。 「忠心」有時看起來就像是失敗！
READ v.8-9. “Already you have all you want! Already you have become rich! You have begun to reign—and that without us! How I wish that you really had begun to reign so that we also might reign with you! For it seems to me that God has put us apostles on display at the end of the procession, like those condemned to die in the arena. We have been made a spectacle to the whole universe, to angels as well as to human beings.” (v.8-9)請讀 8-9 節。 「你們已經飽足了，已經豐富了，不要我們，自己就作王了。我願意你們果真作王，叫我們也得與你們一同作王。我想神把我們使徒明明列在末後，好像定死罪的囚犯；因為我們成了一臺戲，給世人和天使觀看。」（8-9 節）
Ill. In the Roman world, games in the arena were the highlight of public holidays. Excited crowds packed in, waiting for entertainment! The games begin with a procession into the stadium. VIPs at the front, ready to take their seats in the royal box. (Paul says) you won’t find the apostles among them. You will find them with the chained criminals dragged in at the rear. NB the ones who provide the grand finale. Either forced to fight armed gladiators or fed to the lions.例子：在羅馬世界，競技場上的比賽是公眾假期的高潮。 興奮的群眾蜂擁而至，等待娛樂上演！ 比賽以進場的巡遊揭開序幕。 貴賓走在最前頭，準備在皇家包廂就座。 （保羅說）你不會在他們當中找到使徒。 你會在隊伍最後、被拖着進場的戴鎖鏈罪犯當中找到他們。 留意：就是那些負責壓軸的人—— 要麼被迫與持械的角鬥士搏鬥，要麼被丟去餵獅子。
For Paul and the apostles, ‘faithful’ looked like losing! READ v.10-13. “We are fools for Christ, but you are so wise in Christ! We are weak, but you are strong! You are honoured, we are dishonoured! To this very hour we go hungry and thirsty, we are in rags, we are brutally treated, we are homeless. We work hard with our own hands. When we are cursed, we bless; when we are persecuted, we endure it; when we are slandered, we answer kindly. We have become the scum of the earth, the garbage of the world—right up to this moment.” (v.10-13)對保羅和眾使徒來說，「忠心」看起來就像是失敗！ 請讀 10-13 節。 「我們為基督的緣故算是愚拙的，你們在基督裏倒是聰明的；我們軟弱，你們倒強壯；你們有榮耀，我們倒被藐視。直到如今，我們還是又飢又渴，又赤身露體，又挨打，又沒有一定的住處，並且勞苦，親手做工。被人咒罵，我們就祝福；被人逼迫，我們就忍受；被人毀謗，我們就善勸。直到如今，人還把我們看作世界上的污穢，萬物中的渣滓。」（10-13 節）
If you wanted to go up in the world (if your priorities were status, honour and awards), you wouldn’t hang around with him! Ill. Scum (v.13). Like the stuff you pull out of a blocked drain.如果你想在世上往上爬（如果你看重的是地位、名譽和獎項），你就不會和他走在一起！ 例子：「渣滓」（13 節）。 就像從淤塞的渠道裏挖出來的東西。
Lesson? Reject worldly boasting! Leaders blessed and used by God sometimes look like this. Many never impress – in the usual way.功課是甚麼？ 拒絕屬世的誇口！ 蒙神賜福、被神使用的領袖，有時就是這個樣子。 許多人從來不會以一般的方式令人刮目相看。
Ill. Vaughan Roberts (Rector of St Ebbe’s Church): ‘Of course natural abilities are given by God and can be used by him, but they are far less important than obedience to Christ’s commission. It is much better to sit under the ministry of a faithful plodder with average gifts, who is diligently living and teaching the true message of the Bible, than to attend a church led by a ten-talent star who relies on his own brilliance rather than on the power of God’s Word. Very few pastors have exceptional abilities. Most of those God uses are ordinary people entrusted with an extraordinary gospel. Their task is simply to do their best with the personalities and gifts they have been given; God will do the rest.’ (p.53 of ‘True Spirituality’)例子：羅偉恩（Vaughan Roberts，牛津 St Ebbe’s 教會主任牧師）： 「天然的才能當然是神所賜的，也可以被神使用，但它們遠不及順服基督的大使命重要。與其去一間由一位倚靠自己才華、而非倚靠神話語大能的『十千銀子』明星所帶領的教會，倒不如坐在一位恩賜平平、卻忠心踏實、殷勤活出並教導聖經真理的牧者之下受教，這要好得多。極少牧師擁有非凡的才能。神所使用的人，大多是被託付了非凡福音的平凡人。他們的任務，只是盡力運用神所賜給他們的性格和恩賜；其餘的，神自會成就。」（《True Spirituality》第 53 頁）
Remember that – with our new pastor! And reject worldly boasting!對我們的新牧師，也要記得這一點！ 並且要拒絕屬世的誇口！
………………………………
3. Imitate the right people (v.14-21)三、效法對的人（14-21 節）
In all this they were imitating the city. Stop! Imitate me!在這一切事上，他們都在效法這座城市。 停下來吧！ 要效法我！
READ v.14-17. “I am writing this not to shame you but to warn you as my dear children. Even if you had ten thousand guardians in Christ, you do not have many fathers, for in Christ Jesus I became your father through the gospel. Therefore I urge you to imitate me. For this reason I have sent to you Timothy, my son whom I love, who is faithful in the Lord. He will remind you of my way of life in Christ Jesus, which agrees with what I teach everywhere in every church.” (v.14-17)請讀 14-17 節。 「我寫這話，不是叫你們羞愧，乃是警戒你們，好像我所親愛的兒女一樣。你們學基督的，師傅雖有一萬，為父的卻是不多，因我在基督耶穌裏用福音生了你們。所以，我求你們效法我。因此我已打發提摩太到你們那裏去。他在主裏面，是我所親愛有忠心的兒子。他必提醒你們，記念我在基督裏怎樣行事，在各處各教會中怎樣教導人。」（14-17 節）
He is not big-headed. Nor is he saying “follow me no matter what!” READ 11:1. “Follow my example, as I follow the example of Christ.” (11:1) ‘When I imitate Jesus – imitate me.’ That is what he is doing here!他並不是自大， 也不是說「無論如何都要跟從我！」 請讀十一章 1 節。 「你們該效法我，像我效法基督一樣。」（十一章 1 節） 意思是：「當我效法耶穌的時候，就效法我。」 這正是他在這裏所做的！
Not just the message – the way he lived! Look back to v.8-13… Ill-treated Homeless Cursed – all of that is Jesus!不單是他所傳的信息，還有他生活的方式！ 回看 8-13 節…… 被苦待、 無家可歸、 被咒罵——這一切都是耶穌的樣式！
‘The Son of Man has nowhere to lay his head’ (Matthew 8:20) ‘Oppressed and afflicted’ (Isaiah 53:7) ‘Insulted and threatened’ (1 Peter 2:23)「人子卻沒有枕頭的地方」（馬太福音八章 20 節） 「他被欺壓，在受苦的時候卻不開口」（以賽亞書五十三章 7 節） 「他被罵不還口；受害不說威嚇的話」（彼得前書二章 23 節）
This is a ‘Christ crucified’-shaped life! It always has been and always will be!這就是以「釘十字架的基督」為模樣的生命！ 從來如此，將來也如此！

成熟的標記

📖Summary 摘要

English

The Corinthians thought of themselves as the gold standard of spirituality, proud of their knowledge, gifts and standing. Paul, writing with grief and love, closes the first big section of the letter by showing them what real spiritual maturity looks like. Worldliness — an obsession with power, influence, achievement and status — was driving their divisions, and it is out of step with the gospel of Christ crucified.

1. Aim at pleasing the Lord (v.1-5). Gospel workers are servants and stewards of Christ, and what is required of a steward is faithfulness. Paul is not swayed by applause or criticism; the Lord is his judge, and the "well done" of God is worth more than any award.
2. Reject worldly boasting (v.6-13). Everything we are and have is a gift, so there is no ground for boasting. And the world's measures of success are the wrong measures: for the apostles, "faithful" looked like losing — like condemned prisoners at the end of the arena procession, treated as the scum of the earth.
3. Imitate the right people (v.14-21). As a loving father, Paul urges them to imitate him as he imitates Christ. A "Christ crucified"-shaped life is the pattern; we should imitate faithful people who persevere in obscurity, not celebrities and platforms.
中文

哥林多人自以為是屬靈的黃金標準，以自己的知識、恩賜和地位為傲。保羅懷着憂傷與愛寫信，在這卷書第一大段的結尾，向他們展示真正的屬靈成熟是甚麼樣子。屬世的心態——迷戀權力、影響力、成就和地位——正是導致他們分裂的原因，而這與釘十字架的基督的福音格格不入。

一、以討主喜悅為目標（1-5 節）。福音工人是基督的執事、神奧秘事的管家；所求於管家的，是要他有忠心。保羅不因掌聲或批評而動搖；判斷他的乃是主，而神的一句「好」，勝過任何獎項。
二、拒絕屬世的誇口（6-13 節）。我們的所是所有都是領受的恩賜，所以沒有誇口的理由。而且，世界衡量成功的標準是錯誤的標準：對使徒來說，「忠心」看起來就像失敗——好像在競技場巡遊隊伍末後的死囚，被人看作萬物中的渣滓。
三、效法對的人（14-21 節）。保羅以慈父的身份，勸他們效法他，像他效法基督一樣。以「釘十字架的基督」為模樣的生命才是榜樣；我們應當效法那些默默堅持的忠心之人，而不是名人和舞台。

💡Key Themes 主要主題

English
Faithfulness over results. A steward is measured by faithfulness, not by numbers, recognition or visible success. Results matter, but corners are never cut to reach them.
An audience of One. Neither the praise nor the criticism of people — nor even our own self-assessment — is the final verdict. The Lord judges, and his praise is what counts.
Humble and teachable. Not being a people-pleaser does not mean being beyond correction. Even gifted Apollos received correction gladly (Acts 18:26).
Everything is a gift. Natural abilities and spiritual gifts alike are received, not achieved, which removes every ground for boasting.
A cross-shaped life. Faithfulness often looks like weakness and loss, because it follows the pattern of Christ crucified — and that is the pattern worth imitating.
中文

只向一位觀眾交賬。人的稱讚或批評，甚至我們對自己的評價，都不是最終的判決。判斷我們的乃是主，祂的稱讚才算數。

十字架模樣的生命。忠心往往看起來像軟弱和失敗，因為它跟隨釘十字架基督的樣式——這正是值得效法的樣式。
