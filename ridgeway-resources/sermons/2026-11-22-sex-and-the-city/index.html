Sex and the city

性與城市

1 Corinthians 6:12-20 哥林多前書 6:12-20 · Bible Study Guide 聖經學習指南
"You are not your own; you were bought at a price. Therefore honour God with your bodies." · 「你們不是自己的人；因為你們是重價買來的。所以，要在你們的身子上榮耀神。」(1 Corinthians 哥林多前書 6:19-20)
📖 Summary 摘要
English

Our world both over-rates and under-rates sex: it is treated as almost the meaning of life, and at the same time as just another leisure activity. First-century Corinth was even more extreme. Some believers there had absorbed the culture and even justified it theologically with a slogan: "I have the right to do anything."


Paul does not answer lawlessness with a list of laws. He answers with gospel wisdom: why would you do something that does you no good and has the power to master you? Sin always hides the price-tag. Above all, why would you, given who you are? His big message is v.20b: "Therefore honour God with your bodies."


He gives three reasons rooted in our identity. Our bodies are destined for glory: God will raise them, so they are not disposable shells. Our bodies are united to Christ: sex is never "just a body thing", and to use our bodies immorally is to be unfaithful to him. Our bodies are owned by the Spirit: we are his temple, bought at the price of Christ's blood. So the only course of action that makes sense is to flee sexual immorality. We flee not because it is unforgivable, but because we belong to him.

繁體中文

我們的世界對性既高估又低估：一方面把性視為幾乎是人生的意義，另一方面又把它當作一種普通的消遣。第一世紀的哥林多更加極端。那裡有些信徒被文化同化，甚至用一句口號為此提供神學理由：「凡事我都可行。」


保羅並沒有用一連串律法去對付這種無法無天，而是用福音的智慧回應：為何要做一件對你毫無益處、又能轄制你的事？罪總是把價錢牌藏起來。更重要的是：既然你是這樣的人，為何還要這樣做？他的核心信息在第 20 節下半節：「所以，要在你們的身子上榮耀神。」


他根據我們的身分提出三個理由。我們的身子注定得榮耀：神要叫它復活，所以它不是用完即棄的外殼。我們的身子與基督聯合：性永遠不「只是身體」的事，把身子用於淫亂就是對祂不忠。我們的身子屬於聖靈：我們是祂的殿，是用基督的寶血重價買來的。因此，唯一合理的行動就是逃避淫行。我們逃避，不是因為這罪不可饒恕，而是因為我們屬於祂。

💡 Key Themes 主要主題
1. Freedom is not licence 自由不是放縱
English
The Corinthians twisted grace into "I can do anything." Paul answers not with more rules but with wisdom: is it beneficial, and will it master me? The freedom Christ won is freedom from sin's control, not freedom to sin. Sin always hides the price-tag.
繁體中文
哥林多人把恩典扭曲為「凡事我都可行」。保羅沒有用更多規條回應，而是用智慧提問：這事有益處嗎？它會轄制我嗎？基督為我們贏得的自由，是脫離罪的轄制，而不是犯罪的自由。罪總是把價錢牌藏起來。
2. The body is destined for glory 身子注定得榮耀
English
Against the Greek idea that only the soul is the "real me", Paul insists the body matters because God will raise it. Like gloves made to last rather than disposable ones, our bodies are to be looked after and used well. They are precious and not to be given away cheaply.

3. United to Christ, body and spirit 身體與靈都與基督聯合
English
Each believer is joined to Christ in a marriage-like union, and our bodies are members of his body. Sex makes and breaks bonds: it is never "just sex". Sexual sin is therefore a kind of unfaithfulness to the Lord, who will never be unfaithful to us.

4. Owned by the Spirit, bought at a price 屬於聖靈，重價買來
English
God's presence once dwelt in Eden, the tabernacle and the temple. Now he makes his home in us. Like the flag raised on Iwo Jima, our lives have changed ownership, and at great cost: the precious blood of Christ. We have been redeemed from sin, self and Satan into the care of a gracious master.

5. Flee — the wise response 逃跑才是智慧的回應
English
Paul's command is to flee: not to tinker, tamper or tickle, but to run. It is neither clever nor courageous to put your head in the lion's mouth. In practice this means putting distance between ourselves and temptation. We flee not because sexual sin is unforgivable, but because of who we are.
