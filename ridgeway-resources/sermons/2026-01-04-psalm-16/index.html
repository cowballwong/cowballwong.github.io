Psalm 16 — The LORD our refuge Easter Day, 5th April 2026 詩篇十六篇 — 耶和華是我們的避難所 2026年4月5日 復活節主日
………………………………
Big idea: Those who have made the LORD, their Lord are in a good and safe place now — because He is at their right hand — and will be then — because they will be at His right hand! 核心信息：凡以耶和華為主的人，如今就在美好安穩之處——因為他在他們的右邊；將來也必如此——因為他們將在他的右邊！
………………………………
Days of great insecurity. Hence need for "safe" places/spaces. Reasons? Very few "jobs for life" anymore. Family breakdown = endemic. Social media makes it easier to get to us. Far more competing belief systems in small space. Feel very unsafe/vulnerable. 這是一個極度缺乏安全感的時代。 因此我們需要「安全的」地方和空間。 原因何在？ 「終身職業」已所剩無幾。 家庭破裂已成為普遍現象。 社交媒體讓別人更容易侵入我們的生活。 在狹小的空間裏充斥着更多互相競爭的信仰體系。 我們感到非常不安全、非常脆弱。
But one other? Loss of belief in "God as refuge". If don't have God as… refuge safe place firm foundation — then what is there that is unshakably solid? Literally "all at sea"! Because everything else moves. 但還有另一個原因呢？ 就是失去了「上帝是避難所」的信念。 如果沒有上帝作為…… 避難所 安全之處 堅固的根基——那麼還有甚麼是不可動搖的呢？ 真的是「茫然無所依」！ 因為其他一切都在變動。
………………………………
Illustration: January 1995 — huge earthquake beneath Japanese city of Kobe. Measured 7.2 on Richter Scale. Caused catastrophic destruction. Loss of more than 5000 lives. Terrifying film footage on YouTube. Get a taste of what it felt like at the London Science Museum. Simulator. Stand on it in a mocked-up shop. Lasts about 1 minute — horrible! What makes earthquakes so scary = nothing solid to hold onto. Because everything = moving, even the ground. 例子：1995年1月——日本神戶市地底發生大地震，里氏震級達7.2級。造成災難性的破壞，超過五千人喪生。YouTube上有令人驚恐的影片片段。 在倫敦科學博物館可以體驗那種感覺。那裏有一個模擬器，站在一個模擬商店裏。 大約持續一分鐘——非常可怕！地震之所以如此恐怖，是因為沒有任何堅固的東西可以抓住。 因為一切都在搖動，連地面也是。
A bit like life without God? Nothing immovable/solid. Everything = on the move! 這是否有點像沒有上帝的人生？ 沒有任何不可動搖、堅固的東西。 一切都在變動！
………………………………
King David — "did life" a better way. Psalm 16! Had Lord God as his refuge/safe place. So whether in life or in death = right where he needs to be! 大衛王——以更好的方式「過人生」。 詩篇十六篇！ 以主上帝為他的避難所和安全之處。 所以無論在生在死，他都在他應該在的地方！
A prayer — "Keep me safe, my God, for in you I take refuge." (Psalm 16:1) But no drama or crisis. Just calm — that wherever he is, his God is. Therefore he is secure. 一個禱告——「神啊，求你保佑我，因為我投靠你。」（詩篇16:1） 沒有戲劇性的場面，也沒有危機。 只有平靜——無論他在哪裏，他的上帝就在那裏。 因此他是安穩的。
………………………………
This psalm turns on two "right hand" references (v8, v11). "Right hand" — means slightly different things in different contexts. Strength. Honour. Always = "right there with" as one. "Right hand man/woman." Both cases = David being "right there" with the Lord. But there is a difference as well… V8 = Lord at David's right hand. V11 = David at Lord's right hand. 這篇詩篇的轉折點在於兩處提及「右邊」（第8節、第11節）。 「右邊」——在不同語境中有略為不同的含義。 力量。 尊榮。 總是表示「緊密同在」、合而為一。 「得力助手」。 兩處都表示大衛與主「同在」。 但兩者之間也有分別…… 第8節＝主在大衛的右邊。 第11節＝大衛在主的右邊。
Why the difference? First = about life before death (v8). Second = about life after death (v11). In life — the Lord is at David's right hand (v8). In death — David is at his right hand (v11). 為甚麼有這個分別？ 第一處是關於死前的生命（第8節）。 第二處是關於死後的生命（第11節）。 在生——主在大衛的右邊（第8節）。 在死——大衛在主的右邊（第11節）。
………………………………
Where are we at this Easter? If we have made "the LORD, our Lord". If our eyes are fixed on him. If he = our prayer and song… If we are "in Christ" — It is! And we are in a really good place today! 這個復活節，我們在哪裏？ 如果我們已經以「耶和華為我們的主」。 如果我們的眼目定睛在他身上。 如果他是我們的禱告和詩歌…… 如果我們是「在基督裏」—— 確實如此！ 今天我們就在一個非常美好的地方！
………………………………
1. The LORD our refuge in life 一、耶和華是我們生命中的避難所
"I say to the LORD, 'You are my Lord; apart from you I have no good thing.'" (Psalm 16:2) "LORD, you alone are my portion and my cup; you make my lot secure. The boundary lines have fallen for me in pleasant places; surely I have a delightful inheritance. I will praise the LORD, who counsels me; even at night my heart instructs me. I keep my eyes always on the LORD. With him at my right hand, I shall not be shaken." (Psalm 16:5–8) 「我的心哪，你曾對耶和華說：你是我的主，我的好處不在你以外。」（詩篇16:2） 「耶和華是我的產業，是我杯中的分；我所得的，你為我持守。用繩量給我的地界，坐落在佳美之處；我的產業實在美好。我必稱頌那指教我的耶和華，我的心腸在夜間也警戒我。我將耶和華常擺在我面前，因他在我右邊，我便不至搖動。」（詩篇16:5-8）
………………………………
Is life "good"? Probably — some good and some not good. Good…? Kids doing well at school. Get on with neighbours. etc. Not good…? Health's not great. Money's not going as far as it used to! etc. Mixed bag! 生活「好」嗎？ 大概——有些好，有些不好。 好的方面……？ 孩子在學校表現不錯。 跟鄰居相處融洽。 等等。 不好的方面……？ 健康狀況不太理想。 錢不像以前那麼耐用了！ 等等。 好壞參半！
But David = "It's all good!" (v2) Why? Not because… Always in great health. Plenty of money in the bank. Kids top of the class. But because — the Lord is his refuge. 但大衛說：「一切都好！」（第2節） 為甚麼？ 不是因為…… 身體一直健康。 銀行裏有大量存款。 孩子名列前茅。 而是因為——主是他的避難所。
………………………………
Discontentment = huge issue! Always something else we need. One more thing to be content… Friendship. Holiday. Grade. Promotion. That's a lie! Then = one more thing! But = second nature to many. 不知足是一個巨大的問題！ 我們總覺得還需要別的東西。 再多一樣就會滿足了…… 友誼。 假期。 成績。 升遷。 那是謊言！ 之後又會是「再多一樣」！ 但這已成為許多人的第二天性。
Why? Lack of contentment = two basic roots… Sometimes caused by pure greed. Sometimes caused by insecurity. Need to surround myself with things = make me secure. David: "I don't need that. I've got the Lord." What about you? 為甚麼？ 缺乏滿足有兩個基本根源…… 有時是出於純粹的貪婪。 有時是出於不安全感。 需要用許多東西圍繞自己來獲得安全感。 大衛說：「我不需要那些。我有主。」 你呢？
………………………………
The language of verses 5–6… Portion/cup/lot/boundaries/inheritance = all "land" language. Echoes of distributing the Promised Land. An important source of security for the people. But David = speaking of the Lord! HE is David's portion/cup/lot/inheritance! Hence why he can say = "all good"! 第5至6節的用語…… 產業、杯中的分、地界、基業——全都是「土地」的語言。 呼應了分配應許之地的歷史。 那是百姓安全感的重要來源。 但大衛所說的是主！ 主才是大衛的產業、杯中的分、基業！ 這就是為甚麼他能說「一切都好」！
Doesn't really matter what is going on — in that sense. Sometimes = loads to give thanks for. Other times = loads of challenges. But either way he knows the Lord = right there with him, so in a safe/secure place. "I keep my eyes always on the LORD. With him at my right hand, I shall not be shaken." (Psalm 16:8) 從這個角度來看，外在環境如何其實並不重要。 有時候有許多值得感恩的事。 有時候則有許多挑戰。 但無論如何，他知道主就在他身旁，所以他在安全穩妥之處。 「我將耶和華常擺在我面前，因他在我右邊，我便不至搖動。」（詩篇16:8）
………………………………
The wonder and beauty of the gospel = the Lord doesn't just give us "things". He gives us himself! 福音的奇妙與美麗之處，在於主不只是賜給我們「東西」。 他把自己賜給了我們！
Every Christmas we are reminded that Jesus is "Immanuel", "God with us." "…and they shall call his name Immanuel, which means, God with us." (Matthew 1:23) For those 30 years = he was uniquely and bodily present! For the whole world to see. But = no less present with those who are his today. In the person of his Spirit. Actually means = closer now than he was then! (Better to be us!) 每逢聖誕節，我們都會想起耶穌就是「以馬內利」，就是「上帝與我們同在」。「……人要稱他的名為以馬內利。以馬內利繙出來就是『神與我們同在』。」（馬太福音1:23） 在那三十年間，他以獨特的、有形有體的方式同在！ 全世界都能看見。 但他今天與屬他之人的同在，絲毫不減。 藉着他的聖靈。 實際上這意味着——他如今比當時更加親近！ （作今天的我們其實更好！）
So — don't be shaken! By the tremors of life. If you are "in Christ" = he is at your "right hand" too! You = in a safe and secure place right now! 所以——不要被動搖！ 不要被生命中的震動所搖撼。 如果你是「在基督裏」的，他也在你的「右邊」！ 你現在就在一個安全穩妥的地方！
………………………………
2. The LORD our refuge in death 二、耶和華是我們在死亡中的避難所
"Therefore my heart is glad and my tongue rejoices; my body also will rest secure, because you will not abandon me to the realm of the dead, nor will you let your faithful one see decay. You make known to me the path of life; you will fill me with joy in your presence, with eternal pleasures at your right hand." (Psalm 16:9–11) 「因此我的心歡喜，我的靈快樂，我的肉身也要安然居住。因為你必不將我的靈魂撇在陰間，也不叫你的聖者見朽壞。你必將生命的道路指示我。在你面前有滿足的喜樂，在你右手中有永遠的福樂。」（詩篇16:9-11）
………………………………
"Therefore" i.e. because THAT, THIS. Because God is "with him" now; he knows he will be "with God" then. He knows that — even the grave… Cannot shake him. Can't pull them apart. Can't drive a wedge. On the contrary — things can only get better! From "my right hand" (v8) to "your right hand" (v11)! 「因此」 也就是說，因為有前面那些，才有後面這些。 因為上帝現在「與他同在」；他知道將來他也會「與上帝同在」。 他知道——即使是墳墓…… 也不能動搖他。 不能把他們分開。 不能在他們之間插入楔子。 相反——事情只會越來越好！ 從「我的右邊」（第8節）到「你的右手」（第11節）！
………………………………
True that Old Testament believers (in the God of Israel) = less clarity about the afterlife. Less clarity than us. [We'll come back to that.] But some say they had no concept or interest in "life after death". That it was all about this life. But = very hard to believe… 的確，舊約時代的信徒（信靠以色列的上帝的人）對來生的認識不如我們清楚。 不如我們清晰。（這一點我們稍後再談。） 但有些人認為他們對「死後的生命」完全沒有概念或興趣。 一切只關乎今生。 但這很難令人信服……
1. Seems highly unlikely. If every other ancient culture (e.g. Egyptians) needed "life after death" — would be very surprising if they didn't! 2. A few Old Testament references — seem to point there. And two or three seem to go even further. And anticipate bodily "resurrection": "Multitudes who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake: some to everlasting life, others to shame and everlasting contempt." (Daniel 12:2) "Your dead shall live; their bodies shall rise. You who dwell in the dust, awake and sing for joy!" (Isaiah 26:19) 一、這似乎極不可能。 如果所有其他古代文化（例如埃及人）都需要「死後的生命」——他們沒有的話才真令人意外！ 二、舊約中有一些經文似乎指向這個方向。 其中有兩三處甚至走得更遠。 預示了身體的「復活」： 「睡在塵埃中的，必有多人復醒。其中有得永生的，有受羞辱永遠被憎惡的。」（但以理書12:2） 「死人要復活，屍首要興起。睡在塵埃的啊，要醒起歌唱！」（以賽亞書26:19）
………………………………
The most natural reading of Psalm 16 — His body = not being abandoned to the grave! (v10–11) He can't have known much about the details… e.g. What kind of body? What will we be doing most of the time? Who will be there? Just details! The main thing is — he is going to be near God. From whom comes every "good thing" (v2). That means — there must be an even greater joy to come! (v11) 詩篇十六篇最自然的解讀—— 他的身體不會被撇棄在墳墓中！（第10至11節） 他不可能知道太多細節…… 例如 甚麼樣的身體？ 大部分時間在做甚麼？ 誰會在那裏？ 那些只是細節！ 最重要的是——他將與上帝同在。 一切「好處」都從上帝而來（第2節）。 這意味着——必有更大的喜樂將要來臨！（第11節）
So = not just "secure in God", and then abandoned to some other fate in some other place. But secure forever! 所以，不只是「在上帝裏面安穩」，然後被丟棄到另一個地方、面對另一種命運。 而是永遠安穩！
………………………………
Do you have faith in Christ? Then — sing this song! Nothing can separate us from the love of God! Not even the grave! 你有在基督裏的信心嗎？ 那麼——唱這首歌吧！ 沒有甚麼能使我們與上帝的愛隔絕！ 連墳墓也不能！
Not a Christian? Think "faith" is stupid? How do YOU handle… the storms of life and the dark mystery of death? 你還不是基督徒嗎？ 覺得「信仰」是愚蠢的？ 那麼你怎樣面對…… 人生的風暴 和死亡那幽暗的奧秘？
………………………………
How do we know it's not… "Pie-in-the-sky-till-we-die"? Naive optimism — to medicate the pain of life and the meaninglessness of death? 我們怎麼知道這不是…… 「畫餅充飢」？ 天真的樂觀主義——只是用來麻醉人生的痛苦和死亡的空虛？
Answer? Today! Easter Day! Psalm 16 — has already happened! 答案？ 就是今天！ 復活節！ 詩篇十六篇——已經應驗了！
………………………………
Acts 2. Pentecost Sunday. In verses 25–28, Peter says Psalm 16 = about Jesus and the resurrection! "Seeing what was to come, he spoke of the resurrection of the Messiah, that he was not abandoned to the realm of the dead, nor did his body see decay." (Acts 2:31) 使徒行傳第二章。 五旬節主日。 在第25至28節中，彼得說詩篇十六篇是指着耶穌和復活說的！ 「就預先看明這事，講論基督復活說：他的靈魂不撇在陰間；他的肉身也不見朽壞。」（使徒行傳2:31）
………………………………
It is about David. It is about us. But in the first place = about Jesus! It is in him and his experience that this has become concrete and real. Psalm 16 has already happened! 這篇詩篇是關於大衛的。 也是關於我們的。 但首先——是關於耶穌的！ 正是在他裏面、在他的經歷中，這一切才成為具體而真實的。 詩篇十六篇已經應驗了！
………………………………
Illustration: Prof Stephen Hawking = one of the giants of modern cosmology. Although as famous for having motor neurone disease. His funeral was at St Mary the Great Church, Cambridge. But his ashes = actually interred in Westminster Abbey, London. Alongside Sir Isaac Newton and a host of other famous figures from history — including several kings and queens. Westminster Abbey = millions of visitors annually. 例子：霍金教授（Prof Stephen Hawking）是現代宇宙學的巨人之一，同時也因患有運動神經元疾病而聞名。他的葬禮在劍橋的大聖瑪利教堂舉行。但他的骨灰實際上安放在倫敦的西敏寺。與牛頓爵士（Sir Isaac Newton）以及歷史上許多著名人物長眠在一起——包括數位國王和女王。西敏寺每年有數以百萬計的訪客。
Very surprising about Jesus — given his stature/importance — his tomb never became a tourist attraction or place of pilgrimage. Does that say something? Because he wasn't there! (Now — we can't even be sure where it was!) 但對於耶穌來說，有一件非常令人驚訝的事——鑑於他的地位和重要性——他的墳墓從來沒有成為旅遊景點或朝聖之地。 這是否說明了甚麼？ 因為他不在那裏！ （到了今天，我們甚至無法確定墳墓在哪裏！）
………………………………
So how is Psalm 16 about David and us? It is through this being about him that this also becomes about us. If "in him" / joined to him by faith — then this becomes our story too! "And if the Spirit of him who raised Jesus from the dead is living in you, he who raised Christ from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies because of his Spirit who lives in you." (Romans 8:11) 那麼，詩篇十六篇如何與大衛和我們有關呢？ 正是因為這首先是關於耶穌的，它才同時成為關於我們的。 如果我們是「在他裏面」的、藉着信心與他聯合——那麼這也成為我們的故事！ 「然而，叫耶穌從死裏復活者的靈若住在你們心裏，那叫基督耶穌從死裏復活的，也必藉着住在你們心裏的聖靈，使你們必死的身體又活過來。」（羅馬書8:11）
………………………………
If He is "with us" now; we will be "with him" then! No fingers crossed here! No hit and hope. If you are a believer in Jesus — be at peace. He loves you. He is with you. And one day — you'll be with him! 如果他現在「與我們同在」；我們將來就會「與他同在」！ 這裏沒有碰運氣！ 沒有心存僥倖。 如果你是一個相信耶穌的人——平安吧。 他愛你。 他與你同在。 而有一天——你將與他同在！
