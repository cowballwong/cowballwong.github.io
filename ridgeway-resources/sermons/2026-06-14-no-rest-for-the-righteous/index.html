Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
No rest for the righteous! 義人不得安歇！
(Hebrews 希伯來書 6:4-12)

Introduction: no reverse gear引言：沒有倒車檔
Big idea: salvation is for those who persevere in obedient faith to the end — not for those who have merely been part of a community and environment where God is at work.中心思想：救恩是給那些以順服的信心堅忍到底的人，而不是給那些僅僅置身於神工作的群體和環境中的人。
Summer is here, and many of us will be flying off. Picture the moment on a plane: boarded, doors closed, checks done, engines running. But before it heads for the runway, it is pushed back from the gate. Why? A plane has no reverse gear. It can only move forward.夏天到了，不少人都會飛去旅行。想像飛機上的一刻：登了機、艙門關上、檢查完畢、引擎已開動。但在滑行往跑道之前，它要先被車輛從閘口推後。為甚麼？因為飛機沒有倒車檔，只能向前行。
This morning’s truth: truly converted people have no reverse gear either. They keep moving forward. In that sense — there is no rest for the righteous!今早的信息：真正歸主的人同樣沒有倒車檔。他們只會繼續向前。從這個意義上說——義人不得安歇！
This passage is knotty for many reasons, yet its overall point is clear: real faith is persevering faith. If we abandon the journey of trust and obedience, nothing else counts. Whatever happened in the past becomes irrelevant — and none of us is the exception.這段經文有許多難解之處，但整體信息卻很清楚：真信心是堅忍到底的信心。我們若離棄這條「信靠與順服」的路，其他一切就都歸於徒然。過去發生過甚麼都不再算數——而我們沒有一個人是例外。
Hebrews is famous for its repeated warnings about the danger of falling away. This passage, with chapter 10, is the most blunt and forceful — so blunt that some tender consciences have been left anxious about their own salvation. But that is not its intention. It is a gift of love from God to keep us going, precisely so that we do not throw it all away.希伯來書以一再警告「離道反教」的危險而著稱。這段（連同第十章）是其中最直接、最有力的——直接得令一些良心敏感的信徒，為自己的得救而忐忑不安。但這並非作者的本意。這是神出於慈愛的恩賜，為要扶持我們繼續前行，正是要我們不致前功盡棄。
God does not want us to worry about the future — we have his promises for that. Nor are we to lean on the past — that is what this passage is for. So however long you have been a Christian, whatever your track record, this is for you. The most important day of your journey is not yesterday, but today and tomorrow. The passage gives us two things: a warning, and a lesson.神不要我們為將來憂慮——關乎將來，我們有祂的應許。我們也不可倚靠過去——這正是這段經文的用意。所以無論你信主多久、過往的記錄如何，這信息都是為你而設。你旅程中最重要的日子不是昨天，而是今天和明天。這段經文給我們兩樣東西：一個警告，和一個功課。

1. The warning: participation is not regeneration (v.4-8)一、警告：參與不等於重生（4-8節）
In other words: it is possible to be part of what God is doing — part of a church where he is at work — and even to receive something good yourself, yet not be truly converted, not born again.換句話說：一個人有可能置身於神的工作之中——在神動工的教會裡有份——甚至自己也得着了一些美好的東西，卻仍未真正歸主、未曾重生。
“Those who have once been enlightened, who have tasted the heavenly gift, who have shared in the Holy Spirit, who have tasted the goodness of the word of God and the powers of the age to come…” (v.4-5)「論到那些已經蒙了光照、嘗過天恩的滋味、又於聖靈有份、並嘗過神善道的滋味、覺悟來世權能的人……」（4-5節）
These five things can all be true of real Christians, yet on their own they prove nothing. ‘Enlightened’ heads the list and sums it up. But enlightenment can be merely external — like walking into a dark room and switching on the light. The light shines on you, but says nothing about what is inside.這五樣東西在真信徒身上固然真實，但單憑它們本身卻甚麼都證明不了。「蒙光照」位居首位，也是全部的總括。然而「光照」可以只是外在的——就像走進漆黑的房間、把燈一開：光照在你身上，卻絲毫說不出你裏面的光景。
To taste the heavenly gift is to have heard the word of God (2:3); to share in the Holy Spirit is to have experienced something of the powers of the coming age — the signs, wonders and gifts of 2:4. This is what they were caught up in. But one can have all of that and still not be regenerate.「嘗過天恩」就是聽過神的道（2章3節）；「於聖靈有份」就是經歷過來世權能的一些彰顯——即2章4節的神蹟、奇事和恩賜。這就是他們所投身其中的。但一個人縱然擁有這一切，仍可能未曾重生。
Think of the football World Cup — five weeks, 104 games, 48 teams for the first time. Only eleven start each match, but every team has a squad of 23 to 26. Whichever side lifts the trophy, every squad member gets a winner’s medal, even those who never play in the final.想想世界盃足球賽——五個多星期、一百零四場比賽、首次有四十八支球隊。每場只有十一人正選上陣，但每隊都有二十三至二十六人的大軍。無論哪一隊捧盃，每位隊員都會得到冠軍獎牌，即使在決賽中從未上過場。
But the kingdom of God does not work that way. If we are never on the pitch, the medal will not be ours. Those who are not truly converted may ‘start’, but they will not finish. At some point along the way they fall away. That is apostasy — and it will be terminal.但神的國卻不是這樣運作。我們若從未踏足球場，那獎牌就不屬於我們。那些並非真正歸主的人或會「起步」，卻不會「跑到終點」。在路途上的某一刻，他們會離棄真道。這就是「離道反教」——而且是致命的。
“It is impossible for those who have fallen away to be brought back to repentance.” (v.4-6)「……若是離棄道理，就不能叫他們從新懊悔了。」（4-6節）
Why is further repentance ‘impossible’? There are two main readings. First, that this apostasy is terminal like the unforgivable sin of Mark 3:28-30 — a hardening of heart so complete that nothing can get through. That may be right; but then what do we make of those who do return later?為甚麼「不能再懊悔」？主要有兩種解釋。第一，這種離道反教是致命的，如同馬可福音3章28-30節所說不可赦免的罪——心硬到一個地步，再無甚麼能打動他們。這或許說得對；但若是這樣，那些日後確實回轉的人，我們又該如何理解？
More likely is a second reading. These were Jewish believers tempted to go back to what their faith looked like before Jesus — the Old Covenant of temple, priests and sacrifices — and to be made right with God that way again. But that is no longer possible, because Jesus has come. You cannot ‘renew’ your repentance by that route; you cannot go back. Either way, such apostasy is a suicidal move.更可能的是第二種解釋。這些是猶太裔的信徒，受試探想回到信耶穌以先的信仰模樣——即聖殿、祭司、獻祭那一套舊約制度——再藉此與神和好。但這如今已不可能，因為耶穌已經來了。你不能再循那條路「重新懊悔」，也不能走回頭路。無論採哪一種解釋，這樣的離道反教都是自取滅亡。
Land that drinks the rain and bears a useful crop receives God’s blessing; but land that yields thorns and thistles is worthless and near to being cursed. (v.7-8)就如一塊田地，吃過屢次下的雨水，生長菜蔬，合乎耕種的人用，就從神得福；若長荊棘和蒺藜，必被廢棄，近於咒詛。（7-8節）
Only real, life-changing repentance from sin and personal faith in Jesus — the kind that endures to the end — will count in the end.唯有真實、使生命改變的離罪悔改，以及對耶穌個人的信靠——那種堅忍到底的信心——到最後才算數。

2. The lesson: confidence is not complacency (v.9-12)二、功課：有信心不等於可鬆懈（9-12節）
The possibility of being self-deceived and falling away does not mean the writer wants his readers to live in fear, or that he lacks confidence in them. Quite the opposite.「可能自欺、可能離道」這個事實，並不表示作者要讀者活在懼怕中，也不表示他對他們沒有信心。恰恰相反。
“Though we speak like this, dear friends, we are convinced of better things in your case — things that have to do with salvation. God is not unjust; he will not forget your work and the love you have shown him.” (v.9-10)「親愛的弟兄們，我們雖是這樣說，卻深信你們的行為強過這些，而且近乎得救。因為神並非不公義，竟忘記你們所做的工和你們為他名所顯的愛心。」（9-10節）
He loves them and is greatly encouraged — full of hope. The blunt warning was meant to grab their attention, not to predict their doom. Why is he so confident their repentance and faith are real? Because he looks at this corner of the kingdom and sees good soil and a useful crop. The rain of God’s word has fallen, and wheat is growing.他愛他們，並大得鼓勵——滿懷盼望。那直接的警告是要喚起他們的注意，而不是斷定他們必然失喪。他為甚麼如此確信他們的悔改與信心是真的？因為他看着神國的這一角，看見好土、看見有用的莊稼。神話語的雨已經降下，麥子正在生長。
Their ‘work’ and their ‘love’ are the evidence — just as Paul gave thanks for the Thessalonians’ “work produced by faith and labour prompted by love” (1 Thess 1:2-3). And that does count for something. What is more, it is still happening — they continue to serve their fellow believers.他們的「工作」和「愛心」就是憑據——正如保羅為帖撒羅尼迦人感謝神，記念他們「因信心所做的工夫，因愛心所受的勞苦」（帖撒羅尼迦前書1章2-3節）。這確實是有價值的。而且，這仍在持續——他們仍舊服事其他信徒。
Yes, under pressure some are beginning to wonder whether following Jesus was the right move (see ch.10:32-35). But the vital signs are good. They are wobbling, not collapsing. Ridgeway — I am hugely encouraged by most of you, for the very same reasons. The vital signs here are really good, and that gives great hope for the future.誠然，在壓力之下，有些人開始懷疑跟從耶穌是否走對了路（參10章32-35節）。但生命的徵象是好的。他們是在搖晃，而非倒下。廣道堂——為着同樣的原因，你們大部分人都叫我大得鼓勵。這裏生命的徵象實在很好，叫人對將來充滿盼望。
But being confident does not mean being complacent — because things can change. They have come far, but they are not there yet. Neither are we. That is why he must still urge them to press on; they still need verses 4-8.然而，有信心並不等於可以鬆懈——因為情況是會變的。他們已走了很遠，卻仍未到達終點。我們也是一樣。正因如此，他仍須催促他們竭力前行；他們仍然需要4至8節的警告。
“We want each of you to show this same diligence to the very end… so that you may not become lazy, but imitate those who through faith and patience inherit what has been promised.” (v.11-12)「我們願你們各人都顯出這樣的殷勤，使你們有滿足的指望，一直到底……總要效法那些憑信心和忍耐承受應許的人。」（11-12節）

Conclusion: keep pedalling結語：繼續踏下去
In times past, the holy grail of physics was the perpetual-motion machine — something that, once started, would run forever with no new energy from outside. Ball-bearings, magnets, inert gases, even Newton’s-cradle desk toys: all sorts of designs were tried. Some lasted longer than others, but every one eventually stopped. We now know it is impossible: everything needs an input of energy, or it stops.從前，物理學的「聖杯」是永動機——一旦啟動，便能永遠運轉，無需外來的任何能量。滾珠軸承、磁石、惰性氣體，甚至牛頓擺那類桌面玩具：人們試過各式各樣的設計。有些撐得比較久，但每一個最終都停了下來。我們如今知道這是不可能的：凡物都需要能量的輸入，否則就會停止。
So does discipleship. It is more like a push bike than an electric bike: it needs energy and effort if it is to keep going (2 Peter 1:10). Keep pedalling! Yes, by his power and energy — but it still has to be done. It will not just happen. It cannot just happen.作門徒也是如此。它比較像單車，而不是電動單車：要持續前行，就需要力氣和努力（彼得後書1章10節）。繼續踏下去！是的，靠着祂的能力和力量——但這仍須我們去做。它不會自動成就，也不可能自動成就。
So will you make sure it does? Will you press on? You must press on. You are not there yet. If you have made it this far, do not give up now. There is too much at stake. However tempting the way back may look — do not throw it all away.那麼，你會否確保它真的成就？你會否竭力前行？你必須竭力前行。你還未到終點。你既已走到這一步，如今切勿放棄。這其中關乎的太大了。無論回頭的路看來何等吸引——千萬不要把一切都丟棄。

No rest for the righteous!

義人不得安歇！

Hebrews 希伯來書 6:4-12 · Bible Study Guide 聖經學習指南
"We want each of you to show this same diligence to the very end." · 「我們願你們各人都顯出這樣的殷勤……一直到底。」(Hebrews 希伯來書 6:11)
📖 Summary 摘要
English

Hebrews 6:4-12 sounds a stark warning and then offers a warm reassurance. The warning (v.4-8): a person can be deeply involved in the life of a Spirit-filled church — enlightened, having tasted the heavenly gift, sharing in the Holy Spirit — and still not be truly born again. Real faith is proved not by a strong start but by perseverance to the end. Those who finally fall away show that their repentance was never the real, life-changing kind.


Then the lesson (v.9-12): this warning is not meant to crush sensitive believers. The writer is confident of ‘better things’ for his readers, because their work and love for God’s people are genuine evidence of saving faith. Yet confidence is never an excuse for complacency — they must show the same diligence to the very end, imitating those who inherit the promises through faith and patience. Salvation does not coast like a perpetual-motion machine on past experience; like a push bike it calls for ongoing, grace-empowered effort. Keep moving forward — there is no rest for the righteous.

繁體中文

希伯來書6章4-12節先發出嚴厲的警告，繼而給予溫暖的安慰。警告（4-8節）：一個人可以深深投入一間滿有聖靈的教會生活——蒙了光照、嘗過天恩、於聖靈有份——卻仍未真正重生。真信心不在於起步有力，而在於堅忍到底。那些最終離道的人，正顯明他們的悔改從來就不是那種真實、使生命改變的悔改。


接着是功課（9-12節）：這警告並非要壓垮良心敏感的信徒。作者深信讀者有「強過這些」的好行為，因為他們為神子民所付出的工作與愛心，正是得救信心的真憑據。然而，有信心絕不能成為鬆懈的藉口——他們必須一直顯出同樣的殷勤，效法那憑信心和忍耐承受應許的人。救恩不像永動機，靠過去的經歷便能無止境運轉；它更像單車，需要持續、靠恩典而發的努力。繼續向前——義人不得安歇。

💡 Key Themes 主要主題
English

Perseverance is the mark of real faith — not a strong start, but finishing the race.


Participation is not regeneration — belonging to a church and tasting good things is not the same as being born again.


Apostasy is real and serious — turning back is a ‘suicidal move’.


Assurance is grounded in fruit — genuine work and love are evidence of saving faith.


Confidence is not complacency — the saved are kept by pressing on, with grace-empowered effort.

🌱 Application 生活應用
English

Don’t rest on past decisions or experiences. Ask: what does trusting and obeying Jesus look like for me today and tomorrow?


Look for the evidence of real faith — ongoing work and love for God’s people. Serve someone in the church family this week.


If the warning unsettles you, receive it as a gift of love meant to keep you going, not to crush you. Bring your assurance to Christ and rest on his promises.


Keep pedalling: name one spiritual habit (prayer, the word, gathering) you will renew by God’s power rather than letting it coast to a stop.

繁體中文

若這警告叫你不安，要把它接受為一份出於慈愛、要扶持你前行的恩賜，而非要壓垮你。把你的確據帶到基督面前，安息在祂的應許上。


繼續踏下去：說出一個你要靠神能力重新得力的屬靈習慣（禱告、讀經、聚會），不要任由它滑行至停頓。

🙏 Prayer 禱告
English

Father, thank you that you do not want us to live in fear, but to keep moving forward in obedient faith. Guard us from a faith that merely participates but never truly trusts. Where we are wobbling under pressure, hold us fast; where we are tempted to look back, fix our eyes on Jesus. By your Spirit, give us the energy to keep pedalling, and the diligence to finish the race — for there is too much at stake to throw it all away. In Jesus’ name, Amen.

繁體中文

天父，感謝祢不願我們活在懼怕中，乃願我們以順服的信心繼續向前。求祢保守我們，免得我們只有「參與」卻從未真正「信靠」的信心。當我們在壓力下搖晃，求祢緊緊抓住我們；當我們受試探回望，求祢叫我們定睛仰望耶穌。願祢的靈賜我們力量繼續踏下去，賜我們殷勤跑到終點——因為這其中關乎的太大，斷不可把一切丟棄。奉耶穌的名求，阿們。
