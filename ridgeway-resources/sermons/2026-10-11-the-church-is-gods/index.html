Bilingual Sermon 中英雙語講道
The Church Is God's 教會是屬神的
(1 Corinthians 3:1-17 哥林多前書 3:1-17)

Intro: The church引言：教會
Our topic this morning is the church. Because our passage this morning is all about who we are as God's people and how we should view our leaders.今天早上我們的主題是教會。 因為今天早上的經文，所講的全是我們作為神的子民是怎樣的人，以及我們應當怎樣看待我們的領袖。
Two key truths兩個關鍵的真理
In particular it teaches us two key truths. Two truths which fundamentally affect the way we view the church and, in particular, its leaders. Because the church is unlike any other institution or community. Two simple yet profound truths that are easy to remember. Firstly, the church is God's. And secondly, leaders are servants. Let's then delve into our passage and see that's the case.特別是，它教導我們兩個關鍵的真理。 這兩個真理從根本上影響我們怎樣看待教會，尤其是怎樣看待教會的領袖。 因為教會與任何其他機構或群體都不一樣。 這兩個真理簡單而深刻，也容易記住。 第一，教會是屬神的。 第二，領袖是僕人。 那麼，讓我們深入這段經文，看看是否如此。
………………………………
Context: The church was full of compromise and conflict背景：教會充滿妥協與衝突
We're in 1 Corinthians chapter 3 and what you need to know is this. Ever since chapter one, Paul has been dealing with one particular issue that was plaguing the Corinthian church: infighting. Because the Corinthians were quarrelling about and dividing over their leaders. And not for good reason. No! The Corinthians were quarrelling because they were proud and worldly.我們現在讀到哥林多前書第三章，大家需要知道的是： 從第一章開始，保羅一直在處理一個困擾哥林多教會的問題：內鬥。 因為哥林多人為他們的領袖爭吵，並因此而分裂。 而且並非出於正當的理由。 不！哥林多人爭吵，是因為他們驕傲，並且屬世。
Context: Paul has been dealing with their pride背景：保羅一直在處理他們的驕傲
And so, Paul has spent the last two chapters dealing with their pride and worldliness. How? Well, by reminding them that God, in his wisdom, saves weak and foolish looking people through a weak and foolish looking method: the cross. And reminding them that spiritual people understand and accept God's wisdom. With the result that they boast only in him, and not in any human wisdom or ability, whether their own or someone else's.所以，保羅用了前面兩章來處理他們的驕傲和屬世。 他怎樣處理？ 他提醒他們：神按着祂的智慧，藉着一個看似軟弱愚拙的方法——十字架——拯救看似軟弱愚拙的人。 他又提醒他們：屬靈的人明白並接受神的智慧。 結果是，他們只誇耀神，而不誇耀任何人的智慧或能力，無論是自己的，還是別人的。
In chapter 3 Paul returns to the issue在第三章，保羅回到問題本身
And this brings us to chapter three, verses one to four. Because, having explained that spiritual people accept God's wisdom, Paul now returns to the issue at hand in Corinth.這就把我們帶到第三章一至四節。 保羅解釋了屬靈的人會接受神的智慧之後，現在回到哥林多眼前的問題。
The issue: The Corinthians are acting like unspiritual infants問題：哥林多人的行為像不屬靈的嬰孩
Just listen as I read from verse one. And as I read, see if you can spot the issue. Verse one: "Brothers and sisters, I could not address you as people who live by the Spirit but as people who are still worldly – mere infants in Christ. I gave you milk, not solid food, for you were not yet ready for it. Indeed, you are still not ready. You are still worldly." (vv.1-3) Now, I'm sure you spotted the issue. The Corinthians – says Paul – are acting like unspiritual infants.請聽我從第一節讀起。 我讀的時候，看看你能否找出問題所在。 第一節： 「弟兄們，我從前對你們說話，不能把你們當作屬靈的，只得把你們當作屬肉體，在基督裡為嬰孩的。我是用奶餵你們，沒有用飯餵你們。那時你們不能吃，就是如今還是不能。你們仍是屬肉體的。」（1-3 節） 我相信你已經找到問題所在。 保羅說，哥林多人的行為像不屬靈的嬰孩。
The shock: They thought they were spiritual and mature震驚：他們自以為屬靈而成熟
And these words would have horrified the Corinthians. Because they viewed themselves as very spiritual and wise. After all, they were blessed with all sorts of spiritual gifts, such as speaking in tongues and prophecy. And so, they thought they were spiritual and mature.這番話必定令哥林多人大吃一驚。 因為他們自視為非常屬靈、非常有智慧。 畢竟，他們蒙福得着各種屬靈恩賜，例如說方言和說預言。 所以，他們以為自己既屬靈又成熟。
The shock: Paul says they're acting like infants震驚：保羅說他們的行為像嬰孩
But Paul says, "No! You're not." You're acting like infants. You're behaving like babies who need formula rather than solid food. And so, I cannot address you as spiritual people.但保羅說：「不！你們不是。」 你們的行為像嬰孩。 你們的舉止像需要喝奶粉、不能吃固體食物的嬰兒。所以，我不能把你們當作屬靈的人。
Clarification 1: Not that there are two categories of Christian澄清一：並非有兩類基督徒
Now, at this point we need to pause to make two important clarifications. Firstly, Paul is not saying that there are two tiers of Christian. Spiritual Christians who have the Spirit, and carnal Christians who don't. No! That would completely contradict everything Paul has said so far. Because Paul has been clear: every Christian has the Holy Spirit dwelling inside them, including the Corinthians. And so, the issue isn't whether the Corinthians have the Spirit; it's how they're behaving with the Spirit. Because they're acting like unspiritual infants. And that's the first clarification. Paul isn't saying that there are two tiers of Christian.在這裡，我們需要停一停，作兩個重要的澄清。 第一，保羅並不是說基督徒分為兩個等級： 有聖靈的屬靈基督徒， 和沒有聖靈的屬肉體基督徒。 不！這會完全違背保羅到目前為止所說的一切。 因為保羅說得很清楚：每一個基督徒都有聖靈住在他裡面， 哥林多人也不例外。 所以，問題不在於哥林多人有沒有聖靈，而在於他們有了聖靈之後，行為是怎樣的。 因為他們的行為像不屬靈的嬰孩。 這是第一個澄清：保羅並不是說基督徒分為兩個等級。
Clarification 2: Not that there are two tiers of teaching澄清二：並非有兩個層次的教導
Nor is he saying that there are two levels of Christian teaching. As if you start with the milk of Christ crucified, and then move on to the solid food of something else. No! This would also completely contradict everything Paul has said so far. Because Paul has been clear: Christ crucified is God's wisdom. And so, there aren't two levels of Christian teaching. Christians never move on from the message of the cross.保羅也不是說基督教的教導分為兩個層次， 彷彿先從釘十字架的基督這「奶」開始， 然後再進到其他東西這「飯」。 不！這同樣會完全違背保羅到目前為止所說的一切。 因為保羅說得很清楚：釘十字架的基督就是神的智慧。 所以，基督教的教導並沒有兩個層次。 基督徒永遠不會從十字架的信息「畢業」。
The issue is that they haven't matured問題在於他們沒有長大成熟
However, whilst Christians never move on, they do mature. Like babies, who go from eating puréed pork to real roast pork. The issue is: the Corinthians haven't grown up. They're still behaving like babies. And so, Paul still has to feed them purée!然而，基督徒雖然永遠不會離開十字架的信息，卻一定會長大成熟。 就像嬰兒從吃豬肉泥，漸漸長大到吃真正的烤豬肉一樣。 問題是：哥林多人沒有長大。 他們的行為仍然像嬰孩。 所以，保羅仍然要餵他們吃泥糊！
By arguing about leaders表現在為領袖爭吵
The Corinthians are behaving like unspiritual infants. Because, like little children arguing about whose dad is bigger, the Corinthians are quarrelling over which leader is better (vv.3-4). It's so childish. And it's so worldly.哥林多人的行為像不屬靈的嬰孩。 因為就像小孩子爭論誰的爸爸最高大一樣，哥林多人在爭論哪一位領袖更好（3-4 節）。 這是何等幼稚， 又是何等屬世。
Reason: God's wisdom boasts in Christ, not humans原因：神的智慧是誇耀基督，不是誇耀人
Because remember, God's wisdom is all about boasting in Christ: who he is and what he's done. But the Corinthian Christians are quarrelling about human leaders: what they're like and what they've done.要記得，神的智慧全在於誇耀基督：祂是誰，祂成就了甚麼。 但哥林多的基督徒卻為屬人的領袖爭吵：他們是怎樣的人，他們做過甚麼。
What Paul does: He rebukes, then corrects them保羅的做法：先責備，後糾正
And so, Paul rebukes them, and then he corrects them.所以，保羅先責備他們， 然後糾正他們。
Three pictures三幅圖畫
And his correction comes in verses five to seventeen. Because in verses five to seventeen, Paul shows the Corinthians – and us – how we should think about the church and its leaders. And the way Paul does this is just brilliant. Because he paints three pictures. Three vivid pictures of the church and its leaders. Three pictures which teach us two key truths: the church is God's, and leaders are servants.他的糾正在第五至十七節。 在第五至十七節，保羅向哥林多人——也向我們——指明應當怎樣看待教會和教會的領袖。 保羅的表達方式實在精彩。 因為他描繪了三幅圖畫， 三幅關於教會和教會領袖的生動圖畫。 這三幅圖畫教導我們兩個關鍵的真理： 教會是屬神的， 領袖是僕人。
Plan: Zoom in on the pictures計劃：逐一細看這些圖畫
And so, what we're going to do with the rest of our time is zoom in on each picture in turn and see what it has to teach us. So, what are the pictures?所以，在餘下的時間，我們會逐一細看每一幅圖畫，看看它要教導我們甚麼。 那麼，這些圖畫是甚麼呢？
………………………………
Picture 1: The field第一幅圖畫：田地
Well, the first picture comes in verses five to nine, where Paul wants us to picture a field. Think Clarkson's Farm. Except this is God's farm. God's field. And the workers aren't Kaleb and Charlie, but Paul and Apollos. Paul the planter, because he brought the gospel to Corinth. And Apollos the waterer, because he strengthened the believers in Corinth. That's the picture.第一幅圖畫在第五至九節，保羅要我們想像一塊田地。 大家可以想想電視節目《克拉克森的農場》（Clarkson's Farm）。 只不過，這是神的農場，神的田地。 而工人不是 Kaleb 和 Charlie，而是保羅和亞波羅。 保羅是栽種的，因為他把福音帶到哥林多。 亞波羅是澆灌的，因為他堅固了哥林多的信徒。 這就是那幅圖畫。
Question: What does it teach us?問題：它教導我們甚麼？
But what does this picture show us? Well, two things.這幅圖畫向我們顯明甚麼呢？ 兩件事。
(i) The church is God's（一）教會是屬神的
Firstly, the church is God's. After all, it's his field (v.9). And so, the church is God's. That's the first thing.第一，教會是屬神的。 畢竟，這是祂的田地（9 節）。 所以，教會是屬神的。 這是第一件事。
(ii) Leaders are servants（二）領袖是僕人
The second is that leaders are servants. Did you spot that in verse five? Paul says: "What, after all, is Apollos? And what is Paul? Only servants, through whom you came to believe – as the Lord has assigned to each his task." (v.5)第二，領袖是僕人。 你有沒有留意到第五節？ 保羅說： 「亞波羅算甚麼？保羅算甚麼？無非是執事，照主所賜給他們各人的，引導你們相信。」（5 節）
Different to the way the world works與世界運作的方式不同
Notice how different this is to the way that much of the world works. I used to work for a large financial services firm, and the leaders were all partners who owned the firm. It belonged to them, and the rest of us effectively served them. Not so with the church. Because, when it comes to the church, leaders aren't owners; they're servants. Servants who work for God.請留意，這與世上大部分地方運作的方式何等不同。 我從前在一家大型金融服務公司工作，那裡的領導都是擁有公司的合夥人。 公司屬於他們，而我們其餘的人實際上是在服侍他們。 教會卻不是這樣。 因為在教會裡，領袖不是擁有者，而是僕人， 是為神工作的僕人。
Illustration: Consulting例子：顧問工作
When I worked for that firm, I couldn't simply do whatever I wanted, for whatever purpose I chose. No! I did the tasks I was assigned, for the purposes I was given. Well, says Paul, the same is true when it comes to leaders in the church. They're assigned tasks and given a purpose. Church leaders don't labour for their own fame or gain. No! They labour so that God's field might flourish and bear fruit.我在那家公司工作的時候，不能隨心所欲，想做甚麼就做甚麼，想為甚麼目的就為甚麼目的。 不！我做的是被分派的工作，為的是被交付的目的。 保羅說，教會的領袖也是一樣。 他們被分派了工作，也被交付了目的。 教會領袖勞苦，不是為了自己的名聲或利益。 不！他們勞苦，是要神的田地興旺，結出果子。
Leaders serve God's people領袖服侍神的子民
And so, leaders are servants. Servants who work for God and serve his people. And again, it's worth highlighting how different this is to the way the world usually works. Because usually it is leaders who have servants and get served. But not so with the church! Churches don't exist to serve their leaders; leaders exist to serve their churches.所以，領袖是僕人， 是為神工作、服侍祂子民的僕人。 這一點值得再次強調：這與世界通常運作的方式何等不同。 因為在世上，通常是領袖擁有僕人、受人服侍。 但教會卻不是這樣！ 教會不是為服侍領袖而存在；領袖是為服侍教會而存在。
Servants rely on God僕人倚靠神
Because leaders are servants. Servants who not only work for God but also rely on God. Which is the big point in verse seven. Just listen as I read from verse six. And, as I read, keep an ear out for the repeated phrase. Verse six: "I planted the seed, Apollos watered it, but God has been making it grow. So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but only God, who makes things grow." (vv.6-7)因為領袖是僕人， 是不但為神工作，也倚靠神的僕人。 這正是第七節的重點。 請聽我從第六節讀起。 我讀的時候，請留意那句重複出現的話。 第六節： 「我栽種了，亞波羅澆灌了，惟有　神叫他生長。可見栽種的，算不得甚麼，澆灌的，也算不得甚麼；只在那叫他生長的　神。」（6-7 節）
God makes plants grow神叫植物生長
Why is it that plants grow? Well, says Paul, ultimately, it is because God makes them grow. Even with all our agricultural advances, we're still entirely dependent on him upholding the universe. Without God's nurturing hand, nothing happens. It is God who gives the growth.植物為甚麼會生長？ 保羅說，歸根究底，是因為神叫它們生長。 即使農業技術如何進步，我們仍然完全倚靠祂托住宇宙萬有。 沒有神養育的手，甚麼也不會發生。 叫它生長的是神。
God makes the church grow神叫教會生長
And the same is true with the church. Who is it that makes the church flourish and bear fruit? Answer: God. Without his nurturing hand, nothing happens. It is God who gives the growth, and we are entirely dependent on his work.教會也是一樣。 是誰叫教會興旺、結出果子？ 答案：神。 沒有祂養育的手，甚麼也不會發生。 叫它生長的是神，我們完全倚靠祂的工作。
Clarification: Not that work is irrelevant澄清：並非說工作無關重要
Now again, we need to pause at this point to clarify. Because you may well be thinking, "Hang on a minute. If God gives the growth, doesn't that make our work irrelevant and worthless?" Well, no! The fact that God gives the growth doesn't mean we should sit back and do nothing, any more than it means farmers should cease planting and watering. Because God uses our work. And, what's more, he rewards our work (v.8).在這裡，我們需要再停一停作澄清。 因為你可能會想：「且慢。如果是神叫它生長，那麼我們的工作豈不是無關重要、毫無價值？」 不是的！ 神叫它生長，並不表示我們應該袖手旁觀、甚麼也不做，正如這並不表示農夫應該停止栽種和澆灌一樣。 因為神使用我們的工作。 不但如此，祂還賞賜我們的工作（8 節）。
Correction: For when we see God as harsh糾正：當我們以為神很嚴苛
Sometimes people picture God as a harsh head teacher who is impossible to please. But that is miles from the truth. Because God sees the work of his servants and rewards them.有時候，人把神想像成一位嚴苛的校長，怎樣做也無法令祂滿意。 但這與事實相差太遠了。 因為神看見祂僕人的工作，並且賞賜他們。
God's sovereignty encourages us神的主權激勵我們
Do you see? The fact we're dependent on God never undermines our work or excuses us from work. Actually, it encourages us to work. Confident that God can and will use our work.你看見嗎？我們倚靠神這事實，從不削弱我們的工作，也不容許我們推卸工作。 事實上，它激勵我們去工作， 深信神能夠、也必定會使用我們的工作。
Summary of the first picture第一幅圖畫的總結
And that's the first picture. The church is God's field. And leaders are servants, who work for him and depend on him. And who exist, not to be served or to serve themselves, but to serve the church.這就是第一幅圖畫。 教會是神的田地。 領袖是僕人，為祂工作，倚靠祂； 他們存在不是為了受人服侍，也不是為了服侍自己，而是為了服侍教會。
Teaches us how to view our leaders教導我們怎樣看待領袖
And so, this passage teaches us how we should view church leaders. Not as lords who deserve our devotion, but as servants who work for God and depend on God.所以，這段經文教導我們應當怎樣看待教會領袖： 不是把他們看作配得我們效忠的主人，而是看作為神工作、倚靠神的僕人。
Teaches us how to view church growth教導我們怎樣看待教會增長
What's more, it teaches us how we should view church growth. Not as evidence that our leaders are the best, but as evidence that God, in his kindness, has been at work.不但如此，它也教導我們應當怎樣看待教會的增長： 不是把它看作我們的領袖最出色的證據，而是看作神在祂的恩慈中一直作工的證據。
Application: Removing an abusive leader應用：撤換濫權的領袖
A few years back, I listened to a podcast about an abusive church leader. Tragically, this leader remained in position for many years, despite numerous disqualifying sins. And a key reason why he wasn't removed was that the church was growing. And so, his sins got overlooked. It is a frightening thing to remove a leader who seems responsible for growth, until you realise that it is God who gives the growth.幾年前，我聽過一個播客節目，講述一位濫權的教會領袖。 可悲的是，這位領袖雖然犯了許多足以令他失去資格的罪，卻仍然在位多年。 他沒有被撤換的一個主要原因，是教會正在增長。 所以，他的罪被忽視了。 要撤換一位看似帶來增長的領袖，是一件令人害怕的事， 直到你明白：叫它生長的是神。
Application: A reassuring truth應用：令人安心的真理
And this is a wonderfully reassuring truth. Because just imagine that you become a believer under the ministry of someone who turns out to be a terrible leader. How do you feel when their sin is exposed? Distressed? Doubtful? After all, you became a believer through their ministry. And so maybe your Christianity is counterfeit? Perhaps your faith is fake? But then you remember that it is God who gives the growth. It was God who gave you faith in Jesus, and it is God who has been making you more like Jesus. Leaders are servants who work for God and depend on God.這是一個極其令人安心的真理。 試想像，你是在某人的事奉下信主的，後來卻發現他是一位很差劣的領袖。 當他的罪被揭發時，你有甚麼感受？ 苦惱？懷疑？ 畢竟，你是藉着他的事奉成為信徒的。 那麼，也許你的信仰是冒牌的？ 也許你的信心是假的？ 但這時你記起：叫人生長的是神。 是神賜你信靠耶穌的心，也是神一直在使你更像耶穌。 領袖是為神工作、倚靠神的僕人。
Dividing over leaders is nonsensical為領袖分裂是荒謬的
Are you beginning to see why quarrelling about and dividing over leaders isn't just unhelpful… it's nonsensical! The first picture Paul gives us is of a field.你開始明白為甚麼為領袖爭吵、分裂不單沒有益處……而且是荒謬的嗎！ 保羅給我們的第一幅圖畫，是一塊田地。
………………………………
Picture 2: The building第二幅圖畫：房屋
The second comes in verses ten to fifteen, where Paul gets us to picture a building. Or rather a building site, with the foundation already laid, but the structure not yet complete. Think Grand Designs, or whatever other building show you enjoy of an evening.第二幅圖畫在第十至十五節，保羅要我們想像一座房屋， 更準確地說，是一個建築工地： 根基已經立好，但結構仍未完成。 大家可以想想《夢想家園》（Grand Designs），或者任何你晚上喜歡看的建築節目。
Similar: The church is God's相同之處：教會是屬神的
And in many ways, this picture is similar to the field. After all, it's a picture which shows us, once again, that the church is God's. Because the building belongs to God.在許多方面，這幅圖畫與田地那幅相似。 畢竟，這幅圖畫再一次向我們顯明：教會是屬神的。因為這房屋屬於神。
Similar: Leaders are servants相同之處：領袖是僕人
What's more, it's a picture which shows us, once again, that leaders are servants. Servants who work for God and are rewarded by God. Albeit, in this picture they serve by building rather than farming.不但如此，這幅圖畫也再一次向我們顯明：領袖是僕人， 是為神工作、蒙神賞賜的僕人。 只不過，在這幅圖畫中，他們服侍的方式是建造，而不是耕種。
Question: What's the difference?問題：有甚麼分別？
And so, there are lots of similarities between the two pictures. So, what's the difference?所以，兩幅圖畫有許多相似之處。 那麼，分別在哪裡？
Answer: What God does答案：神所做的事
Well, the main difference is in what God does. Because, with the field, the focus is on God giving the growth. Whilst, with the building, the focus is on God testing the work. After all, on the farm, everyone worked with the same seed, but in this picture the builders can build with different materials. Did you spot that in verses twelve and thirteen? "If anyone builds on this foundation using gold, silver, costly stones, wood, hay or straw, their work will be shown for what it is, because the Day will bring it to light. It will be revealed with fire, and the fire will test the quality of each person's work." (vv.12-13)主要的分別，在於神所做的事。 在田地的圖畫中，重點是神叫它生長； 而在房屋的圖畫中，重點是神試驗工程。 畢竟，在農場上，人人用的都是同樣的種子；但在這幅圖畫中，建造的人可以用不同的材料。 你有沒有留意到第十二、十三節？ 「若有人用金、銀、寶石、草木、禾稭，在這根基上建造，各人的工程必然顯露，因為那日子要將它表明出來，有火發現；這火要試驗各人的工程怎樣。」（12-13 節）
A day is coming那日子將要來到
A day is coming, says Paul, when God will test the work of every leader: Judgment Day. A day of revelation. And a day of fire.保羅說，有一天將要來到，神要試驗每一位領袖的工程：那就是審判的日子。 那是顯明的日子， 也是有火的日子。
Two options兩個結果
And on that day the quality of every leader's work will be brought to light. If they built with quality materials – such as gold, silver and costly stones – then they will be rewarded (v.14). But if they didn't – if they built with wood, hay or straw – then their work will be burned up. And whilst they will survive, it will only be by the skin of their teeth (v.15).在那日，每一位領袖工程的質素都要被顯明出來。 如果他們用優質的材料——例如金、銀、寶石——建造，他們就要得賞賜（14 節）。 但如果不是——如果他們用草木、禾稭建造——他們的工程就要被燒毀。 他們自己雖然得救，卻只是僅僅得救，「乃像從火裡經過的一樣」（15 節）。
Judgment Day motivates us to build well審判的日子激勵我們好好建造
And, I don't know about you, but I found these verses very striking. Because I'm familiar with the idea of Judgment Day being a motivation to turn from sin and become a Christian. But that's not what Paul is saying here. Because he's talking to people who are already Christians, and telling them that, on Judgment Day, their work will be tested. Do you see? Judgment Day doesn't just motivate us to come to faith; it also motivates us to live by faith. To build well, because our work will be tested.我不知道你怎樣想，但我覺得這幾節經文非常震撼。 因為我熟悉的觀念，是審判的日子激勵人離棄罪、成為基督徒。 但保羅在這裡所說的並不是這個。 因為他是對已經是基督徒的人說話， 告訴他們：在審判的日子，他們的工程要被試驗。 你看見嗎？審判的日子不單激勵我們歸信，也激勵我們憑信心生活， 好好建造，因為我們的工程要被試驗。
Question: What does building well involve?問題：怎樣才算好好建造？
But what does building well involve? Well, Paul doesn't explicitly tell us. However, throughout the letter, he holds himself up as an example. And so surely building well involves imitating him: proclaiming Christ and pursuing unity, practising holiness and prioritising others.但怎樣才算好好建造呢？ 保羅沒有明確告訴我們。 然而，在整封書信中，他都以自己為榜樣。 所以，好好建造必定包括效法他： 宣揚基督、追求合一、實踐聖潔、以他人為先。
Building badly建造不善
Whereas building badly looks like the opposite: prioritising self and provoking division, tolerating worldliness and relegating Christ. And, on Judgment Day, good building will be rewarded, whilst bad building will be burned up.而建造不善，則恰恰相反： 以自己為先、挑起分裂、容讓屬世、把基督放在次要的位置。 在審判的日子，好的建造要得賞賜，壞的建造要被燒毀。
Summary of the second picture第二幅圖畫的總結
And that's the second picture. The church is God's building. And leaders are builders, whose work will be tested.這就是第二幅圖畫。 教會是神的房屋。 領袖是建造的人，他們的工程要被試驗。
Implication: We should build with care含意：我們要謹慎建造
And so, says Paul, we should be very careful how we build. Did you spot that at the end of verse ten? "But each one should build with care." (v.10)所以，保羅說，我們要非常謹慎怎樣建造。 你有沒有留意到第十節的末句？ 「只是各人要謹慎怎樣在上面建造。」（10 節）
Question: Do these verses apply to me?問題：這些經文與我有關嗎？
But, you might be thinking, isn't Paul addressing leaders here? And so, isn't this command irrelevant to me?但你可能會想：保羅在這裡不是對領袖說話嗎？ 那麼，這個命令豈不是與我無關？
Applies in the first instance to church leaders首先應用在教會領袖身上
Well, yes, Paul's focus is on church leaders. And so, this call to build carefully does apply, in the first instance, to pastors and elders. And then to those who help lead ministries like Sunday school and small groups, toddler groups and youth groups.是的，保羅的焦點是教會領袖。 所以，這個謹慎建造的呼召，首先應用在牧師和長老身上； 然後也應用在協助帶領各項事工的人身上，例如主日學和小組、 幼兒小組和青少年小組。
Applies to other believers也應用在其他信徒身上
However, that does not mean there is no application to other believers. Firstly, because it shows us what we should look for and pray for in our leaders. And secondly, because building work isn't limited to leaders. No! We all have a role to play. Every believer is called to build up the church.然而，這並不表示這段經文與其他信徒無關。 第一，因為它讓我們知道應當在領袖身上尋找甚麼，並為他們祈求甚麼。 第二，因為建造的工作並不限於領袖。 不！我們每一個人都有份。 每一位信徒都蒙召建立教會。
Application: Let's all be careful應用：讓我們都謹慎
And so, let's be careful how we build. Let's proclaim Christ, pursue unity, practise holiness and prioritise others. Confident that, if we do, then not only will our work survive into eternity, but God himself will reward us. Not because we've merited our salvation, but because he is our gracious and generous Father. The second picture Paul gives us is of a building.所以，讓我們謹慎怎樣建造。 讓我們宣揚基督、追求合一、實踐聖潔、以他人為先。 深信我們若這樣行，不但我們的工程能存到永遠，神自己也必賞賜我們。 這不是因為我們賺得了救恩，而是因為祂是我們滿有恩典、慷慨施予的天父。 保羅給我們的第二幅圖畫，是一座房屋。
………………………………
Picture 3: The temple第三幅圖畫：聖殿
The third comes in verses sixteen and seventeen, where Paul gets us to picture a temple. The church, says Paul, is God's temple.第三幅圖畫在第十六、十七節，保羅要我們想像一座聖殿。 保羅說，教會是神的殿。
Question: What does that mean?問題：這是甚麼意思？
But what does that mean? And why does that matter?但這是甚麼意思？ 又為甚麼重要？
Answer: Temples are holy houses for gods答案：殿是神明的聖所
Well, to answer those questions, we need to know two things about temples. Firstly, temples are sacred spaces. And secondly, temples are dwelling places. They are holy houses for gods. That's what temples are.要回答這些問題，我們需要知道關於殿的兩件事。 第一，殿是神聖的地方。 第二，殿是居所。 殿是神明的聖所。 這就是殿。
The church is God's temple教會是神的殿
And that brings us to verse sixteen. "Don't you know that you yourselves are God's temple and that God's Spirit dwells in your midst?" (v.16) Because in verse sixteen Paul tells us that the one true God has a temple. But his temple today isn't a physical building. No! He dwells among his people. The church is God's temple.這就把我們帶到第十六節： 「豈不知你們是　神的殿，　神的靈住在你們裡頭麼？」（16 節） 在第十六節，保羅告訴我們：獨一的真神有一座殿。 但祂今天的殿，不是一座有形的建築物。 不！祂住在祂的子民中間。 教會就是神的殿。
Application: When the church feels unimpressive應用：當教會看似不起眼
Sometimes, when we look at the church – perhaps nationally, or perhaps here locally – it can look and feel pretty unimpressive. Perhaps even quite pathetic. But only when we look from a human perspective. Because, in reality, the church is God's temple.有時候，當我們看教會——也許是全國的教會，也許是我們本地的教會——它看起來、感覺起來都相當不起眼， 甚至相當可憐。 但這只是當我們從人的角度去看的時候。 因為實際上，教會是神的殿。
The church is utterly unique教會是獨一無二的
Do you see? The church is utterly unique. Ridgeway is unlike any other community or institution in St Albans, except of course the other churches. Because God dwells among you, by his Spirit.你看見嗎？教會是完全獨一無二的。 Ridgeway 教會與聖奧爾本斯（St Albans）任何其他群體或機構都不一樣， 當然，其他教會除外。 因為神藉着祂的靈住在你們中間。
The church really matters教會非常重要
And, to state the obvious, this means that the church really matters. In particular, it matters to God. It's his sacred space, his dwelling place. And so, how we treat the church really matters too. Which is why Paul gives us such a stark warning in verse seventeen: "If anyone destroys God's temple, God will destroy that person; for God's temple is sacred, and you together are that temple." (v.17)顯而易見，這表示教會非常重要， 尤其是在神眼中非常重要。 它是祂神聖的地方，是祂的居所。 所以，我們怎樣對待教會，也非常重要。 這就是為甚麼保羅在第十七節給我們如此嚴厲的警告： 「若有人毀壞　神的殿，　神必要毀壞那人；因為　神的殿是聖的，這殿就是你們。」（17 節）
A shift from the building picture從房屋的圖畫更進一步
Notice, there's an intensification here from the building picture. There the issue was bad building; here the issue is destroying. There the builders were saved; here the destroyers get destroyed.請留意，這裡比房屋的圖畫更進一步、更加嚴重。 那裡的問題是建造不善；這裡的問題是毀壞。 那裡建造的人得救；這裡毀壞的人卻要被毀壞。
Question: What's the difference between building and destroying?問題：建造不善與毀壞有何分別？
And so, you may well be wondering, "What's the difference?" "What distinguishes bad building from destroying?"所以，你可能會問：「分別在哪裡？」 「建造不善和毀壞，怎樣區分？」
We don't know, but we do know to build with care我們不知道，但我們知道要謹慎建造
Well, Paul doesn't tell us. And actually, I don't think we need to nail it down. Because the whole point is that we should steer clear of both! And instead build with care. Because the church is God's temple. The final picture Paul gives us is of a temple.保羅沒有告訴我們。 其實，我認為我們也不需要把它界定得一清二楚。 因為整個重點是：兩者我們都要遠離！ 反而要謹慎建造。 因為教會是神的殿。 保羅給我們的最後一幅圖畫，是一座聖殿。

Conclusion結論
And so, brothers and sisters, this passage teaches us about the church and about its leaders. In particular, it teaches us that the church is God's. It is his field, his building, his temple. His sacred space and his dwelling place. And leaders are servants. Servants who work for him and depend on him. Servants who will be rewarded by him, if they build with care.所以，弟兄姊妹，這段經文教導我們關於教會和教會領袖的真理。 特別是，它教導我們：教會是屬神的。 教會是祂的田地、祂的房屋、祂的殿， 是祂神聖的地方，是祂的居所。 而領袖是僕人， 是為祂工作、倚靠祂的僕人， 若他們謹慎建造，就必蒙祂賞賜。

📖Summary 摘要

English
The problem: The Corinthians thought they were spiritual and mature, but by quarrelling over which leader was better they were behaving like worldly infants (vv.1-4). They have the Spirit, and Christ crucified is still their food; the problem is that they have not grown up.
Picture 1, the field (vv.5-9): The church is God's field. Paul and Apollos are only servants who plant and water; God alone gives the growth. Leaders work for God, depend on God and are rewarded by God.
Picture 2, the building (vv.10-15): The church is God's building, laid on the one foundation, Jesus Christ. On the Day, fire will test each builder's work: work built with gold, silver and costly stones is rewarded; wood, hay and straw burn up. So each one should build with care.
Picture 3, the temple (vv.16-17): The church is God's temple, his sacred space and dwelling place, because his Spirit lives among his people. Anyone who destroys God's temple, God will destroy.
Two truths: The church is God's. Leaders are servants. So dividing over leaders is not only unhelpful; it is nonsensical.

💡Key Themes 主要主題

English
1. Spiritual maturity shows in behaviour. Every Christian has the Spirit, but not every Christian behaves spiritually. Jealousy and quarrelling are marks of infancy, however gifted a church may be.
2. The church belongs to God. It is his field, his building and his temple, not the possession of any leader or faction.
3. Leaders are servants who depend on God. They are assigned their tasks by the Lord, they serve his people, and God alone gives the growth, so growth is never proof that a leader is the best.
4. Our work will be tested. Judgment Day does not only call unbelievers to faith; it calls believers to build carefully, knowing God will reward faithful work.
5. The church is holy because God dwells there. However unimpressive it looks, the local church is God's temple, and harming it is a grave matter.

❓Thinking Questions 思考問題

What evidence does Paul give in verses 1-4 that the Corinthians are still "infants in Christ"? Why would this have shocked them?
在 1-4 節，保羅提出甚麼證據，說明哥林多人仍是「在基督裡為嬰孩的」？為甚麼這番話會令他們震驚？
The sermon made two clarifications: there are not two tiers of Christian, and there are not two levels of teaching. Why do both matter? How do Christians "mature" without ever "moving on" from the cross?
講道作了兩個澄清：基督徒並沒有兩個等級，教導也沒有兩個層次。為甚麼這兩點都重要？基督徒怎樣能夠長大成熟，卻永不離開十字架的信息？
In the field picture (vv.5-9), what do we learn about who owns the church, what leaders are for, and who gives the growth? How does this differ from the way leadership usually works in the world?
在田地的圖畫中（5-9 節），關於教會屬誰、領袖為何而設、以及誰叫它生長，我們學到甚麼？這與世上通常的領導方式有何不同？
If God gives the growth, why should we still work hard? How does the promise of reward in verse 8 correct a picture of God as a harsh head teacher?
既然是神叫它生長，為甚麼我們仍然要努力工作？第 8 節賞賜的應許，怎樣糾正我們把神看作嚴苛校長的想法？
What does "building with gold, silver and costly stones" look like in practice? What does building with "wood, hay or straw" look like? Can you think of examples of each in church life?
「用金、銀、寶石建造」實際上是怎樣的？「用草木、禾稭建造」又是怎樣的？你能想到教會生活中兩者的例子嗎？
When the local church looks unimpressive, how does the truth that it is God's temple (v.16) change the way you see it, and the way you speak about it?
當地方教會看似不起眼時，「教會是神的殿」（16 節）這真理怎樣改變你看待它、談論它的方式？
Are you more prone to elevating a favourite leader, or to criticising leaders you don't like? What would it look like this week for you to "build with care" in your small group, ministry or friendships at church?
你比較容易高舉自己喜愛的領袖，還是批評自己不喜歡的領袖？這個星期，你可以怎樣在小組、事工或教會的人際關係中「謹慎建造」？

🌱Application 生活應用

View leaders rightly. Honour and pray for your leaders as servants of God, not lords to be followed for their own sake. Refuse to join "I follow this one" talk, in person or online.
正確看待領袖。尊重領袖，並為他們禱告，視他們為神的僕人，而不是為了他們自己而跟隨的主人。無論面對面或在網上，都拒絕參與「我是屬某某的」這類言談。
Credit God for growth. When the church grows, or when you grow, thank God first. Do not let apparent success shield a leader from accountability.
把增長歸功於神。當教會增長，或當你自己成長時，先感謝神。不要讓表面的成功成為領袖逃避問責的保護傘。
Build with care. Choose one concrete way to proclaim Christ, pursue unity, practise holiness or prioritise others in your church this week.
謹慎建造。這個星期，在教會中選擇一個具體的方式，去宣揚基督、追求合一、實踐聖潔或以他人為先。
Work with confidence, not fear. Serve knowing that God uses your work and will graciously reward it, not because you earn salvation, but because he is a generous Father.
滿有信心，而非懼怕地工作。事奉時要知道，神使用你的工作，並且必按恩典賞賜；不是因為你賺得救恩，而是因為祂是慷慨的天父。
Treasure the local church. Speak of Ridgeway as God's temple. Guard your words and actions so that you build up, and never tear down, the people among whom God dwells.
珍惜地方教會。談論 Ridgeway 教會時，要記得它是神的殿。謹守你的言行，要建立神所住在其中的群體，永不拆毀。

🙏Prayer 禱告

Heavenly Father, thank you that the church is yours: your field, your building, your temple, where your Spirit dwells. Forgive us when we have been proud and worldly, boasting in people rather than in Christ, and when our quarrels have shown that we are still infants. Thank you for the leaders you have given us; help us to see them as your servants and to pray for them. Lord, you alone give the growth, so keep us from trusting in human ability, and keep us from fearing that our work is wasted. Help each of us to build with care on the one foundation, Jesus Christ: to proclaim him, to pursue unity, to practise holiness and to put others first. Make Ridgeway a holy dwelling place for you in St Albans. In Jesus' name, Amen.
天父，感謝你，教會是屬你的：是你的田地、你的房屋、你的殿，有你的靈住在其中。我們曾經驕傲、屬世，誇耀人而不誇耀基督，我們的紛爭顯出我們仍是嬰孩；求你赦免我們。感謝你賜給我們的領袖，幫助我們視他們為你的僕人，並為他們禱告。主啊，惟有你叫它生長，求你保守我們不倚靠人的能力，也不懼怕我們的工作是徒然的。幫助我們每一個人，在獨一的根基耶穌基督之上謹慎建造：宣揚祂、追求合一、實踐聖潔、以他人為先。求你使 Ridgeway 教會在聖奧爾本斯成為你聖潔的居所。奉耶穌的名求，阿們。
