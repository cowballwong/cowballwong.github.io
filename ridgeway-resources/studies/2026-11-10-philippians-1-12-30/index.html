🧠1. Memory verse 背誦經文

Answers question 6: "What verse would you like to memorise?" 回應第 6 題

The last question asks which verse we would like to memorise, and each of us is encouraged to memorise a portion of Philippians. My suggestion is v. 21: short, central to the passage, and easy to carry into the week. For a longer option, try v. 27, the passage's one command. If you learned 1:6 last time, say it first, then add the new verse.

最後一條題目問大家想背誦哪一節經文；也鼓勵每人背誦腓立比書的一段。我建議背 21 節：簡短，是全段的核心，也容易帶進每天的生活。若想背較長的，可以選 27 節，即全段唯一的命令。若上次已背了 1 章 6 節，先背一次，再加上新的經文。

🧭3. Context and structure 背景與結構

Last time (1:1-11) Paul greeted the church, thanked God for their partnership in the gospel and prayed for them. Now he turns to his own news. He is in prison (1:13), most likely in Rome (Acts 28:16, 30-31); the options are set out in our 13 October overview notes. The Philippians had sent Epaphroditus with gifts for him (2:25; 4:18), so they would want to know how he was. Paul answers in an unexpected way: he says little about his comfort and a great deal about the gospel. Verses 12-26 are about Paul: the gospel is advancing, and Christ will be honoured whether he lives or dies. Verses 27-30 turn to the church: live worthy of the gospel, stand firm together, and do not be afraid.

上次（1 章 1-11 節）保羅向教會問安，為他們同心興旺福音感恩，並為他們禱告。現在他轉而交代自己的近況。他身在捆鎖之中（1 章 13 節），地點多數認為是羅馬（使徒行傳 28 章 16、30-31 節）；各種看法見 10 月 13 日的概覽筆記。腓立比信徒曾託以巴弗提把饋送帶給保羅（2 章 25 節；4 章 18 節），自然關心他的景況。保羅的回答出人意表：他很少談自己的安危，卻大談福音。12-26 節講保羅自己：福音正在興旺，無論他是生是死，基督都要顯大。27-30 節轉向教會：行事為人要與福音相稱，同心站立得穩，不要懼怕。

1:12-18 The gospel advances through Paul's chains 捆鎖叫福音興旺

His imprisonment has made Christ known throughout the imperial guard and has made other believers bold (vv. 12-14). Some preach Christ from rivalry, others from love; either way Christ is proclaimed, and Paul rejoices (vv. 15-18).

他受的捆鎖叫御營全軍都知道是為基督的緣故，也使其他弟兄放膽傳道（12-14 節）。有人出於嫉妒紛爭傳基督，有人出於愛心；無論怎樣，基督被傳開了，保羅就歡喜（15-18 節）。

1:19-26 To live is Christ, to die is gain 活著就是基督，死了就有益處

Through their prayers and the help of the Spirit, Christ will be honoured in his body by life or by death (vv. 19-20). Dying would be far better for him, but staying is more necessary for them, so he expects to remain for their progress and joy in the faith (vv. 21-26).

藉著他們的祈禱和聖靈的幫助，無論是生是死，基督都要在他身上顯大（19-20 節）。離世與基督同在是好得無比的，但為了他們，留在世上更是要緊，所以他知道自己仍要住在世間，使他們在信心上長進和喜樂（21-26 節）。

1:27-30 A life worthy of the gospel 與福音相稱的生活

Live as citizens worthy of the gospel: stand firm in one spirit, strive side by side, and do not be frightened by opponents (vv. 27-28). Suffering for Christ has been granted to them as a gift; they share the same conflict as Paul (vv. 29-30).

行事為人要與基督的福音相稱：同有一個心志，站立得穩，齊心努力，不怕敵人的驚嚇（27-28 節）。為基督受苦是神賜給他們的恩典；他們與保羅經歷同樣的爭戰（29-30 節）。

🔍4. Verse by verse: observe, interpret, apply 逐節研讀：觀察、解釋、應用

Answers question 1: "Read and study the passage verse by verse: – observe, interpret and apply" 回應第 1 題

Verses 12-14: Chains that advance the gospel 第 12-14 節：叫福音興旺的捆鎖

Observe 觀察

  • What Paul wants them to know: “what has happened to me has really served to advance the gospel”. (v. 12)
  • First result: it has become known “throughout the whole imperial guard and to all the rest that my imprisonment is for Christ”. (v. 13)
  • Second result: “most of the brothers” are now “much more bold to speak the word without fear”. (v. 14)
  • 保羅要他們知道：「我所遭遇的事更是叫福音興旺」。（12 節）
  • 第一個結果：他受的捆鎖「在御營全軍和其餘的人中，已經顯明是為基督的緣故」。（13 節）
  • 第二個結果：弟兄「多半」因此「越發放膽傳神的道，無所懼怕」。（14 節）

Interpret 解釋

Paul reads his chains through the gospel, not the gospel through his chains. Prison shut him in but did not shut the gospel in: it reached the soldiers who guarded him, and his courage gave other believers courage. The question he asks is not whether he is comfortable, but whether Christ is being made known.

保羅用福音的眼光看自己的捆鎖，而不是用捆鎖的眼光看福音。監牢關住了他，卻關不住福音：福音傳到看守他的士兵當中，他的勇氣也帶給其他信徒勇氣。他問的不是自己舒不舒服，而是基督有沒有被人認識。

Key words 字詞

vv. 12, 25 advance / progress 興旺／長進 (prokopē)

Forward progress. Paul uses the same word for the gospel in v. 12 (“to advance the gospel”) and for the believers in v. 25 (“your progress and joy in the faith”): the gospel moving outward and faith growing inward.

原文 prokopē，指向前推進。保羅在 12 節用它講福音（「叫福音興旺」），在 25 節用同一個字講信徒（「又長進又喜樂」）：福音向外推展，信心向內增長。

v. 13 the whole imperial guard 御營全軍 (praitōrion)

ESV footnote: “in the whole praetorium”. The word can mean the emperor's elite guard or a governor's headquarters. On the Rome view, soldiers took turns guarding Paul (Acts 28:16), so the gospel reached them one shift at a time.

原文 praitōrion，可指皇帝的禁衛軍，也可指總督的官邸。若保羅是在羅馬，看守他的兵輪班更替（使徒行傳 28 章 16 節），福音就這樣一班一班地傳到他們當中。

v. 14 confident in the Lord ... bold 篤信不疑……放膽 (pepoithotas, tolman aphobōs)

Their confidence is “in the Lord”, not in good circumstances. Seeing Paul in chains did not frighten them; it made them “much more bold to speak the word without fear”.

他們的信心是「在主裡」，不是在順境裡。看見保羅被捆鎖，他們沒有退縮，反而「越發放膽傳神的道，無所懼怕」。

Apply 應用

  1. Name one hard thing that has happened to you this year. Ask God how it could serve the gospel, and who may be watching how you handle it.
  1. 說出今年發生在你身上的一件難事。求神指示這事可以怎樣叫福音興旺，也想想有誰正在留意你怎樣面對它。

For discussion 討論

Paul says what happened to him “has really served to advance the gospel”. How does he see his circumstances differently from how most people would?
保羅說他「所遭遇的事更是叫福音興旺」。他看自己處境的方式，與一般人有甚麼不同？

Verses 15-18: Christ proclaimed, whatever the motive 第 15-18 節：無論動機如何，基督被傳開

Observe 觀察

  • Two kinds of preachers: some “from envy and rivalry”, others “from good will”. (v. 15)
  • The second group act “out of love”, knowing Paul is “put here for the defense of the gospel”. (v. 16)
  • The first group act “out of selfish ambition, not sincerely”, hoping “to afflict me in my imprisonment”. (v. 17)
  • Paul's response: “What then?” Whatever the motive, “Christ is proclaimed, and in that I rejoice”. (v. 18)
  • 兩種傳道的人：有的「出於嫉妒紛爭」，有的「出於好意」。（15 節）
  • 後者「是出於愛心」，知道保羅「是為辯明福音設立的」。（16 節）
  • 前者「出於結黨，並不誠實」，意思要「加增我捆鎖的苦楚」。（17 節）
  • 保羅的回應：「這有何妨呢？」無論動機如何，「基督究竟被傳開了。為此我就歡喜」。（18 節）

Interpret 解釋

These rival preachers are not false teachers. Elsewhere Paul is fierce about a different gospel (Galatians 1:8-9; Philippians 3:2), but here they preach the true Christ with mixed motives. Paul separates the message from the messenger. His joy does not depend on his own reputation, but on Christ being proclaimed.

這些與保羅較勁的傳道人並不是假教師。保羅對別的福音毫不留情（加拉太書 1 章 8-9 節；腓立比書 3 章 2 節），但這些人傳的是真基督，只是動機不純。保羅把信息與傳信息的人分開。他的喜樂不在於自己的名聲，而在於基督被傳開。

Key words 字詞

vv. 15, 17 envy and rivalry ... selfish ambition 嫉妒紛爭……結黨 (phthonos, eris, eritheia)

phthonos is envy, eris is strife. eritheia first meant working for hire and came to mean self-seeking, party spirit. Paul warns the Philippians against the same thing in 2:3.

phthonos 指嫉妒，eris 指紛爭。eritheia 原指受僱為人做事，後來引申為自私、結黨營私。保羅在 2 章 3 節也勸腓立比信徒不可這樣。

v. 16 the defense of the gospel 辯明福音 (apologia)

A reasoned defence, as in a court. Paul sees his prison not as an accident but as a post: he is “put here for the defense of the gospel”.

原文 apologia，指像在法庭上的答辯。保羅不把坐監看成意外，而是一個崗位：他「是為辯明福音設立的」。

v. 18 in pretense or in truth ... I rejoice 或是假意，或是真心……我就歡喜 (prophasis, alētheia, chairō)

prophasis is a pretext, a cover for another motive. Paul does not approve of bad motives, but he will not let them steal his joy: “Christ is proclaimed, and in that I rejoice”.

prophasis 指藉口、掩飾別的動機。保羅並不認同不良的動機，但不讓它奪去他的喜樂：「基督究竟被傳開了。為此我就歡喜」。

Apply 應用

  1. Think of a church or Christian whose success makes you uneasy. Thank God by name for the way Christ is proclaimed through them.
  1. 想一間教會或一位基督徒，他們的成功令你不自在。按名字為基督藉著他們被傳開而感謝神。

For discussion 討論

How can Paul rejoice when some preach Christ “from envy and rivalry”? Where is the line between this and accepting false teaching?
有人「出於嫉妒紛爭」傳基督，保羅怎能仍然歡喜？這與接受錯誤教導的界線在哪裡？

Verses 19-20: Christ honoured in my body 第 19-20 節：基督在我身上顯大

Observe 觀察

  • Joy looking forward: “Yes, and I will rejoice”, because he knows that “this will turn out for my deliverance”. (vv. 18-19)
  • The means: “through your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ”. (v. 19)
  • His hope: “that I will not be at all ashamed”, but that “Christ will be honored in my body, whether by life or by death”. (v. 20)
  • 向前看的喜樂：「並且還要歡喜」，因為他知道這事「終必叫我得救」。（18-19 節）
  • 憑藉甚麼：「藉著你們的祈禱和耶穌基督之靈的幫助」。（19 節）
  • 他的盼望：「沒有一事叫我羞愧」，而是「無論是生是死，總叫基督在我身上照常顯大」。（20 節）

Interpret 解釋

Paul expects a good outcome, but not necessarily release. Whether he lives or dies, his one hope is that Christ will be shown to be great in his body, and that he will not be ashamed. The church's prayers and the Spirit's supply work together to bring this about.

保羅期待美好的結局，但不一定是獲釋。無論是生是死，他唯一的盼望是基督在他身上顯為大，而他自己不至羞愧。教會的禱告和聖靈的供應一同成就這事。

Key words 字詞

v. 19 the help of the Spirit 耶穌基督之靈的幫助 (epichorēgia)

A generous supply, the word for a sponsor who meets all the costs. Paul puts the church's prayers and the Spirit's supply side by side: “through your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ”.

原文 epichorēgia，指充足的供應，好像資助者承擔全部費用。保羅把教會的禱告與聖靈的供應並列：「藉著你們的祈禱和耶穌基督之靈的幫助」。

vv. 19-20 deliverance 得救 (sōtēria)

This could mean release from prison, but v. 20 says Christ will be honoured “whether by life or by death”. So Paul most likely means his final vindication before God, which is sure either way.

原文 sōtēria，可以指獲釋出獄；但 20 節說「無論是生是死」，所以保羅最可能是指在神面前最終得蒙稱義、得著拯救，這是無論生死都確定的。

v. 20 eager expectation ... honored 切慕……顯大 (apokaradokia, megalynthēsetai)

apokaradokia pictures someone craning the neck to look ahead. megalynō means to make great, to magnify: Paul's body is the place where Christ is to be seen as great.

apokaradokia 形容伸長脖子向前張望的熱切期待。megalynō 指使之顯為大：保羅的身體是讓人看見基督偉大的地方。

Apply 應用

  1. Ask one person in the group to pray for you about a specific need this week, and pray for them in return.
  1. 這星期請小組中一位肢體為你一件具體的需要禱告，你也為他禱告。

For discussion 討論

Paul relies on “your prayers and the help of the Spirit”. What does this say about how much our prayers for each other matter?
保羅倚靠「你們的祈禱和耶穌基督之靈的幫助」。這對我們彼此代禱的重要性有甚麼啟示？

Verses 21-26: To live is Christ, to die is gain 第 21-26 節：活著就是基督，死了就有益處

Observe 觀察

  • The key sentence: “For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain.” (v. 21)
  • If he lives: “fruitful labor for me”. Yet “which I shall choose I cannot tell”. (v. 22)
  • His desire: “to depart and be with Christ, for that is far better”. (v. 23)
  • His decision: to remain is “more necessary on your account”, so he will stay “for your progress and joy in the faith”. (vv. 24-25)
  • The goal: that they may “glory in Christ Jesus”, not in Paul. (v. 26)
  • 核心的一句：「因我活著就是基督，我死了就有益處。」（21 節）
  • 若活著：可以「成就我工夫的果子」。然而「我就不知道該挑選什麼」。（22 節）
  • 他的心願：「情願離世與基督同在，因為這是好得無比的」。（23 節）
  • 他的決定：留在肉身「為你們更是要緊的」，所以他要留下，「使你們在所信的道上又長進又喜樂」。（24-25 節）
  • 目標：讓他們「在基督耶穌裡的歡樂」越發加增，而不是以保羅為誇。（26 節）

Interpret 解釋

If to live is Christ, then to die is gain, because death brings Paul to Christ himself. If life were about anything else, death would be pure loss. This is not a wish to die: Paul chooses to stay, because his life is for others' growth. A life that is Christ looks outward, as Christ himself did (2:4-8).

如果活著就是基督，死了就是益處，因為死亡使保羅與基督同在。如果人生是為了別的東西，死亡就只是損失。這不是求死的心：保羅選擇留下，因為他的生命是為了別人的成長。以基督為生命的人，眼光向外，正如基督自己一樣（2 章 4-8 節）。

Key words 字詞

v. 21 to live is Christ ... gain 活著就是基督……益處 (to zēn Christos, kerdos)

The Greek has no verb; word for word it reads: to me the living, Christ; the dying, gain. kerdos is profit, the word for a business gain. Paul gives up profit for Christ in 3:7.

原文沒有動詞，逐字直譯是：對我來說，活，是基督；死，是益處。kerdos 是生意上的盈利；保羅在 3 章 7 節也說他把這些看作有損的，為的是基督。

v. 22 fruitful labor 工夫的果子 (karpos ergou)

Literally fruit of work. For Paul, staying alive means more work that bears fruit in other people's lives.

原文直譯是工作的果子。對保羅來說，活著就是可以繼續做工，在別人生命中結出果子。

v. 23 hard pressed ... depart 兩難之間……離世 (synechomai, analysai)

synechomai: held tight, squeezed from both sides. analyō was used for loosing a ship from its moorings or striking a tent: death as setting off on a journey home, “to depart and be with Christ”.

synechomai 指被兩邊夾緊。analyō 可用於解纜開船或拆下帳棚：死亡好像起程回家，「離世與基督同在」。

Apply 應用

  1. Write down one decision this week that you will make by asking what serves Christ and other people, not only what you would prefer.
  1. 寫下這星期的一個決定：你會按甚麼能服侍基督和別人來決定，而不只是按自己的喜好。

For discussion 討論

Paul would rather “depart and be with Christ”, yet chooses to stay. What decides it for him? What does that teach us about our own choices?
保羅「情願離世與基督同在」，卻選擇留下。是甚麼令他作這決定？這對我們自己的抉擇有甚麼教導？

Verses 27-30: Worthy of the gospel 第 27-30 節：與福音相稱

Observe 觀察

  • The one command of the passage: “let your manner of life be worthy of the gospel of Christ”. (v. 27)
  • Whether Paul is there or not: “whether I come and see you or am absent”. (v. 27)
  • What he hopes to hear: “standing firm in one spirit, with one mind striving side by side for the faith of the gospel”. (v. 27)
  • Facing opposition: “not frightened in anything by your opponents”; their courage is a sign of “your salvation, and that from God”. (v. 28)
  • A gift they did not expect: “not only believe in him but also suffer for his sake”, in “the same conflict” as Paul. (vv. 29-30)
  • 全段唯一的命令：「你們行事為人與基督的福音相稱」。（27 節）
  • 無論保羅在不在場：「或來見你們，或不在你們那裡」。（27 節）
  • 他盼望聽見的：「同有一個心志，站立得穩，為所信的福音齊心努力」。（27 節）
  • 面對反對：「凡事不怕敵人的驚嚇」；這是證明「你們得救，都是出於神」。（28 節）
  • 一份意想不到的恩典：「不但得以信服基督，並要為他受苦」，與保羅經歷同樣的「爭戰」。（29-30 節）

Interpret 解釋

The gospel is the constitution of a new citizenship. In a city proud of its Roman status, the church is to live as citizens of heaven, and that shows in three ways: unity (one spirit, one mind), teamwork (striving side by side), and courage (not frightened). Suffering is not a sign that God has left them; it is part of what he has graciously given, and it joins them to Paul.

福音好像新國籍的憲章。在一個以羅馬身份為榮的城市裡，教會要作天上的國民，並在三方面顯明出來：合一（同有一個心志）、同工（齊心努力）、勇氣（不怕驚嚇）。受苦不表示神離棄了他們；這是神恩典所賜的一部分，也使他們與保羅同行。

Key words 字詞

v. 27 let your manner of life be worthy 行事為人與……相稱 (politeuesthe, axiōs)

ESV footnote: “Greek Only behave as citizens worthy”. Philippi was a Roman colony whose people were proud to be Romans (Acts 16:12, 21); Christians are citizens of heaven (3:20). axiōs means of matching weight, fitting.

原文 politeuesthe 意思是作為公民而生活。腓立比是羅馬的殖民地，居民以羅馬人的身份為榮（使徒行傳 16 章 12、21 節）；而基督徒是天上的國民（3 章 20 節）。axiōs 指分量相稱、配得上。

v. 27 one spirit, one mind, striving side by side 同有一個心志……齊心努力 (en heni pneumati, mia psychē, synathlountes)

synathleō: to contend together, like athletes on one team. Unity here is not just getting along, but “striving side by side for the faith of the gospel”.

synathleō 指一同角力，好像同一隊的運動員。這裡的合一不只是相處融洽，而是「為所信的福音齊心努力」。

v. 28 not frightened 不怕……驚嚇 (ptyromenoi)

A word used of horses that shy or bolt. The church is not to be startled into panic by “your opponents”.

原文 ptyromenoi，曾用來形容馬匹受驚亂竄。教會不要被敵人嚇得驚慌失措：「凡事不怕敵人的驚嚇」。

vv. 29-30 granted ... conflict 蒙恩……爭戰 (echaristhē, agōna)

echaristhē comes from charis, grace: suffering for Christ is given as a gift, alongside faith. agōn is a contest or struggle; the Philippians had seen Paul's in their own city (Acts 16:19-24).

echaristhē 與 charis（恩典）同一字根：為基督受苦和信服基督一樣，都是神所賜的。agōn 指競賽、搏鬥；腓立比信徒曾在本城親眼看見保羅的爭戰（使徒行傳 16 章 19-24 節）。

Apply 應用

  1. Is there someone in the church you need to be reconciled with, so that we can stand “in one spirit”? Take a first step this week.
  1. 教會中有沒有你需要和好的人，好讓我們能「同有一個心志」？這星期踏出第一步。

For discussion 討論

Why does Paul connect being “worthy of the gospel” so closely with unity, “in one spirit, with one mind”?
為甚麼保羅把「與基督的福音相稱」與「同有一個心志」的合一連在一起？

🧩5. Main themes 主要主題

Answers question 2: "Write down the main themes or topics of what Paul said to the church" 回應第 2 題

Observe 觀察

  • Count the words: in these 19 verses (ESV) “gospel” comes 4 times, “Christ” 11 times, and “rejoice” or “joy” 3 times. (vv. 12-30)
  • The passage opens with the gospel advancing (v. 12) and ends with the church striving for “the faith of the gospel” (v. 27). (vv. 12, 27)
  • The first half is about Paul (“what has happened to me”, v. 12); from v. 27 Paul turns to “you”. (vv. 12, 26, 27)
  • 數一數字詞（ESV）：在這 19 節中，gospel（福音）出現 4 次，Christ（基督）11 次，rejoice 或 joy（歡喜、喜樂）3 次。（12-30 節）
  • 經文以福音興旺開始（12 節），又以教會「為所信的福音齊心努力」作結（27 節）。（12、27 節）
  • 前半段講保羅自己（「我所遭遇的事」，12 節）；從 27 節起，保羅轉向「你們」。（12、26、27 節）

Main themes 主要主題

  1. The gospel advances through hardship: Paul's chains spread the gospel and made others bold. (vv. 12-14)
  2. Christ proclaimed matters more than my reputation: Paul rejoices even when rivals preach. (vv. 15-18)
  3. Joy that does not depend on circumstances: He rejoices now, will rejoice, and wants their joy. (vv. 18, 19, 25)
  4. Prayer and the Spirit: The church's prayers and the Spirit's help sustain him. (v. 19)
  5. Christ at the centre of life and death: Life is Christ, death is gain; Christ is honoured either way. (vv. 20-24)
  6. Living for others' progress: Paul puts their growth before his own desire. (vv. 24-26)
  7. A life worthy of the gospel: Citizens of heaven: united, steady and unafraid. (vv. 27-28)
  8. Suffering as a gift: To suffer for Christ is granted, like faith, and shared with Paul. (vv. 29-30)
  1. 福音在困境中興旺：保羅的捆鎖使福音傳開，也使別人放膽傳道。（12-14 節）
  2. 基督被傳開比我的名聲更重要：即使對手傳道，保羅仍然歡喜。（15-18 節）
  3. 不受環境左右的喜樂：他現在歡喜，將來還要歡喜，也要他們喜樂。（18、19、25 節）
  4. 禱告與聖靈：教會的禱告和聖靈的幫助扶持著他。（19 節）
  5. 生與死都以基督為中心：活著就是基督，死了就有益處；無論生死，基督都顯大。（20-24 節）
  6. 為別人的長進而活：保羅把他們的成長放在自己的心願之前。（24-26 節）
  7. 與福音相稱的生活：作天上的國民：同心、堅定、不怕。（27-28 節）
  8. 受苦是恩典：為基督受苦與信服基督一樣是神所賜，並與保羅一同經歷。（29-30 節）

Interpret 解釋

One thread runs through it all: Christ and his gospel at the centre. Paul measures his prison, his rivals, his life and his death by one test: is Christ made known and honoured? Then he asks the church to measure their life by the same test.

全段貫穿一條主線：以基督和他的福音為中心。保羅用同一個標準衡量他的監禁、他的對手、他的生與死：基督有沒有被傳揚、被尊崇？然後他請教會用同一個標準衡量自己的生活。

Apply 應用

  1. Choose the one theme that speaks to you most and write it on the first page of your Bible or in your phone notes.
  1. 選出最觸動你的一個主題，寫在聖經的扉頁或手機的筆記裡。

For discussion 討論

Which of these themes speaks most to where you are this month, and why?
以上哪一個主題最切合你這個月的處境？為甚麼？

📣6. How can we progress the gospel? 怎樣使福音興旺

Answers question 3: "How can we progress the gospel?" 回應第 3 題

Observe 觀察

  • A witness under pressure: people learn “my imprisonment is for Christ”. (v. 13)
  • Ordinary believers made bold: “to speak the word without fear”. (v. 14)
  • Christ is proclaimed, even by imperfect people: “in every way”. (v. 18)
  • Prayer and the Spirit: “through your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ”. (v. 19)
  • Believers growing: “your progress and joy in the faith”. (v. 25)
  • A united church: “striving side by side for the faith of the gospel”. (v. 27)
  • 在壓力下的見證：人知道保羅的捆鎖「是為基督的緣故」。（13 節）
  • 普通信徒變得勇敢：「放膽傳神的道，無所懼怕」。（14 節）
  • 基督被傳開，甚至藉著不完全的人：「無論怎樣」。（18 節）
  • 禱告和聖靈：「藉著你們的祈禱和耶穌基督之靈的幫助」。（19 節）
  • 信徒的成長：「又長進又喜樂」。（25 節）
  • 合一的教會：「為所信的福音齊心努力」。（27 節）

Interpret 解釋

In this passage the gospel progresses mainly through people, not programmes: a believer who suffers well, believers who speak, a church that prays, and a church that stays united. Gospel progress is also two-way: outward (v. 12) and inward, in the believers' faith (v. 25).

在這段經文中，福音主要是藉著人而興旺，而不是藉著活動：在苦難中見證的信徒、開口傳道的信徒、禱告的教會、合一的教會。福音的興旺也有兩方面：向外推展（12 節），以及信徒信心的長進（25 節）。

Apply 應用

  1. Pray by name for one person you would love to know Christ, and look for one natural opening to talk about faith with them.
  2. Be open about why you face hard things the way you do. A short answer such as ‘I am praying about it’ can start a conversation.
  3. Support a gospel worker, at home or overseas, by prayer or giving this month.
  1. 按名字為一位你盼望他認識基督的人禱告，並留意一個自然的機會與他談信仰。
  2. 坦然說出你面對難處時的態度從何而來。簡單說一句自己正為此禱告，也可以開始一段對話。
  3. 這個月以禱告或奉獻支持一位在本地或海外的福音工作者。

For discussion 討論

Most of the brothers became “much more bold to speak the word without fear”. What makes us afraid to speak about Jesus, and what might make us bolder?
弟兄「多半」因此「越發放膽傳神的道，無所懼怕」。甚麼令我們不敢談論耶穌？甚麼可以使我們更勇敢？
What could our group do together, side by side, to help the gospel go forward in St Albans this season?
我們小組在這段時間可以一同做甚麼，齊心推動福音在聖奧爾本斯興旺？

✨7. What does it mean that to live is Christ? 活著就是基督是甚麼意思

Answers question 4: "What does it mean to live is Christ?" 回應第 4 題

Observe 觀察

  • Just before it, Paul's aim: “Christ will be honored in my body”. (v. 20)
  • The sentence itself: “to live is Christ, and to die is gain”. (v. 21)
  • What living means in practice: “fruitful labor”. (v. 22)
  • What dying means: “be with Christ, for that is far better”. (v. 23)
  • How it shapes his choice: he stays “on your account”, “for your progress and joy in the faith”. (vv. 24-25)
  • 前一節保羅的目標：「總叫基督在我身上照常顯大」。（20 節）
  • 這句話本身：「活著就是基督，我死了就有益處」。（21 節）
  • 活著的實際意思：「工夫的果子」。（22 節）
  • 死的意思：「與基督同在，因為這是好得無比的」。（23 節）
  • 這怎樣影響他的抉擇：他留下來是「為你們」，「使你們在所信的道上又長進又喜樂」。（24-25 節）

Interpret 解釋

To live is Christ means Christ is the source, the purpose and the joy of my life. The source: Christ lives in me (Galatians 2:20). The purpose: that Christ be honoured in my body (v. 20). The joy: being with him is far better (v. 23), so death becomes gain (2 Corinthians 5:8). And the shape of such a life is service: Paul stays for others (vv. 24-25).

活著就是基督，意思是基督是我生命的源頭、目的和喜樂。源頭：是基督在我裡面活著（加拉太書 2 章 20 節）。目的：叫基督在我身上顯大（20 節）。喜樂：與基督同在是好得無比的（23 節），所以死了也是益處（哥林多後書 5 章 8 節）。這樣的生命，外在的樣式就是服侍：保羅為別人而留下（24-25 節）。

What it means 意思

  1. Source: my life comes from Christ, who lives in me (Galatians 2:20).
  2. Purpose: my aim is that Christ be honoured in my body, in words, work, money and time (v. 20).
  3. Joy: Christ himself is my treasure, so even death is gain (vv. 21, 23).
  4. Shape: a life that is Christ serves others, as Paul stays for their progress and joy (vv. 24-25).
  1. 源頭：我的生命來自基督，是基督在我裡面活著（加拉太書 2 章 20 節）。
  2. 目的：我的目標是叫基督在我身上顯大，包括言語、工作、金錢和時間（20 節）。
  3. 喜樂：基督自己是我的珍寶，所以連死也是益處（21、23 節）。
  4. 樣式：以基督為生命的人會服侍別人，正如保羅為他們的長進和喜樂而留下（24-25 節）。

A simple test: finish the sentence “For to me to live is” honestly. If it is my career, family, comfort or reputation, then death means losing it. Only if it is Christ can the sentence end “and to die is gain”.

一個簡單的測試：誠實地接續「因我活著就是」這半句。如果答案是事業、家庭、安逸或名聲，死亡就意味著失去它；只有答案是基督，這句話才能接下去說「我死了就有益處」。

Apply 應用

  1. Pick one area (work, money, screen time, family) and ask: what would it look like for Christ to be honoured there this week?
  1. 選一個範疇（工作、金錢、上網時間、家庭），問自己：這星期基督怎樣可以在這方面顯大？

For discussion 討論

Finish the sentence honestly: “For to me to live is” what? What would your week suggest the answer is?
誠實地接續「因我活著就是」這半句。從你這星期的生活看，答案會是甚麼？
How does believing that “to die is gain” change the way we face illness, ageing or danger?
相信「死了就有益處」，會怎樣改變我們面對疾病、年老或危險的態度？

🤝8. A life worthy of the gospel 與福音相稱的生命

Answers question 5: "How would you describe a life worthy of the gospel of Christ?" 回應第 5 題

Observe 觀察

  • Its standard: “worthy of the gospel of Christ”. (v. 27)
  • Its consistency: the same “whether I come and see you or am absent”. (v. 27)
  • Its unity: “in one spirit, with one mind”. (v. 27)
  • Its teamwork: “striving side by side”. (v. 27)
  • Its courage: “not frightened in anything”. (v. 28)
  • Its cost, received as a gift: “it has been granted to you”, “also suffer for his sake”. (v. 29)
  • 標準：「與基督的福音相稱」。（27 節）
  • 始終如一：無論保羅「或來見你們，或不在你們那裡」。（27 節）
  • 合一：「同有一個心志」。（27 節）
  • 同工：「齊心努力」。（27 節）
  • 勇氣：「凡事不怕敵人的驚嚇」。（28 節）
  • 代價，卻是恩典：「你們蒙恩」，「並要為他受苦」。（29 節）

Interpret 解釋

Being “worthy” does not mean earning the gospel. The gospel is a gift. It means living in a way that matches its value, like a citizen who lives up to the name of his city. Paul does not list private virtues here; he describes a church: together, steady, brave, and willing to suffer.

「相稱」不是說要賺取福音；福音是白白的恩典。相稱是指生活與福音的價值吻合，好像一個公民的言行配得上他城市的名聲。保羅在這裡列出的不是個人的美德，而是一個教會的樣子：同心、堅定、勇敢，並且甘願受苦。

Six marks 六個標記

  1. Citizens of heaven in St Albans: our behaviour reflects our true home (3:20).
  2. Consistent: the same whether the leader is watching or not (v. 27).
  3. United: one spirit and one mind, not rival groups (v. 27).
  4. Working together: striving side by side for the faith, like one team (v. 27).
  5. Unafraid: steady under pressure, because salvation is from God (v. 28).
  6. Willing to suffer: accepting hardship for Christ as part of his gift (vv. 29-30).
  1. 在聖奧爾本斯作天上的國民：我們的行為反映我們真正的家鄉（3 章 20 節）。
  2. 始終如一：無論帶領的人在不在場，都是一樣（27 節）。
  3. 合一：同有一個心志，而不是分成不同派別（27 節）。
  4. 同工：像同一隊的隊員，為所信的福音齊心努力（27 節）。
  5. 無懼：在壓力下站穩，因為得救是出於神（28 節）。
  6. 甘願受苦：把為基督受的苦看作他恩典的一部分（29-30 節）。

My answer in one sentence: a life worthy of the gospel is one where the way I live, especially with other believers and under pressure, matches the good news I say I believe.

用一句話回答：與福音相稱的生命，就是我的生活方式，特別是與其他信徒相處和面對壓力的時候，與我所信的好消息相符。

Apply 應用

  1. Choose one of the six marks above and practise it on purpose this week, for example by welcoming someone new on Sunday.
  1. 從上面六個標記中選一個，這星期刻意實踐，例如在主日主動接待一位新朋友。

For discussion 討論

Which mark of a worthy life is strongest in our group, and which is weakest?
與福音相稱的生命有幾個標記：在我們小組中，哪一個最強？哪一個最弱？
Paul says suffering for Christ is “granted”. Where are Christians suffering for their faith today, and how can we stand with them?
保羅說為基督受苦是「蒙恩」。今天哪裡有基督徒為信仰受苦？我們可以怎樣與他們同行？

Prayer points from 1:12-30 代禱事項

  1. Thanks that God can use even hard circumstances to advance the gospel; boldness to speak the word without fear. (vv. 12, 14)
  2. Joy whenever Christ is proclaimed, and hearts free from envy and rivalry. (vv. 15, 18)
  3. For our prayers for one another, and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ. (v. 19)
  4. That Christ will be honoured in our bodies, whether by life or by death. (vv. 20-21)
  5. That we live for one another's progress and joy in the faith. (vv. 24-25)
  6. That our church lives worthy of the gospel, standing firm in one spirit and one mind. (v. 27)
  7. Courage when opposed, and strength for Christians who suffer for Christ today. (vv. 28-30)
  1. 感謝神連困境也能用來叫福音興旺；求神使我們放膽傳講他的道，無所懼怕。（12、14 節）
  2. 凡基督被傳開，我們就歡喜；求神除去我們心中的嫉妒紛爭。（15、18 節）
  3. 為我們彼此的代禱，並求耶穌基督之靈的幫助。（19 節）
  4. 求神使基督在我們身上顯大，無論是生是死。（20-21 節）
  5. 求神使我們為彼此在信心上的長進和喜樂而活。（24-25 節）
  6. 求神使我們的教會行事為人與福音相稱，同有一個心志，站立得穩。（27 節）
  7. 求神在我們遇到反對時賜下勇氣，並堅固今天為基督受苦的弟兄姊妹。（28-30 節）

Father, thank you that nothing that happens to us is outside your hands, and that you can use even chains to advance the gospel. Make us bold to speak your word without fear, glad whenever Christ is proclaimed, and free from envy and rivalry. By your Spirit and through our prayers for one another, let Christ be honoured in our bodies, whether by life or by death. Teach us to say that to live is Christ. Help us to live as citizens worthy of the gospel, standing firm together and not afraid. Strengthen all who suffer for Christ today. In Jesus' name, Amen.

天父，感謝你，臨到我們的事沒有一樣在你的掌管之外，連捆鎖你也能用來叫福音興旺。求你使我們放膽傳講你的道，無所懼怕；凡基督被傳開，我們就歡喜，不存嫉妒紛爭的心。求你藉著聖靈的幫助和我們彼此的代禱，使基督在我們身上顯大，無論是生是死。教導我們能說：我活著就是基督。求你幫助我們作天上的國民，行事為人與福音相稱，同心站立得穩，毫不懼怕。求你堅固今天為基督受苦的弟兄姊妹。奉主耶穌的名求，阿們。
