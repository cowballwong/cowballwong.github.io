📖 Summary 摘要
English

An introduction to Philippians. Before the letter, we read how the church in Philippi began (Acts 16, about AD 49–50, Paul's second missionary journey).

Blocked by the Spirit in Asia and Bithynia, Paul sees a man of Macedonia pleading for help. He crosses from Troas to Neapolis and walks inland to Philippi. There the Lord opens Lydia's heart by the river; a slave girl is freed from a spirit of divination; her owners have Paul and Silas beaten and jailed; at midnight they pray and sing, an earthquake opens the doors, and the jailer and his household believe. Next day Paul, a Roman citizen, insists the magistrates themselves escort them out.

Before you study: read Acts 16 at least twice and note what you observe.

繁體中文

腓立比書導論。讀書信之前，先看腓立比教會如何開始（使徒行傳十六章，約主後49至50年，保羅第二次宣教旅程）。

聖靈禁止保羅在亞細亞講道，也不許他往庇推尼去；他在異象中見一個馬其頓人求他過去幫助。他從特羅亞渡海到尼亞坡利，再往內陸到腓立比。在河邊，主開導呂底亞的心；被巫鬼所附的使女得釋放；她的主人們使保羅和西拉被打下監；半夜二人禱告唱詩，地大震動，監門全開，禁卒和他全家都信了主。翌日，身為羅馬公民的保羅要求官長親自領他們出監。

研讀前：請把使徒行傳十六章至少讀兩遍，並記下觀察。

🗺️ Map, Background & Setting 地圖、背景與鋪墊

Map 地圖

Background 背景

English
  • A Roman colony. Founded by Philip II of Macedon; after the battle of Philippi (42 BC) and again under Augustus it was settled with Roman veterans. Citizens were proud to be “Romans” (16:21), ran the city on Roman law, and the magistrates had lictors with rods (16:22, 35).
  • No synagogue. The Jewish community seems too small for one, so Paul looks for a “place of prayer” by the river (probably the Gangites) outside the gate (16:13).
  • Lydia is from Thyatira in Asia, a centre of the purple-dye trade: a businesswoman, a “God-fearer” (a Gentile worshipper of Israel's God), head of a household large enough to host the church (16:40).
  • The jailer was likely a public servant or retired soldier answerable with his life for the prisoners (compare 12:19), hence his drawn sword (16:27).
  • Paul's Roman citizenship. Beating a citizen without trial was illegal. Paul raises it only afterwards (16:37–38), which protects the new believers left behind.
繁體中文
  • 羅馬駐防城（殖民地）。此城由馬其頓王腓力二世建立；腓立比之戰（主前42年）後及奧古斯都年間安置羅馬退伍軍人。居民以「羅馬人」自豪（16:21），按羅馬法治理，官長有手執棍杖的差役（16:22、35）。
  • 沒有會堂。當地猶太人似乎太少，未能設立會堂，所以保羅出城到河邊（或為甘吉提斯河）尋找「禱告的地方」（16:13）。
  • 呂底亞來自亞細亞的推雅推喇，當地以紫色染料業聞名。她是商人，是「敬畏神」的外邦人，家大可接待教會（16:40）。
  • 禁卒大概是公職人員或退伍軍人；若囚犯逃脫，他要以性命抵償（參徒12:19），所以他拔刀要自殺（16:27）。
  • 保羅的羅馬公民身分。未經審判鞭打羅馬公民是違法的。保羅事後才提出（16:37–38），藉此保護留下來的初信者。

Acts 16 sets up Philippians 為腓立比書鋪路

English

Paul later writes to this church from prison (Phil 1:7, 13). Acts 16 explains its tone:

  • Joy in suffering: the letter from a prisoner who once sang in the Philippian jail (16:25; Phil 4:4).
  • Partnership in the gospel “from the first day until now” (Phil 1:5): from Lydia's open home onwards they supported Paul (Phil 4:15–16).
  • Citizenship in heaven (Phil 3:20): written to people proud of Roman citizenship, by a man who used his, yet counted a better one.
  • Knowing Christ (Phil 3:10): the heart of the letter.
繁體中文

保羅後來在監中寫信給這教會（腓1:7、13）。使徒行傳十六章正好解釋這封信的語調：

  • 苦中的喜樂：寫信的囚犯，曾在腓立比監中唱詩（16:25；腓4:4）。
  • 興旺福音的同工：「從頭一天直到如今」（腓1:5），自呂底亞開放家庭起，他們一直供給保羅（腓4:15–16）。
  • 天上的國民：「我們卻是天上的國民」（腓3:20），寫給以羅馬公民身分自豪的人，出自一位用過這身分、卻更看重另一個國籍的使徒。
  • 認識基督：「使我認識基督」（腓3:10），正是全書的核心。
🤔 Thinking Questions 思考問題

Where is Philippi located geographically? (use the map above)

In eastern Macedonia (north-east Greece today), about 16 km inland from the port of Neapolis (modern Kavala), on the Via Egnatia, the Roman road linking the Adriatic with Byzantium. From Troas in Asia it was a two-day sail via Samothrace (16:11). It was the first stop in Europe for Paul's team, and the road led on to Amphipolis, Apollonia and Thessalonica (17:1).

腓立比在地理上位於哪裡？（參看上圖）

在馬其頓東部（今希臘東北），離海港尼亞坡利（今卡瓦拉）約十六公里，位於連接亞得里亞海與拜占庭的羅馬大道「埃格那提亞大道」上。從亞細亞的特羅亞經撒摩特喇航行兩天可達（16:11）。這是保羅一行在歐洲的第一站，大道再往西通往暗妃波里、亞波羅尼亞和帖撒羅尼迦（17:1）。

📍 Acts 16:11–12; 17:1

What is the religious and political climate in Philippi?

Politically: a proud Roman colony governed by magistrates, quick to defend “Roman customs” and suspicious of Jews (Claudius had recently expelled Jews from Rome, 18:2). Religiously: pagan and superstitious; a slave girl with a “spirit of divination” (literally a “python spirit”, linked to Apollo's oracle) earned money for her owners. When the gospel threatened profit, the owners dressed a money complaint up as patriotism and anti-Jewish feeling, the crowd joined in, and due process was dropped.

腓立比的宗教和政治氣氛如何？

政治上：以羅馬為傲的殖民地，由官長治理，維護「羅馬人的規矩」，對猶太人存有戒心（革老丟剛把猶太人逐出羅馬，18:2）。宗教上：異教盛行、迷信普遍；使女被「巫鬼」（原文為「皮同之靈」，與阿波羅神諭有關）所附，為主人占卜賺錢。福音威脅到財路時，主人把金錢問題包裝成愛國和反猶的控訴，眾人附和，官長連審訊也省略了。

📍 Acts 16:16–24

Why and how did Paul get to Philippi? And whom did he travel with?

Paul set out to strengthen the churches (15:36, 41; 16:5). The Holy Spirit forbade preaching in Asia, and “the Spirit of Jesus” did not allow Bithynia (16:6–7); at Troas a night vision of a man of Macedonia led them to conclude God had called them there (16:9–10). With him were Silas (15:40), Timothy, whom he took on at Lystra (16:1–3), and, from the “we” in 16:10, very likely Luke. God guided by closed doors and a clear call, and the team reasoned together to obey.

保羅為何及如何來到腓立比？與誰同行？

保羅出發是為了堅固各教會（15:36、41；16:5）。聖靈禁止他們在亞細亞講道，「耶穌的靈」也不許他們往庇推尼去（16:6–7）；在特羅亞，夜間異象中的馬其頓人使他們確定神召他們前往（16:9–10）。同行的有西拉（15:40）、在路司得加入的提摩太（16:1–3），以及從16:10的「我們」看來，很可能還有路加。神藉關上的門和清晰的呼召引導，眾人一同分辨並順服。

📍 Acts 15:36–16:11

How did Lydia and the jailer become converted?

Lydia: a God-fearer at prayer by the river; she listened, and “the Lord opened her heart” to pay attention to Paul's message (16:14). Quiet, gradual, the Lord's work through the word. The jailer: a pagan in crisis; after the midnight earthquake he was about to kill himself; Paul stopped him; trembling, he asked, “What must I do to be saved?” and was told, “Believe in the Lord Jesus” (16:30–31). Paul and Silas then spoke the word of the Lord to all his household (16:32). Very different people and routes, one gospel, and both by grace through faith.

呂底亞和禁卒如何歸信？

呂底亞：一位在河邊禱告、敬畏神的人；她聽道時，「主就開導她的心」，叫她留心聽保羅的話（16:14）。安靜、漸進，是主藉著道作工。禁卒：一位陷入危機的異教徒；半夜地震後他要自殺，保羅阻止他；他戰戰兢兢地問：「我當怎樣行才可以得救？」答案是：「當信主耶穌」（16:30–31）。二人又向他全家講主的道（16:32）。人不同、途徑不同，卻是同一福音，都是本乎恩、因著信。

📍 Acts 16:12–14, 25–32

How did Lydia and the jailer demonstrate their faith?

Both were baptised at once with their households (16:15, 33), a public identification with Jesus. Both showed hospitality: Lydia opened her home and “persuaded” the missionaries to stay (16:15), later hosting the believers (16:40); the jailer washed the wounds he may have helped inflict, brought them into his home, fed them, and rejoiced with his whole household (16:33–34). Faith showed itself in baptism, hospitality, care and joy.

呂底亞和禁卒如何表明他們的信心？

兩人都立刻與全家受洗（16:15、33），公開表明歸屬耶穌。兩人都熱誠接待：呂底亞開放家庭，「強留」宣教士住下（16:15），後來更接待眾信徒（16:40）；禁卒為他們洗傷（這些傷或許正出於他手），領他們回家擺飯，與全家大大喜樂（16:33–34）。信心見於受洗、接待、關顧和喜樂。

📍 Acts 16:15, 33–34, 40

What happened to Paul and Silas?

Seized and dragged to the marketplace, accused falsely, attacked by the crowd, stripped and beaten with rods without trial, thrown into the inner prison with their feet in the stocks (16:19–24). Yet at midnight they prayed and sang hymns (16:25). God sent an earthquake, the jailer was saved, and next day Paul made the magistrates escort them out publicly (16:35–39). Paul would later recall this: “we had suffered before and been shamefully treated at Philippi” (1 Thess 2:2).

保羅和西拉遭遇了甚麼事？

他們被揪住拉到市上，被誣告，被眾人攻擊，未經審判就被剝衣用棍打，下在內監，兩腳上了木狗（16:19–24）。然而半夜他們禱告唱詩（16:25）。神使地大震動，禁卒得救；翌日保羅要求官長公開親自領他們出監（16:35–39）。保羅後來追述：「我們在腓立比被害受辱」（帖前2:2）。

🎯 Application 生活應用
English
1. Closed doors. Where has God said “not here” to you? How did Paul's team respond to a closed door, and to a clear call (16:6–10)?
2. Your Lydia, your jailer. Name one person you are praying for. Is the Lord perhaps opening a heart quietly, or is someone in crisis asking the question? Only the Lord opens hearts; our part is to speak and to be ready (16:14, 30–31).
3. Midnight songs. In a hard place this season, what would it look like to pray and praise with others “listening” (16:25)?
繁體中文
一、關上的門。神在哪裡對你說過「不是這裡」？保羅一行怎樣回應關上的門和清晰的呼召（16:6–10）？
二、你身邊的呂底亞、禁卒。說出一位你正為他禱告的人。主是否正在安靜地開導他的心，還是他正在危機中發問？唯有主能開人的心；我們的本分是傳講和預備好（16:14、30–31）。
三、半夜的詩歌。在這季節的難處中，若禱告讚美時有人「側耳而聽」（16:25），會是怎樣的光景？
