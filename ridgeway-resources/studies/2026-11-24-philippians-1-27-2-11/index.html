🧭3. Context and structure 背景與結構

Last time (1:12-30) Paul told the church how his imprisonment was advancing the gospel, and he closed with one command: live worthy of the gospel (1:27). This study takes that command as the start of a new section that runs on into chapter 2. Many commentators read 1:27 to 2:18 as one unit: the call (1:27-30), the appeal for unity through humility (2:1-4), the example of Christ (2:5-11), and then a fresh 'Therefore' (2:12-18), probably our next passage. The chapter break hides the link: the 'So' at the start of 2:1 ties the appeal for unity back to the struggle of 1:27-30. Philippi was a Roman colony whose people were proud to be Romans (Acts 16:12, 21), and the church had met opposition from the start (Acts 16:19-24). Later in the letter Paul has to urge two women in the church to agree in the Lord (4:2), so unity was a real need, not a theory.

上次（1 章 12-30 節）保羅說明他的捆鎖怎樣叫福音興旺，並以一個命令作結：行事為人要與福音相稱（1 章 27 節）。今次以這個命令作為新一段的開始，一直延續到第 2 章。不少釋經者把 1 章 27 節至 2 章 18 節視為一個單元：呼召（1 章 27-30 節）、勸勉藉謙卑達致合一（2 章 1-4 節）、基督的榜樣（2 章 5-11 節），然後是另一個這樣看來（2 章 12-18 節），大概就是下一次的經文。分章把兩段的關係遮蓋了：2 章 1 節開頭的「所以」，把合一的勸勉與 1 章 27-30 節的爭戰連在一起。腓立比是羅馬的殖民地，居民以羅馬人的身份為榮（使徒行傳 16 章 12、21 節），教會從一開始就遇到反對（使徒行傳 16 章 19-24 節）。保羅在信的後段要勸兩位姊妹在主裡同心（4 章 2 節），可見合一是實際的需要，不是空談。

1:27-30 The call: live as citizens worthy of the gospel 1 章 27-30 節 呼召：作與福音相稱的國民

Live as citizens worthy of the gospel: stand firm together, strive side by side for the faith, and do not be frightened (vv. 27-28). Believing in Christ and suffering for him are both gifts, and they share Paul's struggle (vv. 29-30).

行事為人要與基督的福音相稱：同心站立得穩，為所信的福音齊心努力，不怕敵人的驚嚇（27-28 節）。信服基督和為他受苦都是神所賜的，他們與保羅經歷同樣的爭戰（29-30 節）。

2:1-4 The appeal: one mind through humility 2 章 1-4 節 勸勉：存心謙卑，意念相同

Since they have encouragement, love, fellowship in the Spirit and compassion in Christ, they are to complete Paul's joy by being of one mind (vv. 1-2). That means no rivalry or empty glory, but humility that counts others more significant and looks to their interests (vv. 3-4).

既然他們在基督裡有勸勉、愛心的安慰、聖靈的交通和慈悲憐憫，就要意念相同，使保羅的喜樂可以滿足（1-2 節）。這就是不結黨、不貪圖虛浮的榮耀，而是存心謙卑，看別人比自己強，也顧別人的事（3-4 節）。

2:5-11 The example: Christ humbled, God exalted 2 章 5-11 節 榜樣：基督降卑，神將他升為至高

Have the mind of Christ, who was in the form of God yet emptied himself, took the form of a servant and obeyed to death on a cross (vv. 5-8). Therefore God exalted him, so that every knee will bow and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the Father's glory (vv. 9-11).

要以基督的心為心：他本有神的形象，卻虛己，取了奴僕的形象，存心順服以至於死，且死在十字架上（5-8 節）。所以神將他升為至高，叫萬膝跪拜，萬口承認耶穌基督為主，使榮耀歸於父神（9-11 節）。

🔍4. Verse by verse: observe, interpret, apply 逐節研讀：觀察、解釋、應用

Answers question 1 回應第 1 題

Verses 1:27-28: Citizens who stand firm together 第 1 章 27-28 節：同心站立得穩的國民

Observe 觀察

  • The one command, introduced by “Only”: “let your manner of life be worthy of the gospel of Christ”. (1:27)
  • What Paul hopes to hear: “standing firm in one spirit, with one mind striving side by side for the faith of the gospel”. (1:27)
  • Facing opponents: “not frightened in anything”, and this is “a clear sign”. (1:28)
  • 全段唯一的命令：「只要你們行事為人與基督的福音相稱」。（1 章 27 節）
  • 保羅盼望聽見的：「同有一個心志，站立得穩，為所信的福音齊心努力」。（1 章 27 節）
  • 面對敵人：「凡事不怕敵人的驚嚇」，這是一個「證明」。（1 章 28 節）

Interpret 解釋

Paul's word for living is a citizen's word. In a city proud of Rome, the church is a colony of heaven (3:20). Living worthy of the gospel is not earning it; it is living in a way that matches its value. Notice that every mark is shared: one spirit, one mind, side by side. And courage under pressure is itself a message: it tells opponents they are on the losing side, and it tells believers that their salvation is God's work.

保羅用的是公民的字眼。在一個以羅馬為榮的城市裡，教會是天上的殖民地（3 章 20 節）。與福音相稱不是要賺取福音，而是生活與福音的價值吻合。留意每一個標記都是群體的：同一個心志，齊心努力。在壓力下的勇氣本身就是一個信息：它告訴敵人他們站錯了邊，也告訴信徒，他們得救是神的工作。

Key words 字詞

1:27 let your manner of life be worthy 行事為人與……相稱 (politeuesthe, axiōs)

ESV footnote: “Greek Only behave as citizens worthy”. This is the subtitle on the study sheet. politeuomai means to live as a citizen; axiōs means of matching weight, fitting. In a Roman colony, the church is to live as citizens of heaven (3:20).

原文 politeuesthe 意思是作為公民而生活；axiōs 指分量相稱、配得上。題目紙的副題正是這個意思：「行事為人與基督的福音相稱」。在羅馬的殖民地裡，教會要作天上的國民（3 章 20 節）。

1:27 standing firm ... striving side by side 站立得穩……齊心努力 (stēkete, synathlountes)

stēkō is to stand your ground, a word often used of soldiers holding a line. synathleō is to contend together, like athletes on one team. Both verbs are plural: the picture is a company, not a lone hero.

stēkō 指堅守崗位，常用來形容士兵守住陣線。synathleō 指一同角力，好像同一隊的運動員。兩個動詞都是複數：這是一群人，不是單打獨鬥的英雄。

1:28 not frightened ... a clear sign 不怕……驚嚇……證明 (ptyromenoi, endeixis)

ptyromai was used of horses that shy or bolt. endeixis is a proof, something that points. The church's calm courage points two ways: to the opponents' destruction, and to “your salvation, and that from God”.

ptyromai 曾用來形容馬匹受驚亂竄。endeixis 指證據、指標。教會鎮定的勇氣指向兩方面：敵人的沉淪，以及「你們得救，都是出於神」。

Apply 應用

  1. Think of one setting where you behave differently when nobody from church is watching. Ask God to make you the same person in both places.
  1. 想一個當沒有教會的人看見時，你會表現得不一樣的場合。求神使你在兩個地方都是同一個人。

For discussion 討論

Paul writes “whether I come and see you or am absent”. Why does a church's life when no leader is watching matter so much?
保羅說「叫我或來見你們，或不在你們那裡」。為甚麼教會在沒有領袖看著的時候怎樣生活，是那麼重要？

Verses 1:29-30: Granted to believe and to suffer 第 1 章 29-30 節：蒙恩信服，也為他受苦

Observe 觀察

  • Two gifts: “not only believe in him but also suffer for his sake”. (1:29)
  • The same struggle as Paul: “engaged in the same conflict that you saw I had”. (1:30)
  • 兩份恩賜：「不但得以信服基督，並要為他受苦」。（1 章 29 節）
  • 與保羅同樣的掙扎：「你們的爭戰，就與你們在我身上從前所看見、現在所聽見的一樣」。（1 章 30 節）

Interpret 解釋

Suffering is described as a gift, alongside faith. That does not make pain good in itself. It means that suffering for Christ is not a sign that God has left us. It joins us to Christ and to his people, as the Philippians were joined to Paul's struggle.

受苦與信心並列，都被形容為恩賜。這不是說痛苦本身是好事，而是說為基督受苦並不表示神離棄了我們。受苦使我們與基督和他的子民連在一起，正如腓立比信徒與保羅的爭戰連在一起。

Key words 字詞

1:29; 2:9 granted ... bestowed 蒙恩……賜給 (echaristhē, echarisato)

Both verbs come from charizomai, to give freely, from charis, grace. God “granted” the Philippians to suffer for Christ (1:29), and God “bestowed” on Christ the name above every name (2:9). The same gracious giving runs through both.

兩個動詞都來自 charizomai（白白賜予），字根是 charis（恩典）。神叫腓立比信徒「蒙恩」為基督受苦（1 章 29 節），神也「賜給他」那超乎萬名之上的名（2 章 9 節）。同一種恩典的賜予貫穿兩處。

1:30 conflict 爭戰 (agōna)

agōn is a contest, the word for an athletic event or any hard struggle. They “saw” Paul's struggle in Philippi itself (Acts 16:19-24) and “now hear” that it goes on in his imprisonment.

agōn 指競賽或艱苦的搏鬥。他們曾在腓立比親眼看見保羅的爭戰（使徒行傳 16 章 19-24 節），現在又聽見他在捆鎖中仍然爭戰：「從前所看見、現在所聽見」。

Apply 應用

  1. Name one believer you know who is under pressure for their faith. Send them a message or pray for them by name this week.
  1. 說出一位你認識、正因信仰而受壓的信徒。這星期給他發個信息，或按名字為他禱告。

For discussion 討論

How can suffering for Christ be something “granted” to us? How would that change the way we see hard times?
為基督受苦怎能是「蒙恩」？這會怎樣改變我們看困難的角度？

Verses 2:1-2: Since you have all this in Christ, be of one mind 第 2 章 1-2 節：既在基督裡，就要意念相同

Observe 觀察

  • Four grounds: “encouragement in Christ”, “comfort from love”, “participation in the Spirit”, “affection and sympathy”. (2:1)
  • The request, “complete my joy”, in four phrases that say nearly the same thing: “of the same mind”, “the same love”, “in full accord”, “of one mind”. (2:2)
  • 四個根據：「勸勉」、「安慰」、「交通」、「慈悲憐憫」。（2 章 1 節）
  • 請求是「使我的喜樂可以滿足」，用了四個意思相近的短語：「意念相同」、「愛心相同」、「有一樣的心思」、「有一樣的意念」。（2 章 2 節）

Interpret 解釋

Paul does not start with a rule but with what they already have in Christ. Because they have been encouraged, loved, joined by the Spirit and shown mercy, they are to share one mind. Unity is not everyone holding the same opinion on everything; it is one shared direction of heart: the same love and the same aim. Paul says his own joy depends on it.

保羅不是從規條開始，而是從他們在基督裡已經擁有的開始。既然他們蒙了勸勉、愛、聖靈的交通和憐憫，就要意念相同。合一不是每件事都意見一致，而是心的方向一致：同樣的愛，同樣的目標。保羅說，他自己的喜樂也在乎此。

Key words 字詞

2:1 encouragement ... participation in the Spirit ... affection 勸勉……聖靈的交通……慈悲 (paraklēsis, koinōnia pneumatos, splanchna)

The four “if” clauses are not doubts. This Greek form treats the condition as true: since there is encouragement in Christ, comfort from love, fellowship in the Spirit, and deep affection. splanchna literally means the inner organs, the seat of the deepest feelings.

四個「若」不是懷疑。這種希臘文句式把條件當作事實：既然在基督裡有勸勉、愛心有安慰、聖靈有交通、心中有慈悲憐憫。splanchna 原指內臟，古人視之為最深情感的所在。

2:2, 5 same mind ... one mind ... have this mind 意念相同……一樣的意念……以基督耶穌的心為心 (phronēte, phronountes, phroneite)

phroneō means a way of thinking that shapes how you live: a mindset, not just an opinion. Paul uses it twice in 2:2 and again in 2:5, “Have this mind”. It returns in 4:2, where two women are urged to agree, literally to think the same, in the Lord.

phroneō 指支配生活方式的思想，即心態，不只是意見。保羅在 2 章 2 節用了兩次，在 5 節再用：「以基督耶穌的心為心」。4 章 2 節勸兩位姊妹在主裡同心，原文也是這個字，直譯是思想相同。

Apply 應用

  1. Before the next church gathering, thank God for one specific encouragement you have received in Christ, and pass one on.
  1. 下一次教會聚會之前，為一件你在基督裡得到的具體鼓勵感謝神，並把鼓勵傳給另一個人。

Which of the four grounds in 2:1 do you most need to remember when unity is hard?
合一困難的時候，2 章 1 節的四個根據中，哪一個是你最需要記住的？

Verses 2:3-4: Humility that puts others first 第 2 章 3-4 節：存心謙卑，顧別人的事

Observe 觀察

  • Two things to stop: “selfish ambition or conceit”. One thing to start: “in humility count others more significant than yourselves”. (2:3)
  • The balance: “not only to his own interests, but also to the interests of others”. (2:4)
  • 要停止的兩件事：「結黨」和「貪圖虛浮的榮耀」。要開始的一件事：「存心謙卑，各人看別人比自己強」。（2 章 3 節）
  • 平衡：「不要單顧自己的事，也要顧別人的事」。（2 章 4 節）

Interpret 解釋

Humility is not thinking badly of yourself; it is thinking of yourself less, and of others more. To count others more significant is to treat their needs as weightier than your own, whatever their gifts or position. The cure for rivalry is not a better rule but a different mind, and so Paul turns at once to Christ.

謙卑不是看低自己，而是少想自己，多想別人。看別人比自己強，就是把別人的需要看得比自己的更重，不論對方的恩賜或地位如何。醫治紛爭的不是更好的規條，而是一顆不同的心，所以保羅立即轉向基督。

Key words 字詞

2:3 selfish ambition or conceit 結黨……虛浮的榮耀 (eritheia, kenodoxia)

eritheia is the word used in 1:17 of the rival preachers; it means self-seeking, party spirit. kenodoxia is literally empty glory: wanting to be seen and praised. Paul names the two things that most often break a church.

eritheia 就是 1 章 17 節形容那些與保羅較勁的傳道人的字，指自私、結黨營私。kenodoxia 直譯是空洞的榮耀：渴望被看見、被稱讚。保羅點出了最常拆毀教會的兩樣東西：「結黨」和「虛浮的榮耀」。

2:3, 8 humility ... humbled himself 謙卑……自己卑微 (tapeinophrosynē, etapeinōsen)

tapeinophrosynē is lowliness of mind. In the Greek and Roman world, lowliness was usually seen as a weakness, not a virtue. Christians came to prize it because Christ “humbled himself” (2:8), the same root.

tapeinophrosynē 指心裡謙卑。在希臘羅馬世界，卑微通常被看作軟弱，不是美德。基督徒卻珍視謙卑，因為基督「自己卑微」（2 章 8 節），原文同一字根。

2:3, 6 count 看……以……為 (hēgoumenoi, hēgēsato)

The same verb, hēgeomai, to consider or count, appears in 2:3 (“count others more significant than yourselves”) and in 2:6, where Christ “did not count equality with God a thing to be grasped”. What Paul asks of us in 2:3 is what Christ did in 2:6.

同一個動詞 hēgeomai（看作、認為）出現在 2 章 3 節（「各人看別人比自己強」）和 2 章 6 節（「不以自己與神同等為強奪的」）。保羅在 3 節要求我們的，正是基督在 6 節所做的。

2:4 look ... to the interests of others 顧別人的事 (skopountes)

skopeō is to keep your eye on something; English scope comes from the same family. Note “not only”: Paul does not forbid caring for your own needs, he forbids looking only at them.

skopeō 指定睛注視。留意「不要單顧」：保羅沒有禁止我們照顧自己的需要，而是不要只顧自己。

Apply 應用

  1. In your next conversation, ask about the other person's concerns before you share your own.
  1. 在下一次談話中，先問對方關心的事，然後才說自己的。

For discussion 討論

What is the difference between humility and low self-esteem? What does “count others more significant than yourselves” look like at home or at work?
謙卑與自卑有甚麼分別？「各人看別人比自己強」在家中或工作上是怎樣的？

Verses 2:5-8: Christ went down 第 2 章 5-8 節：基督降卑

Observe 觀察

  • The bridge: “Have this mind among yourselves, which is yours in Christ Jesus”. (2:5)
  • Where he started: “in the form of God”. What he did not do: he “did not count equality with God a thing to be grasped”. (2:6)
  • What he did: “emptied himself”, “taking the form of a servant”, “being born in the likeness of men”. (2:7)
  • How low he went: “he humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross”. (2:8)
  • 連接的一句：「你們當以基督耶穌的心為心」。（2 章 5 節）
  • 他的起點：「他本有神的形象」。他沒有做的：「不以自己與神同等為強奪的」。（2 章 6 節）
  • 他所做的：「虛己」、「取了奴僕的形象」、「成為人的樣式」。（2 章 7 節）
  • 他降得多低：「自己卑微，存心順服以至於死，且死在十字架上」。（2 章 8 節）

Interpret 解釋

This is the heart of the passage, and perhaps of the whole letter. Many scholars think verses 6-11 were an early Christian hymn, either quoted or written by Paul. Christ had every right, and he used none of it for himself. He went down step by step: from the form of God to the form of a servant, to human likeness, to death, to a cross. Paul tells the story not only to teach who Christ is, but so that the church will think the same way.

這是全段的核心，也可能是全封信的核心。不少學者認為 6-11 節是初期教會的一首詩歌，或由保羅引用，或由他所寫。基督擁有一切的權利，卻沒有一樣用在自己身上。他一步一步往下走：由神的形象到奴僕的形象，到人的樣式，到死，到十字架。保羅講述這段經歷，不只是要教導基督是誰，更是要教會有同樣的思想。

Key words 字詞

2:5 have this mind ... in Christ Jesus 以基督耶穌的心為心 (touto phroneite)

ESV footnote: “Or which was also in Christ Jesus”. Two readings: think the way Christ thought (an example to copy), or think in the way that fits your life in Christ (your union with him). Both are true, and the story that follows suits the first.

這句有兩種理解：照著基督的思想去想（效法榜樣），或按著在基督裡的身份去想（與基督聯合）。兩者都合乎真理，而接著的經文較配合第一種理解。

2:6 form of God ... a thing to be grasped 神的形象……強奪的 (morphē, harpagmon)

morphē is a form that truly shows what something is: Christ was really God. harpagmos is a rare word; the ESV footnote gives “a thing to be held on to for advantage”. The point is not that he lacked equality with God, but that he did not use it for his own advantage.

morphē 指真實顯出本質的形狀：基督確實是神。harpagmos 是罕見的字，可解作可以抓住、為自己謀利的東西。重點不是他沒有與神同等的地位，而是他不利用這地位為自己謀益處：「不以自己與神同等為強奪的」。

2:7 emptied himself ... servant 虛己……奴僕 (ekenōsen, doulou)

He did not empty himself of being God. The next words say how: “by taking the form of a servant”. He gave up his rights and status by taking on a servant's form. ESV footnote: doulos can be rendered “slave”, the lowest place in that society.

他並沒有放棄神的本質。經文接著說明他怎樣「虛己」：「取了奴僕的形象」。他是藉著取了奴僕的樣式，放下自己的權利和地位。doulos 指奴隸，是當時社會最低微的位置。

2:8 obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross 順服以至於死，且死在十字架上 (hypēkoos, staurou)

The steps go lower and lower: God, servant, man, death, and then the most shameful death. Crucifixion was the punishment for slaves, rebels and criminals, and Roman citizens were normally spared it. In a city proud of being Roman, this line would have shocked.

一步一步往下走：由神到奴僕，到人，到死，再到最羞辱的死法。十字架是刑罰奴隸、叛徒和罪犯的，羅馬公民通常可以免受。在一個以羅馬身份為榮的城市，這句話必然令人震驚。

Apply 應用

  1. Choose one right you would normally insist on this week (being first, being thanked, having the last word) and let it go for someone else's sake.
  1. 這星期選一項你平常會堅持的權利（排第一、被感謝、說最後一句話），為了別人的緣故放下它。

For discussion 討論

Christ “did not count equality with God a thing to be grasped”. What rights or status do we hold on to most tightly?
基督「不以自己與神同等為強奪的」。我們最緊緊抓住的是甚麼權利或地位？

Verses 2:9-11: God lifted him up 第 2 章 9-11 節：神將他升為至高

Observe 觀察

  • The turn: “Therefore God has highly exalted him”, and gave him “the name that is above every name”. (2:9)
  • So that “at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth”. (2:10)
  • And “every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father”. (2:11)
  • 轉捩點：「所以神將他升為至高」，又賜給他「那超乎萬名之上的名」。（2 章 9 節）
  • 叫「一切在天上的、地上的和地底下的，因耶穌的名無不屈膝」。（2 章 10 節）
  • 並且「無不口稱耶穌基督為主，使榮耀歸於父神」。（2 章 11 節）

Interpret 解釋

God answers Christ's self-humbling with exaltation; the way of the cross is vindicated. The exaltation does not cancel his humility; it shows that humble, obedient love is what God honours. The name above every name is most likely Lord, the title that belongs to God himself. One day all creation will acknowledge him, and that will bring glory to the Father.

神以升高回應基督的自我降卑，十字架的道路得著肯定。升高並沒有取消他的謙卑，反而顯明神所尊崇的，正是謙卑順服的愛。那超乎萬名之上的名，最可能是「主」，就是屬於神自己的稱號。終有一天，萬有都要承認他，這也使榮耀歸於父神。

Key words 字詞

2:9 therefore ... highly exalted 所以……升為至高 (dio, hyperypsōsen)

hyperypsoō is to super-exalt, to lift above all. “Therefore” links the exaltation to the obedience: the way down became the way up. Christ did not exalt himself; God did.

hyperypsoō 指升到極高、超乎一切。「所以」把升高與順服連在一起：往下走的路成了往上升的路。基督沒有高抬自己，是神將他升為至高。

2:10-11 every knee ... every tongue ... Lord 無不屈膝……無不口稱……為主 (kampsē, exomologēsētai, kyrios)

These lines echo Isaiah 45:23, where God says that every knee shall bow to him and every tongue swear. There it is said of the LORD, the one God of Israel; Paul now says it of Jesus. kyrios, Lord, is the word the Greek Old Testament used for God's own name.

這兩節呼應以賽亞書 45 章 23 節：神說萬膝必向他跪拜，萬口必憑他起誓。那裡說的是耶和華，以色列獨一的神；保羅現在把這話用在耶穌身上。kyrios（主）是希臘文舊約用來代表神名字的字。

Apply 應用

  1. Begin each day this week by saying aloud, Jesus Christ is Lord, and name one area of your life you put under him that day.
  1. 這星期每天開始時，開口說：耶穌基督是主，並說出當天你要交給他掌管的一個生活範疇。

For discussion 討論

What difference does it make today that one day “every knee should bow”?
知道終有一天萬膝「無不屈膝」，對我們今天的生活有甚麼影響？

Question 3 (V27-30): What are the results of living a life consistent with the gospel of Christ the King? 第 3 題：過與君王基督的福音相符的生活，會帶來甚麼結果？

Paul lists what follows when a church lives in a way that matches the gospel. Each result is in the text.

保羅列出教會過與福音相稱的生活會帶來的結果，每一項都在經文裡。

  • Steadiness: “standing firm in one spirit”. (1:27)
  • Unity in mission: “with one mind striving side by side for the faith of the gospel”. (1:27)
  • Consistency: the same whether Paul can “come and see” them or is “absent”. (1:27)
  • Courage: “not frightened in anything by your opponents”. (1:28)

  • A share in suffering for Christ, and in Paul's struggle: “the same conflict”. (1:29-30)
  • Joy in others: Paul asks them to “complete my joy” (2:2). (2:2)
  • 堅定：「站立得穩」。（1 章 27 節）
  • 在使命上合一：「為所信的福音齊心努力」。（1 章 27 節）
  • 始終如一：無論保羅「或來見你們，或不在你們那裡」。（1 章 27 節）
  • 勇氣：「凡事不怕敵人的驚嚇」。（1 章 28 節）
  • 清楚的證明：「證明他們沉淪、你們得救，都是出於神」。（1 章 28 節）
  • 分擔為基督受的苦，也與保羅經歷同樣的「爭戰」。（1 章 29-30 節）
  • 帶給別人喜樂：「使我的喜樂可以滿足」（2 章 2 節）。（2 章 2 節）

On Christ the King: citizenship language (1:27; 3:20) implies a king. In Philippi people looked to the emperor; the church looks to Jesus, whom every tongue will one day confess as Lord (2:11). A worthy life is a loyal subject's life.

題目說到基督是君王：公民的字眼（1 章 27 節；3 章 20 節）意味著有一位君王。腓立比人仰望羅馬皇帝；教會仰望耶穌，萬口終必承認他為主（2 章 11 節）。與福音相稱的生命，就是忠於君王的子民的生命。

Question 4 (V27-30): What is involved in following Christ by faith? 第 4 題：憑信心跟隨基督包括甚麼？

Verses 27-30 show that faith is more than private belief. Following Christ by faith involves:

27-30 節顯示信心不只是個人的信念。憑信心跟隨基督包括：

  • Trusting him: it is granted to us to “believe in him”. (1:29)
  • Belonging: living as citizens of his kingdom, with a new loyalty. (1:27)
  • Holding to the content of the faith: striving “for the faith of the gospel”. (1:27)
  • Doing it together: “side by side”, not alone. (1:27)
  • Suffering “for his sake”, and not being surprised by it. (1:29)
  • Relying on God: salvation is “from God”, so our courage does not rest on ourselves. (1:28)
  • Taking on his mind: following Christ means learning to think as he thought (2:5). (2:5)
  • 信靠他：神叫我們「得以信服基督」。（1 章 29 節）
  • 歸屬：作他國度的國民，有新的效忠對象。（1 章 27 節）
  • 持守信仰的內容：「為所信的福音」努力。（1 章 27 節）
  • 一同去做：「齊心努力」，不是單打獨鬥。（1 章 27 節）
  • 「為他受苦」，並且不以為奇。（1 章 29 節）
  • 倚靠神：得救「都是出於神」，所以我們的勇氣不是靠自己。（1 章 28 節）
  • 以他的心為心：跟隨基督就是學習像他一樣思想（2 章 5 節）。（2 章 5 節）

Question 8 (2:5-11): Describe the main aspects of Christ example? 第 8 題：描述基督榜樣的主要方面。

  • 他不利用自己的權利為自己謀益處：「不以自己與神同等為強奪的」。（2 章 6 節）
  • 他捨棄自己：「虛己」。（2 章 7 節）
  • 他取了最低的位置：「取了奴僕的形象」。（2 章 7 節）
  • 他真正成為人：「成為人的樣式」，「既有人的樣子」。（2 章 7-8 節）
  • 他順服到底：「存心順服以至於死，且死在十字架上」。（2 章 8 節）

Notice the words that link back to 2:3-4: “count” (2:3 and 2:6) and humility (2:3 and 2:8). Christ is the picture of exactly what Paul has just asked for.

留意這幾節與 3-4 節的連繫：「看」與「不以自己與神同等為強奪的」中的以為，原文是同一個字（2 章 3、6 節），「謙卑」與「卑微」也是同一字根（2 章 3、8 節）。基督正是保羅剛才所要求的最好寫照。

Question 9 (2:5-11): What are the two purposes of Christ’s exaltation? 第 9 題：基督被升為至高，有哪兩個目的？

Verses 10-11 give the purpose with “so that”, and the final goal with “to the glory of God the Father”.

10-11 節用「叫」字帶出目的，最終的目標是「使榮耀歸於父神」。

  • First purpose: that all creation will acknowledge Jesus as Lord, “every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth”, and “every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord”. (2:10-11)
  • Second purpose: that all this will be “to the glory of God the Father”. (2:11)
  • 第一個目的：萬有都承認耶穌是主，「一切在天上的、地上的和地底下的，因耶穌的名無不屈膝」，「無不口稱耶穌基督為主」。（2 章 10-11 節）
  • 第二個目的：這一切都「使榮耀歸於父神」。（2 章 11 節）

Another way to read the two: the bowing knee (worship and submission) and the confessing tongue (open acknowledgement). Either way, the honour given to Jesus does not compete with the Father's glory; it serves it.

另一種讀法是把兩個目的看作：屈膝（敬拜和降服）與口稱（公開承認）。無論哪種讀法，耶穌得的尊榮與父神的榮耀並不衝突，而是彰顯父神的榮耀。

For discussion 討論

Paul uses the greatest truth about Christ to answer an ordinary church problem: people not getting on. What does that teach us about how belief and daily life connect?
保羅用關於基督最偉大的真理，去回應教會中一個很平常的問題：肢體之間不和睦。這對信仰與日常生活的關係有甚麼啟發？

🤝8. Application and prayer 生活應用與禱告

Answers question 10 回應第 10 題

Question 10: Write down at least 1 application 第 10 題：寫下最少一項生活應用。

The last question asks for at least one application. Here are some options from the passage. Choose one, make it specific, and share it with the group.

最後一條題目請每人寫下最少一項應用。以下是從經文而來的一些選擇：選一項，寫得具體，並與小組分享。

Options 選擇

  1. Do one hidden act of service this week for someone in the church, something nobody will thank you for (2:3-4, 7).
  2. If there is someone you are at odds with, take the first humble step towards them: a message, an apology, an invitation (2:2-3).
  3. At home or at work, when your idea competes with someone else's, look first for what is good in theirs and say so (2:3-4).
  4. When you feel the urge to be noticed or praised, pray Philippians 2:3 silently before you speak.
  5. Stand with someone who is suffering for their faith, near or far: pray, write, or give (1:29-30).
  6. Memorise 2:3 this week, or 2:5-11 over the next fortnight.
  1. 這星期為教會中一位肢體做一件不為人知、也不會有人感謝你的服侍（2 章 3-4、7 節）。
  2. 如果你與某人有嫌隙，主動踏出謙卑的第一步：一個信息、一句道歉、一個邀請（2 章 2-3 節）。
  3. 在家中或工作上，當你的意見與別人的有衝突時，先找出對方意見的好處，並說出來（2 章 3-4 節）。
  4. 當你想被人注意或稱讚的時候，在開口之前，先默禱腓立比書 2 章 3 節。
  5. 與一位為信仰受苦的人同行，無論遠近：禱告、寫信或奉獻（1 章 29-30 節）。
  6. 這星期背誦 2 章 3 節，或在未來兩星期背誦 2 章 5-11 節。

An example of one written application: This week, in one meeting at work, I will ask about my colleagues' concerns first and listen fully before I make my own point (2:4).

一項寫好的應用作例子：這星期在工作的一個會議上，我會先問同事關心的事，完整聽完，才提出自己的意見（2 章 4 節）。

For discussion 討論

Which application will you share with the group, and who will ask you about it in two weeks?
你會與小組分享哪一項應用？兩星期後誰會問你做得怎樣？

Prayer points from Philippians 1:27-2:11 代禱事項

  1. That our church lives worthy of the gospel of Christ, standing firm in one spirit and one mind. (1:27)
  2. Courage when opposed, and strength for Christians who suffer for Christ today. (1:28-29)
  3. That we would be of the same mind and have the same love. (2:2)
  4. Freedom from selfish ambition and conceit; humility to look to the interests of others. (2:3-4)
  5. That we would have the mind of Christ, who humbled himself and was obedient to death. (2:5, 8)
  6. Thanks that Jesus Christ is Lord; prayer for people we know who do not yet confess him. (2:10-11)
  1. 求神使我們的教會行事為人與基督的福音相稱，同有一個心志，站立得穩。（1 章 27 節）
  2. 求神在我們遇到反對時賜下勇氣，並堅固今天為基督受苦的弟兄姊妹。（1 章 28-29 節）
  3. 求神使我們意念相同，愛心相同。（2 章 2 節）
  4. 求神除去我們的結黨和虛浮的榮耀，賜我們謙卑的心，顧別人的事。（2 章 3-4 節）
  5. 求神使我們以基督耶穌的心為心，他自己卑微，存心順服以至於死。（2 章 5、8 節）
  6. 感謝神，耶穌基督是主；為我們認識但仍未承認他的人禱告。（2 章 10-11 節）

Lord Jesus, you were in the form of God, yet you did not hold on to your rights. You emptied yourself, took the form of a servant, and humbled yourself to death on a cross. Thank you that the Father has highly exalted you and given you the name above every name. Give us your mind. Take away our selfish ambition and our hunger for empty glory. Teach us to count others more significant than ourselves and to look to their interests. Make our church stand firm in one spirit, unafraid, striving side by side for the faith of the gospel. We confess that you are Lord, to the glory of God the Father. Amen.

主耶穌，你本有神的形象，卻沒有緊抓自己的權利。你虛己，取了奴僕的形象，自己卑微，存心順服以至於死，且死在十字架上。感謝父神將你升為至高，又賜給你那超乎萬名之上的名。求你使我們以你的心為心。除去我們結黨的心和對虛浮榮耀的貪圖，教導我們看別人比自己強，也顧別人的事。求你使我們的教會同有一個心志，站立得穩，毫不懼怕，為所信的福音齊心努力。我們承認你是主，使榮耀歸於父神。阿們。
