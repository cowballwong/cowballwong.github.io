The Grace of Giving

奉獻的恩典

Discussion Guide 討論指南
2 Corinthians 8:1-9; 9:6-11 | 哥林多後書 8:1-9; 9:6-11
"Excel in this grace of giving!" — 2 Corinthians 8:7
「你們既然在……凡事都充足，就當在這慈惠的事上也格外顯出滿足來。」——哥林多後書 8:7

📖 Sermon Overview 講道概覽

English

This sermon on "The Grace of Giving" from 2 Corinthians 8-9 examines Christian giving through three lenses: What we give to (4 areas), How we give (4 attitudes), and Why we give (4 motivations). The key message: giving is not a burden but a grace — an overflow of hearts transformed by the gospel of Jesus Christ, who "though He was rich, yet for your sake He became poor" (2 Cor 8:9).

繁體中文

這篇以哥林多後書第八至九章為基礎的講道「奉獻的恩典」，從三個角度審視基督徒的奉獻：我們奉獻給誰（四個範疇）、如何奉獻（四種態度）、以及為何奉獻（四個動機）。核心信息是：奉獻不是重擔，乃是恩典——是被福音更新的心靈之流溢，因為基督「本來富足，卻為你們成了貧窮」（林後8:9）。

What — 奉獻給誰 (x4)
1. Family dependants 家人 Children, elderly parents 子女、年邁父母 1 Timothy 5:8 提前 5:8
2. Poor & needy 窮乏人 Closer need = greater responsibility 越近的需要＝越大的責任 Galatians 2:10; 6:10 加 2:10; 6:10
3. Church ministries 教會事工 Preaching, teaching, outreach, youth 講道、教導、佈道、青少年 1 Cor 9:14; 1 Tim 5:17-18
4. Gospel work elsewhere 其他福音事工 Mission, church planting 宣教、植堂 Phil 4:15-18 腓 4:15-18
How — 如何奉獻 (x4)
1. Generously 慷慨地 Not "how little" but "how much can I give" 不是「最少給多少」而是「最多能給多少」 2 Cor 8:1-4 林後 8:1-4
2. Proportionally 按比例地 According to income; not recklessly 按收入；非魯莽行事 1 Cor 16:2; 2 Cor 8:12
3. Cheerfully 甘心樂意地 God doesn't want reluctant gifts 上帝不要勉強的禮物 2 Cor 9:7 林後 9:7
4. Quietly 隱秘地 In secret — cuts against showing off or buying favours 暗中行——避免炫耀或賄賂 Matthew 6:2-4 太 6:2-4
Why — 為何奉獻 (x4)
1. Everything is God's gift 一切都是上帝的禮物 Nothing truly "earned" — all given by the Lord 沒有甚麼是真正「賺來的」——都是主所賜 1 Chron 29:14 代上 29:14
2. Can't take it with us 帶不走 "Shrouds have no pockets" 「裹屍布沒有口袋」 Matt 6:19-20 太 6:19-20
3. It comes with reward 有賞賜 God always more than compensates 上帝的回報總是超越付出 Luke 6:38; 2 Cor 9:6
4. The gospel 福音 Christ gave Himself — will we not give to the Giver? 基督捨了自己——我們豈不奉獻給這位賜予者？ 2 Cor 8:8-9 林後 8:8-9

💬 Discussion Questions 討論問題

Which of the "what" categories did you find most difficult to do and why?
在「奉獻給誰」的四個範疇中，你覺得哪一個最難實踐？為甚麼？
Reading Aid 輔助閱讀

The sermon identified four areas of Christian giving:

  • Family dependants — especially children and elderly parents. Paul warns that failing to provide for one's household is to deny the faith (1 Tim 5:8). The sermon highlighted absent fathers as "a great scourge and source of great shame."
  • Poor and needy — with two guiding principles: (a) the closer the need, the greater our responsibility (e.g. a next-door neighbour over someone in a magazine); (b) fellow believers come first (Gal 6:10). The sermon acknowledged this is "complicated — coz endless need!"
  • Our church ministries — supporting pastors/teachers (1 Tim 5:17-18) and ministries we may not directly benefit from (children/youth work, organised outreach like Passion for Life).
  • Gospel work elsewhere — missions and church planting around the world. Paul thanked the Philippians for their financial "partnership in the gospel" (Phil 4:15-18). Even supporting one or two workers makes a difference.
Think about: Which of these four areas feels most uncomfortable or most neglected in your own giving? Is it that the need feels too distant (missions)? Too close to home (parents)? Too abstract (church budget)? Or too overwhelming (the poor)?
輔助閱讀

講道指出基督徒奉獻的四個範疇：

  • 家人——尤其是子女和年邁的父母。保羅警告，不看顧自己家人的，比不信的人還不好（提前5:8）。講道特別提到缺席的父親是「極大的禍害和羞恥」。
  • 窮乏人——有兩個指導原則：(a) 需要越近，責任越大（例如隔壁鄰居比雜誌上的人更優先）；(b) 主內肢體優先（加6:10）。講道承認這是「複雜的——因為需要是無盡的！」
  • 教會事工——支持牧者和教師（提前5:17-18），以及我們未必直接受惠的事工（兒童/青少年事工、有組織的佈道如 Passion for Life）。
  • 其他福音事工——世界各地的宣教和植堂。保羅感謝腓立比人在福音上的「同工」（腓4:15-18）。即使只支持一兩位工人也有意義。
思考：這四個範疇中，哪一個在你的奉獻中最令你感到不安或最被忽略？是因為需要太遙遠（宣教）？太貼身（父母）？太抽象（教會預算）？還是太龐大（窮乏人）？
What gospel work outside of Ridgeway Church are you currently giving to? [Don't say how much!]
你目前有支持 Ridgeway 教會以外的甚麼福音事工？（不用說金額！）
Reading Aid 輔助閱讀

This question relates to the 4th "what" — gospel work elsewhere. The sermon drew on Paul's relationship with the Philippian church:

Philippians 4:15-18 — "Not one church shared with me in the matter of giving and receiving, except you only... I have received full payment and have more than enough. I am amply supplied, now that I have received from Epaphroditus the gifts you sent."
腓立比書 4:15-18 — 「除了你們以外，沒有別的教會供給我……我樣樣都有，並且有餘。我已經充足，因我從以巴弗提受了你們的餽送。」

Paul described the Philippians' giving as "partnership in the gospel" — their financial support was not charity but co-labouring. Two ways to support gospel work beyond our local church:

  • Prayer — the first and most accessible form of support
  • Finance — even supporting "one or two" workers matters
Think about: This is a sharing question, not a testing one. Consider mission organisations, church planting networks, overseas workers, evangelistic ministries, or Christian charities you may support. If you're not currently supporting any, what might a first step look like? Remember Jesus' instruction to give "in secret" (Matt 6:3-4) — the point is mutual encouragement, not comparison.
輔助閱讀

這個問題涉及第四個「奉獻給誰」——其他福音事工。講道引用了保羅與腓立比教會的關係：

保羅將腓立比人的奉獻稱為「福音的同工」——他們的經濟支持不是施捨，而是同工。支持本地教會以外福音事工的兩種方式：

  • 禱告——最首要、最容易參與的支持方式
  • 金錢——即使只支持「一兩位」工人也有意義
思考：這是一個分享問題，不是測驗。想想你可能支持的宣教機構、植堂網絡、海外宣教士、佈道事工或基督教慈善機構。如果你目前沒有支持任何事工，第一步可以是甚麼？記得耶穌教導我們「在暗中」施捨（太6:3-4）——重點是彼此鼓勵，而非比較。
Which of the "how's" did you find most encouraging or challenging and why?
在「如何奉獻」的四種態度中，你覺得哪一個最令你受鼓勵或最具挑戰？為甚麼？
Reading Aid 輔助閱讀

The sermon outlined four attitudes for Christian giving:

  • Generously (2 Cor 8:1-4) — The Macedonian churches in extreme poverty gave "as much as they were able, and even beyond their ability." The question is not "how little can I get away with?" but "how much can I possibly afford to give?" The sermon asked pointedly: "Has giving ever hurt?"
  • Proportionally (1 Cor 16:2; 2 Cor 8:12) — Generous but not reckless. God expects those with more to give more. "Giving away 10% if a millionaire = easy. Giving away 10% on minimum wage = impossible." Making ourselves destitute is normally sinful because it makes us a burden (1 Thess 4:10-12). If you're giving what you can — be encouraged!
  • Cheerfully (2 Cor 9:7) — "Jesus doesn't want our money if we don't want to give it!" Giving that is reluctant or under compulsion is "tainted." The OT required a 10% tithe, but this is not a NT requirement (for some it's not even possible). The real question: "How much can you give cheerfully?"
  • Quietly (Matt 6:2-4) — Give "in secret" with as few others knowing as possible. This guards against two ungodly motives: showing off and buying favours. The sermon shared an illustration of a missionary in Africa who decided public offerings were unbiblical based on Matthew 6.
Think about: "Encouraging" and "challenging" can both be positive! Proportional giving is encouraging because it removes guilt — you give what you can. Generous giving is challenging because it pushes beyond comfort. Cheerful giving is liberating because it removes obligation. Quiet giving is counter-cultural because our world rewards visibility. Which resonates most with where you are right now?
輔助閱讀

講道概述了基督徒奉獻的四種態度：

  • 慷慨地（林後8:1-4）——馬其頓眾教會在極度貧窮中「按着力量，而且也過了力量」地奉獻。問題不是「最少可以給多少？」而是「最多能給多少？」講道直接問：「奉獻曾經令你心痛嗎？」
  • 按比例地（林前16:2；林後8:12）——慷慨但不魯莽。上帝期望擁有更多的人給予更多。「百萬富翁奉獻10%＝容易。最低工資奉獻10%＝不可能。」使自己陷入赤貧通常是有罪的，因為這會使自己成為別人的負擔（帖前4:10-12）。如果你已盡力奉獻——要受鼓勵！
  • 甘心樂意地（林後9:7）——「如果我們不願意給，耶穌不要我們的錢！」勉強或被迫的奉獻是「有瑕疵的」。舊約要求十分之一的奉獻，但這不是新約的要求（對某些人來說甚至不可能）。真正的問題是：「你能甘心樂意地給多少？」
  • 隱秘地（太6:2-4）——盡量讓越少人知道越好地「在暗中」施捨。這防範兩種不虔誠的動機：炫耀和收買人心。講道分享了一位在非洲的宣教士，根據馬太福音第六章，認定公開奉獻不合聖經。
思考：「鼓勵」和「挑戰」都可以是正面的！按比例奉獻令人鼓勵，因為它消除了內疚——你盡力而為就好。慷慨奉獻具挑戰性，因為它推動我們超越舒適區。甘心樂意的奉獻令人釋放，因為它消除了義務感。隱秘奉獻是反文化的，因為這個世界獎賞能見度。哪一個最切合你現在的處境？
What sorts of things can make it harder for us to give generously and cheerfully?
有甚麼因素會令我們更難慷慨且甘心樂意地奉獻？
Reading Aid 輔助閱讀

The sermon touched on several obstacles, both practical and spiritual. The four "why" motivations actually address the heart barriers that make giving hard:

Practical barriers:

  • Cost of living pressures — genuine financial constraints
  • Debt obligations — limiting disposable income
  • Overwhelming scale of need — "the poor you will always have with you" (John 12:8)
  • Uncertainty about where money goes — lack of transparency in organisations

Heart barriers (and the sermon's antidotes):

  • Sense of ownership → Antidote: "Everything comes from You, and we have given You only what comes from Your hand" (1 Chron 29:14). If we think it's "our" money, giving feels like loss.
  • Fear of the future → Antidote: We can't take it with us anyway! "Shrouds have no pockets." Sensible saving is wise (Prov 6:6-8), but hoarding is foolish.
  • Desire for recognition → Antidote: Give quietly; God "who sees what is done in secret will reward you" (Matt 6:4). If no one notices our giving, we may feel unmotivated.
  • Forgetting the gospel → Antidote: "Though He was rich, yet for your sake He became poor" (2 Cor 8:9). The greatest motivation is remembering what Christ gave for us.
Think about: Winston Churchill said, "We make a living by what we get but we make a life by what we give." The world teaches us that getting and grabbing is what life is all about. But for Christians, the meaning of life is Jesus, and the purpose of life is service. When that grips our heart — "everyone wins: others get blessed, we find peace and joy, and God gets the glory!"
輔助閱讀

講道觸及了幾個障礙，包括實際和屬靈層面。四個「為何」的動機正好回應了使奉獻變得困難的心靈障礙：

實際障礙：

  • 生活成本壓力——真實的經濟限制
  • 債務負擔——限制了可支配收入
  • 需要的規模令人不知所措——「常有窮人和你們同在」（約12:8）
  • 不確定金錢的去向——機構缺乏透明度

心靈障礙（及講道的解藥）：

  • 擁有感 → 解藥：「萬物都從你而來，我們把從你而得的獻給你」（代上29:14）。如果我們認為是「我們的」錢，奉獻就像損失。
  • 對未來的恐懼 → 解藥：反正帶不走！「裹屍布沒有口袋。」有智慧的儲蓄是明智的（箴6:6-8），但囤積是愚蠢的。
  • 渴望被認可 → 解藥：隱秘地施捨；「你父在暗中察看，必然報答你」（太6:4）。如果沒有人注意到我們的奉獻，我們可能會失去動力。
  • 忘記福音 → 解藥：「他本來富足，卻為你們成了貧窮」（林後8:9）。最大的動力是記住基督為我們所付出的。
思考：邱吉爾說：「我們靠所得來謀生，但靠所給予來創造生命。」世界教導我們，索取和搶奪才是人生的全部。但對基督徒來說，生命的意義是耶穌，生命的目的是服事。當這抓住我們的心——「人人都贏：別人得到祝福，我們得到平安和喜樂，上帝得到榮耀！」

🙏 Closing Prayer 結束禱告

English

Gracious Father, thank You for the grace of giving — that it is not a burden You place upon us, but a privilege You invite us into. Thank You that everything we have comes from Your generous hand.

Forgive us for the times we have held back — whether through fear, selfishness, forgetfulness, or simply the pressures of daily life. Open our eyes to the needs around us: our families, our church, the poor, and the gospel work that stretches across the world.

Teach us to give generously but wisely, proportionally but sacrificially, cheerfully and not reluctantly, quietly and without seeking praise. May we be like the Macedonian churches — who gave beyond their ability because they first gave themselves to You.

Above all, keep the gospel at the centre of our giving. May we never forget that our Lord Jesus Christ, though He was rich, became poor for our sake. As we have freely received, may we freely give.

In the name of Jesus, Amen.

繁體中文

慈愛的天父，感謝祢賜下奉獻的恩典——這不是祢加在我們身上的重擔，而是祢邀請我們參與的特權。感謝祢，我們所擁有的一切都來自祢慷慨的手。

求祢赦免我們退縮的時刻——無論是出於恐懼、自私、遺忘，還是日常生活的壓力。求祢開我們的眼睛，看見周圍的需要：我們的家人、我們的教會、窮乏人，以及延伸到世界各地的福音事工。

教導我們慷慨而有智慧地奉獻，按比例卻帶著犧牲地奉獻，甘心樂意而非勉強地奉獻，隱秘地而不求讚賞地奉獻。願我們像馬其頓眾教會——因為先把自己獻給了祢，所以能超過力量地奉獻。

最重要的是，讓福音成為我們奉獻的核心。願我們永遠不忘記，我們的主耶穌基督本來富足，卻為我們的緣故成了貧窮。我們既白白地領受了，也願白白地施予。

奉耶穌基督的名禱告，阿們。
