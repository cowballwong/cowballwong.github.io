The Enemy Within

敵在內心

Part One — The Power of Sin in What It Is · Chapter 1: Evil at My Elbow
第一部 — 罪的本質與權勢 · 第一章：危機就在身旁
“So I find it to be a law that when I want to do right, evil lies close at hand.” — Romans 7:21
「我覺得有個律，就是我願意為善的時候，便有惡與我同在。」— 羅馬書 7 章 21 節
Author 作者: Kris Lundgaard · Based on John Owen 根據約翰·歐文

📖 Summary 摘要

English

Kris Lundgaard opens with a deeply personal confession: while repairing his refrigerator, he loses his temper at his children — and feels as if “an alien had invaded my body and was forcing me to do his bidding.” This honest opening sets the tone. The book is not a theological lecture about sin in general; it is straight talk about indwelling sin — the sin that remains in every believer after conversion.

Building on Paul's experience in Romans 7, Lundgaard introduces four key truths about this enemy: (1) it operates as a law — a constant, gravitational force, not occasional weakness; (2) we discover it inside ourselves, not abstractly; (3) it most aggressively opposes us when we are at our best; and (4) it never rests. The chapter's claim is sobering yet liberating: only believers can truly feel this law, and recognising it is the first step in waging war against it.

繁體中文

Kris Lundgaard 以一段極為坦誠的個人經歷開卷：他在維修雪櫃時，竟然向著兒子大發雷霆，事後形容自己「像被外星人入侵身體，強迫去做他要做的事」。這種誠實的開場奠定了全書的調子——這不是一場有關罪的學術講論，而是直白地討論「內住之罪」——即信徒得救之後仍然殘留在心中的罪。

作者以羅馬書 7 章保羅的經歷為根基，提出四個關於這位內在敵人的真理：(1) 它以「律」的方式運作——是恆常的、重力般的力量，而非偶然的軟弱；(2) 我們是在自己裡面發現它，而不是停留於抽象的神學概念；(3) 當我們最想討神喜悅的時候，它反而最猛烈地反抗；(4) 它從不止息。本章的信息既使人警醒，亦帶來釋放：唯有信徒才真正感受到這律的運作；認識它，就是與它爭戰的第一步。

§ I Feel the Same Way Too — 「我也有同感」

English Paraphrase

Lundgaard opens with a deliberately mundane scene: a Thursday afternoon, his wife at work, the long-procrastinated refrigerator-handle repair, and three boys whose timing for a sibling squabble could not be worse. He loses his temper — and immediately watches himself losing it, as if from outside his own body. The boys' faces register confusion; his own face registers, in his words, evil.

What stuns him is not the anger itself but the inability to stop. Lundgaard describes the feeling with one striking image — “as if an alien had invaded my body and was forcing me to do his bidding.” The fit ends only after the children have fled. Days of feeling like a whipped puppy follow. He asks the questions every honest believer has asked: Was I really that wicked? Had I done irreparable harm? Would God forgive me?

The relief comes when he opens Romans 7 and discovers that the apostle Paul — apostle, theologian, missionary — felt the same way. Paul gives Lundgaard a vocabulary for what just happened in the kitchen: the law of sin, this body of death, my flesh, sin that dwells within me. Theologians call it indwelling sin. Lundgaard's whole book is built on one premise: to fight this enemy, we have to know it first. And the most reliable map of it is Paul's own experience in Romans 7.

繁體中文 撮要

Lundgaard 以一個刻意平凡的場景開卷：一個普通的星期四下午，妻子上班，他終於動手做拖延已久的雪櫃門鉸維修，三個兒子卻偏偏選在這時候鬧爭執。他發脾氣——並且立即從一個彷彿身外的位置，看著自己發脾氣。兒子們臉上滿是錯愕，他自己面上——用他的話說——浮現出邪惡。

真正令他震驚的，不是憤怒本身，而是無法停下來。Lundgaard 用一個非常傳神的比喻形容那刻：「彷彿有外來者侵入我的身體，強迫我順從他的旨意。」怒火直到兒子們躲開後才止息。之後他像被打的小狗一樣消沉好幾天，問每一個誠實的信徒都會問的問題：我真的這麼邪惡嗎？我會否對孩子造成永久的傷害？神還會原諒我嗎？

他的安慰來自打開羅馬書第 7 章——使徒保羅，這位使徒、神學家、宣教士，也曾有同樣的掙扎。保羅為他剛剛在廚房發生的事，提供了一套詞彙：罪的律、取死的身體、我的肉體、住在我裡頭的罪。神學家稱之為內住之罪（indwelling sin）。Lundgaard 整本書建基於一個前提：要與這敵人爭戰，必先認識牠。而最可靠的地圖，就是保羅自己在羅馬書 7 章的親身經歷。

“It was as if an alien had invaded my body and was forcing me to do his bidding.”
「彷彿有外來者侵入了我的身體，強迫我順從他的旨意。」
— Kris Lundgaard, ch. 1

§ Four Key Truths — 「四個關鍵真理」（導言）

English Paraphrase

If we want to follow Paul into conquest, we have to follow him into the fight. Lundgaard locates four foundational truths about indwelling sin in one verse: Romans 7:21. From this single sentence — “So I find it to be a law that when I want to do right, evil lies close at hand” — he draws the entire framework of the chapter.

The four truths are: (1) indwelling sin operates as a law; (2) we discover this law inside ourselves; (3) we feel its full force when we are trying to be at our best; and (4) it never rests. Each will be developed in turn below.

繁體中文 撮要

若想跟隨保羅得勝，必先跟隨他進入戰場。Lundgaard 從同一節經文——羅馬書 7 章 21 節——擷取出本章四個核心真理：「我覺得有個律，就是我願意為善的時候，便有惡與我同在。」整章的框架，從這一節擴展而出。

四個真理分別是：(1) 內住之罪以「律」的方式運作；(2) 我們是在自己裡面發現這律；(3) 當我們最想行善時，這律的力量最強烈；(4) 它從不止息。以下逐一展開。

§ The Sin That Lives in Us Is a “Law” — 「住在我們裡面的罪是一個『律』」

English Paraphrase

Paul's choice of the word law is, Lundgaard notes, deliberately ironic. Paul had just been writing about God's law — the law that ought to rule our lives. To turn around and call sin a law too is to dramatise the contrast: another competing authority is operating in us, often winning head-to-head battles.

Lundgaard unpacks two layers in the word law. There is moral law — a rule that commands, forbids, rewards and punishes (“honor your parents”, “do not trespass”). And there is natural law — gravity, hunger, thirst — a force that compels objects to behave a certain way. Indwelling sin operates on both levels at once. It commands. It seduces. It threatens. It also impels — physical pull as well as moral pressure.

The question this raises is sharp: in what sense, then, has Christ defeated sin in the believer? Lundgaard's answer follows classic Reformed sanctification doctrine: Christ has overthrown sin's reign, weakened its power, and killed its root (so it cannot bear the fruit of eternal death in us) — but its nature and purpose remain unchanged. The sin in us is the same sin it always was; it just no longer owns the throne.

He concludes the section with a striking analogy. Christ's first coming inaugurated his kingdom — he reigns, the devil is defeated — but battles continue and final consummation waits for the second coming. Likewise, the new birth is Christ's first coming into the believer's soul: he truly reigns there now, but the defeated enemy remains. Our glorification at death is Christ's second coming into our soul — the day when every trace of the law of sin is gone.

繁體中文 撮要

保羅選用「律」這字，Lundgaard 認為是刻意的反諷。保羅前面剛在講神的律——本應管理我們生命的律。突然把罪也稱為「律」，是要凸顯一個對比：另有一個敵對的權威正在我們裡面運作，並且常常勝過神的律。

Lundgaard 拆解「律」這詞的兩重意義。一層是道德律——一條規則，命令我們做甚麼、禁止我們做甚麼，並以賞罰相隨（如「孝敬父母」、「不可擅闖」）。另一層是自然律——例如重力、飢餓、口渴——是一股力量，迫使物件按某種方式運作。內住之罪同時在兩層運作：它命令我們、引誘我們、威嚇我們；同時它亦強行推動我們——既是道德上的壓力，也是肉體上的拉扯。

由此引出一個尖銳的問題：那麼，基督在信徒身上究竟「擊敗」了罪到甚麼地步？ Lundgaard 的回答緊跟改革宗成聖論的傳統：基督已推翻罪的統治、削弱牠的能力、斬斷牠的根（因此在信徒身上不能再結出永死的果子）——但牠的本質與目的並無改變。我們裡頭的罪，仍是當年那個罪，只是不再坐在寶座上。

他以一個動人的比喻作結。基督的第一次降臨已開啟了祂的國——祂在寶座上掌權，魔鬼已被擊敗——但爭戰仍在繼續，最終的得勝有待第二次再來。同樣，信徒的重生是基督第一次降臨進入我們的靈魂：祂真實地在我們心中掌權，但被擊敗的敵人仍然存在。我們死後得榮耀，就是基督第二次降臨進入我們的靈魂——那日，罪的律將連一絲痕跡也不存。

“Sin is sin; its nature and purpose remain unchanged; its force and success still grab us by the throat.”
「罪就是罪；牠的本質與目的並無改變；牠的力量與成功仍會掐住我們的咽喉。」
— Kris Lundgaard, ch. 1

§ We Find This Law inside Us — 「我們在自己裡面發現這律」

English Paraphrase

The word Lundgaard pivots on is find. Paul does not say “I theorise this law” or “I have been taught this law”. He says, I find. Indwelling sin moves from doctrine into experience — from the seminary library into the kitchen at 3pm on a Thursday.

Lundgaard sharpens this with two analogies. First: there is a vast gulf between sitting in a seminar critiquing dissertations on original sin, and being personally subdued by sin's actual madness. Second: there is a vast gulf between listening to a lecture about how cancer spreads, and hearing your own doctor say to you, “Stage 4 pancreatic cancer.” The first is information. The second is reality bending around you.

Then comes the chapter's most quoted distinction: only believers feel the law of sin at work in them. Unbelievers cannot. The law of sin, for unbelievers, is a raging river carrying them along — they have surrendered to the current and cannot measure its force because they have no resistance to it. Believers, by contrast, swim upstream. They meet sin head-on and strain under its strength. The very pain of the struggle is evidence of the new life within.

This reframes a common Christian fear. If you find this struggle inside you — exhausting, repeated, humiliating — it is not evidence that you were never saved. It is evidence that you are. The dead do not strain. Only the living swim upstream.

繁體中文 撮要

Lundgaard 將焦點放在「發現」這個字。保羅沒有說「我推論出這律」、也沒說「我被人教過這律」。他說：我發現。內住之罪從教義走進經歷，從神學圖書館走進星期四下午三點的廚房。

Lundgaard 用兩個比喻將這分別放大。第一個：坐在研討會上批判原罪的論文，與親身被罪的瘋狂壓服，兩者中間有極大的鴻溝。第二個：聽人講解癌症如何擴散，與聽見自己的醫生說「第四期胰臟癌」，兩者中間也有極大的鴻溝。前者是資訊，後者是現實在你面前轉彎。

然後本章一句最常被引用的話出現了：唯有信徒才感受到罪的律在他們裡面運作。非信徒感受不到。對非信徒而言，罪的律是一條把他們捲走的洶湧大河——他們已順服於水流，也測不到水流的力量，因為他們對牠根本沒有抵抗。信徒卻是逆流而上。他們正面遇見罪，並在牠的力量下竭力掙扎。掙扎中的疼痛，本身就是裡面有新生命的證據。

這幾乎將許多基督徒常有的恐懼徹底翻轉。若你在自己裡面發現這場掙扎——疲累、反覆、令人羞愧——那並不證明你從未得救，反而證明你已經得救。死人不會掙扎，唯有活著的人才會逆流而上。

“Believers are the only people who find the law of sin at work in them. Unbelievers can't feel it.”
「唯有信徒才會在自己裡面發現罪的律在運作。非信徒感受不到。」
— Kris Lundgaard, ch. 1

§ We Find This Law When We're at Our Best — 「我們最想行善時最察覺這律」

English Paraphrase

The most unsettling part of Romans 7:21 is the timing. Paul says sin lies close at hand when I want to do right. Not when he is indifferent. Not in seasons of backsliding. It is when his mind is set on obeying his Savior — when Christ rules his heart — that the assault is sharpest.

Lundgaard is careful to balance this with two truths. First, indwelling sin, though powerful, does not rule the believer. The believer's deepest desire — by grace — is to please God. We march to a different drummer. The default direction of our heart has changed. Second, that grace never works perfectly in this life (Gal 5:17). Even at our most generous, a touch of pride creeps in. Even our righteous works need to be covered by Christ's blood.

He then anchors this in 1 John 3:9 — “No one born of God makes a practice of sinning.” To “make a practice of” sinning means to make a career of it. The believer has a new nature (God's seed) that cannot be at peace with sin. This is what distinguishes believers at their worst from unbelievers at their best: even when believers stumble most violently, their new hearts still hate the sin they are committing. Unbelievers, however polished externally, do not — and would not, without restraining grace, surrender themselves more deeply still.

繁體中文 撮要

羅馬書 7 章 21 節最令人不安的，是這場攻擊的時機。保羅說罪「就在手邊」——而那是當他想要為善的時候。不是在冷淡的日子，不是在退後的季節，而是在他立定心志要順服救主的那一刻——當基督掌權於他心中時——攻擊反而最為猛烈。

Lundgaard 小心地在這兩個真理之間平衡。第一，內住之罪雖然強大，並不統治信徒。靠著神的恩典，信徒最深處的渴望是討神喜悅。我們的心已經跟著另一位鼓手前進，內裡的方向已經改變。第二，恩典在今生從不會完全運作（加 5:17）。即使在我們最慷慨的時刻，仍有一絲驕傲悄然滲入。連我們最公義的善行，也仍需基督的寶血遮蓋。

他繼而以約翰一書 3 章 9 節為錨：「凡從神生的，就不犯罪。」這裡的「犯罪」原指把罪當作生命的常業。信徒擁有新的本性（神的種存留在他裡面），這新性情無法與罪和平共處。這正是信徒最壞時仍與非信徒最佳時有本質區別的關鍵：信徒即使跌倒得最厲害，新心仍然恨惡所犯的罪。非信徒即使外表斯文，若無神普遍恩典的約束，將會更深地陷入罪中。

“The desire to obey him ordinarily prevails in us, even against this insidious enemy within.”
「縱有這個陰險的內在敵人，順服神的心願通常仍在我們裡面佔上風。」
— Kris Lundgaard, ch. 1

§ This Law Never Rests — 「這律從不止息」

English Paraphrase

Lundgaard distinguishes two layers of the believer's desire to do good. There is the general and constant desire to please God (Rom 7:18) — the underlying disposition of the renewed heart. And there are specific moments — when we set ourselves to a particular duty: private prayer, a tithed gift, an honest conversation, patience with a difficult child. The law of sin opposes both.

The general desire is in constant tug-of-war (the “law of sin and death” against the law of the Spirit of life, Rom 8:2). But the specific duty triggers a sharper attack. The moment we resolve to pray, drowsiness arrives. The moment we resolve to give, calculation arrives. The moment we resolve to speak honestly, a softer half-truth presents itself.

Hence Lundgaard's Jekyll-and-Hyde framing. Galatians 5:17 is the verse he reads through this lens: the flesh and the Spirit are opposed to each other — set against one another so that, in the apostle's words, we cannot do the very things we want to do. This is not failure of will; it is the daily anatomy of every believer who is also still a sinner.

The section ends on Paul's cry: Who shall deliver me? Lundgaard wants us to feel this cry on our own lips — not as a theological exercise, but as the desperate need that drives us to Christ.

繁體中文 撮要

Lundgaard 將信徒想要行善的渴望分為兩層。一層是整體、恆常的渴望——願討神喜悅（羅 7:18），這是被更新的心底層性向。另一層是具體的時刻——當我們立志要做某件事時：個人禱告、十分之一的奉獻、一次誠實的對話、對難相處兒女的耐心。罪的律兩層都反對。

整體的渴望，與罪的律處於持續拉鋸（羅馬書 8 章 2 節中「罪與死的律」對抗「賜生命聖靈的律」）。但具體的職責一旦被立定，攻擊便更銳利。我們決心要禱告——倦意立即湧上來。我們決心要奉獻——計算立刻湧上來。我們決心要誠實——一個比較柔和的「半真話」立刻浮上心頭。

所以 Lundgaard 引用「變身怪醫」（Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde）的比喻。他將加拉太書 5 章 17 節放在這視角下讀：情慾與聖靈彼此相敵——彼此對抗，以致正如使徒所言，我們不能行所願意行的。這不是意志的失敗，而是每一個既是信徒又仍是罪人的人——每天的真實寫照。

整段以保羅的呼喊作結：誰能救我脫離？ Lundgaard 想我們的口也親自發出這呼喊——不是當作神學練習，而是真切的迫切，把我們直接推向基督。

“Don't you sometimes feel like Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde? Every believer who is also a sinner (that is, every believer) does.”
「你有沒有時候覺得自己像變身怪醫？每一個既是信徒、亦仍是罪人的人（也就是說，每一個信徒），都有這感覺。」
— Kris Lundgaard, ch. 1

§ Our Wisdom — 「我們的智慧」（結語）

English Paraphrase

Lundgaard closes the chapter by stepping back to remind us where we are: at the beginning of a war. The four truths above are not a complete strategy; they are an arming for the fight that the rest of the book will guide. He insists, however, on one indispensable prior truth: alongside the law of sin, the believer must hold equally fast to the free, justifying grace of God won by Christ's blood. These are the two springs from which all of our holiness and sin, joy and sorrow, refreshment and trouble, flow.

He offers an extended kingdom analogy. Imagine a kingdom with two mighty opposing forces inside its walls — always plotting, always feuding. Without a wise king, that kingdom falls. The law of sin and the law of the Spirit of life (Rom 8:2) are precisely such mortal opponents within us. To manage our souls badly — that is, to ignore this internal war — is to invite the wreck of ourselves.

His most pointed line is also his most ordinary observation: we go to regular doctor's checkups; we work out at the gym four times a week; we count our calories. Yet how often do we give the same attentive, scheduled, disciplined care to our souls? If the body — soon enough to return to dust — is worth this much daily care, how much more the soul that lives forever?

Lundgaard's closing exhortation is therefore not defeatist but wise: getting to know indwelling sin, however humiliating, is the wisdom of those who actually want to find out what pleases the Lord (Eph 5:10) and avoid everything that grieves his Holy Spirit (Eph 4:30).

繁體中文 撮要

Lundgaard 在章末退後一步，提醒我們所站之地：這只是這場戰爭的開端。以上四個真理並非完整的策略，而是讓我們為接下來整本書所要展開的戰鬥裝備自己。但他同時堅持一個不可或缺的前提：在認識「罪的律」的同時，信徒也必須同等緊握住神在基督裡白白賜下的、稱義的恩典。這兩者，是我們一切聖潔與罪、喜樂與愁苦、平安與爭戰的兩個源頭。

他引申一個王國的比喻：設想一個城牆之內藏有兩股強大對立勢力的王國，二者不斷謀算、互相廝殺。若沒有一位有智慧的君王統管，這王國必歸荒廢。在我們裡面，罪的律與賜生命聖靈的律（羅 8:2）正是這樣不共戴天的兩股勢力。若我們不善於管理自己的靈魂——亦即漠視這場內戰——所招來的就是自我的崩潰。

他最尖銳的一段話，其實也是最平凡的觀察：我們會定期去看醫生；一週四次去健身室；計算卡路里。但有多少人對靈魂，付出同樣專注、有規律、有紀律的照顧？身體不久之後就要歸塵，仍值得這麼用心；那永遠存在的靈魂呢？

所以 Lundgaard 結尾的呼籲，並不是失敗主義，而是真正的智慧：認識內住之罪，無論如何令人羞愧，都是那些真正想知道甚麼是主所喜悅的（弗 5:10）、並避免一切叫聖靈擔憂之事的人（弗 4:30）——所必走的路。

“If it is important to watch over and care for our bodies, which will soon turn to dust, how much more important is it for us to guard our immortal souls?”
「我們連即將歸於塵土的身體，都看得這麼重要去照管；那永不朽壞的靈魂，豈不更值得我們守護？」
— Kris Lundgaard, ch. 1

💡 Four Key Truths 四個關鍵真理

  1. Indwelling sin is a “law” · 內住之罪是一個「律」

    Paul calls sin a law (Rom 7:21) because it has the same kind of force as gravity. It is not just an idea or an occasional impulse — it is a constant pressure, a power, a principle that constrains and compels. The metaphor is deliberate: it is both a rule that commands and a force that drives.
    保羅將罪稱為「律」（羅馬書 7 章 21 節），因為它具有像重力般的力量。它不只是觀念或偶發的衝動，而是恆常的壓力、能力、原則，會限制人、驅使人。這個比喻是刻意的：它既是命令我們的法規，亦是推動我們的能力。

  2. We find this law inside us · 我們在自己裡面發現這律

    It is one thing to hear sermons about sin; another to discover, in our own actions and desires, that this law is real. Paul moves from theory to experience in Rom 7:21 — he finds it. Lundgaard compares this to the difference between hearing about cancer and being diagnosed with it. Believers are the only ones who truly feel this law, because unbelievers float along with sin's current; only believers swim upstream.
    聽道講罪是一回事，從自己的言行慾望中親自發現這律的存在，又是另一回事。保羅在羅馬書 7 章 21 節由理論走入經歷——他發覺了它。Lundgaard 形容這種發現如同從聽人講解癌症，到被醫生告知「你患癌」的分別。唯有信徒能真正感受到這律的力量，因為非信徒順流而下；只有信徒逆流而上。

  3. We find this law when we're at our best · 我們最想行善時最察覺這律

    Paul says the sin he meets “close at hand” arrives when he wants to do right. It is not when we are indifferent that sin shows its full strength, but when we set ourselves to obey God — prayer, generous giving, honest speech, parenting in love. At those moments the law of sin opposes us most. Like Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde, the two natures are at war within every believer.
    保羅形容罪「就在手邊」，是在他想為善的時候出現。罪最強烈的反撲，不在我們冷淡之時，而是當我們立志順服神之時——禱告、奉獻、誠實言語、以愛養兒。就是在這些時刻，罪的律最劇烈地對抗我們。如同《變身怪醫》（Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde）所喻，兩種本性在每個信徒裡面交戰不息。

  4. This law never rests · 這律從不止息

    Sin is not a foe that retreats after one defeat. It works around the clock. It opposes both our general desire to please God (Rom 7:18) and every specific duty we set ourselves to (Rom 7:21). Because the war never stops, our vigilance must never stop either (Matt 26:41).
    罪不是被打敗一次就退場的敵人，它二十四小時運作。它既反抗我們整體上想討神喜悅的心願（羅 7:18），亦反抗我們每一件具體立志要做的善行（羅 7:21）。既然這場戰爭永不停息，我們的警醒也不能停息（太 26:41）。

“The law of sin is a raging river, carrying [unbelievers] along … Believers, however, swim upstream: they meet sin head-on and strain under its strength.”
—— Kris Lundgaard, The Enemy Within, ch. 1

「罪的律對〔非信徒〕而言，是一條洶湧的河，把他們捲走……但信徒卻是逆流而上——他們正面遇見罪，並在其力量下竭力掙扎。」

🤔 Thinking Questions 思考問題

The first seven questions are Lundgaard's. Questions 8–10 are for our group's deeper dialogue.
第一至七題出自原書；第八至十題是我們小組進一步討論用。

1. Observation 觀察 — Read Romans 7:14–25. Which phrase or phrases best describe your own experience?
慢讀羅馬書 7 章 14–25 節。哪一句或哪幾句，最能形容你自己的經歷？

2. Interpretation 解釋 — Can you think of ways the law of sin offers rewards for obeying it and threatens punishment for ignoring it?
你能想到哪些方式，罪的律會「獎賞」我們順從它，又「威嚇」我們若不順從就要承擔代價？

3. Reflection 反省 — Think of a time when you “found” the law of sin in you — when it seemed to take over and bend you to its will, even as it were, against your own will. Describe how it happened, as you understand it.
回想一次你「發覺」罪的律在你裡面——彷彿它接管了你，將你彎曲到順從它，即使違背你自己的意願。請用你自己的話描述當時的情形。

4. Reflection 反省 — What do you think is the most frustrating thing about the sin in your heart?
對你來說，心中之罪最令你沮喪的，是哪一方面？

5. Reflection 反省 — If it is true that the law of sin in you never rests, what hope do you have?
如果罪的律真的從不止息，你的盼望在哪裡？

6. Application 應用 — Read Luke 12:15 and Matthew 26:41. Considering this chapter, describe the daily diligence you need in order to heed Jesus's warnings.
讀路加福音 12 章 15 節和馬太福音 26 章 41 節。對照本章內容，描述你每日需要怎樣的儆醒，才能留意主耶穌的提醒？

7. Forward 行動 — What do you hope to gain through studying this book? Write a prayer asking God to make it so in your life.
你期望透過研讀本書得著甚麼？請寫一段禱文，求神在你生命中成就。

8. Group sharing 分享 — Lundgaard's refrigerator story is mundane and specific. Share one recent moment — also mundane — where you experienced something similar: trying to do something good, but the “alien” rose up. No need to dramatise; the small ones reveal the law just as clearly as the big ones.
Lundgaard 雪櫃那個故事，看似平凡而具體。請分享你最近一個同樣平凡的時刻，那時你正想做一件好事，卻有一個「外來者」湧上心頭。不需要誇張；小事跟大事一樣，都能清楚顯出這律的存在。

9. Group sharing 分享 — Lundgaard says we sometimes feel like Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde. Is there a specific area of your life where this two-natures struggle is most acute right now? (Work? Marriage? Parenting? Speech? Money? Phone use?)
Lundgaard 說我們有時感覺自己像《變身怪醫》中的 Jekyll 與 Hyde。在你目前的生命中，哪一個具體領域，這「兩種本性的爭戰」最為激烈？（工作？婚姻？教養？言語？金錢？手機使用？）

10. Mutual prayer 互相代禱 — Pair up and name one specific aspect of indwelling sin you'd like the group to lift up to God for you between now and our next session.
兩人一組，彼此分享一個你希望小組為你代禱的具體層面——求神在這次到下次聚會之間，在你身上動工。

🎯 Application 生活應用

English
  • Name it daily. Each morning, name one specific area where you expect the law of sin will press against you that day. Pre-empt it with prayer.
  • Listen to the “alien” moments. When you over-react, snap, or say something you regret — don't bury it. Pause and ask: what law was at work in me just then?
  • Tend the soul as you tend the body. If you would visit the doctor or gym for the body, give the same regular care to your soul: Word, prayer, confession, fellowship, sabbath.
  • Don't despair. The presence of indwelling sin is not proof you are unsaved — it is proof you are now aware. Unbelievers feel no current.
  • Stay close to grace. Self-effort intensifies the war but doesn't win it. Christ's blood — applied daily — is the only ground we stand on.
繁體中文
  • 每日點名。 每朝命名一個你預期罪的律當日會在哪方面壓向你的領域，先以禱告搶在前頭。
  • 聆聽「外來者」的時刻。 當你過度反應、發火、講了後悔的說話——不要掩埋。停一停，問自己：剛才那一刻，是哪一條律在我裡面運作？
  • 顧靈魂如顧身體。 你會為身體去看醫生、去健身室；亦要以同樣規律的心態餵養靈魂——讀經、禱告、認罪、團契、安息。
  • 不要絕望。 內住之罪的存在，不代表你未得救——反而證明你已被喚醒。非信徒感受不到那股水流。
  • 常靠恩典。 單靠自我努力只會令戰爭更激烈，卻不能得勝。唯有每日將基督的寶血應用在自己身上，才是我們站立得穩的根基。

🙏 Prayer 禱告

English
Father in heaven,

With Paul we cry, “What a wretched man I am! Who will rescue me?” Thank you that the answer is already given: “Thanks be to God, who delivers me through Jesus Christ our Lord.”

Open our eyes to see the law of sin at work in us — not so that we despair, but so that we fight. Help us to recognise it especially when we are trying our best to please you, for that is when it strikes hardest. Grant us watchfulness, grant us the daily discipline of grace, grant us honest fellowship with one another.

Where the alien rises in us, hold us. Where we feel weary, remind us that the war is yours, not ours. Make our hearts soft toward you, hard toward sin, and humble toward each other.

In the name of Jesus Christ, our Deliverer, Amen.
繁體中文
天上的父啊，

我們與保羅一同呼喊：「我真是苦啊！誰能救我脫離這取死的身體呢？」感謝祢，這答案早已賜下：「感謝神，靠著我們的主耶穌基督就能脫離了。」

當「外來者」在我們裡面湧現，求祢扶持我們。當我們疲倦時，求祢提醒：這場仗是祢的，不是我們的。願祢使我們的心對祢柔軟，對罪剛硬，對彼此謙卑。

奉我們救主耶穌基督的名祈求，阿們。

📚 Further Reading 延伸閱讀

John Owen, The Mortification of Sin (1656) · 約翰·歐文，《治死罪》

The Puritan classic that Lundgaard distils. Dense but life-changing. Owen's famous rule: “Be killing sin or it will be killing you.”
清教徒經典，正是 Lundgaard 所要重新表達的源頭。文字密實但能改變生命。歐文名言：「你不治死罪，罪就治死你。」

John Owen, Indwelling Sin in Believers (1668) · 約翰·歐文，《信徒心中的內住之罪》

Owen's longer companion to Mortification — exposits Romans 7 in depth. The book Lundgaard is most directly compressing.
《治死罪》的姊妹篇，深入闡釋羅馬書 7 章。Lundgaard 本書最直接取材的對象。

J. I. Packer, Keep in Step with the Spirit (1984), pp. 263–270 · 巴刻，《與聖靈同步》

Lundgaard cites Packer's reading of Romans 7 as a description of the believer's experience, not the unbeliever's. Helpful background for question 5.
Lundgaard 引用巴刻：羅馬書 7 章描述的是信徒的經歷，而非未信者。問題第五題的背景閱讀。

John R. W. Stott, Men Made New: An Exposition of Romans 5–8 (1966), pp. 71–75 · 斯托得，《新造的人——羅馬書 5–8 章解經》

A concise, pastoral exposition of Romans 7. Useful complementary read for Question 1.
羅馬書 7 章簡明而具牧者心腸的釋經。第一題的補充材料。

Sinclair Ferguson, The Christian Life · 傅格森，《基督徒生命》

Wider Reformed treatment of sanctification, putting indwelling sin within the larger arc of growth in grace.
從更廣闊的改革宗角度看成聖之道，將「內住之罪」放入「在恩典中成長」的整體框架。
