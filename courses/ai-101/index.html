About the book關於本書

It began as a daily series of short posts. Here they are rebuilt into one book, in an order that teaches: what AI is, how to talk to it, what happens inside, how to give it your own knowledge, how to make it act, how to run it well, and whether to trust it.

本書原是一系列每日短文，現按學習次序重新編排成書：AI 是甚麼、如何與它溝通、內部如何運作、如何把你的知識交給它、如何讓它動手做事、如何運行得順暢而划算，以及可否信任它。

If you would rather build an agent of your own, the one-to-one course starts where this book ends.

如想親手建立自己的代理人，一對一課程正好由本書的終點開始。
