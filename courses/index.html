Class 1第一步
Basic基礎班

Your own AI agent你的私人 AI 代理人

Five one-to-one lessons online. We start from an agent that already works, open it up to see how it runs, then reshape it step by step into an assistant that is yours alone. Very little code.

五堂一對一網上課。由一個已經能運作的代理人開始，拆開來看清它的原理，再一步步改造成只屬於你的助理。所需編程極少。

Format形式One to one, online一對一，網上授課
Length堂數5 lessons共 5 堂
Times時間Shown in UK and Hong Kong time同時顯示英國及香港時間
Why背景

The layer underneath an agent (memory, scheduling, a control panel, messaging) used to take weeks to put together. Now it is all there in the first lesson, so the course spends its time on the part that matters: making the agent do your work.

代理人的底層（記憶、排程、控制面板、通訊）以往要花上數星期才能搭建。現在第一堂已一應俱全，課程的時間便可全部用在真正有價值的部分：讓代理人做你的工作。

Two tracks兩個版本

One course, the same five lessons and the same agent design. The only difference is the tool you build with.

同一個課程，五堂內容及代理人架構完全相同，分別只在於所用的工具。

Track A

Hermes + Ollama

An open-source agent with no Claude account needed. For places where Claude is not available, such as Hong Kong and mainland China.

開源代理人，毋須 Claude 帳戶。適合未能使用 Claude 的地區，例如香港及中國內地。

Track B

Claude app

Built in Anthropic's Claude app, which needs a Claude Pro plan. For places where Claude is available, such as the UK.

使用 Anthropic 的 Claude 應用程式，需訂閱 Claude Pro 月費計劃。適合可使用 Claude 的地區，例如英國。

Not sure which one? Choose "not sure" when you book and we decide together before the first lesson.

未能決定？報名時選擇「未知」，我們會在第一堂前一同決定。

Lessons課堂
  1. Boot it up開機Install the agent and let it interview you.安裝代理人，讓它即場訪問你。
  2. Anatomy拆解原理Lift the lid and see how it actually works.揭開外殼，看清它實際如何運作。
  3. Rebuild it一同改造Make a workflow out of your own material.用你自己的資料建立一套工作流程。
  4. Give it hands加上工具Email, calendar, documents and online services.電郵、日曆、文件及雲端服務。
  5. One of a kind獨一無二Shape its control panel and keep it on call.設計它的控制面板，讓它隨傳隨到。
What you leave with成果

Not an assistant you downloaded, but one you changed yourself: it knows your work, remembers, connects to your tools and has a control panel of its own.

不是一個下載回來的助理，而是你親手改造、獨一無二的代理人：熟悉你的工作、有記憶、連接你的工具，並有屬於自己的控制面板。

From tender to handover由投標到交樓

準備中 · Coming soon

You take one project from the tender to handover, using your own agent at every stage, with Revit.

你會用自己的代理人，配合 Revit，把一個項目由投標一路做到交樓。

Length堂數5 lessons共 5 堂
Fee學費HK$2,000 for the whole course全期 HK$2,000
Status狀態Coming soon, not yet open for sign-up準備中，暫未接受報名
Before you join入讀條件

You need to finish the basic class first. Exemptions are possible on application; the details will come when the class opens.

須先完成基礎班。可申請豁免，詳情將於開班時公布。

What you need所需準備
  • Your own Windows PC with Revit自己的 Windows 電腦，並已安裝 Revit
  • Homework handed in the day before each lesson每堂的功課須於上堂前一天交回
Lessons課堂
  1. Tender投標Read the tender, check compliance and write the technical proposal and fee forms.閱讀招標文件、核對合規要求，撰寫技術建議書及收費表格。
  2. Feasibility可行性研究Site constraints, floor area and massing options, and a feasibility statement.地盤限制、樓面面積及體量方案，並撰寫可行性報告。
  3. Scheme and statutory submission方案設計及法定審批The scheme in Revit, the general building plans and a reply to the authority's comments.以 Revit 建立方案，製作一般建築圖則，並回覆審批部門意見。
  4. Detailed design and works tender詳細設計及工程招標LOD 300 drawings, tender documents and a tender report.LOD 300 圖則、招標文件及回標分析報告。
  5. Construction to handover施工至交樓Site minutes, RFIs, a defects list and the handover pack.工地會議紀錄、資料索取（RFI）、缺陷清單及交樓文件。
