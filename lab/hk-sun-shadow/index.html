HK Sun Shadow 香港日照陰影

Place a block, pick a day, see whose sun it takes. 放一座樓，選一天，看看它遮擋了誰的陽光。

Teaching sketch only 教學草圖 Not a sunlight, daylight or right-to-light study, and not for any submission. Buildings are simple boxes from Lands Department block heights; no trees, balconies or fins. 並非日照或採光研究，不可用於入則或任何正式用途。

1 Place 地點

    Or click the map. A 400 m square around the point is loaded. 或直接點選地圖。

    2 Your block 你的建築

    Click corners on the plan; click the first corner (or double-click) to close. 在平面圖逐點點選，點回第一點即完成。

    Drag the block on the plan to move it. Existing blocks under it are taken as cleared. 在平面圖拖動即可移位；其下原有建築視作已拆卸。

    3 Day and time 日期及時間

    4 Show 顯示

    5 Neighbours in its shadow 受影響的鄰近建築

    Sun-facing walls of neighbouring blocks, checked every 10 min while the sun is at least 5° up. 鄰近建築向陽的外牆，太陽仰角 5° 以上時每 10 分鐘檢查一次。

      Limits 限制

      • Shadows from block outlines and their recorded top heights, on a 2 m grid, over the 5 m terrain model. Hills more than 200 m outside the square are not counted. 採用地政總署建築物輪廓及頂部高度，2 米格網，5 米地形。
      • Existing shade is shown so you can see what your block adds; it is still a sketch. 原有陰影只供比較。
      • For a real scheme, a qualified consultant runs a proper sunlight and daylight study. 正式項目須由合資格顧問進行日照及採光研究。
