Spec Builder HK 建築規範產生器

Draft a Hong Kong architectural specification set the way practices issue them. Answer four questions on the statutory route, Use Class, licence and delivery, pick one of 13 building types, then adjust the clauses, rooms, doors and materials. The export is a set: cover and issue sheet, a statement adopting the ArchSD General Specification by reference, a Particular Specification for each trade numbered to the GS sections, material data sheets, a Finishes Schedule, a Door and Ironmongery Schedule and an Open Items list, all linked by codes. The specification is in English.

    How it works
    1. Drivers first. In Hong Kong the submission route, the FS Code Use Class, the licence and the delivery mode move more clause text than the building type does. The four answers set the fire rating, the licence clauses and the A&A, MiC, heritage and notice clauses.
    2. Building type. Thirteen types in three groups tick the Particular Specification sections and clauses that usually apply, and load default rooms, doors, hardware sets and materials.
    3. Source marks. Every clause says why it is there: S statutory, LC licence condition, LL landlord rule or BP best practice. Strip the BP layer for a lean tender or keep it for quality.
    4. Set, not a single document. The export follows the way HK practices issue specifications: GS adopted by reference with its section numbers, a Particular Specification per trade (PS-18 for Finishes, and so on), each with its own page numbers and revision log, then data sheets and schedules keyed by codes.
    5. Open items. Anything not yet decided appears in the documents as TBC with an item number and is listed in the Open Items document, so the clause text never shows raw blanks.
    6. Your office master. An office can import its own clause library in the same JSON shape. Clauses with the same id replace ours, new ones are added. It stays in this browser.
    Limits
    • Own wordsNo text from the ArchSD General Specification for Building, the Housing Authority Specification Library, NBS or any standard is reproduced. The GS is adopted by reference using its section numbers and titles only.
    • Statutory valuesValues from codes, practice notes and licence guides are defaults with their source marked. The project's Authorized Person and consultants confirm them for each building.
    • GapsHong Kong has no statutory source for acoustics, glazing safety classes, kitchen slip ratings or wet-area waterproofing outside guesthouses and MiC. Those clauses are marked BP and are the first to review.
    • LanguageThe specification is English only, as HK tender specifications are. Where a statute requires a bilingual sign, the clause asks for the Chinese wording on the sign itself.
    • Local AIA clause drafted by your local model is marked "AI draft, verify" in the tool and in every export until you clear the mark.
