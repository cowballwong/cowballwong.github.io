About關於我

I am Anzon Wong, an architect based in the United Kingdom. I am from Hong Kong.

我是 Anzon Wong，現居英國的建築師，來自香港。

Architecture建築

I have worked on hotels, restaurants, shops and homes in Hong Kong, mainland China and London, from the first sketch through to facade and interior detail.

Today my work sits on the safety side of design: Building Regulations, and the Principal Designer duties under the CDM Regulations and the Building Safety Act.

我曾參與香港、中國內地及倫敦的酒店、餐廳、零售店及住宅項目，由最初的草圖一直做到外牆及室內細部。

現時我的工作著重設計中的安全範疇：建築規例，以及《建築（設計及管理）規例》與《建築安全法》下主要設計師的職責。

Agents and teaching代理人與教學

In my own time I build AI agents and small tools for architects, and I teach people to build agents of their own, one to one. The tools in the Lab are hobby projects and say so on every page.

業餘時間，我為建築師製作 AI 代理人及小工具，並以一對一形式教人建立自己的代理人。實驗室內的工具均屬個人興趣項目，每頁都有清楚說明。

AW Design

AW Design is the label I put on my own design work and tools.

I work in English and Cantonese, and write in English and Traditional Chinese.

AW Design 是我個人設計作品及工具所用的名稱。

我以英語及粵語工作，以英文及繁體中文書寫。
