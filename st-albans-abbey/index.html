    About

    Seventeen centuries on one hill, from Roman Verulamium to today's cathedral, as an interactive model. Drag the timeline or press play to watch the abbey being built, rebuilt and surrounded by its town.

    anzonwong.com

    Anzon Wong is an architect living in St Albans. His website gathers his architecture portfolio, interactive project showcases, hobby tools such as St Albans Live, and his AI course.

    How it was made and sources

    The cathedral is modelled from the 1908 Victoria County History ground plan (measured to its scale bar) and John Carter's c.1813 sections, with heights from Grimthorpe's published figures; some heights are estimates. Town buildings come from OpenStreetMap and appear on the timeline at estimated dates. The Roman street grid is schematic; only the surviving wall segments are real.
