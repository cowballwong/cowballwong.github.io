02為甚麼是現在 · Why now

不必等 AI 成熟，先由一項工作開始。

No need to wait for AI to mature. Start with one task.

  1. 初稿，AI 已經做得到

    門窗表、規格條款、回標比較，這類文件工作的初稿，AI 現在已經可以做。同事只需要審閱和修改。AI can already draft this kind of document work: schedules, specification clauses, tender comparisons. Your team reviews and corrects.

  2. 不會過時的，是你們自己的東西

    工具每隔幾個月就換一代，但你們的圖則、文件和做事方法不會過時。現在由小處開始，這些資料就一點一點整理好，下一代工具來了也用得上。Tools change every few months. Your drawings, documents and way of working do not go out of date. Start small now and they get built up, ready for whatever tool comes next.

  3. 風險，留在你們手上

    拍板和簽名的永遠是建築師；資料留在公司裡；先做一項工作，每一步都可以停下來。The architect always decides and signs. The data stays in the office. Start with one task, and stop after any step.

03AI Agent · Agents at work

文件在樓層之間流動，不再在電郵裡迷路。

Documents move between floors, not lost between emails.

每個 AI Agent守住一層：讀圖、填表、對照條款、整理回標。完成的文件交到上一層，每一步都留下紀錄，誰做過甚麼、改過甚麼，一查便知。

Each agent holds one floor: reading drawings, filling schedules, checking clauses, sorting tender returns. Finished work goes up to the next floor, and every step leaves a record of who changed what.

04BIM 碰撞檢查 · Clash triage

幾千個碰撞結果，收窄到三十個問題。

2,847碰撞結果 → 待決定問題Clash results → issues to decide

一個 BIM 模型跑一次碰撞檢查，動輒幾千個結果，大部分是同一個問題重複出現。AI Agent先歸類、去重、排次序，剩下真正要處理的，才交回給人。

One clash run can return thousands of results, most of them the same problem repeated. The agent groups, de-duplicates and ranks them, then hands back the few that matter.
